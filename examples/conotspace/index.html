Not all is not none

In a closed, enumerated group of four, seeing one counterexample can refute all while three examples still exist. An incomplete or open-ended search cannot justify none. Negation needs its quantifier, scope and time.

An open-world catalogue does not convert an unlisted edge into a false edge. A qualified, time-bounded absence witness may support a local negative claim; it cannot establish universal nonexistence. Conceptual, spiritual, nonmaterial and counterfactual ideas are permitted as labeled hypotheses, not demonstrated physical facts. No private records or personal information are included.
