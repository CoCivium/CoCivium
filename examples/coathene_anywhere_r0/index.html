    What's actually optional?

    Learning can be personal, cooperative, facilitated or institutionally supported. A physical venue, synchronous timetable, online link, XR headset, organization or permanent Clan are not prerequisites to exploring the public lesson. A real school, credential, child supervision or funded programme is a separate qualified service, not something this page silently creates.

    Virtual sessions are designed as temporary experiences around a stable question and context. A local printable learning object should remain useful if a browser, platform or school building is unavailable.
