01 / Scale without fictional pixels
Semantic LOD adds different *kinds* of information at deeper layers. A finite display cannot expose infinite detail. At the evidence boundary, zoom stops inventing content.
02 / The spaces between
Typed edges, uncertainty, inversions and absent links carry information too. Reverse edges are candidate questions, not logically proven inverse statements.
03 / Render and not-render
Authorized, relevant, visible, budgeted payloads may render. Offscreen, expensive, unqualified, private, stale or machine-only payloads can stay as typed data.
