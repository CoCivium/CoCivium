The human test

Could someone unfamiliar with CoCivium answer: What can it do now? Did it work? How much effort did it save? Does it recover tomorrow?

Until a real task and baseline are measured, the honest answer to the last two is we do not yet know.

Green: a bounded check has a real receipt. Amber: an acceptance requirement is unresolved. Unknown: no measured basis; not zero. Counter-needle: failures and human effort are presented alongside successful tests. A product-level gain requires receiver-side usefulness, cost and time comparison, and recovery evidence.

Do not infer an exponential: a comparable multiday time series, stable units, independent measurement and normalization are required.
