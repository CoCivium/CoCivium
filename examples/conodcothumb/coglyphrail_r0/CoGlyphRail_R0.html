Human-readable meaning always survives

The glyph is a navigation aid. Labels, typed endpoints, source identity, time, evidence class, rights, and alternatives remain mandatory. CoMap, CoOrb, CoBar, CoStead and future devices may eventually render the same evidence-bound record, subject to their own receiver and permission tests.

R0 boundary: Local browser file, no native mouse binding, global input capture, network calls, live graph edges, persistent memory, automatic worker spawning, private data reading, effects, delivery or ACK. The MX Master 4 middle thumb remains proposed as CoNod; native middle-click, existing top-middle Ctrl+0 and thumb-wheel mappings are unchanged. This model extends existing CoNod/CoThumb and CoInterstice/CoGlyph donors as a candidate vocabulary rather than another input controller.
