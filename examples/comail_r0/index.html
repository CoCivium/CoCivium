03 · Proof and boundary

What this example proves, and does not

Shows: fixed message identity, three content-hashed versions, exact predecessor links, visible source differences, an explicit current pointer, and an optional unsent feedback capsule. A reader can choose an older version without losing the newest one.

Does not show: a sent email, verified consent, automatic publication, a payment processor, community adoption, recipient feedback, an authenticated editor, hosted live updates, or delivery receipts.

Other CoAll relatives: CoEvergreen+ tracks lineage; CoLivingHighlight+ renders source-backed versions; CoMap+ maps relationships; CoOrbWare+ selects views; CoReach+ tracks actual delivery; CoTurtle+ challenges new claims; CoFree+ separates knowledge access from voluntary support.

No donation solicitation operates here. Contributions or publicity must never purchase epistemic authority. View a static Atom feed example (not a subscribed or hosted service).
