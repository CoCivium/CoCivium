# CoMediaGraph+ | CoCulturalSubstrate+ | CoConnectables R0

**Decision:** a single typed relationship model can expose *connections* across diagrams, SVG heroes, film, audio, time, diagrams-of-diagrams, physical evidence, cultural narratives and future media. It must NOT assume those media are semantically equivalent, equally authoritative or available as live assets.

**Source of this request:** user's 2026-11-09 session, building from public candidate CoWet+ and CoSeed/CoPath work. This is a PUBLIC-SAFE design delta, not a complete capture of the attached WhatsApp history.

## Evidence-to-experience path
`WorkID / CoSeed → typed CoPath relation → medium-specific anchor → render/interpret → provenance/challenge → reviewed CoEcho`

Each connection has a source reference, direction, predicate, projection or interpretation type, author/reviewer (nullable), permission, temporal anchor, confidence and evidence state. A reverse path can find who references a source, **but not invert the predicate's truth**.

## Media connector requirements
- **Diagram:** stable IDs for nodes, ports, edges, relation type, alternate layouts and an accessible text representation.
- **Image / hero:** asset digest, meaningful regions/hotspots, crop or focal point, alt text, media ownership/licence, source/interpretation link.
- **Video:** timecode ranges and shot IDs, captions, transcription, still fallback, provenance, rights and consent; don't invent a video from an HTML sample.
- **Audio / sonification:** segment time, synthesis source, transcript/cues as appropriate, explicit audio permission, immediate CoAudioFkOff+, hard-silent by default.
- **Timeline:** authored/publication time vs event/idea time vs observation time; estimated dates marked as estimated.
- **Cultural substrate:** language, context, community provenance, interpretation, ambiguity and right to challenge/correct; do not treat culturally situated meaning as a solved universal equivalence.
- **Spatial / physical substrate:** location/coordinate reference and precision, consent for private places, calibration and unit provenance, reversible render.
- **Speculative / G_dSide:** `HYPOTHESIS` with alternative explanations, optional test and falsifier, not a privileged authority.

## Not all rich media are created equal
Media representations may differ in crucial ways. The shared graph preserves *reference and evidence*; expressive freedom remains at the edges. The graph is a routing/index substrate, not a machine claiming to own artistic meaning.

## CoWave diversity and career-style work
Workers may own different bounded workstreams, *not autonomous employment or unsupervised authority*. Better parallel decomposition: (A) precise evidence/receiver, (B) accessible human hero, (C) real media annotation/rights, (D) historical CoEcho, (E) adversarial competitor challenge, (F) cultural translation review. Each lane has distinct objective, declared tools, permissions, test, stop condition, conflict fence, public-safe receipt. Multiple independent reviewers may challenge the same result. A self-named 'career' cannot grant resource, financial, messaging or public-deployment privileges.
