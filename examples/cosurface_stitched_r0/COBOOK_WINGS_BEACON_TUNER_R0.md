## CoSeed | One Golden Thread
A person can regain play, wonder and option-making **while alive**. CoGoldenThread connects the narrative, the sonic beacon and the tactile tuner; CoBraid preserves their different expressive roles. Each presentation points to this same interpretive seed while retaining its own authored meaning.

## Screen / page 1 | Child-readable two-panel diagram
1. **Wings are constrained:** A very young child is drawn with translucent symbolic wings. Around them, non-villainous clocks, rules, social expectations, timetables and boxes gradually fold or tug feathers away. Emotion: loss without despair, not a depiction of actual mutilation.
2. **Wings regrow in life:** The same person older, visibly alive, chooses to play, question a rule and recover wonder. The first new feathers appear while the social world remains present. Emotion: agency and hope.
3. Proposed captions: **“We begin with wings.”** / **“We can grow them back, one feather at a time.”**
4. Alternative interpretations remain open. “Wings” are metaphor, not a statement that death is required or desirable. The central claim is about living reengagement.

## Screen / page 2 | CoBeacon
- Start with a **three-note seed**, then permit many interacting layers: harmonies, dissonance, irregular pulses, silence, bright/harsh/soft timbres, evolving density, and user feedback.
- Mood words like *lonely*, *whale-like*, *hopeful* and *otherworldly* describe subjective experience and creative direction. They are not claims that whales have witnessed past civilizations or that ET contacts have occurred.
- Reactive inputs must be authorized, legible and bounded; sensory experience alone does not prove consciousness.
- **Audio consent:** silent default; user-initiated audition; independent cap and immediate CoAudioFkOff+, no continuous covert listening or inference of emotional state without appropriate consent.
- Low-data, no-sound and reduced-motion alternatives remain first-class. Define accessible captions and visual cue maps.

## Screen / page 3 | CoTuner
- Retro-futurist dial with chunky physicality, readable tick marks, tactile/keyboard interaction and a visible current channel.
- Channels as hypotheses: heartbeat/pulse, history/memory, discovery/anomaly, harmony/discord, playful synthesis, and separately flagged symbolic/G_dSide.
- **Sticky** = observable dwell/return behavior, not magical preference-reading. Retention itself is not consent to profiling. Keep tuning transparent, resettable, locally scoped where possible.
- Interaction loop: **turn → preview → choose → listen/observe → remain or leave → inspect the source**.
- One-turn “quiet/dry” switch and explicit channel information before any sound.
