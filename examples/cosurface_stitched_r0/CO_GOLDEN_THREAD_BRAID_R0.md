# CoGoldenThread+ / CoBraid+ R0 | Preserve Every Known Front Without Collapsing It

**Status:** public-safe source-candidate contract. Not a complete history, governing CoAll canon, live agent scheduler or proof that all proposed fronts have active workers.

## Golden-thread invariant
For every **discovered** source artifact, front, hypothesis, media projection or challenge, retain a recoverable identity and relation to its own evidence. Never silently treat "not active" as "does not exist." Never treat "named" as proof of live runtime. Unknown history stays **UNKNOWN / HELD**, not certified complete.

## The braid, not the blob
- **CoThread:** immutable reference to a source revision; include a source locator, digest when independently available, scope, event/observation time, and authority.
- **CoStrand:** a bounded front with an independent purpose, WorkID, active/held status, optional worker and next test. "Front" is not identical to "agent" or "daemon".
- **CoBraid:** directed typed relationships among threads/strands, including `DERIVED_FROM`, `CHALLENGES`, `ALTERNATIVE_TO`, `PROJECTS_AS`, `DEPENDS_ON`, `SUPERSEDES_WITH_PROOF`, `REQUIRES_REVIEW`, and `FUTURE_OPTION`. Reverse traversal shows incoming references, not reversible truth.
- **CoGolden:** source continuity and custody invariant crossing all presentations and media. Multiple contradictory hypotheses may remain linked without forced consensus.
- **CoEcho / CoTime:** recover history in observed chronological order while distinguishing source creation, event, observation, landing and pickup times. A partial sample is not all history.
- **CoBoogie / CoWave:** fan-out over genuinely different hypotheses, tests, devices or human needs. Fan-in only evidence and differences; retain independent lineage, negative results and rollback.
- **CoMediaGraph:** a single source can have diagram, text, image, hero, video, sonification or culturally situated interpretation, but projections have separate provenance and rights and are not semantic duplicates by default.
- **CoHog / CoChallenge:** challenges are first-class source-linked alternatives. Preserve failures, dissent and negative information gain, not just optimistic outcomes.
- **CoNull / CoBreakGlass:** hold or stop unsafe/unknown transitions without deleting the source or dangling the graph edge.

## Minimum per-strand record
`strand_id | parent_ids | source_refs | scope | status | purpose | current_work_id | evidence_refs | relations | observed_at | next_discriminating_test | owner_or_receiver | receiver_ack | supersession_refs | public_safe_projection`.

All fields have explicit meaning; a null receiver or missing hash must remain null. Privacy-gated entries must not leak into the public graph. Public-safe projections can point to a separately authorized disclosure procedure without revealing private identifiers.

## Lifecycle and source truth
`DISCOVERED_LOCAL → VERIFIED_LOCAL → LANDED → PICKED_UP → INTEGRATED → COEX`.
Every transition requires its own exact-object evidence. GitHub source readback is not evidence that X2, CoStead, Meta AI or other sessions independently integrated it. "All fronts" is a requested coverage target, not a verified count.

## CoThread challenge / QA
1. Can each sampled public source be located through the registry, then its parent and child references?
2. Can a held strand be rediscovered after a restart without masquerading as active?
3. Can two contrary proposals remain distinct while contributing to one shared review?
4. Can an alternative medium follow a source reference without inheriting false authority?
5. Can a reviewer trace a partial retro branch to its exact evidence and identify missing coverage?
6. Does a privacy or authority gate prevent public disclosure and fake receiver ACK?
7. Can duplicate contributions be associated with prior receipts rather than overwriting them?
