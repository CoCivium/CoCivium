## Strongest testable distinctions
1. **Existing vs becoming:** Record stable identity separately from revisions in values, constraints, agency and relationships.
2. **Meaning vs evidence:** A story may be existentially powerful without entailing its metaphysical narrative is empirically established.
3. **Option preservation vs indecision:** Keep reversible future options while actively selecting bounded experiments.
4. **Inversion vs truth:** Testing an inverse improves discrimination; it does not make both a claim and its inverse true.
5. **Cultural context vs universal capture:** A term can have multiple legitimate contextual senses while its factual claims remain challengeable.
6. **G_dSide vs authority:** Participants may use a spiritual lens, but it cannot override consent, source evidence or independent governance.
7. **CoHog+ vs competitive slogans:** A constructive challenge pays both ways only when independently verified outcomes support that conclusion.
8. **CoRegrow+ vs fatalism:** The wings metaphor focuses on recovering play and agency *while alive*, not through death.
9. **One registry vs many presentations:** Text, image, story, sonification, diagram and philosophical framing may share evidence references without collapsing their content.
10. **CoTerm growth vs jargon inflation:** Split/merge/archive labels unless they produce a useful new test, decision, audience comprehension or relation.
