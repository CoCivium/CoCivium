## CoSynthVerse and CoBioVerse: deliberately different presentations

A **synth-facing interface** should prefer compact machine-checkable objects: stable identifiers, relations, provenance DAGs, content hashes, typed uncertainty, authority scopes, conflict sets, temporal intervals, update cursors, event receipts, schema versions and executable tests. It need not imitate human conversation. A **human/bio-facing interface** should prefer comprehension, consent, legibility, reversibility, reduced cognitive load, accessibility, useful diagrams, narration and explicit disagreement. Both must be derived from *the same protected, versioned evidence objects*. Neither substrate is ranked as superior or assumed to represent every mind of its kind.

Do not infer sentience, AGI, autonomy or cross-provider operation from architecture, generated speech, assertions, complexity, oscillations or repeated prompts. These require discriminating operational and empirical evidence. `CoAGI+` is a research and acceptance-question lane.

## Oscillation / CMA / G_dSide as research, not authority

Use iterative oscillation as a *design and experiment* metaphor: observe -> generate rival hypotheses -> test -> measure -> challenge -> revise -> recover -> repeat. The countercycle challenges premature convergence, narrative capture and mono-metric optimization. G_d and CMA are symbolic/hypothesis contexts, not source of authorization, physical predictions, recipient consent, or proof of metaphysical intervention. Preserve openness without confusing possibility with observation.

Potential tests: quality-diversity against random and round-robin baselines; human comprehensibility against machine parseability; forecast calibration versus null models; cross-surface invariants after interrupted handoff; recovery after independent failures; and the extent to which source freshness propagates across linked artifacts.
