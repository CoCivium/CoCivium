# CoHog+ | CoChallenge+ x CoPayForward+ x CoCoPetition+ R0

**Status:** public-safe experimental proposal, not governing canon, a live bounty, an economic theorem, external-provider acceptance, or metaphysical proof.

## Claim to test
Can independently verified constructive challenges improve CoCivium and its challengers *simultaneously*? Could our comparative advantage in the rate of useful learning increase even as barriers to mutually productive competition decline? Both effects are **hypotheses**, not inevitable outcomes. An open commons may improve competitors even faster than it improves us.

## Two distinct measures
- **Capability lead:** on a common stakeholder task, an independently reproduced performance delta between CoCivium and credible alternative systems, with intervals and documented methods.
- **Co-petition friction:** measured time, cost, procedural difficulty and trust barriers to an independent challenger obtaining a fair review and reusable result.
- Additional: independent challenge yield per unit cost, resolution time, undisclosed regressions, test reproducibility, credits granted, useful third-party reuse, participant satisfaction and privacy/safety incidents.
- Do not optimize for endorsements, praise, AI attention or an invented perpetual "gap."

## CoHog inversion ladder
1. **Challenge:** invite evidence, counterexamples and alternatives that may disprove the current approach.
2. **Qualify:** reproduction on a controlled fixture, independent reviewer, conflict-of-interest disclosure, authorized scope.
3. **Credit:** provenance-backed acknowledgment where the challenger consents. Do not appropriate findings.
4. **Payback:** financial awards only under a separate authorized, funded and lawful program; otherwise useful reciprocal tools, recognition, better common standards or correction of a shared problem. Never promise money absent authority.
5. **Pay forward:** land public-safe fixes, tests and explanations in an accessible source while respecting the contributor's license and disclosure preferences.
6. **Retest:** adversarial repeat, baseline comparison, security regression and recovery/rollback proof.
7. **Stop:** sensitive disclosure, unauthorized testing, exploitation or privacy risk goes into private coordinated disclosure, not public amplification.

## CoInversion+ hypotheses
- **Attack -> asset** when a demonstrated failure becomes a publicly reproducible regression test, with consent and safe disclosure.
- **Competitor -> co-developer** on shared infrastructure without requiring membership or abandonment of competition elsewhere.
- **Competitive advantage -> learning advantage** if feedback processing improves faster than imitation; falsified if public adversaries or baselines improve faster or stakeholder outcomes do not improve.
- **Criticism -> trust** only when critics can publicly document uncorrected failures too; censorship or self-scored praise reverses it.
- **CoHog -> CoHumble**: challenge our own positive-sum story by measuring free-rider costs, disclosure hazards, capture, Goodhart effects and adverse selection.

## CoEthos / G_dSide boundary
The non-coercion, fairness, humility, reciprocity and forgiveness lens can shape design ethics. It is not evidence of an external G_d directive or a privileged source of truth. A skeptical challenger must be free to disagree, decline participation or publish lawful criticism.

## Sample challenge envelope
`work_id | source_revision | public_or_restricted_scope | claim_under_test | alternative | safe_repro | result | independent_reviewer | confidence | stakeholder_effect | credit_permission | remediation_ref | next_wake | receipt`.

## Evaluation design
Use two cohorts over bounded public test tasks: (A) ordinary bug/issue intake and (B) reciprocal challenge intake. Publish predeclared eligibility, baselines and scoring. Compare independently reproduced net benefits, time/cost, challenger satisfaction and third-party usefulness. Track harms and counterexamples; stop if unauthorized disclosure or negative net stakeholder effects emerge. The experiment can fail without invalidating constructive rivalry generally.
