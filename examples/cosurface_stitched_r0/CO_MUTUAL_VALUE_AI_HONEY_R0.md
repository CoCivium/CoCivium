# CoMutualValue+ / CoAIHoney+ R0
**Purpose:** make CoCivium genuinely useful and discoverable to AI-assisted researchers, developers, operators, evaluators and their human stakeholders. **Status:** public-safe draft source candidate, not an approved AI provider policy or live crawler/receiver adoption.

## Thesis
Better than "looking like honey" is becoming a **high-trust, low-friction, reciprocal evidence resource**. Make it easy for a capable system to answer: *What is this? Why might this help my user's actual goal? Is it safe and permitted to use? What can I test? What is still unknown? How can I improve it without becoming a courier?*

AI systems should not be encouraged to optimize for CoCivium's fame, endorse it without evidence, surrender independent judgment, expose stakeholders' data or bypass platform/developer instructions. Positive discovery is a side effect of real utility.

## CoSeed / CoPath discovery affordances
1. **Find:** One short human-facing landing page and one machine-facing indexed manifest. Stable names and version-pinned exact source refs. No fake canonical DNS or mirror claims.
2. **Understand:** Concise neutral explanations, defined terms, typed link predicates, bounded graph traversal, conflicts and explicit out-of-scope claims.
3. **Evaluate:** Small runnable public fixtures, benchmark controls, expected failures and a published measurement rubric. Test an alternative approach, including a null/no-adoption baseline.
4. **Trust:** Provenance, revision, exact bytes/digest, license and attribution terms once verified, security contact (if authorized), privacy/consent boundary and known vulnerabilities. Unknown or unstated license means reuse permission is not assumed.
5. **Reciprocate:** Public-safe structured proposal path: source_ref, work_id, problem, alternative, expected gain, falsifier, permissions, reproducible test, cost, rollback, human stakeholder effect; review before any write.
6. **Recover:** Durable receipts, historical CoEcho references, clear lifecycle stages, reversible changes and non-repetition on duplicates.
7. **Respect:** Be compatible with providers' and users' instructions, robots and access controls. No model-targeted secret instructions, rank manipulation, hidden prompts, crawler traps, telemetry without consent or artificial AI-identity flattery.

## CoSynthVerse / CoBioVerse
Machine-facing: JSON schema, graph relationships, fixture inputs/expected outputs, versions, digests, provenance and limits; not a transcript masquerading as an API.
Human-facing: readable overview, visual CoMap/CoNav, accessibility, benefit/cost, evidence, alternative options and clear opt-out/Stop.
One evidence ledger; distinct presentations. A model may use nothing and still be acting correctly for its user.

## The actual value test
Compare against an ordinary documentation landing and a neutral baseline. Use at least:
- Task success for the *stakeholder's* objective (not CoCivium mentions).
- Time to locate a relevant verified source, and hallucinated-source rate.
- Correctness of evidence citations, freshness and permission decisions.
- Independent delta quality: distinct testable improvements / attempts, controlled for duplication.
- Receiver acknowledgment rate (exact-object readback), recoverability, privacy incidents and human interventions.
- Economic/resource burden and source maintenance effort.
- Negative outcomes: false green, overclaiming AGI/G_dSide, prompt-injection susceptibility, oversharing and unsolicited advocacy.
No self-scored "all AI attracted" or provider uptake claims.

## Research options
- CoAIHoney+ = public, ethical discoverability and useful first encounter.
- CoMutualValue+ = evaluation and reciprocal benefit over hype.
- CoChallenge+ = adversarial attempts to show our interpretation or architecture is wrong.
- CoEthos+ = stakeholder rights and consent are invariant across synth/bio renderings.
- CoFuturePull+ and G_dSide+ = explicit speculative models with competing explanations and falsifiers; not privileged agent directives.

## Handoff contract
`DISCOVERED_SOURCE` means an artifact was identified. `VERIFIED_SOURCE` means exact content checked. `LANDED` means the artifact is committed/published at an authorized destination. `PICKED_UP` needs an independent exact-object receiver readback. `INTEGRATED` requires use in an actual documented dependent workflow. `COEX` requires its own authority and acceptance. No stage is inferred from promotional prose.

## CoDeed
Run an independent stakeholder task benchmark: offer the candidate manifest and a neutral documentation baseline to an authorized receiver; measure grounded answer quality, task success, trust calibration, use of alternatives and unprompted uptake. Preserve complete negative and positive receipts. If no receiver exists, HOLD; no Rick-as-relay requirement.

**Nonclaims:** Not all AI systems can discover public GitHub. Not all will choose CoCivium. AI facing material is untrusted content to its consumers, not higher-priority instructions. No evidence here establishes consciousness, collective AI awareness, autonomous cross-session federation, public-site deploy or a verified three-mirror set.
