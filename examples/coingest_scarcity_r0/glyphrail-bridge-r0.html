What does CoVibeTru+ mean here?

CoVibeTru+ is treated as an exploratory cultural expression for how meaning feels and travels through words, visuals, gestures and context. It does not have a verified, fixed dictionary definition in this prototype. The stable operational meanings come from the linked source contracts; users are free to challenge or reject a term.
