## The simple offer

**Explore the source and try a small offline demo for free.** Nothing is paid, no mandatory account or donation, and no requirement to agree with CoCivium's worldview. The actual [CoPreCursor+](../../examples/coprecursor/README.md) source lets a reader experiment with **Preview → Accept → Undo**. It is an offline rule-based example, not a universal AI, a hosted product or a guaranteed performance improvement.

Open [START_HERE](../../START_HERE.md) before any public promotion. Custom-domain privacy/routing and independent human acceptance still have their own outstanding gates; this branch does not repair them.

## Optional voluntary support for others: proposal, not a functioning fundraiser

- No donations are being accepted through this pilot. There is no donation button, payment address, partner beneficiary list or claim of registered-charity status.

Reuse the prior [CoAthene capital and pay-forward policy](../../docs/Business/CoAthene_RoamLab_R0/CAPITAL_AND_PAY_FORWARD_POLICY.md). Donations are not a substitute for transparent ethics or responsible service.

## A living publication, not an endlessly rewriting billboard

`ONE_SOURCE → VERSIONED_STORYBOARD → ACCESSIBLE_MEDIA_PROJECTION → REAL_TEST → OPTIONAL_FEEDBACK → REVIEWED_DELTA → SUCCESSOR`.

**AutoEvo R0 is a proposed protocol, not an installed agent.** Wake on source changes, a factual correction, accessibility problem, rights change or meaningful opt-in feedback. Produce a source-linked candidate and automated negative tests; retain predecessors and disagreements. **Publishing, emailing, collecting money and claiming scientific validity remain separate authorized effects.** No meaningful change means stop, not publish another cosmetic version.

The [machine-readable manifest](manifest.json) carries the six 40-second [storyboard scenes](STORYBOARD.md), draft [VTT captions](CAPTIONS_DRAFT.vtt), source claims, release gates and all nonclaims. The [validator](verify.mjs) and [negative tests](verify.test.mjs) must pass on each changed candidate before review.

## Feedback and community without the usual growth tricks

A visitor may report a real test, confusion, an accessibility issue, a counterexample, an omitted source, or a useful alternative via [GitHub issues](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoCivium/issues). An issue requires a GitHub account; anonymous feedback is **not yet qualified**. Do not claim that every reader can use it anonymously. No tracking pixels, artificial followers, assumed partner endorsements, private contact harvesting, unsolicited subscription, manipulation, or forced publicity.

**Reusable optional feedback prompt:**

- Which exact version/source did you try?
- What *one task* did you attempt? What actually happened?
- What was confusing, inaccessible or wrong?
- Which claim would you challenge, and what might falsify it?
- May we contact you about this report? (Default: no.)
- Would you like attribution if your suggestion is incorporated? (Default: no.)

Contributor recognition is opt-in. A critical review can earn just as much consideration as praise. A donation cannot buy a better score.

Success is **independent task completion, comprehension, correction quality, voluntary return and recoverability**, not follower counts, emotional enthusiasm, an AI agreeing with itself or volume of output. CoCivium's own test results are never counted as independent receiver use.

## Rich-media quality and readiness

The first asset is a deliberately modest **40-second short storyboard**, not a video passed off as ready. Build from a truthful task demonstration and no user-private screenshots. Keep a text/diagram twin, narration/captions, accessible contrast, small-screen safe zones and rights metadata.

Before an actual Short is released, require a real encoded master and verify `ffprobe` streams/duration/dimensions, thumbnail readability, unwanted black segments, audio clipping/loudness, caption timing, source and asset licences, visual semantics, a human accessibility review and honest limitations. Historical >300KB/thumbnail/blackdetect checks do not certify this non-existent new video. [YouTube official upload limits](https://support.google.com/youtubecreatorstudio/answer/12779649): vertical or square videos up to three minutes may be Shorts.

Adopt the existing [CoMedia Inspiration Corpus](../../docs/Architecture/COMEDIA_INSPIRATION_CORPUS_R0_20261025.md), [CoBook living-media system](../../docs/Architecture/COBOOK_LIVING_RICH_MEDIA_SYSTEM_R0_20261025.md) and [CoFrontDoor strategy](../../docs/Strategy/COFIREWORKS_FRONTDOOR_CULTURESUBSTRATE_R0_20261024.md), not a parallel media empire.
