## Quality gates before any upload

1. **Narrative:** first 4 seconds explain why someone cares; every frame contributes to one clear human task. Keep an explicit uncertainty/limitations beat, and no unverifiable superlatives.
2. **Visual:** responsive safe margins; test on small phones and desktop; balanced white space; captions never overlap UI; accessible contrast and reduced-motion counterpart.
3. **Audio:** clean voice, no unlicensed score/likeness; verify loudness and clipping with actual output. A silent edition must remain understandable.
4. **Technical:** on the actual encoded master, measure dimensions, codec, frame/duration continuity, ffprobe parse, blackdetect and unintended freezes, thumbnail accuracy, caption sync and filesize. Historical “>300 KB, thumbnail OK” checks are not quality certification.
5. **Science and theology:** G_d, quantum/Planck and ET can be optional symbolic/research *lenses*, not claimed operating mechanisms, divine instructions, scientific breakthroughs or observed contact.
6. **Public and legal:** explicitly state WIP until render, rights, privacy and factual claim review pass. Confirm any third-party asset licences; do not reuse user screenshots containing mail or WhatsApp content.
7. **Audience:** obtain one consenting outside reader/viewer understanding the benefit and limitations before scaling; retain genuine negative feedback.

**Audience action:** [Read the 60-second public first-use guide](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoCivium/blob/main/START_HERE.md). Open the actual offline example if interested, then challenge one unclear step via existing GitHub issues. No account or donation is required to view the source.
