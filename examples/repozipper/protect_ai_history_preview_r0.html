What this preview can and cannot prove
Demonstrates: a one-step accessible first-use journey, a deterministic synthetic inventory, a downloadable example receipt.
Does not demonstrate: real ZIP ingestion, complete account recovery, encrypted custody, an independent restore, autonomous backup or provider deletion.
Storage: sample data is embedded in this page and processed in memory. No local database is created.
Network: a restrictive content-security policy blocks connections. No external libraries, tracking pixels, cookies or third-party scripts.
