What does keep, export or walk away mean?

Keep: stores only the current idea draft under this page's browser origin, if storage works. This is not an independent backup. Other apps or shared devices may have access.

Export: requests a JSON file download containing your written idea and the selected mockup identity. It stays with you until you deliberately share it separately. Download requested is not download verified.

Walk away: no assignment or invitation. Unsaved text is lost if you close the page; saved text may remain on this browser. Use Clear first on a shared computer.

Future federation: would require a real receiving service, a clear contributor license, privacy safeguards, moderation, reversibility and receiver receipt. None exists in this demo.

A little thought can have a long future

Considered → Kept locally (optional) → Explicitly shared somewhere (not connected) → Challenged → Improved → Useful to someone (unverified).

CoWalkAway+ means participation is never debt. CoFed+ would preserve consent and credit across independent communities, not quietly copy someone's words into a company ledger.
