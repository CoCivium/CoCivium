State: `SYNTHETIC_MATCHING_SHADOW__NO_REAL_MEMBERS_OR_INVITES__NOT_ON_GROUPBUILD_ORG`.

This candidate extends the existing `docs/Architecture/GROUPBUILD_COTEAMING_COPAYBACK_R0_20261101.md`, `docs/Outreach/IMPROVEMENT_CLUB_KIT_R0.md`, and `docs/Governance/COREVIEW_SPINE_R0.md`. It is not a second CRM or club platform.

One sample project: accessible open UX. Four fictional participant objects state skills they *offer*, skills they *need*, topics and separately granted pair-specific consent. The engine proposes mutually complementary links for **review only**, never sends introductions or discloses private identities.

- Both partners must opt into the circle AND specifically approve the proposed introduction to each other for `PROJECT_CONTEXT_ONLY`, with consent unrevoked and unexpired.
- Suppression overrides matching. One-sided skill complements, missing shared topics, identity collisions, unsourced private fields and a public `LIVE` club claim fail closed.
- Each pair includes source digest, why A helps B, why B helps A, held reasons, review status, unstaffed reviewer roles and zero outbound effects.
- Human review is a real separate gate: simulated reviewer placeholders are not staffed people or signatures. A `SHADOW_READY_FOR_REVIEW_NOT_SENT` candidate is not an introduction ready for dispatch.
- Avoid covert persuasion, invented memberships, inferred personal connections or linking private contact graphs. Anyone may decline or leave; corrections and consent revocation override outdated records.
