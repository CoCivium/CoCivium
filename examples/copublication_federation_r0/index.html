CoLivingHighlight+ / CoAmplificatory+ / research candidate

Can a useful cycle renew itself without getting louder?

A living highlight can preserve a question, a source and its corrections between cycles. A new reader can re-open it when useful. The four results here are synthetic normalized examples, not physical measurements, proof of G_d, or audience outcomes.

Explore four bounded cycle behaviors

Without replenishment

Decay when the modeled drive is zero. A quiet system may still preserve its knowledge.

Finite, driven renewal

Explicit input offsets losses until a finite capacity ceiling is reached.

Uncorrected gain

Amplifying the wrong thing is a risk, not progress. Saturation and rollback matter.

Corrective damping

Reducing noisy amplitude can increase usefulness when errors and agitation are being removed.

CoTurtle test: What independent improvement survived the cycle? What resources paid for it? What did we learn from failure? What should be damped, retired or allowed to sleep?
