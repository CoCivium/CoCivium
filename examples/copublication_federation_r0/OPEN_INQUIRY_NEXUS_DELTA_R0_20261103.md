## Decision
A question too large for a soundbite must not be forced into a soundbite. Its *entry* can be concise while its meanings, competing interpretations, sources, objections and successors remain recoverable.

**Target outcome:** a voluntary, plural, source-bound, evolving inquiry commons. Not a religion, a universal answer, an automatic proselytizing machine, or a campaign that profiles people by faith. CoCivium's public description explicitly says it is not a religion or faith.

Reuse existing capabilities:
- **CoOrb**: one receiver-relative focused question, explanation, claim status and next permitted action.
- **CoNull / CoNotSpace**: represent unknown, not observed, observed absent *under scoped conditions*, withheld, contradicted and not-yet-materialized without conflation. In particular, no detection ≠ proof that something cannot exist.
- **CoNexus (working role, NOT verified as a separate deployed contract)**: navigate typed links between interpretations, sources, lineages, methods, disagreements, communities *where consented*, and alternative futures using the CoMap/CoRelation graph.
- **CoQuanta**: portable minimal packet of question, identity, source, viewpoint, provenance, scope, time, uncertainty, receiver, permission, version and next authorized deed.
- **CoTime / CoDelta**: distinct historical record, current edition, observed feedback, future scenario, and what changed/why. Forecast is never evidence of a divine or extraterrestrial event.
- **CoTurtle**: recursively challenge assumptions under a finite budget, preserving minority and no-answer branches.
- **CoPublication / CoOutreach / CoVIA**: many voluntary entrances, one honest lineage, feedback via authorized receivers, no broadcast merely because a channel exists.

## One seed, multiple chosen lenses
**Common seed question:** “How might beings find meaning, recognize trustworthy knowledge, and live together amid profound uncertainty?”

No lens inherits empirical authority from another:

| Reader-selected lens | Respectful content boundary | Example qualified next invitation |
|---|---|---|
| **Faith and revelation traditions** | Faith claims, scripture, testimony, interpretation, communal practices and theological dissent are attributed to their own sources and speakers. Claims of revelation are not uttered by CoCivium as newly revealed fact. | Compare how two traditions frame responsibility or mystery, and flag what each account *actually claims*. |
| **Philosophy and doubt** | Arguments, assumptions, counterarguments, coherence and different views of meaning; neither belief nor disbelief is presumed. | State the strongest charitable objection to the chosen argument. |
| **Empirical inquiry / ET** | Testable hypotheses, methods, detection thresholds, alternatives, biases and falsifiers; no contact, detection or anomalous causation inferred. | Find what observation could discriminate two nonhuman-intelligence explanations. |
| **Personal experience and culture** | First-person reported experience, story, ritual, art and meaning as meaningful reports, without presuming external mechanism. Consent and attribution first. | Offer an optional interpretation or counter-narrative without exposing private testimony. |
| **Design, coexistence and futures** | What these perspectives imply for voluntary cooperation, AI UX, accessibility, governance and resilience; possible futures stay hypothetical. | Prototype one inclusive interaction, measure whether a newcomer understands and can challenge it. |

Lenses are **self-selected navigation**, not identities inferred from clicks, names, contacts, demographic traits or reading behavior. No religious targeting, pressure to convert, covert persuasion, false endorsement, fake urgency, or blanket cross-channel consent.

## More than one soundbite: reversible depth
1. **Glance (≤15 seconds):** one accurate invitation; the current question; explicit “open / disputed / established for scope” status; multiple entrances; exit.
2. **Orientation (60–120 seconds):** why the question matters, what it does *not* claim, one strong counterview, route to primary sources.
3. **Long-form (reader-elected):** essays, dialogues, scripture/historical text where permitted, methods, diagrams, translations, audio, accessible summary, competing premises, source scope and loss notes.
4. **Inquiry workbench:** CoOrb/CoMap graph, CoNull gaps, claim-by-claim compare, falsifier or interpretive challenge, CoTime trail and past editions.
5. **Contribution (opt-in only):** challenge, disagreement, translation, correction or independent experiment enters as a distinct attributed **candidate**, reviewed before editorial integration.
6. **Return:** versioned successor/change note, predecessor preserved, correction visible; subscribe to only chosen topics/relations or leave freely.

**One truth-store identity ≠ one official interpretation.** Preserve incompatible legitimate interpretations in separate typed branches; do not flatten doctrine to make the UI tidy. Contradiction is an object of inquiry, not a license to deduce arbitrary conclusions.

Visual adjacency is **not** causality; agreement across models is **not** spiritual proof.

## Distinguishing editorial evolution from automated influence
\`SOURCE -> READER_CHOICE -> EXPLANATION -> OPTIONAL_CRITIQUE -> INDEPENDENT_REVIEW -> PROPOSED_DELTA -> EDITORIAL_ACCEPT_OR_HOLD -> VERSIONED_SUCCESSOR -> RECEIVER_READBACK\`.

Automate safe preparation (citation mapping, alternative phrasing, accessibility, rights review, uncertainty tagging, translation proposals, dedupe and comparison). Do not automate unsolicited person-specific outreach, conversion, public posts, faith inference, worldview manipulation, or acceptance of a proposed interpretation. Disclosure and withdrawal remain first-class.

**Receiver ladder:** \`DISCOVERABLE != READ != UNDERSTOOD != CHALLENGED != USED != ACCEPTED != ADOPTED\`. Track benefits and harms: comprehension, voluntary corrections, dissent retention, trust calibration, return by choice, accessibility, unsubscribe/leave ease, privacy incidents, manual relay burden, and independent source recall. Views/likes/subscribers are not evidence of truth or useful impact.

## First bounded, falsifiable acceptance task
Re-use *the existing* six-door offline reader rather than launching another platform. Add, on a later tested branch if warranted, **one opt-in "Big questions" topic** that is independent of reader identity. Compile **three** self-chosen lenses from the same pinned source record and differing reading depth. Have one consenting, independent reader:
1. identify what is an attributed faith claim, a philosophical argument, and an empirical hypothesis;
2. find the same source identity from at least two doors;
3. locate a true CoNull gap without treating unknown as false;
4. inspect and recover an earlier edition;
5. optionally challenge or leave without data submission.

**PASS scope** needs accurate comprehension, working exit, accessible controls, explicit citation and version recovery, and a separately qualified external receiver. A spec or offline policy fixture alone does not prove public reader adoption. No content is emailed, posted, broadcast or targeted by this delta.
