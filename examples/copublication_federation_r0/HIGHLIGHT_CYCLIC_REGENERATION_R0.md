## 1. The three distinct amplitudes

- **Physical amplitude:** energy, position, current or other units in an explicitly defined system. Damping removes energy from oscillatory motion; a real recurring drive can replenish it. Ordinary resonant growth is not free energy, and all realizations have finite constraints.
- **Useful relational impact:** independently useful tasks, error corrections, resilient recovery, accessible comprehension, verified source reuse and reduction in human relay burden. Such value can accumulate even while the visible pulse or attention fades.
- **Interpretive or cultural continuity:** a question, motif, G_d hypothesis, commitment to care, or source lineage can stay discoverable and reopenable. It need not be continuously active and it must remain challengeable.

These are **different measures**. An organizational cycle is not a literal oscillator. A powerful metaphor does not establish a common physical law for software, cosmology and theology.


A useful highlight carries: stable ID, exact source version, epistemic class, accessible plain and technical views, green/neutral aesthetic projection, challenge path, currentness, successor link, receiver scope, read cursor, correction and projection-loss disclosure.

**One highlight is not a marketing badge or proof.** It is a *reconstructible relation* between a source, a question, a possible receiver and a tested learning path. A beautiful new visual should not increase the authority of an untested claim.

Proposed relation verbs: `projects_from | tests | illustrates | challenges | corrects | passes_to | reseeds | archives | supersedes | receiver_used_in | costs | saturates | wakes_on`. Unknown or absent relations remain typed as unknown; direct authority is not inherited across edges.

## 3. Finite numerical canary, not an oscillator experiment

The fixture models a unitless proxy `x` across eight discrete cycles:

```
x[n+1] = min(cap, max(0, (1 - damping) * x[n] + drive - correction[n]))
```

The word `drive` here is an **explicit numerical input**. It does not account for real electrical/chemical energy, organizational trust or dollars. This is a structural comparison, not a physical balance law.

| Synthetic regime | Initial proxy | After eight cycles | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Damped, no drive | 1.000 | 0.100 | Decays without replenishment |
| Bounded replenishment | 1.000 | 1.500 | External drive compensates for losses, reaches a ceiling |
| Unchecked positive feedback proxy | 1.000 | 7.591 | Amplifies a potentially bad instability; mathematical cap imposed |
| Explicit corrective damping | 1.000 | 0.930 | Reduces raw amplitude to model correction, not failure |

The numbers are reproducible from the [typed fixture](cyclic_regeneration_canary_r0.json) but **are not performance results or evidence of energy creation**. In particular, the negative damping in the unchecked model is an abstract growth coefficient and does not describe an inputless physical system.

A quiet corrective cycle could be more successful than a loud uncontrolled cycle. The success metric is **independent, useful outcome per resource and consent cost**, not simply `x[n+1] > x[n]`.

## 5. G_d / godstuff as a revisable question family

Treat G_d as an **explicitly typed philosophical, symbolic or mathematical hypothesis lane**. It may be a useful recurring question about meaning, pattern, distance, ethics or ultimate boundaries. If a mathematical G_d field is proposed, define variables, units or dimensionless conventions, baseline models, falsifiers and an observation plan **before** suggesting it is physically real.

CoCivium can preserve **continuity of inquiry**, not guarantee a claim's strength or existence. The safest persistence loop is:

```text
question -> source & prior alternatives -> challenge or experiment
-> preserve learning even if the current claim fails -> sleep
-> reopen only on meaningful evidence, new interpretation or receiver need
```

Whether universes themselves cycle, or each cosmic cycle becomes larger than an earlier one, remains a separate speculative cosmology question. A software/control-theory loop cannot prove or enforce such behavior. Likewise, no brand or philosophy should be intentionally made immune to fading when evidence weakens.

## 6. What would count as a receiver-useful next cycle?

A future authorized pilot should measure **each separately**:

1. **Correction quality:** did new evidence reduce error or distinguish hypotheses better than a no-drive control?
2. **Independent benefit:** did a consenting participant complete a useful task, understand an uncertainty, or contribute a source-backed challenge?
3. **Resource efficiency:** time, energy, compute, accessibility burden and human attention cost per verified result.
4. **Resilience:** can the same insight be reconstructed from a pinned source after a browser/session/worker interruption?
5. **Plurality:** are competing explanations, negative results, opt-outs and historical versions preserved?
6. **Failure:** is an amplified false claim suppressed faster than it spreads?

If use fails, do not increase volume. Adjust the question, reduce the load, disclose uncertainty, restore a prior source, or retire that particular projection.
