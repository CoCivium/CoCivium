Celebrate work that genuinely helps

Useful discoveries, repairs, thoughtful dissent, accessible teaching, original media and open tools deserve credit. A good award points to evidence and its limitations, not just popularity.

CoAwards+ · Proposed recognition, not issued honors

We are developing twelve opt-in recognition categories. No nominations are open, no recipient is named, no prize is funded and no award has been issued. Any future public recognition must pass rights, evidence, conflict checks and recipient consent, with a correction path.
