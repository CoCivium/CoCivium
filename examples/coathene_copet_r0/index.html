How can my progress travel without a login?

This first version only tracks which questions were explored in this visit. Refresh or close and the information is gone. It is not an identity system or child progress service. A later, optional CoPassport+ would let a learner and guardian control a protected progress record across devices. A passkey or another approved recovery check may be required, without a username/password form. That feature has not been built, encrypted, security-reviewed or deployed. A pet name is never proof of a child's identity.

Architecture: CoPassport+ R0.

What if the device or learning passport is lost?

Right now there is no saved passport to lose. If a future version offers optional saved progress, it should support a second independently authorized recovery path, such as a guardian-controlled trusted device or passkey. If every recovery key is missing, the record may be unrecoverable. Face or fingerprint recognition, when available, should remain optional and device-local, with a PIN or other non-biometric alternative. CoAthene should never collect a central fingerprint or face database to identify children. A pet's appearance, a name or a shared tablet does not prove who owns a record.

Does teaching a pet always help learning?

No. Research supports learning by teaching under some conditions, including active preparation and correction. A pet can also repeat wrong ideas. Moss is a fictional exercise with fixed explanations, not an AI friend or qualified teacher. The claim and counterclaim guide explains the evidence and how educators could test it fairly without replacing teachers.

Want to build a solo or group learning plan instead?

The sibling CoAthene Anywhere Studio offers local solo, group-role, mentored-plan and institution-supported-plan choices without creating actual groups, accounts, mentors or institutions. It remains a source prototype, not an accredited school.

What about a real syllabus, a teacher or AI checking my work?

A syllabus can help see what has or hasn't been covered; curiosity can determine the path. A broad coverage map here is not an official school curriculum, a mastery score or evidence that a specific child completed a course. Real educators and guardians remain responsible for consequential educational and child-safety decisions. Moss's answers are fixed example content; there is no model chatting, profiling or independent synthetic arbitration.

Research lineage and rights

This concept borrows the general idea of learning by teaching from Vanderbilt's teachable-agents research without copying their characters or software. Canada's Children's Privacy Code and UNICEF child-centred AI guidance inform the privacy and safe-companion boundaries. These are sources, not endorsements or a certified child-learning pilot.
