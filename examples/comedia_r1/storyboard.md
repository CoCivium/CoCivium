## Production notes

- Art direction: distinctive, readable, original 2D/3D capability tokens; avoid generic metallic brains, invented dashboard statistics or tiny glow-on-glow copy. Use a dark background only if contrast and compression survive phone tests. Favor simple shapes that animate purposefully.
- Sound: a calm narration, music only with verified rights, no dramatic sound meant to imply a proven scientific or economic conclusion. Check loudness, peaks and intelligibility separately, beyond the basic FFmpeg gate.
- Evidence: do not claim any vendor has consented or endorsed this model; do not depict a named real person as speaking or taking actions without authorization. Source-to-video claim card should include uncertainty and a path to the underlying CoCivium work.
- Access: subtitles and text transcript, readable alt explanation for charts, no information conveyed through color alone, avoid excessive flashing/motion, include a mute-friendly presentation.
- Quality: at least one non-creator reader can accurately say what was proposed versus observed; one counterexample or point of disagreement is easy to locate.
- Audience channels: YouTube Shorts (vertical candidate), RickPublic/Substack embed or article image/clip, CoOrbWare interactive card, a plain text version. Publish only under independently verified platform and rights permissions.
