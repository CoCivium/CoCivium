Inspect design and audio rules

Audio stays yours.

This page begins silent. If you choose to enable its original synthesized ambient tones, scrolling can gently raise the volume to a deliberately low ceiling. The ceiling is adjustable, the sound can be switched off instantly, and Sound off, please stores that preference on this device when local storage is available. It never tracks or sends the preference elsewhere.

Sound is never needed to understand or use the page. If you don't want it, the entire experience still works.
