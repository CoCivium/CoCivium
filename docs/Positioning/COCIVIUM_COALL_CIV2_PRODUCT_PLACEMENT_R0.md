# CoCivium / CoAll / Civ2+ | Product placement R0

**State:** Public-source positioning **candidate**, not an elected brand standard, deployed service, product launch, independent adoption, or claim that Civ2+ has been achieved.  
**Prepared:** 2026-11-05 (America/Toronto).  
**Audience:** Newcomers, potential contributors, human and machine clients, and reviewers.

## In one sentence

**CoCivium is an open-source research and product-development initiative exploring relational coordination infrastructure: tools that help people and AI systems work across applications, devices and organizations while preserving consent, evidence, continuity and freedom to exit.**

## The practical promise

A useful first CoCivium experience should help someone complete one genuine task, make uncertainty understandable, preserve their work across interruptions, show what was authorized, and let them stop or switch tools without being trapped.

This describes the product direction, **not** a promise that all those capabilities are already integrated or available as a service. Start with [one real task](../../START_HERE.md) and inspect [current public evidence](../../EVERGREEN.md).

## Relationship of names

| Name | Working role | Not a claim of |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Civ2+** | A proposed direction and evaluative field for more trustworthy, equitable human/AI cooperation across society | A completed civilizational transition, a government mandate, or a Kardashev classification |
| **CoCivium** | The open-source project, collaborators, stewardship, research, onboarding and eventual product families | Ownership of participants or automatic public-service readiness |
| **CoAll+** | The proposed provenance-aware relation, continuity, capability, evidence and coordination fabric | A fully deployed universal operating system |
| **CoOrbWare+ / CoSoftware+ / CoWare+** | Candidate software, device, workflow, adapter and capability substrate families | Working replacements for all legacy software |
| **CoCivia / CoBar / CoOrb / CoMap** | Participant-facing and developer-facing experience and projection candidates | A generally installed local companion or autonomous agent |
| **Civic RelOps** | Candidate name for an operational practice: source-bound relationships, scoped permission, currentness, recovery, explicit dissent and evidence | The sole product category, government certification or a standard already adopted by others |

**CoCivium is the initiative; CoAll is the fabric; Civ2+ is the wider hypothesis and direction.** All may evolve, be questioned, forked or replaced by demonstrably better approaches.

## Product taxonomy, by demonstrated maturity

These are different deliverable classes, not a claim that each is already shipped:

1. **Research and shared specifications:** documented assumptions, candidate semantic types, standards mappings, provenance and falsifiable claims.
2. **Open developer tools:** portable data, schemas, verification harnesses, APIs, adapters, reproducible examples and modules.
3. **Human-first experiences:** voluntary browser demos and, when independently qualified, local-first CoBar/device experiences.
4. **Independent services:** permission-bounded, accessible, observable, recoverable offerings, only after real-world release and receiver tests.
5. **Civic and other applications:** research, education, collaboration, public information and other domains. Civic participation is one application family, not the entire scope.

A file being public does **not** prove it is hosted correctly; a CI test passing does **not** prove an outsider can accomplish a task; a copied repository does **not** prove independent recovery.

## How to describe the work today

**For newcomers:** “Open-source tools in development to help people and AI work together without losing control of their data, decisions and ongoing work.”

**For developers:** “Experimental, provenance-aware relational coordination and continuity infrastructure with replaceable adapters, source-linked state and bounded action authority.”

**For a Civic RelOps conversation:** “A candidate practice for coordinating civic and social workflows transparently, with consent, evidence, reversibility and nonpartisan human agency.”

These are candidate receiver-relative descriptions. Compare reader comprehension and actual first-use outcomes before electing a stable category.

## What would make the promise real?

- Two independent external browsers can reach the intended current public entry point, and people using keyboard/mobile/accessibility tooling can accomplish a useful first task.
- One work object survives a provider or machine failure and is restored by an independent receiver using exact source/version/receipt evidence.
- Real participants can inspect, consent, refuse, correct and leave, without a hidden need for the founder to transport context between providers or devices.
- Any automation compares real outcomes with a baseline, including doing nothing, preserves negative findings, and stops when further action has no verified benefit.

Current gaps and scope-limited observations are documented in the [Civ2+/Civ3+ evidence-led gap map](../Strategy/CIV2_CIV3_BOUNDARY_GAPMAP_R0.md), the [pre-launch statement](../../PRELAUNCH.md), and the [Evergreen evidence index](../../EVERGREEN.md).
