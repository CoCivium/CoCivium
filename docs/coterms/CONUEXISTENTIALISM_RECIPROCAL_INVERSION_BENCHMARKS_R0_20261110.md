## CoHereNow

“No one gets out of alone” is a poetic thesis about interdependence, not proof that every human must pair with another person or that solitude is defective. Being alone can be chosen, restorative and worthy of respect.

A useful **inversion benchmark** is a comparison that can reveal what one's own standpoint misses, with informed participation, dissent, context, and the possibility that both viewpoints are wrong. It is *not* a ranking of who has more human value.

The parent-child application must be especially careful. A parent can willingly step back as a child grows, and a child can lead an age-appropriate project, while the parent remains responsible for care, safety and boundaries. The child never inherits an adult's ideology, work, emotional needs, reputation, assets or mission by default. A parent remains a whole person, not a dependent or vicarious passenger in the child's life.

## Six independent reciprocal lenses

1. **Child ↔ adult:** creativity tests inherited norms; experience tests feasibility and harm. Not a claim that childhood automatically outranks expertise.
2. **Meek ↔ powerful:** affected-party testimony challenges privilege; capacity and evidence challenge proposals. Neither may override another's basic rights.
3. **Skeptic ↔ believer:** evidence standards test causal claims; values and meaning questions broaden inquiry. No mandatory worldview.
4. **Human ↔ AI:** human perspectives test usefulness, dignity and accountability; AI can offer bounded criticism and synthesis. Model output grants neither agency nor authority automatically.
5. **Source ↔ receiver:** authorship cannot certify delivery; independent receiver readproof challenges sender claims. Receipt does not establish truth of content.
6. **Past ↔ future:** historical evidence challenges novelty; future-oriented imagination challenges lock-in. Forecasts are not future observations.

## Operating relation

`CANDIDATE → independent challenge → evidence or explicit dissent → revise / retain / reject → preserve provenance`.

Every comparison should record: `work_id`, participant roles, possible power asymmetry, voluntary consent, what each standpoint initially predicted, counterevidence, changed understanding, unresolved dissent, time, and safety boundaries. When minors are involved, consent is age-appropriate and guardian safeguards apply; no identifiable child data is necessary to test the idea.

## First bounded CoBookCiv2+ experiment

Show the wings-regrown two-panel page to an opt-in, appropriately safeguarded range of readers without collecting identifying child data. Compare two endings: **“The adult shows the child how to play”** versus **“The adult and child discover together.”** Ask whether each respects the child's distinct agency, the adult's continuing duties and the right of either to disagree. Preserve contradictory interpretations and test before recommending a canonical ending.
