## CoHereNow

**CoNuExistentialism+** is a *proposed research umbrella*: live meaning arises from choices under finite time, relationships, play and learning, responsibilities and corrigible uncertainty. Its inversion is not “death creates meaning” or “adult structure is evil.” Instead: **constraints can give shape to meaning, but we must notice when they prematurely close possibility**. A child can teach openness; adults contribute memory, care, consent and hard-won judgment.

**CoG_dSide+** is a related but **distinct perspective** encompassing questions of sacredness, transcendence, belief, nonmaterial interpretations and moral imagination. It neither presumes a universal deity nor proves spiritual experiences are physical external causes. Secular, spiritual, agnostic and skeptical participants retain equal standing.

**One key new consequence:** a CoTerm is a *versioned, source-bound semantic hypothesis*. An elegant name is not a measurement, an ontology license or an authority claim.
