## Predictive input forwarding
Inputs may be projected beyond the cursor as bounded candidates, not silently committed actions:
1. preserve CoAckRAW: exact raw input and source context;
2. derive one or more ReWis+ interpretations with explicit uncertainty;
3. predict likely next destinations / relations / actions;
4. render previews ahead of the cursor;
5. require authority at the actual mutation or external-action boundary;
6. retain exact readback / receiver evidence before pickup or integration claims.

Predictive forwarding is therefore assistive precomputation, not permission laundering.

## CoScale+ / CoFold+ / CoUnfold+ / CoEndlessProcess+

Scale is fluid rather than privileged. The same relational state may render as quanta, object, task, cluster, system, ecosystem or open-ended possibility space.

Reduction is not automatically permanent:
- CoFold+ compresses or hides detail while retaining lineage where possible.
- CoUnfold+ restores or reveals retained detail.
- Every reduction should declare REVERSIBLE / PARTIALLY_REVERSIBLE / IRREVERSIBLE / UNKNOWN.
- Lossy reduction records what was discarded, why, authority, and whether recovery exists elsewhere.
- CoScaleDebt+ records compression whose reconstruction evidence is incomplete.

Processes may be intentionally ongoing without implying infinite execution. CoEndlessProcess+ means open-ended improvement through finite bounded waves, each with finite inputs, budget, output, receipt and wake condition.

CoMultiProcess+ allows build / verify / challenge / document / benchmark / recover / render / observe lanes to run concurrently against one durable relational state. Processes themselves are graph objects with dependencies, challenges, wake conditions and stop rules.

CoDynamicLOD+ and CoSemanticZoom+ let CoOrbWare+ change level of detail with attention, task, privacy, uncertainty and resource budget. Quiet surfaces may hide enormous underlying complexity without destroying it.

CoExpansionWake+ may unfold a folded region when contradiction, changed evidence, failure, uncertainty or explicit focus makes detail useful again. CoBackgroundFold+ may fold low-attention regions after preserving state.

The default invariant is: conceptual spaces may remain open-ended; actual executions stay bounded.


## CoAwarenessGap+

No visible public awareness is a signal to investigate, not proof of suppression. Keep a ranked hypothesis tree and test the funnel:

PUBLISHED -> INDEXED -> DISCOVERABLE -> SEEN -> UNDERSTOOD -> TRUSTED -> TRIED -> SHARED -> RETURNED

Priority hypotheses:
1. discoverability or indexing weakness;
2. comprehension or front-door overload;
3. insufficient distribution;
4. fragmented identity and surfaces;
5. insufficient simple public demonstration;
6. audience mismatch;
7. trust deficit;
8. measurement blindness;
9. algorithmic de-ranking or moderation;
10. deliberate coordinated suppression, retained as a low-prior hypothesis until direct evidence appears.

Every awareness claim should bind observable evidence. Absence of visible attention is not, by itself, evidence of interference.

## 2026-10-24 | CoLargestPath+ / AutoCycle+ / AutoEvo+ bounded expansion

The search space may be enormous, but execution remains sparse. In addition to the smallest useful next deed, each material wave should compare at least one largest admissible path: the highest expected decision-changing or continuity-improving path that still fits current authority, evidence, resource, recovery and verification budgets.

CoLargestPath+ does not mean maximum compute, maximum fanout, longest chain or most concepts. Prefer paths that maximize verified useful delta across dependency centrality, information gain, receiver value, recovery value, public comprehension, failure-domain independence, and reduction of human relay.

Candidate giant spaces may remain virtual. Materialize only bounded experiments or role sets whose expected value exceeds their resource and verification burden. Preserve REPRESENT_VASTLY_EXECUTE_SPARSely, LARGE_ADDRESS_SPACE_NE_LIVE_WORK, MANY_CANDIDATES_NE_MANY_RESOURCES, and FANOUT_WITHOUT_FANIN_IS_NOISE.

AutoCycle+ / AutoEvo+ means recurring reevaluation of material changes, wake conditions, failed assumptions, stale evidence, benchmark drift, new resources, new receivers and new counterexamples. It does not mean endless busy loops. Each cycle must expose stop conditions, resource ceilings, dedupe, receipts, next wake and a no-material-delta quiescent state.

Default wave:
OBSERVE -> RECOVER CURRENT STATE -> EXPAND CANDIDATE FIELD -> DEDUPE -> SCORE SMALL AND LARGE PATHS -> ELECT BOUNDED PATH -> FAN OUT QUALIFIED READ-ONLY/ISOLATED WORK -> FAN IN -> VERIFY -> LOG PASS/FAIL/HOLD/UNKNOWN -> UPDATE PRIORS/PASSPORTS/BENCHMARKS -> COMPACT -> SLEEP UNTIL MATERIAL CHANGE.

Historical work remains eligible for REUSE / REVERIFY / REPLAY / REINTERPRET / REFACTOR / REPLACE / ARCHIVE_ONLY. AutoEvo should preferentially metabolize old failures into regression fixtures and new qualification tests rather than replay unchanged work for activity.
