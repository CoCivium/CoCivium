## Ontario after-school / child-care boundary

### Child Care and Early Years Act, 2014
https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/14c11

The Act distinguishes, among other categories, certain programs whose primary purpose is academic study/tutoring or specified recreational/artistic/musical/athletic/cultural/language instruction from child care, subject to the statutory/regulatory details.

### O. Reg. 137/15: General
https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/150137

Includes factors relevant to determining the primary purpose of academic/recreational programs. Operators should obtain qualified guidance for their actual program design.

## Ontario buses / school-purpose operation

### Commercial Vehicle Operator's Registration
https://www.ontario.ca/page/commercial-vehicle-operators-registration-cvor

Current Ontario guidance states that CVOR is required for buses with seating capacity of 10 or more passengers unless an exemption applies, as well as other listed commercial vehicles.

### MTO Bus Handbook — School bus licence classes B and E
https://www.ontario.ca/document/official-ministry-transportation-mto-bus-handbook/school-bus-licence-classes-b-and-e

Current handbook describes class B/E school-bus licensing context and daily/semi-annual inspection responsibilities. Exact requirements depend on the vehicle and use.
