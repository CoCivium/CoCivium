# Capital and Pay-It-Forward Policy

## 1. Purpose

The open RoamLab plan should help people start useful education businesses without creating a hidden obligation to CoCivium.

The right to use, fork or compete with the open plan is separate from any funding relationship.

FREE_PLAN_NE_EQUITY_CLAIM

## 2. No automatic investment right

If an entrepreneur builds a RoamLab-like business from this plan:

- CoCivium does not automatically own equity;
- Rick does not automatically receive an investment option;
- the operator does not owe a royalty merely for reading or using the open plan;
- refusing investment does not reduce access to the open materials;
- accepting investment does not create extra CoAll authority.

## 3. Funding forms

Depending on the operator and jurisdiction, funding may include:

- founder/self funding;
- grant;
- charitable or community sponsorship;
- equipment-in-kind;
- donation;
- debt;
- revenue-based financing;
- recoverable grant;
- minority equity;
- co-operative/community financing;
- institutional partnership.

This file does not elect one form as universally best.

## 4. Pay-it-forward pattern

A funded operator can voluntarily agree to one or more impact commitments such as:

- sponsor a number of free learner seats;
- publish one reusable lesson per year;
- contribute accessibility improvements;
- contribute de-identified operating lessons;
- mentor another independent operator;
- donate retired but usable equipment;
- fund a community day;
- publish corrections and failure learnings.

Impact commitments should not require surrendering learner privacy, operator independence or core business viability.

## 5. If Rick / CoCivium considers an investment

Treat it as a separate diligence event.

Minimum diligence should include:

- legal entity and ownership;
- cap table;
- founder/operator background relevant to the business;
- customer evidence;
- paid-pilot evidence;
- repeat demand;
- unit economics;
- cash runway;
- insurance;
- child-safety model;
- privacy/data practices;
- accessibility;
- vehicle/transport scope;
- intellectual-property/licensing position;
- material contracts;
- liabilities;
- tax/securities consequences;
- valuation;
- dilution;
- exit rights;
- governance rights;
- conflicts of interest.

No investment should be made solely because the founder used CoCivium materials or shares CoCivium terminology.

## 6. Example evidence gate before considering equity

Illustrative, not a recommendation:

- several paid pilots completed;
- at least one repeat customer;
- credible willingness-to-pay evidence;
- no major unresolved safety/privacy defect;
- positive contribution-margin path;
- insurance availability demonstrated;
- transport scope bounded;
- founder/operator can execute without constant CoCivium intervention;
- clear use of funds;
- independent legal/financial review.

## 7. Prohibited capital rights

Capital should not buy:

- learner personal data;
- behavioural targeting rights;
- curriculum truth authority;
- hidden advertising;
- unilateral control of open-source materials;
- exclusive use of generic RoamLab concepts;
- suppression of competing forks;
- privileged CoAll governance;
- authority over unrelated CoCivium participants.

## 8. Conflict transparency

If CoCivium, Rick, an affiliated entity or a related participant has a financial interest in an operator, public material discussing that operator should disclose the relevant interest where material.

The educational evidence layer should remain challengeable regardless of ownership.

## 9. Diversification

A pay-it-forward model may eventually support multiple independent operators rather than betting the whole idea on one company.

Possible pattern:

open plan -> many experiments -> compare evidence -> support some -> preserve forks

This reduces the chance that one operator's failure becomes the failure of the educational model.

ONE_COMPANY_NE_COATHENE_TOTALITY

## 10. Return and impact

An investor may care about both financial return and public benefit. Keep them as separate dimensions.

Possible impact measures:

- free/sponsored learner seats;
- accessibility;
- open lessons;
- underserved/community reach;
- teacher satisfaction;
- destination/community partnerships;
- reusable public artifacts.

Possible commercial measures:

- revenue;
- contribution margin;
- repeat bookings;
- customer acquisition cost;
- utilization;
- cash runway.

Do not hide weak economics behind impact language, or weak impact behind revenue language.

## 11. Exit / failure

If an operator fails:

- open materials remain usable;
- learner/customer data follows the operator's lawful retention/deletion commitments;
- CoCivium does not inherit data merely because it supplied the plan;
- useful public lessons may survive if properly licensed/published;
- investors bear normal investment risk.
