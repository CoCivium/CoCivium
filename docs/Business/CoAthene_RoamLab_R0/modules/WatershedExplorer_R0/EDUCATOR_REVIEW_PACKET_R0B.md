# Educator Adversarial Review Packet — Watershed Explorer R0B

**Purpose:** find defects before a real pilot.

This is **not** an endorsement request.

A useful review may conclude:

- the lesson is not worth piloting;
- the age/grade fit is wrong;
- the science model is misleading;
- the timing is unrealistic;
- the accessible path is weak;
- the VR layer adds no value;
- the curriculum relation is overstated;
- a cheaper lesson would work better.

All of those outcomes are useful.

## Reviewer context

Please state only what you are comfortable making public.

Helpful context:

- role (teacher, EA, administrator, curriculum lead, researcher, parent, student, other);
- grade/subject experience;
- accessibility/inclusion experience;
- Ontario experience or other jurisdiction;
- whether you reviewed the non-headset path, immersive path, or both.

No credential is required to identify a defect.

## Adversarial questions

### 1. Learning objective

- What do you think learners are actually supposed to learn?
- Is that objective worthwhile for the proposed time?
- Is any part decorative rather than educational?
- What cheaper activity could achieve the same objective?

### 2. Scientific validity

- Where can the tray/model create a false causal picture?
- Which variables are missing badly enough to matter?
- Does the distinction between model observation and world observation work?
- Which facilitator wording risks creating misconceptions?
- What claim should the module explicitly prohibit?

### 3. Inquiry quality

- Is the prediction genuinely open enough to matter?
- Does the learner have any real agency?
- Does the source challenge change thinking or merely confirm the intended lesson?
- Is uncertainty treated seriously?
- Is the "next measurement" task meaningful?

### 4. Curriculum fit

- Which current expectation, if any, does this genuinely support?
- Which mapping in CURRICULUM_ALIGNMENT_R0B.md is overstated?
- What important Grade 8 Water Systems content is absent?
- Would you call this a lesson, activity, demonstration, enrichment resource, or something else?

### 5. Classroom realism

- Can this be set up by a normal educator/facilitator?
- What will consume more time than the plan assumes?
- What will students misunderstand?
- What will break first?
- Is the 45-minute fallback credible?
- What class size changes the design materially?

### 6. Accessibility / inclusion

- Does the non-headset path preserve the same core objective?
- Which learner needs remain poorly served?
- Is any critical information unnecessarily visual, auditory, motor-dependent, colour-dependent or language-heavy?
- What accommodation should be designed in rather than improvised later?
- Does any immersive element create an avoidable barrier?

Ontario guidance emphasizes Universal Design for Learning and multiple accessible forms of engagement, representation and expression. The review should treat accessibility as part of lesson quality, not a side checklist.

### 7. Assessment

- Does the rubric measure the intended learning?
- Is any criterion too subjective?
- Could the rubric bias the facilitator toward the desired conclusion?
- Is an 8-point score useful, or would descriptive evidence be better?
- What should never be used for grading?

### 8. Privacy / technology

- Is any learner data actually necessary?
- Does the current no-account/no-recording default work?
- What device/platform telemetry would still exist despite our own defaults?
- If VR adds little learning value, should it be removed from this module?

### 9. Safety

- What physical, sensory, emotional or field-safety risk is under-specified?
- Is water near electronics handled sufficiently?
- What should trigger immediate stop?
- What requires school/board policy review?

### 10. Final disposition

Please choose one, with reasons:

- **PILOT_CANDIDATE_AFTER_MINOR_FIXES**
- **PILOT_CANDIDATE_AFTER_MAJOR_FIXES**
- **REVIEW_AGAIN_BEFORE_PILOT**
- **RETIRE_OR_REDESIGN_MODULE**
- **INSUFFICIENT_CONTEXT_TO_JUDGE**

This is not a ranking of reviewers. Different reviewers may reasonably disagree.


## Independence

Please disclose if you:

- contributed to CoCivium;
- have a financial interest in a RoamLab operator;
- supplied hardware/software used by the module;
- are reviewing on behalf of a named institution.

Disclosure does not invalidate criticism. It gives readers context.

## What CoCivium will do with critique

Material critique should be dispositioned as:

- ACCEPT;
- ACCEPT_WITH_MODIFICATION;
- DEFER_WITH_REASON;
- DISAGREE_WITH_EVIDENCE;
- NEEDS_TEST;
- DUPLICATE;
- OUT_OF_SCOPE_FOR_THIS_MODULE.

No silent deletion of inconvenient criticism.

`CRITIQUE_NE_ENDORSEMENT`
`REVIEW_NE_APPROVAL`
`DISAGREEMENT_NE_REJECTION_OF_REVIEWER`

## Human-review gate

The module may not advance its state to `EDUCATOR_REVIEWED` merely because this packet exists.

Require at least one identifiable independent human review artifact plus a response/disposition record.
