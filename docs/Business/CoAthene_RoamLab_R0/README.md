# CoAthene+ RoamLab R0

**Public StrawBe+ candidate. Open business-plan exemplar. Oakville, Ontario pilot lens.**

CoAthene+ RoamLab is a proposed mobile immersive-learning business: VR/AR/MR, simulation, AI-assisted inquiry, science, history, design and place-based learning delivered to schools and communities, then connected where useful to real museums, parks, conservation areas, businesses and institutions.

## Core idea

A useful learning relation is:

    virtual preparation
    -> question or hypothesis
    -> real-world place / evidence
    -> observation
    -> compare model with reality
    -> revise
    -> create / explain
    -> next question

The bus is a substrate, not the curriculum.

## Launch architecture

1. Start with a portable kit.
2. Prove paid learning sessions.
3. Move to a parked mobile lab only when useful.
4. Use existing lawful school/charter transport for destination visits.
5. Consider transporting learners only after the separate Ontario passenger/school-bus requirements are fully qualified.
6. Add CoAll relations only when the operator/users choose them.

The first product therefore does **not** need a giant bus and does **not** need CoAll.

## Product modes

- **Standalone:** lesson content, devices, scheduling, exports and safety workflow work locally.
- **Sidecar:** augment a school, tutor, museum, camp, library, attraction or employer.
- **CoAll-native:** optional provenance, CoTime+, curriculum/destination relations, privacy/authority controls, fleet health and federation.
- **Transport-enabled:** later qualified variant only.

## Business model

Potential revenue:

- school bookings;
- after-school cohorts;
- homeschool/family sessions;
- camps;
- corporate/community events;
- local-business sponsorship;
- destination-linked programs;
- equipment/operator training;
- optional hosted fleet/scheduling/integration services.

The open plan remains free. Commercial operators pay only for scarce services they choose.

## Oakville hypothesis

Oakville is a plausible pilot because current public sources show a large business base, curriculum-linked Town and Museum school programs, an Oakville Transit school-charter pilot, Conservation Halton education programs, Sheridan immersive/design talent, and municipal/business sponsorship channels.

These are candidate relations, not endorsements or partnerships.

## 90-day proof

**Days 1-30:** customer interviews, one lesson, portable equipment, safety/accessibility review, demo.  
**Days 31-60:** paid pilots, measured setup/failure/throughput, sponsor-funded access experiment.  
**Days 61-90:** repeat pilots, after-school test, destination bridge, contribution-margin review.

Do not buy or convert a flagship bus until repeat demand, insurability, accessibility, operating economics, storage/parking, staffing and working capital are demonstrated.
