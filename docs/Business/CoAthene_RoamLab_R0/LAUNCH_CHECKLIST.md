# Launch Checklist

## A. Before first public demonstration

- define one learning objective;
- define one audience/grade range;
- choose devices and manufacturer-use requirements;
- create a non-headset equivalent;
- write facilitator instructions;
- write privacy notice;
- write data-retention rule;
- define emergency/stop procedure;
- test device reset and sanitation;
- test accessible participation;
- confirm site permission;
- confirm insurance scope;
- qualify business-licence requirements;
- identify who supervises minors;
- rehearse setup and teardown;
- create one feedback form that does not require a persistent learner identity.

Exit: FIRST_PUBLIC_DEMO_READY

## B. Before first paid school pilot

- map lesson to relevant curriculum outcomes;
- provide teacher pre-brief;
- provide school/parent privacy sheet where appropriate;
- define exact participant capacity;
- define facilitator-to-learner ratio;
- confirm electrical/network/space needs;
- provide accessibility alternative;
- define device failure fallback;
- define motion-sickness/comfort stop rule;
- define photography/recording policy;
- confirm invoice/payment process;
- define cancellation/weather policy;
- measure setup/reset time;
- collect only necessary operational data.

Exit: FIRST_PAID_SCHOOL_PILOT_READY

## C. Before after-school operation

- qualify whether the actual service is tutoring/recreation or regulated child care;
- confirm supervision and pickup/release procedure;
- confirm emergency contacts;
- define late-pickup policy;
- define medical/allergy information handling only where necessary;
- confirm venue rules;
- confirm staff screening/training requirements applicable to the model;
- confirm insurance;
- confirm accessibility;
- avoid marketing claims that conflict with the real legal operating model.

Exit: AFTER_SCHOOL_SCOPE_QUALIFIED

## D. Before dedicated vehicle purchase

Require evidence of:

- at least several paid pilots;
- repeat-purchase signal;
- positive contribution-margin path;
- vehicle utilization forecast;
- storage/parking solution;
- commercial insurance quote;
- fit-out quote;
- electrical/HVAC plan;
- accessibility plan;
- maintenance reserve;
- device security/storage plan;
- staff capacity.

Do not buy a bus merely because a bus is visually excellent.

Exit: VEHICLE_CAPITAL_GATE_PASSED

## E. Before carrying learners

Separate transport qualification required.

Check, as applicable:

- exact vehicle classification;
- seating capacity;
- CVOR;
- correct driver's licence class;
- daily inspection;
- semi-annual inspection;
- school-purpose rules;
- commercial/passenger insurance;
- board/vendor qualification;
- accessibility;
- seat/restraint requirements;
- emergency plan;
- driver hours/duties;
- passenger supervision;
- pickup/drop-off policy.

Exit: PASSENGER_TRANSPORT_LEGALLY_AND_OPERATIONALLY_QUALIFIED

## F. Before accepting a sponsor

- written sponsorship terms;
- public acknowledgement format;
- no access to learner personal data;
- no behavioural advertising to learners;
- no editorial/curriculum veto;
- no claim of endorsement by school/municipality/CoCivium unless actually authorized;
- sponsored claims remain evidence-linked;
- conflicts disclosed;
- cancellation/ethics exit path.

Exit: SPONSOR_RELATION_BOUNDED

## G. Before optional CoAll connection

- standalone mode already works;
- user/institution chooses connection;
- exact data classes listed;
- authority ceiling listed;
- retention listed;
- export/disconnect path tested;
- no retroactive ingestion;
- no requirement to use CoAll for core functionality.

Exit: OPTIONAL_ONRAMP_VALIDATED

## H. Before replication to another city

- replace Oakville-specific sources and regulations;
- build local destination graph;
- rebuild local sponsor/business graph;
- verify local education rules;
- verify local transport rules;
- verify privacy/accessibility rules;
- retain common open schemas;
- preserve local operator independence.

Exit: LOCALIZATION_NOT_COPY_PASTE
