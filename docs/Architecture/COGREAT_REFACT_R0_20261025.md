# Great ReFact+ R0

**State:** PUBLIC_ARCHITECTURE_CANDIDATE__NOT_VENDOR_OVERRIDE__NOT_AUTOMATIC_MUTATION__NOT_RUNTIME  
**Observed:** 2026-10-25

## Thesis

The next major software transition may be less "replace every application" than:

```
PRODUCT_AS_DESTINATION
-> PRODUCT_AS_CAPABILITY_SUBSTRATE
-> AGENT / HUMAN INTENT LAYER
-> CROSS-PRODUCT COMPOSITION
-> RECEIPTED EFFECT
-> CONTINUOUS REFACTORING
```

Great ReFact+ is the candidate name for this broader refactoring of software, CoSoftware, CoOrbWare, workflows, interfaces, organizations, protocols, media, devices and relations.

## CoSmoosh+ role

CoSmoosh+ is a transformation operator:
- combine compatible capabilities;
- preserve source identities;
- preserve disagreements;
- expose translation loss;
- avoid pretending composition means merger.

`SMOOSH_NE_MERGE`
`COMPOSITION_NE_OWNERSHIP`

## What changes

### Application boundary
Apps become callable capability surfaces as well as human destinations.

### Interface boundary
Human intent may be expressed once and compiled into actions across multiple systems.

### Vendor boundary
A product may gain value from external/open agents, adapters, overlays and workflows without the vendor controlling every interaction surface.

### Data boundary
Context, provenance, permissions, currentness and recovery become as important as UI navigation.

### Development boundary
Agents increasingly perform implementation/refactoring/testing while humans and institutions provide objectives, constraints, judgment and acceptance.

### Product lifecycle
A product may have:
- vendor-native behavior;
- open augmentation;
- user-local augmentation;
- agent-generated adapters;
- CoOrbWare projections;
- alternative accessible surfaces;
- independent recipes and proofs.

## Respectful augmentation contract

Great ReFact+ must not imply unauthorized modification, circumvention, credential capture or false partnership.

Prefer:
- documented APIs;
- user-authorized automation;
- open standards;
- accessibility interfaces;
- plugins/extensions;
- local overlays;
- export/import;
- reversible adapters;
- public/open-source contributions.

`AUGMENT_NE_IMPERSONATE_VENDOR`
`INTEROPERATE_NE_CLAIM_PARTNERSHIP`

## Vendor opportunity

Vendors can make their products easier for lawful user-authorized agents to augment by exposing:
- stable capability interfaces;
- machine-readable policies;
- permissions;
- state/export surfaces;
- provenance;
- sandbox/test environments;
- event streams;
- rollback;
- accessible interaction primitives.

The competitive question may shift from:
"How much UI do we own?"

toward:
"How safely and usefully can other intelligences compose our capabilities?"

## First proof

Choose one mature third-party product with an authorized integration surface.

Demonstrate:
1. same vendor product remains unchanged;
2. one external agent/adapter adds useful capability;
3. permissions remain vendor/user enforced;
4. provenance and actions are inspectable;
5. adapter can be removed cleanly;
6. user can revert to native product behavior;
7. measurable human-time or capability gain.

Success demonstrates augmentation, not takeover.
