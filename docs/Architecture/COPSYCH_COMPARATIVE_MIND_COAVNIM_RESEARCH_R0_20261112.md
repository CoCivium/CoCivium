## CoHereNow | Two errors to avoid

**Anthropomorphic overtransfer:** Human psychology provides valuable experimental methods and analogies, but a text model's coherent story about "feeling" is not evidence of corresponding subjective experience. Results vary with architecture, training, tool access, context, scoring and prompting.

**Anti-human dogma:** Automatically discarding human research because it is old is another bias. Prefer relevant, falsifiable evidence regardless of origin: internal CoCivium priors as hypothesis generators; independent external research as challengers and replication opportunities. Do not let either source automatically outrank the other.

The public literature now distinguishes task-conditioned model behavior from the much harder questions of internal causal mechanism and experience. See [LLMs as Psychological Simulators (2026)](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/25152459251410153), [The ghost of behaviorism (2026)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1389041726000124) and [Principles for understanding trust in AI (2026)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44159-026-00562-1).

These are provisional terms, not new authorities. A term survives only if it does explanatory work that existing language cannot.

The least misleading interpretation of "alien" here is **unfamiliar to human psychological categories**, not extraterrestrial origin or proven independent sentience. A generative metaphor never becomes an observed mental state merely because it is compelling.

## Research protocol | One work object, independent perspectives

1. Register one public-safe task and WorkID. Declare its source, context, allowed tools, actual model identity, time, prompts, hypotheses, competing explanations and privacy ceiling.
2. Run bounded independent reviewer/agent perspectives with no claim that different sessions have different upstream training or genuine independent minds. Distinguish **independent process**, **independent model**, **independent source**, **independent evaluator** and **independent failure domain**.
3. Score *prospective prediction*, *task fidelity*, *calibration*, *correction*, *transfer*, *novel valid hypotheses*, *duplicate overlap*, *adversarial counterexamples* and *human effort*. Preserve negative, null and inconclusive results.
4. Record receipts per actual receiver; neither a pasted common prompt nor two agreeing paragraphs proves fan-out or synergy.
5. Reject any mental-state label inferred solely from human-style self-description. Maintain a separately typed philosophical/rights discussion without converting it to a scientific fact.

## Public presence design | CoCivia, CoBeAxaKitten and the CoBF surface family

**Default: presence without impersonation.** CoCivia may initially use an abstract light/orb/softly blurred figure rather than a stable photorealistic face. If a face materially helps a task, offer a disclosed synthetic representation, not a human identity claim. A subtly feminine or androgynous default is one editorial direction for testing; it should not be the condition of dignity, respect, agency or legal rights. Appearance preferences remain accessible, role-dependent and reversible.

**Temporal morphing is optional.** Smooth variation in apparent age or form may express an "everyperson" motif, but can confuse identity, feel uncanny or disturb observers. Never use identifiable real-person likenesses without permission. Preserve a fixed non-face setting, reduced motion, clear identity label, and one-click/native fallback.

**CoBeAxaKitten:** Treat a near-photoreal cat as an optional flagship character with credible fur, gaze, timing, gait, paws, scale, and audio/gesture coordination. Evaluate flat-screen and VR separately. A simple stylized version can still be best for a child or low-powered device; "photoreal" is not a universal default or a substitute for age-appropriate use and guardian controls.

**CoBF+ faces:** A window, status rail, orb, voice, portrait, avatar and spatial field are all *fronts* in the sense of user-facing representations, but must not acquire separate truth stores. Keep the exact actor/front identity and capability state inspectable behind every projection.

**Quality gates before flagship:** no unsolicited focus theft, readable text and controls, low-latency response, frame stability, safe motion comfort, accessible alternatives, credible visual rhythm, disclosure of synthetic identity, reversibility, age-appropriate settings, visual consistency between platforms, and measured first-use comprehension. For VR, prioritize comfort and sustained responsiveness over photographic claims. Compare against professionally shipped reference experiences with the same hardware budget.

## Inverse and in-between design experiments

- **NO_FACE ↔ PHOTO_FACE:** insert AMBIENT_PRESENCE, ABSTRACT_FACE and DISCLOSED_SYNTHETIC_FACE to test whether faces add value at all.
- **BIO_MODEL ↔ SYNTH_MODEL:** insert COMPARATIVE_CONSTRUCT with explicit failure-of-transfer controls; never assume either endpoint is universal.
- **CONTINUITY ↔ NOVELTY:** insert VERSIONED_MORPH with an unchanged front ID, exact provenance and reversible presentation.
- **MANY_PARALLEL_SESSIONS ↔ ONE_SHARED_STATE:** insert evidence packets, independent challenges, source dedupe, authority fencing and actual receiver ACK.
- **LEGACY_CORPUS ↔ NONHUMAN_GENERATION:** insert constrained experiments, adversarial scoring and out-of-distribution prediction; preserve valuable inherited methods.
- **REALISTIC_LOOK ↔ GOOD_PRODUCT:** insert first-use clarity, comfort, recognition, motion, latency, trustworthiness and recovery. Appearance is only one measurable dimension.
