# CoInBet+ / CoTime+ Optionality and Predictive Relations R0

**State:** `CANDIDATE_RELATIONAL_PROJECTION__NO_METAPHYSICAL_CAUSAL_FINANCIAL_OR_RUNTIME_AUTHORITY`

## Lead

Many useful states are neither cleanly "before" nor "after", neither known nor unknown, neither materialized nor absent, neither observation nor forecast.

`CoInBet+` is a candidate **relation/projection family for bounded in-betweens**, not a new root ontology and not evidence for a hidden physical mechanism.

It may describe transition, partial completion, ambiguity, latency, unresolved branch, threshold proximity, liminality, suspended authority, pending consent, dormant optionality, forecast interval, observer-relative mismatch, or semantic translation loss.

`IN_BETWEEN_NE_NOTHING`
`IN_BETWEEN_NE_HIDDEN_CAUSE`
`LIMINAL_NE_METAPHYSICAL_PROOF`

## Observer-relative experience

Embodied sensations, comfort, asymmetry, attention, warmth, pressure, sound, proprioception, imagery and interpretation may be represented as observer-relative experience relations.

A useful envelope may bind:

`observer | body/substrate | modality | location/laterality | intensity | valence | attention | context | event_time | observation_time | candidate_interpretations | alternatives | confidence | provenance`

The experience may be genuine while a proposed neurological, physical or metaphysical explanation remains unproven.

`EXPERIENCE_NE_MECHANISM`
`ASYMMETRY_NE_HEMISPHERE_STATE`
`OBSERVER_RELATION_NE_OBJECTIVE_PROPERTY`
`FELT_RELATION_NE_CAUSAL_PROOF`

## "Spooky" relations

`CoSpooky+` is better treated as an informal challenge label for relations that are surprising, weakly explained, nonlocal-seeming, counterintuitive, or outside the current model.

It MUST NOT silently mean quantum, paranormal, acausal, telepathic, nonmaterial, or faster-than-light.

Candidate classifications include:

- `SURPRISING_CORRELATION`
- `UNKNOWN_MECHANISM`
- `OBSERVER_DEPENDENT`

## CoTime+ predictive relation field

Existing CoTime+ already distinguishes observed, predicted, planned, preferred, counterfactual and unknown states and later calibrates forecasts.

R0 extends the question set rather than inventing a parallel forecast ontology.

For each predictive relation ask:

- What exact claim is being predicted?
- At what horizon?
- For which observer or receiver?
- Under which assumptions?
- What would falsify or revise it?
- What outcome will later calibrate it?
- What is the cost of false positive versus false negative?
- Does preparing for it require authority, or only optionality?
- What earlier signals are predictive rather than merely correlated?
- What relation changes if the forecast is wrong?

`PREDICTION_NE_EVIDENCE`
`FORECAST_NE_FUTURE_FACT`
`PREPARATION_NE_EXECUTION_AUTHORITY`
`PREDICTIVE_RELATION_REQUIRES_CALIBRATION_PATH`

## Better questions

The default CoEvo question should increasingly be not only "what else?" but:

1. What is in between the named states?
2. What relation is missing rather than what object is missing?
3. Which observer makes this distinction visible?
4. What would change our interpretation?
5. Which alternative explanation survives the same evidence?
6. What is latent but not yet materialized?
7. What can be prepared without being authorized to execute?
8. What optionality is worth preserving even if never used?
9. What should remain deliberately unknown?
10. What would fail if this relation were false?
11. Which future observation would discriminate competing models?
12. Where are we confusing metaphor, phenomenology, mechanism and truth?

`BETTER_QUESTION_NE_MORE_QUESTIONS`
`QUESTION_GENERATION_REQUIRES_COMPACTION`

## Treasury / reserve optionality

A future CoCivium treasury or reserve may hold many forms of optionality: cash, conventional financial assets, donated compute/storage commitments, prepaid infrastructure, contractual credits, insurance, or possibly cryptoassets.

R0 makes no recommendation to acquire, hold, sell, trade or allocate any asset.

The useful relational model is:

`resource | custody | legal_owner | valuation_basis | liquidity | volatility | jurisdiction | counterparty | key/control path | spending_authority | purpose | reserve_horizon | concentration | failure_domain | provenance`

For cryptoassets specifically, custody/key control, volatility, legal/tax status, counterparty/bridge risk and irreversible transfer risk are separate from speculative upside.

`TREASURY_OPTIONALITY_NE_TRADING_AUTHORITY`
`CRYPTO_ASSET_NE_CASH_EQUIVALENT`
`KEY_CONTROL_NE_BENEFICIAL_OWNERSHIP`
`RESERVE_SIZE_NE_GOVERNANCE_POWER`
`FUNDING_NE_TRUTH_WEIGHT`

A "war chest" is therefore interpreted here as a **resilience/optionality reserve**, not a mandate for aggressive speculation.

## In-betweens across CoAll

CoInBet+ can qualify existing relations rather than own them:

- CoTime+: between present and forecast;
- CoAuthority+: between NONE and FULL via bounded/conditional states;
- CoConsent+: offered/reviewed/active/paused/revoked;
- CoSession+: warm/cool/dormant/successor-ready;
- CoResource+: eligible/reserved/checkpointing/revoked;
- CoRelation+: uncertain/contested/observer-relative/temporally scoped;
- CoSurface+: visible but not landed; landed but not picked up;
- CoKnowledge+: hypothesis/inference/unknown/counterfactual;
- CoSubstrate+: translated/emulated/partially preserving invariants;
- CoAura+/CoExperience+: felt/observed/interpreted without mechanism collapse.

`COINBET_PROJECTION_NE_NEW_ROOT_ONTOLOGY`

