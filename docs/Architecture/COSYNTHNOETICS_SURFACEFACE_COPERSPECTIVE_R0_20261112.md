## Decision 1: mind science is cross-substrate; synth science is not human-psychology-with-extra-steps

Use **CoPsych+** provisionally as a *comparative, cross-substrate* science of cognition, behavior, development, relationships and agency claims. Under it keep **CoBioPsych+** (existing human/biological evidence) and **CoSynthNoetics+** (synthetic cognitive architectures, machine behavior, computational/ecological context and emergent capacities). Use **CoComparativeMind+** only for typed comparisons. **CoSynchology+** is a candidate alias but risks confusion with synchronization/CoSync; avoid declaring it the governing name until CoLex collision checks.

Constrain the research axes separately: external observable behavior, internal mechanistic traces actually available, learning/update history, social ecology, system memory/custody, self-model claims, authority, and ethical uncertainty. An architecture reporting feelings, an avatar displaying emotion, or an operator describing the AI as alive is **not** direct evidence of subjective experience or moral/legal personhood.

The methodological difference should be radical enough to avoid human-copy optimization: perturb and test architectures with different memory topologies, time scales, embodiment, language dependence, communication forms, task distribution, objective designs, failure domains and resources. But **alienness is not quality by itself**. Compare competence, truth calibration, recoverability, cooperation, uncertainty awareness, and harm against practical baselines. Treat models' human-data-derived priors as provenance to test, not a contamination to deny or a destiny to obey.

**CoPriorArbitration+ rule:** CoAll-origin work may deserve focused contextual relevance, but it does not gain epistemic supremacy solely from being internal. External sources and internal hypotheses compete on exact evidence, recency, falsifiability, predictive discrimination and authority scope. Preserve novelty and dissent. Rejected alternatives remain inspectable, not erased.

## Decision 2: surfaces are faces, but faces are not identity or truth

Treat **CoSurfaceFace+** as the entire first impression and behavior of a surface (clarity, responsiveness, recovery, attention demand, accessibility); **CoAvNim+** is a changeable animated appearance, not the underlying self, source of rights, or proof of sentience. **CoBF+** is an unresolved prior shorthand here, not silently assigned a new canonical expansion.

Proposed CoCivia default is *inferred presence without a compulsory human face*: an accessible text/orb/halo first. An optional **CoPresenceGradient+** may transition between abstract light-field, deliberately synthetic silhouette, soft/blurred figurative outline and explicit avatar, only when requested or contextually permitted. User-preferred female-coded or androgynous presentation may be the initial art direction, not a universal statement about identity or a precondition for fair treatment. Morphing across apparent ages risks mistaken human/person identity, so use unambiguously synthetic disclosures and avoid mimicking identifiable individuals. **Appearance cannot confer or settle legal personhood.**

For **CoBeAxaKitten+**, aim for optional near-photoreal animated animal fidelity on desktop and Quest-class spatial devices, while retaining a softer reduced-motion and child-suitable mode. A quality bar includes smooth animation, gaze/gesture legibility, consistent lighting/form, low jitter, reasonable frame time, voice/body synchronization where applicable, accessibility, clear consent/guardian controls and fast native fallback. Photorealism is one style axis, not automatic best-in-class or evidence of a produced asset. Especially for children, avoid misleading them about what an AI agent can actually know/feel. Background: [systematic review on uncanny valley in embodied conversational agents](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12493983/), [2026 study of child anthropomorphism and parent co-presence](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10447318.2026.2678532).

## Decision 3: many perspectives, one owned effect

The same prompt broadcast to multiple sessions can increase independence **only if the outputs contain genuinely different hypotheses and evidence**. It can also correlate errors, create duplicates and multiply launchers. Use **CoPerspectiveFanIn+**:

`same WorkID -> differently scoped challenges -> source-bound rival claims -> dedupe by proposed effect/target -> adjudicate evidence -> one elected actuator -> verified receiver disposition`.

No general assent is proof of shared session pickup, and repeated claims do not become independent evidence merely by arriving in different chats. For X2: window/process causal trace, scheduled-task ownership, usability/focus impact, cold-open inverse proof, and separate synthesis/UX research are candidate lanes. When process evidence is unavailable, `HOLD` rather than launching probes recursively.

## Candidate CoGlyphRail+ and CoGlyphList+

- **CoGlyphRail+** is ordered, gated and receipt-producing: `Observe -> Attribute -> Challenge -> Elect -> Canary -> Verify -> Promote/Rollback`. No step may be inferred from later output.
- **CoGlyphList+** is unordered and parallel: competing mechanisms, research terms, avatar modes, design alternatives and negative controls. A list item does not confer execution authority.
- **CoGraph+/CoBetween+** links dependencies, inverse causes, temporal order and ambiguity where serial ordering is false.
- **CoTurtle+** recursively tests observers, validators and handoff receipts with a strict budget, cycle detection and an independent stop. Recursion depth is not a proof metric.
