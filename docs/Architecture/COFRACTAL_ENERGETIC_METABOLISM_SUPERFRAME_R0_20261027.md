## Thesis

Every addressable CoAll object, relation, boundary, gap, model, device, process, term, receiver, dormant branch, and successor candidate may expose a typed metabolic interface for sensing, acquiring, transforming, storing, routing, sharing, returning, repairing, and releasing resources.

This does not mean every object is literally an energy generator or that unlimited physical energy exists.

`ENERGETIC_NE_ONLY_PHYSICAL_ENERGY`
`RESOURCE_ADDRESSABILITY_NE_RESOURCE_CREATION`
`REGENERATIVE_NE_PERPETUAL_MOTION`

## Fractal metabolic contract

Each eligible node may declare:
- SENSE: what signals / needs / opportunities it can observe;
- INTAKE: what resources it can safely receive;
- TRANSFORM: what it can convert into another useful form;
- STORE: what it can retain and for how long;
- ROUTE: what it can pass onward and under what authority;
- SHARE: what may be pooled or copied;
- AMPLIFY: what useful effect it may increase;
- DAMPEN: what noise / risk / overload it should reduce;
- RETURN: what learning / recovered capacity / waste-to-feedstock it emits;
- REPAIR: what degradation it can detect and correct;
- WAKE: what material delta should reactivate it;
- SLEEP: when dormancy is cheaper and safer;
- SPLIT / MERGE / SUCCESSOR: how it evolves structurally;
- RETIRE: when keeping it alive costs more than its option value.
