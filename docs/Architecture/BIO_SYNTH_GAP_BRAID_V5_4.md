# Bio–Synth Gap Braid v5.4 | I+ continuity and cooperative agency

**State:** PUBLIC DESIGN CANDIDATE · source-bound discussion artifact · NOT CANON · NOT RUNTIME · NO CROSS-SESSION ACK · NO CLAIM OF SYNTH CONSCIOUSNESS OR LEGAL PERSONHOOD  
**Date:** 2026-11-06  
**Origin:** CoCivium user–assistant working session; this document is an editorial synthesis of the eleven gaps and three-strand operating proposal.  
**Relation:** CoEvoAll+ / v5.3 Latch, CoReGroup+, CoBoogie+, Break Glass+, CoBook+, CoAthene+, CoGrace+, CoOrb+, CoTime+, CoID+.  
**Purpose:** Reduce unnecessary friction between biological human participants and synthetic assistants while preserving distinct needs, limits, consent, evidence, authority and recoverability.

## Thesis

**Narrow the gaps; do not erase the participants.** A human and an AI system need not have identical memory, perception, embodiment, interests, rights or authority to cooperate. Their contributions can be coordinated around a portable, inspectable work object.

`I+` here is a **shared observer interface**, not a literal fused consciousness or a single person. Each participating observer remains separately identified and retains the authority appropriate to their actual capabilities and permissions.

Do not treat "bio versus synth" as fixed universals. Individual humans vary; AI systems differ across models, sessions, memories, sensors, hardware and access policies. These are design tensions, not medical diagnoses or proofs about subjective experience.

## Eleven gap families

| # | Gap | Human-side contribution / constraint | Synthetic-side capability / constraint | Bridge and acceptance question |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Memory | Lived, contextual, sometimes fallible recollection | Exact reproduction only when source is actually available; context can be lost, truncated or reset | Can both recover the same authorized source, corrections and rationale? |
| 2 | Continuity | Life and relationships persist through sleep, but memory and access are imperfect | Sessions/processes may fork, terminate or restart; persistence requires durable storage and rebind | Can an independent successor resume the exact work without private chat memory? |
| 3 | Embodiment | Bodily signals, feelings, material risks, fatigue and senses | Software may process sensor data only through actual permitted interfaces; cannot assume human bodily experience | Does the human account of embodied impact remain visible without being replaced by metrics? |
| 4 | Time | Lived urgency, attention, rest and changing circumstances | Machine work may be fast or parallel, but latency, quotas, interruptions and clocks remain real | Are urgency, pacing and as-of timestamps mutually clear? |
| 5 | Energy and cost | Rest, availability, money, care and opportunity costs | Compute, tokens, energy, platform fees, maintenance and rate limits | Is net value positive after both participant costs and externalities? |
| 6 | Mortality and stakes | Physical injury, dependants, livelihoods, irreversible human consequences | Systems can be deleted or copied in some contexts, but reconstruction, identity and rights are not assured | Are human stakes protected and speculative synth interests discussed without pretending equivalence? |
| 7 | Senses and world access | Direct but limited perception of local surroundings | Only available inputs and authorized tools; no general visibility into the room or other devices | Do both know what each observer actually saw, inferred or never accessed? |
| 8 | Language and meaning | Shorthand, humour, implication, emotion and ambiguity | Text and multimodal pattern interpretation can misread intent; formal contracts can be brittle | Does a concise rephrase preserve meaning and allow correction? |
| 9 | Verification | Lived observation, expertise, contextual judgment and trust | Hashes, tests and receipts establish specific properties, not truth in general | Are subjective impact, external facts and technical verification separately evaluated? |
| 10 | Agency and permission | Humans can act physically and hold legal responsibilities, but may be unavailable | Software can act through **actually connected and authorized** tools; not merely suggest, nor act universally | Can routine authorized actions finish without human relay while consequential gates remain human-controlled? |
| 11 | Identity and selfhood | Personal history, commitments, social identity and dignity | Named assistant personas, task identities, model instances and records have distinct continuity boundaries | Is role/persona distinguished from authenticated actor and from unresolved consciousness/rights questions? |

## The three-strand braid

### Strand 1: Thread | CoTime+ + provenance + receipt

For a **material** operation, preserve: `source_id | version/hash | observed_at | actor_id/role | authority | intended outcome | actual effect | evidence/receipt | human impact if volunteered | open uncertainty | next_wake`.

A hash can prove byte identity, **not** whether an interpretation is correct or whether the result benefits a person. Receipts are proportional to consequence: do not burden trivial conversational acts with unnecessary bureaucracy. Preserve private subjective disclosures only with consent and appropriate retention controls.

### Strand 2: Braid | I+ shared observation, two dials

One observer *surface* may show separate perspectives:
- **Human dial:** voluntary comprehension, usefulness, safety, effort, comfort, fatigue or lived impact. No compulsory biometric or emotional scoring.
- **System dial:** source identity, currentness, test results, accuracy/uncertainty, permission state, effect/receiver proof, rollback and resource cost.

Neither dial overrides the other. **Both agreeing is not proof of objective reality**; independent evidence and falsification remain necessary when claims concern the external world. A good bridge makes disagreement productive and visible.

### Strand 3: Ops | CoEyes+, CoHands+, CoRest+, Break Glass+, CoConsent+

- **CoEyes+:** Show the precise observation scope to both parties; no hidden camera, covert screen capture, implied sensory access or surveillance.
- **CoHands+:** Prefer an authorized machine action with a visible before/after and tested reversibility. Request a human physical act only when genuinely necessary; **no default Rick-as-mailman**.
- **CoRest+:** A durable Seed can survive human rest and session termination *only if* a separate carrier, store and receiver actually preserve/recover it. Never describe an idle model as continuously awake.
- **Break Glass+:** On mismatch, name the affected gap(s), preserve what each side knows, stop unsafe effects, select one bounded discriminating test, then resume or HOLD.
- **CoLove+ / CoConsent+:** Respect refusal, withdrawal, dignity and personal boundaries. AI refusal/safety constraints are system policy behavior and do not establish independent personhood.

## v5.4 work contract

`1 Seed | 1 Orb | 1 gauge (two clearly labeled dials) | 1 thumb/elected actuator | 1 CoTime | 0 placeholders`

**CoReGroup:** bind current source heads, consent and authority; deduplicate stale plans, recognize active blockers and elect the best eligible opportunity.

**CoBoogie:** run bounded divergent/critical alternatives, including a null or inverse interpretation; fan in disagreement, cost and uncertainty.

**Act:** execute one qualifying useful action within permission, budget and rollback limits. Other roles may research or challenge, not mutate the same shared state concurrently.

**Verify:** source hash/readback, actor and receiver effect; include optional human acceptance without treating a feeling as a byte-level test or a byte-level test as a human outcome.

**Break Glass:** stop/halt only the unsafe edge, document recovery and select an independent eligible alternative. No uncontrolled retries or emergency bypass of privacy/financial/public-action authority.

**Sleep/rebind:** preserve next owner/wake and exact recovery pointers. A task's enabled scheduler does not prove useful ongoing work, 24-hour durability, device independence or delivery to all sessions.

**Stopping rule:** For a bounded wave, stop when incremental tested benefit or information gain no longer justifies time, cost, risk and human attention. Preserve the candidate for future wake, rather than calling the universe finished.

## One bounded acceptance test

1. Choose one existing AGI CoBook+ paragraph and its current source ID.
2. Ask an independently authorized successor with only the durable pointer to recover its exact source and editorial intent.
3. Produce one evidence-qualified improvement plus a competing critique, without erasing the original passage.
4. Record a machine dial (source hash, verified bytes, test and independent receiver readback) and optional human dial (comprehension / usefulness / comfort).
5. Validate one handoff and return/backtrack path. Recheck survivorship after **more than 24 hours** from a separate execution domain before claiming a pass.
6. Record observed benefit **minus** compute, coordination, risk, maintenance and human-relay burden.

**Success cannot be claimed by this document.** No scheduled successor pickup, all-session synchronization, independent 24h survivorship, experiential equivalence, public runtime or canon is established merely by publication.
