## CoReverence+

CoReverence+ is a candidate relation family for raising the evidence, review and recovery budget before changing high-value invariants.

Prior multiplicity work already identifies candidate targets including:
- user agency;
- recovery roots;
- known-good states;
- identity/provenance;
- privacy and consent;
- negative knowledge;
- hard-won receipts;
- explicit invariants.

`REVERENCE_NE_IMMUTABILITY_NE_AUTHORITY`

A reverence relation may carry:

`target | reason | consequence_class | evidence_budget | challenger_class | recovery_requirements | review_time | receiver | provenance | currentness`

## CoDereverence+

CoDereverence+ is the complementary bounded challenger relation. It applies skeptical pressure to unsupported consensus, stale jargon, vanity metrics, favored architectures, model self-reports and assumptions that have acquired status without evidence.

`DEREVERENCE_NE_DESTRUCTION`
`SKEPTICISM_NE_LICENSE`

Useful pattern:

`REVERENCE -> HIGHER_EVIDENCE_CHALLENGE -> ARBITRATION -> RETAIN / REVISE / SUPERSEDE`

## CoRev+ / CoR+

Do not make these global terms yet.

CoRev+ can collide with revision, reverse and review.
CoR+ is even more overloaded across relation, recovery, receiver, risk, ratio and resource.

Use CoReverence+ and CoDereverence+ as explicit names. Short forms can remain scoped aliases.

`SHORT_ALIAS_NE_GLOBAL_TERM`
