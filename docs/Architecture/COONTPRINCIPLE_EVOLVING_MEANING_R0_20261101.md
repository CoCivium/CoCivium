## CoHereNow

Human meanings and ontologies have evolved through generations, languages, cultures, disciplines, institutions, and civilizations. No one generation or AI owns their meanings. CoAll should preserve enough stable identity to coordinate change, **without freezing meanings or making every representation interchangeable**.

CoOntPrinciple+ is proposed as a **meta-principle and validation lens** layered on existing CoMeaning, CoLex, CoTerm, CoRelation, CoTime+, CoSource, CoIndex/GIBindex, CoMethodField and CoProjectField. It is **not** a replacement lexicon, a universal ontology, or a claim that all truths are merely opinions.

## Meta-principles

1. **Identity versus meaning:** Keep stable addressable concept IDs separate from words, definitions, examples, labels and current deployments. A label can change while identity persists; a split in meaning may require new IDs.
2. **Contextual meaning:** Record language, community, discipline, locale/jurisdiction if applicable, purpose, observer role, audience, scope, time and source. Never infer universal scope from a local definition.
3. **Historical lineage:** Preserve predecessor senses, provenance, attribution and reasons for change. Earlier cultural and intellectual sources should be treated as priors and credited where appropriate, not copied into a fictitious original invention.
4. **Plural interpretations:** Allow context-dependent and disputed senses, with mutual challenge, translation, and negotiated agreements. Avoid winner-takes-all harmonization.
5. **Epistemic class:** Distinguish empirical claims, formal definitions, hypotheses, value judgments, symbolic interpretations, fiction, unknowns, and legal/contractual definitions. They require different tests and cannot borrow certainty from one another.
6. **Typed relation integrity:** Distinguish `IS_A`, `PART_OF`, `ANALOGOUS_TO`, `CORRELATES_WITH`, `CAUSES`, `DEPENDS_ON`, `PERMITS`, `CLAIMS_AUTHORITY_OVER`, `CONTRADICTS`, `SUPERSEDES`. Do not collapse them merely because a graph looks more elegant.
7. **Absence and nonrelation:** Preserve `NOT_OBSERVED`, `EXPLICITLY_ABSENT`, `NOT_CONNECTED`, `INACCESSIBLE`, `WITHHELD`, `UNKNOWN`, and `CONTRADICTED` as distinct states. Missing evidence is not evidence of impossibility.
8. **Translation-loss accounting:** When crossing natural languages, CoTerms, mathematics, stories, media, models, cultures or devices, mark what's preserved, lost, ambiguous or newly introduced. Make a human-inspectable fallback.
9. **Meaning is consequential:** Definitions can grant or deny power, responsibility, access, benefit, reputation or compensation. Any change affecting consent, privacy, rights, money, safety or governance must trigger affected-party review, not silent label migration.
10. **Composability without capture:** Allow semantic overlaps, inversions, nulls, boundary concepts and generative extensions, but materialize only what adds discriminating value over existing CoLex/CoIndex structure.
11. **Evidence and challenge:** Every nontrivial adoption should expose counterexamples and a repair path. A popular name is not evidence of physical truth, ethical legitimacy, product impact or legal entitlement.
12. **Meta-evolution:** These principles are themselves versioned candidates, open to revision, challenge, comparative testing, partial supersession, and revival of rejected alternatives. Preserve why a principle changed.

## Meaning record sketch

A relation or term sense may carry:

```text
concept_id                 stable identity; not name or repo location
sense_id                   one versioned/contextual sense
term_aliases               language-specific labels
definition                 plain language and technical optional projection
context                    language, culture, community, discipline, role, audience
valid_time                 when sense applied / could apply
observed_at                when source was inspected
source_refs                primary provenance and earlier credited priors
epistemic_class            observation, model, preference, myth, unknown, etc.
status                     working / contested / qualified / superseded / retired
relations                  typed, scoped, source-linked graph edges
translation_loss           what changed between projections
authority_confidentiality  who can make which changes / who can see what
dissent                    alternatives and counterexamples
successor_refs             fork, merge, alias, split, revise, retire
receiver_effects           UI, project method, policy, contract, access, payment...
verification               exact tests, reviewer, acceptance and negative cases
```

Not all fields must exist on every user-facing card; missing mandatory effects-related data means HOLD, not invented certainty.

## Lifecycle of meanings

```
NOTICE -> SOURCE PRIORS -> CONTEXTUALIZE -> QUALIFY ->
RELATE -> COMPARE -> CHALLENGE -> SELECT CONTEXTUAL SENSE ->
PROJECT TO RECEIVERS -> VERIFY INTERPRETATION -> PRESERVE LINEAGE ->
REVISE / ALIAS / TRANSLATE / SPLIT / MERGE / RETIRE / REOPEN
```

Unreviewed generated strings stay `UNNAMED_RELATION` or `WORKING_LABEL` per the existing CoSubscriptionEcology; do not mint a global CoTerm merely because a synthetic worker found a catchy name.

## CoProjectField+ and GroupBuild+ application

Project management must not silently treat different stakeholder senses of `value`, `success`, `contribution`, `owner`, `volunteer`, `paid`, `complete`, `evidence`, `safe` or `fair` as equivalent.

**Example: GroupBuild park project.**
- For a resident, success might mean increased access and enjoyment.
- For an accessibility advocate, success might mean specific safe routes and inclusive participation.
- For a city official, success might include legal compliance, maintenance and budget.
- For a contributor, success might include recognition and learning.
- For a sponsor, success might include explicit, permitted public acknowledgement.

The project can build a **shared, negotiated acceptance contract** while preserving these different senses. It should never retroactively change a volunteer's `contribution` into an assignment of commercial IP or redefine `consent` as implied by participation.

**GroupBuild.org / .com semantic firewall:** A common concept can have separate community and commercial projections, but contributor ownership, contracts, permission, payback and legal entity status must be proved per context. The .org and .com labels do not themselves establish rights or non-profit status.

**CoMethodField linkage:** choose science for falsifiable empirical claims; participatory methods for shared values; engineering methods for requirements and system behavior; creative practice for artistic quality; governance for legal/ethical decisions. One method's evidence cannot silently become another method's acceptance criterion.

## Semantic change propagation

A source-bound change candidate to an affected sense should produce:
1. Exact predecessor/successor identifiers and reasons, with CoTime and source references.
2. A typed delta: `ALIAS`, `BROADEN`, `NARROW`, `TRANSLATE`, `SPLIT`, `MERGE`, `DISAGREE`, `SUPERSEDE`, `RETRACT`, or `REOPEN`.
3. Affected receiver inventory, not a blind global rewrite: CoLex/GIBindex, CoIndex, CoTheoryAll, CoMethodField, GroupBuild/CoProjectField, CoOrb/CoMedia, CoRails and public explanatory surfaces.
4. Impact triage for rights, money, child/guardian safety, privacy, project status and contractual terms.
5. Parallel independent review proposals and **one elected writer per target**, with diff, conflict checking and explicit receiving acknowledgements.
6. Positive/negative tests, translation-loss and a meaningful human comprehension check where needed.
7. Source receipt and separate receiver-effect receipt. No silent data migration.

## First discriminating tests

- Two GroupBuild participants say they want a `successful` project but specify incompatible outcomes. Does the onramp expose that disagreement before assigning work?
- A label changes from `volunteer contribution` to `commercial deliverable`. Does the rights/payment impact gate HOLD the change?
- An `ANALOGY_TO` relation is rendered as `CAUSES`. Does the validation reject that translation?
- A concept has two translations with competing cultural senses. Can both be preserved while allowing a context-specific local working agreement?
- A previously useful term becomes misleading or harmful. Can it be retired or renamed without erasing history or relabeling old consent?
