## Lead

CoBoogie+ and CoRegroup+ should become first-class, substrate-neutral recovery and reconciliation relations rather than informal session rituals.

They exist to answer two different questions:

- **CoBoogie+:** what materially changed, drifted, disappeared, appeared, or became stale?
- **CoRegroup+:** given those deltas, what is the finite reconciled state and the smallest safe continuation?

Historical operating form:

`Detect -> Pause -> Inventory -> Regroup -> Choose -> Commit -> Resume`

CoRegroup is a phase within the broader CoBoogie cycle, but may also be invoked independently when a bounded reconciliation is already justified.

## CoBoogie+ R1 candidate semantics

CoBoogie+ is a bounded freshness/drift/surface audit.

### Trigger classes

A CoBoogie MAY be triggered by:

- substantive human return after absence;
- device or execution-surface return;
- receiver currentness becoming stale;
- contradictory evidence;
- meaningful new donor material;
- failed or missing receiver pickup;
- lifecycle transition such as Twilight, succession, close or reawakening;
- suspected duplicate or stale control surface;
- provider/local failure-domain change;
- large semantic delta requiring reconciliation before mutation.

It SHOULD NOT run merely because time passed.

`TIME_ELAPSED_NE_BOOGIE_REQUIRED`

### Required observations

A bounded CoBoogie SHOULD inspect only relevant surfaces and collect:

- observed current heads;
- source-time and observation-time;
- proven receipts/readproof;
- stale claims;
- missing expected artifacts;
- duplicate/supersession candidates;
- authority and confidentiality boundaries;
- receiver health/currentness;
- effect leases or mutation collisions;
- newly available capabilities;
- route changes;
- unresolved negative knowledge;
- candidate wake conditions.

### Output

CoBoogie emits a **delta field**, not a verdict.

Candidate relation form:

`subject | relation | object | observed_at | source | evidence | currentness | epistemic_class | authority_ceiling | confidentiality | collision_domain | next_receiver`

## CoRegroup+ R1 candidate semantics

CoRegroup+ is a finite reconciliation/disposition over an observed delta field.

It SHOULD:

1. preserve predecessor evidence;
2. bind exact current observations;
3. distinguish changed fact from changed interpretation;
4. relate duplicates without silently deleting them;
5. mark stale witnesses as historical rather than pretending they never existed;
6. preserve unresolved contradictions;
7. elect one bounded next transition per collision domain;
8. externalize the reconciled frontier to an appropriate durable receiver;
9. stop.

Possible dispositions:

- `KEEP_CURRENT`
- `SUPERSEDE_FOR_SCOPE`
- `RETAIN_HISTORICAL_WITNESS`
- `MERGE_RELATION_ONLY`
- `BRANCH_FOR_CHALLENGE`
- `HOLD_MISSING_EVIDENCE`
- `WAKE_RECEIVER`
- `ROUTE_TO_LOCAL_EXECUTION`
- `ROUTE_TO_GITHUB_PROJECTION`
- `PARK_DORMANT`
- `CLOSE_SAFE_BOUNDED`

`REGROUP_NE_REWRITE_HISTORY`  
`REGROUP_NE_AUTOMATIC_PROMOTION`

## CoSync / CoResync distinction

- **CoSync+**: bounded alignment/routing of verified state between relevant receivers.
- **CoResync**: explicit realignment after detected drift/loss.
- **CoBoogie+**: discovers and characterizes the material drift/currentness field.
- **CoRegroup+**: reconciles that field into finite dispositions and next deeds.

A full recovery can therefore be:

`CoBoogie -> CoRegroup -> CoSync/CoResync where warranted -> bounded CoDeed`

but the stages are not required to collapse into one operation.

`SYNC_NE_DISCOVERY`  
`DELIVERY_NE_PICKUP`  
`POINTER_NE_ACK`
