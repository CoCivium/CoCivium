# CoAuraRel+ / CoTime+ Temporal Evidence Braid R0

**State:** `SYNTHETIC_TEMPORAL_EVIDENCE_BRAID__NO_NEUROLOGICAL_METAPHYSICAL_MEDICAL_FINANCIAL_OR_EFFECT_AUTHORITY`

## Lead

The useful evolution is not to decide whether a striking experience is "really physical" or "really spooky."

It is to preserve the whole evidence braid:

```text
observation
  -> candidate interpretations
  -> competing alternatives
  -> precommitted predictions
  -> later observations
  -> calibration
  -> revised relations
  -> better next questions
```

without allowing any one layer to impersonate another.

This makes CoAuraRel+, CoInBet+ and CoTime+ mutually useful.

## Why the braid matters

A flat note tends to encourage one of two bad outcomes:

1. the original experience gets dismissed because mechanism is unknown; or
2. the preferred explanation quietly becomes "what happened."

The braid preserves:

- the experience as an experience;
- the metaphor as a metaphor;
- the hypothesis as a hypothesis;
- the forecast as a forecast;
- the outcome as a later observation;
- the calibration relation;
- the unresolved alternatives.

That allows learning without ontology laundering.

## Better-question compiler

The highest-value next state is often not another answer.

R0 compiles competing interpretations into discriminating questions and elects the highest-information questions without granting any execution authority.

For this synthetic braid, the top questions are:

- does the relation change when context changes while observer/object remain fixed?
- does the relation change when attention changes while context remains fixed?

The compiler may select a question.

It may not perform an experiment, make a medical judgment, trade an asset, or mutate runtime authority.

`QUESTION_SELECTION_NE_EFFECT_AUTHORITY`

## CoTime+

Every forecast in the braid must remain precommitted before the observed outcome.

Later hits, misses, invalidations and unresolved cases remain in history.

A successful forecast updates relational evidence. It does not retroactively turn its source hypothesis into fact.

The existing CoTime probabilistic-skill layer remains the place for proper scoring against baselines over enough precommitted cases.

`FORECAST_NE_FUTURE_FACT`

`PREDICTION_NE_EVIDENCE`
