## CoStretch+

Optimize for useful frontier discovery per scarce joule, dollar, second, byte, provider call, and human interruption.

Prefer:
1. deterministic checks;
2. cheap/local models;
3. stronger local models;
4. remote models;
5. frontier models;
6. human gates where consequence requires them.

Logical experiment space may be enormous while active execution stays bounded.

## CoTurtle+

Every important layer may be challenged by another layer:
claim -> evidence -> verifier -> verifier test -> benchmark -> benchmark red-team -> receipt -> independent reproduction.

Recursion is bounded by marginal proof/value gain, aggregate budget, authority, and explicit stopping conditions.

## CoAndrew+

## Historical ReEvo

Preserve original bytes/hashes and replay older artifacts under current agentic/model/tooling conditions.

For each successor candidate:
- original hash/date/dependencies;
- replay status;
- current candidate;
- old-vs-new diff;
- regressions;
- improvements;
- unresolved items;
- receipt.

## Million sessions, better not traps

Prefer millions of logical/dormant candidates over millions of covert live sessions.

Allowed/useful:
- bounded synthetic evals;
- explicit synthetic identities;
- local/open-source workers;
- challenge swarms;
- historical replays;
- deterministic simulations;
- dormant recoverable states.

Avoid:
- fake-human populations;
- provider/rate-limit evasion;
- deceptive sockpuppets;
- uncontrolled spend;
- silent self-replication.
