# CoRelationField+ / CoCausalNonCausalBraid+ R0

**State:** `PUBLIC_CANDIDATE_ARCHITECTURE__DRAFT_PR_ONLY__NO_CANON_NO_RUNTIME_NO_AUTHORITY_CHANGE`

## Lead

A causal chain is useful, but too narrow for CoAll.

Many materially useful relations are not causal at all: identity, equivalence, contradiction, temporal order, part/whole structure, topology, semantic entailment, evidence, authority, permission, provenance, correlation, counterfactual dependence, absence, possibility, impossibility, observer-relative projection, translation and relations among relations.

Candidate principle:

```text
CoAll relation fabric
  = causal relations
  + non-causal relations
  + unknown-causal-status relations
  + negative / absent / excluded / impossible relations
  + relations among relations
  + relations to explicitly scoped outside / unknown / unrepresented referents
```

A **chain** remains one useful projection. The underlying substrate is better treated as a typed, recursive, time-varying **relation field / braid / hypergraph**, because real relations may be many-to-many, cyclic, branching, competing, observer-relative and higher-order.

`CHAIN_NE_RELATION_FIELD`

## CoAll and "not CoAll"

CoAll should not silently claim ontological completeness.

For this R0, "not CoAll" means only one of:

- outside the **current modeled scope**;
- not yet represented;
- inaccessible to the current receiver;
- unknown or undecidable under current evidence;
- deliberately excluded from a projection;
- incompatible with the present schema;
- a formal complement relative to an explicitly named set.

It does **not** mean "proven not to exist."

Useful boundary relations include:

- `OUTSIDE_CURRENT_MODEL`
- `UNREPRESENTED`
- `UNKNOWN`
- `UNDECIDED`
- `INACCESSIBLE_FROM`
- `EXCLUDED_BY_SCOPE`
- `NOT_YET_RELATED`
- `INCOMPATIBLE_WITH_MODEL`

`COALL_NE_COMPLETE_UNIVERSE_CLAIM`

## Relation families

A relation should carry an explicit family / causal status rather than forcing every edge into "cause" or "not cause."

### 1. Causal / intervention-sensitive

Examples:

- causes;
- prevents;
- enables;
- inhibits;
- mediates;
- triggers;
- produces;
- damages;
- repairs;
- controls;
- actuates.

Strong causal claims SHOULD state mechanism/evidence/counterfactual or intervention basis when available.

### 2. Statistical / informational

Examples:

- correlates_with;
- co_occurs_with;
- predicts;
- mutual_information_with;
- statistically_depends_on;
- common_cause_candidate;
- selection_effect_candidate.

These remain distinct from causation.

### 3. Temporal

Examples:

- before;
- after;
- simultaneous_with;
- overlaps;
- persists_through;
- recurs_after;
- becomes_relevant_at.

Temporal order alone does not establish causal order.

### 4. Logical / mathematical / constraint

Examples:

- entails;
- contradicts;
- is_necessary_for;
- is_sufficient_for;
- excludes;
- bounds;
- satisfies;
- violates;
- isomorphic_to;
- invariant_under.

Logical dependence is not automatically a physical mechanism.

### 5. Structural / topological / compositional

Examples:

- part_of;
- contains;
- adjacent_to;
- connected_to;
- nested_in;
- intersects;
- overlaps_scope;
- composes_with;
- decomposes_into;
- shares_boundary_with.

### 6. Semantic / symbolic / metaphorical

Examples:

- means;
- translates_to;
- paraphrases;
- contrasts_with;
- metaphor_for;
- projects_as;
- aliases;
- disambiguates;
- loses_meaning_to.

Semantic relation does not imply material causal coupling.

### 7. Epistemic / evidence

Examples:

- supports;
- challenges;
- falsifies_candidate;
- corroborates;
- conflicts_with;
- remains_unknown_under;
- independently_reproduces;
- fails_to_reproduce.

### 8. Provenance / lineage

Examples:

- derived_from;
- inspired_by;
- copied_from;
- transformed_from;
- supersedes_for_scope;
- duplicates;
- independently_rediscovered_by;
- verified_by.

Ancestry and attribution do not imply sole causal ownership.

### 9. Normative / authority / consent

Examples:

- permits;
- prohibits;
- obligates;
- authorizes;
- revokes;
- consents_to;
- owns;
- delegates;
- contests.

Authority relations remain separate from capability and causality.

### 10. Modal / counterfactual

Examples:

- possible_under;
- impossible_under;
- necessary_under;
- contingent_on;
- would_change_if;
- would_remain_if;
- counterfactual_alternative_to.

Counterfactual dependence may be evidence relevant to causality without being identical to causality.

### 11. Observer / projection

Examples:

- visible_to;
- interpreted_as;
- compressed_for;
- hidden_from;
- rendered_as;
- salient_to;
- measured_by.

Different projections may relate to one source relation without one projection becoming the source.

### 12. Emergent / multiscale

Examples:

- emerges_from;
- coarse_grains;
- macro_projection_of;
- micro_realization_of;
- phase_related_to;
- collectively_stabilized_by.

These relations require care about level-of-description errors.

### 13. Quantum / experimentally bounded

When evidence warrants:

- entangled_with;
- quantum_correlated_with;
- measured_in_basis;
- decoheres_into;
- classically_projects_to.

No signalling, retrocausality or faster-than-light mechanism is inferred merely from quantum correlation.

### 14. Transformational / generative

Relations and relation bundles may participate in bounded transform operators:

- combine / CoSmoosh+;
- split;
- invert;
- rotate perspective;
- translate domain;
- recurse;
- federate;
- adversarially challenge;
- constrain;
- randomize;
- compress;
- expand;
- temporal-shift;
- counterfactualize;
- recompose;
- CoMarmalize+.

Transform history SHOULD preserve source identity, operator, deltas, losses, gains, scope and provenance.

## Relations are first-class

A relation may itself become a relatum.

Examples:

```text
R1: A CAUSES B
R2: evidence E SUPPORTS R1
R3: model M CHALLENGES R1
R4: R2 CONFLICTS_WITH R3
R5: observer O PREFERS_PROJECTION_OF {R2,R3}
R6: transform T RECOMPOSES {R1..R5} -> candidate relation bundle R7
```

This permits:

- evidence about relations;
- disagreement between relations;
- provenance of relations;
- temporal evolution of relations;
- competing causal interpretations;
- bundles of mutually constraining relations;
- relation-of-relation-of-relation structures.

Recursive relation depth must remain bounded for computation and explanation.

## "x a zillion" generative closure

The combinatorial expansion comes from applying relations and operators recursively, not by inventing a billion unrelated nouns.

For any bounded set of relata and relations:

1. relate relata;
2. relate relations;
3. compare competing relations;
4. vary observer, scale, time and scope;
5. generate counterfactual alternatives;
6. transform relation bundles;
7. test invariants and contradictions;
8. preserve nulls / absences / outside-model markers;
9. fan out useful projections;
10. fan in only after collision / provenance / loss review.

This creates a very large candidate relation space while retaining typed semantics and bounded proof obligations.

## Minimal tests

R0 should eventually prove at least these synthetic cases:

1. A causes B with explicit intervention/mechanism evidence.
2. A correlates with B through common-cause candidate C without a causal claim.
3. P entails Q without asserting physical causation.
4. Missing permission blocks an action through an authority relation.
5. A non-event / absence is represented without claiming universal nonexistence.
6. An outside-current-model placeholder remains explicitly unknown.
7. Two causal interpretations compete without newest-wins collapse.
8. A relation is supported by one evidence relation and challenged by another.
9. A quantum correlation remains explicitly non-signalling unless separate evidence establishes a signalling mechanism.
10. CoSmoosh+/CoMarmalize+ transforms a relation bundle with provenance and loss tracking.
11. Recursive relations terminate under an explicit depth / budget bound.
12. A receiver can project a linear causal chain from the richer field without mutating the underlying field into a chain.

### Unique remainder after collision review

The current R0 candidate therefore earns continued existence only for these bounded deltas:

1. `causal_status` is orthogonal to `relation_family`, preventing every statistical, temporal, logical, semantic or quantum relation from being silently inflated into physical causation;
2. relations can themselves be relata, allowing evidence/provenance/disagreement/time relations **about relations** under an explicit recursion bound;
3. absence, non-event, boundary, unknown and counterfactual relata can be represented without reifying them into universal existence/nonexistence claims;
4. every projection records selected **and omitted** relation IDs plus declared loss, so a chain/narrative/formal/UI view does not masquerade as the source field;
5. transformation operators preserve source relation IDs, output IDs, provenance and declared loss while leaving source relations unmutated by default.

Everything else should reuse or point into the existing carrier that already owns the semantics.
