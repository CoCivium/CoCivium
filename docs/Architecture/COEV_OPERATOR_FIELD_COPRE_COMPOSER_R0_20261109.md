## CoHereNow

The human-visible CoPre footer and native user-message composer are different surfaces. Assistant output in an ongoing chat generally contributes to that chat's context, but there is no proven native interface for silently injecting that text into the reply field, no guarantee of complete context retention, and no inherited across-provider/session authority. A request to **echo** the exact CoPre back as a new user turn is a possible **explicit user-controlled** option, but not a robust long-term custody root; unbounded recursive quoted prompts are both redundant and fragile.

The real requirement is an **exact, readable, durable CoPrePack** plus a separately controlled, testable input projection. Prefer one-click deliberate continuation or a verified machine-owned worker adapter, rather than Rick copying a huge text blob or mobile browser keyboard automation.

[CoPre R0 compiler](../../scripts/CoPreContinuationR0.py) now provides an explicit `--emit-prompt` output derived from a valid, source-bound pack. Its important continuation fields include WorkID and sequence, source revision, evidence, parent and packet hash, next deed and falsifier, observer/receiver, purpose, consent/authority limit, recovery, wake and stop. The projection **does not** alter the native ChatGPT composer, wake old tabs, grant permission, or prove a new receiver has read it. It is designed to be user-controlled and falsifiable.

### Push, pull, sides, inverses, intervals

Asset-level **push from within** includes introspection, recorded defects, CoMetaTrainables, CoCTA and CoJustify prior labels. The latter three require exact source discovery before their historical definitions can be promoted as implemented methods: a prior label is not the implementation. Asset evolution never self-certifies a mutation.

**Pull from a proposed future** includes anticipated receiver needs, predicted tasks, and revisable G_dSide candidate futures. This is *prospective planning*, not a physical retrocausal force. Actual demand and external contribution are separate inputs. Supply describes known capabilities or costs; side forces describe accessibility, ecological effects, third-party rights and constraints.

Each vector has a deliberate inverse, and each pair may be separated by a **typed in-between**: interface, missing source, translation loss, latency, consent boundary, nonexistent capability, or uncertainty. The direction and magnitude need not share physical units. Some are qualitative/normative or symbolic. No equation can legitimately add moral dignity, a latency metric, and divine revelation into one empirical number.

### Turtle nesting and GRAIL/CoPriMath

Recursive work operates over types: **Asset -> Operation -> Operation-selection rule -> Rule-selection method -> ...**. A larger CoTurtle is the relation and evidence *about* a smaller one, not a demand to spin up a new agent or compute infinity. Bound maximum depth, width, time, authority, and provenance fan-in. Nested loops should ask whether the operator selection improved outcomes, not whether it generated more terms.

GRAIL is retained as a lineage/recovery/graph design family pending exact mapping; do not collapse it into CoEv or infer that its letters represent a verified universal theory. CoPriMath can type-check functions, causal assumptions, mathematical objects and uncertainty. It cannot guarantee that metaphors, symbolic theology or unverified 'spooky' candidates describe new physics.

**Epistemic separation:** physical claims require observations; mathematical proofs require formal validity; life/awareness/sentience need scope-specific operational study and philosophical humility; spiritual, mythic, soul and G_d fields are legitimate symbolic/interpretive or hypothesis-generating lanes, **not self-evidencing observations or delegated authority**.

### Operator registry

The [machine-readable CoEv registry](../../ai/evolution/coev-operator-field-r0.json) proposes ten named forces and ten composable operator families: DISCOVER, MEASURE, CHALLENGE, MUTATE, RECOMBINE, SELECT, COMMIT, RECOVER, EVOLVE_SELECTION and EXPLORE_MYTH. Each distinct operator must specify semantic input/output typing, evidence class, constraints, source, receiver, falsifier, stopping rule, and safe effect boundary. The registry contains only candidate definitions and limited nesting, not executable evolution or universal interchangeability.

The four illustrative hyperrelations braid internal push/future pull/demand/supply, inversions/interstices, G_dSide/externalities, and recursion of selection itself. Before claiming synergy, test each composite *against its individual components and a no-change baseline*. A combined procedure can be worse than its best constituent.

## Minimal product experience

- CoOrb/CoBar shows one current WorkID, one source-bound NEXT deed, one CoPrePack indicator, source age and an optional deep link to related CoEv operators.
- Native composer is never overwritten silently. In current ChatGPT, a UI control may submit a user-confirmed CoPre prompt **as a new turn**, but this is not an automatic cross-session bridge and should not be required to keep work alive.
- On local/owned interfaces, an explicitly installed, permissioned adapter may prefill a composer and show the before/after preview, with undo and no background key logging. It must demonstrate actual API/device availability.
- A worker can act on durable CoPrePack within its own authorized substrate; it must never infer external effect permissions merely from an instruction string.
- If CoPrePack becomes large, use a content-addressed `CoPrePack+` link with loss-aware summaries, receiver-specific slices, checkpoints and bounded delegated work rather than echo the whole corpus into one mobile prompt.
- Quiet no-material-delta waves produce no repetitive CoPre message. A meaningful new wave derives different content from *actual material state changes*, not randomized prose.

## Acceptance and negative controls

1. **Rendering trap:** displayed CoPre is not asserted to be present in the composer.
2. **Prompt-only trap:** manually echoing text does not claim a new source version, new permissions, or receiver pickup.
3. **Hash trap:** modified source or predecessor invalidates exact successor identity.
4. **Bias/force trap:** a value-laden G_dSide candidate remains a revisable normative lens even if others agree.
5. **Nested turtle trap:** recursion depth is bounded; every nested cycle must prove its marginal information gain.
6. **Semantic conversion trap:** no conversion between spiritual metaphors, mathematics and physical measurement without a justified typed map.
7. **Synergy trap:** combined operators are compared against individual operators, inverses, preserved old version and no-change.
8. **Human/synth trap:** capability/maturity comparison does not score human worth, prove sentience, or confer power.
9. **Offload trap:** logical agent labels are not autonomous workers or independent failure domains.
10. **Independence trap:** a shared model/source produced in multiple sessions is not independent corroboration.
11. **UX trap:** a beautiful CoPre footer does not prove the user can practically recover or act without manual relay.
12. **Stop trap:** no bound material change or authorized receiver yields QUIESCENT/HOLD, not another invented wave.
