## Relation operators

Reuse existing operators wherever possible:
`DISCOVER | SOURCE | QUALIFY | RELATE | COMPARE | CHALLENGE | INVERT | NEGATE | ELSE | NULL | BOUND | COMPOSE | SPLIT | PROJECT | TRANSLATE | SIMULATE | CANARY | PROVE | RECOVER | SUBSTITUTE | AMPLIFY | DAMPEN | RETURN | EVOLVE`

Add Bloom-specific operators:
- `CROSS` — cross two or more typed families;
- `PERMUTE` — reorder relation roles;
- `MIX` — create bounded composite candidates;
- `INTERSTICE` — inspect relations between categories;
- `ANTI` — model explicit contrary/anti-structure without assuming symmetry;
- `META` — act on the grammar itself;
- `UNDISCOVER` — deliberately remove unsupported interpretation while preserving lineage;
- `REDISCOVER` — re-open dormant or retired material on new evidence;
- `REFRAME` — change axes without claiming source reality changed;
- `DECOUPLE` — separate concepts that were incorrectly bound;
- `ENTANGLE_CANDIDATE` — mark potentially coupled relations for testing without asserting physical entanglement.

## Chaos / drift / evolution

Treat these as distinct dynamics:
- **Chaos**: sensitive dynamics that may remain deterministic but unpredictable;
- **Drift**: accumulated change without necessarily selected improvement;
- **Evolution**: variation + selection + inheritance under a stated fitness regime;
- **Noise**: variation without established useful structure;
- **Perturbation**: bounded disturbance;
- **Succession**: replacement of one viable regime by another;
- **Collapse**: loss of viability;
- **Phase change**: qualitative regime transition.

`CHAOS_NE_DRIFT_NE_EVOLUTION`

## Physical / nonphysical / spiritual lanes

CoAllBloom permits these lanes to coexist but not borrow evidential status from one another.

### Spiritual / theological lane
