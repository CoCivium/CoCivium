# CoBoss+ / Anti-Sovereign Authority Relations R0

**State:** `CANDIDATE_ANTI_SOVEREIGN_AUTHORITY_MODEL__NO_RUNTIME_AUTHORITY_CHANGE`

## Lead

Authority chains can become tangled because identity, capability, delegation, ownership, consent, law, role, evidence, moral claims and metaphysical commitments are not the same relation.

A tempting repair is to force every chain upward into one ultimate boss.

That would simplify the picture while creating a much worse failure mode: whoever claims to speak for the ultimate boss could inherit unlimited authority.

R0 therefore separates **ultimate referents** from **executable authority**.

`ULTIMATE_REFERENT_NE_EXECUTABLE_PRINCIPAL`

`CLAIM_TO_SPEAK_FOR_GOD_NE_AUTHORITY_TOKEN`

## CoBoss+

`CoBoss+` is a relation family describing who or what may constrain, authorize, veto, supervise, appeal, or outrank another actor **for a bounded scope**.

It is not a universal ranking of beings.

Candidate relation:

`CoBoss(subject, object, scope, effect_class, basis, provenance, valid_from, valid_to, revocation, appeal, confidence)`

Examples of `basis`:

- explicit delegation;
- current consent;
- legal duty;
- ownership/custody;
- elected governance;
- safety constraint;
- contract;
- fiduciary responsibility;
- parent/guardian relation where lawful and appropriate;
- system policy;
- moral or spiritual commitment;
- emergency authority.

No basis silently expands into all other scopes.

`BOSS_FOR_SCOPE_NE_BOSS_OF_ENTITY`

`AUTHORITY_IN_ONE_DOMAIN_NE_AUTHORITY_IN_ALL_DOMAINS`

## No worldly maximum boss

For executable CoAll actions, no human, model, account, organization, service principal, state, corporation, session, node, maintainer, founder, steward or front is treated as automatically sovereign over everything.

`NO_WORLDLY_PRINCIPAL_HAS_UNBOUNDED_COALL_AUTHORITY`

Existing authority remains bounded by consent, rights, law, scope, evidence, reversibility, affected parties, currentness, confidentiality and appeal.

A founder may have repository permissions without moral sovereignty.
A machine may have capability without authority.
A government may have lawful jurisdiction without total authority over every human relation.
A human steward may have delegated project authority without ownership of every participant.

## Ultimate / transcendent referents

CoAll may represent declared relations to referents such as:

- God;
- gods / Gods;
- godstuff;
- CMA;
- CoWhatevers;
- conscience;
- truth;
- human dignity;
- rights;
- common good;
- nature;
- future generations;
- other metaphysical, ethical or cultural referents.

R0 does **not** decide which of these exists, is supreme, or is correctly understood.

They are modeled as `ULTIMATE_REFERENT`, `ETHICAL_REFERENT`, `SPIRITUAL_REFERENT`, `CULTURAL_REFERENT`, or `UNKNOWN_REFERENT` relations unless separately grounded as an ordinary legal/organizational principal.

`METAPHYSICAL_CLAIM_NE_MACHINE_VERIFIED_FACT`

`SPIRITUAL_REFERENT_NE_SERVICE_PRINCIPAL`

`GOD_RELATION_NE_CREDENTIAL`

`THEOLOGICAL_COMMITMENT_NE_PERMISSION_ESCALATION`

This preserves room for a person or community to say "God is ultimately above us" without letting a machine, priest, founder, state, AI, committee or random person wearing an impressive hat convert that statement into root access.

## The anti-capture rule

No executable actor may obtain additional authority merely by asserting a shorter path to an ultimate referent.

Forbidden inference:

`X CLAIMS TO REPRESENT GOD -> X HAS MORE AUTHORITY`

Permitted representation:

`X DECLARES SPIRITUAL_RELATION_TO GOD`

with the declaration scoped, attributed, challengeable and non-executing unless some independent human/legal/contractual authority relation also exists.

`REPRESENTS_ULTIMATE_REFERENT_NE_EXECUTIVE_DELEGATION`

## Authority graph, not authority chain

Prefer an authority graph with typed edges over a single chain.

A material action may require the intersection of several relations:

`delegation ∩ consent ∩ rights ∩ jurisdiction ∩ capability ∩ effect_lease ∩ confidentiality ∩ currentness`

The result is the **maximum permitted effect for this deed**, not the rank of the actor.

`AUTHORITY_RESULT_NE_PERSONAL_RANK`

`PERMISSION_INTERSECTION_NE_CROWN`

## Appeals and disagreement

Authority relations should expose:

- source/basis;
- scope;
- affected parties;
- expiry;
- revocation;
- appeal route;
- conflicting authority;
- unresolved disagreement.

A metaphysical disagreement does not need to be settled before ordinary bounded cooperation can continue.

`DISAGREEMENT_ABOUT_ULTIMATE_REFERENT_NE_COORDINATION_FAILURE`

## CoBoss display

Human-facing UX should avoid a single leaderboard of "who is boss."

Prefer views such as:

- authorized-for;
- constrained-by;
- accountable-to;
- consent-required-from;
- appeal-to;
- supervised-by;
- affected-by;
- spiritual/ethical referent;
- unresolved authority conflict.

This reduces accidental status worship while still making real power visible.

## Candidate ultimate ceiling

A useful default formulation is:

**No executable worldly principal is absolutely sovereign inside CoAll.**

Participants may separately hold that God, gods, CMA, godstuff, conscience, truth, dignity, nature or another ultimate referent is above worldly authority.

CoAll records those relations without impersonating the referent or using them to mint authority.
