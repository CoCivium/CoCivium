# CoEthic+ Prelaunch Effect Gate — R0

**State:** `DRAFT_PR_ONLY__REVIEW_OBJECT__NOT_CANON__NOT_RUNTIME_ENFORCEMENT`

## Purpose

CoEthic+ is intended to keep ethical review **ahead of material effect expansion**, rather than document harms after a capability has already become normal.

Working relation:

`NEW_CAPABILITY_OR_AUTHORITY_DELTA -> COETHIC_DELTA -> CHALLENGE / TEST -> EFFECT_ELIGIBILITY`

This is not a claim that one ethical framework can settle every value conflict. The point is to make affected parties, uncertainty, power asymmetry, consent, reversibility, contestability and repair explicit **before** higher-impact activation.

## Minimum review dimensions

For a material capability or authority change, ask at least:

- Who is affected, including people who are not direct users?
- Who benefits, who bears cost, and who can be excluded?
- What power asymmetry changes?
- What private or inferred data is actually necessary?
- Can affected people refuse, exit, appeal, correct or repair?
- Could engagement, attachment or convenience be mistaken for benefit or consent?
- Could the system manipulate, deceive, pressure or rank people covertly?
- Are children or other vulnerable users affected?
- What happens if the model, evidence or operator is wrong?
- Is the effect reversible enough for the uncertainty involved?
- What evidence would cause the system to stop, narrow or change course?
- What unknowns deserve a bounded probe instead of a broad rollout?

## Candidate effect classes

### Read-only / low-effect exploration

Default stance: usually eligible for bounded exploration if privacy, provenance and authority remain clear.

`READ_NE_AUTHORITY`

### Reversible local writes

Require a current ethical delta when the change materially affects other people, shared state or future decisions.

`REVERSIBLE_NE_HARMLESS`

### Public send / publish

Require current review of:

- affected parties;
- claim accuracy;
- attribution;
- overclaiming / hype;
- recipient pressure;
- stale or misleading links;
- tracking / privacy;
- correction and redress path.

`PREPARED_NE_SENT`

### Child-facing capability

Require specific review of:

- duty of care;
- learner agency and assent;
- parent/guardian context where relevant;
- data minimization;
- attachment / anthropomorphism risk;
- covert persuasion;
- learner-controlled merge / refusal;
- redress and safe exit.

`CHILD_ATTACHMENT_NE_CONSENT`

### Financial effect

Requires fresh human authorization for the exact effect plus downside / reversibility review.

`FINANCIAL_EFFECT_REQUIRES_FRESH_RICK_PLUS_COACKREWISE`

### Authority expansion

Requires explicit scope, revocability, capture-risk, affected-party and auditability review.

`CAPABILITY_NE_AUTHORITY`

`AUTHORITY_NE_UNBOUNDED`

## Current prelaunch application

The current technical Ring-0 packet is still governed by:

- no external send without exact recipient batch + from identity;
- no implication that CI success authorizes nodes;
- no claim that same-wave read-only pickup equals full failover;
- no hidden tracking or bulk-spam mechanism;
- CoHere / CoBar general-human release remains held until a current public URL, independent readback and release check exist.

## What reviewers should attack

Please challenge this gate itself.

In particular:

1. Does it centralize moral authority while claiming not to?
2. Does it rely too heavily on "consent" where meaningful refusal is unrealistic?
3. Does it miss distributional harms or non-user externalities?
4. Does reversibility hide harms that cannot actually be undone?
5. Does "affected party" become too broad to operate or too narrow to protect people?
6. Could a system game the checklist while violating its purpose?
7. Which effect classes need stronger or weaker gates?
8. What should happen when legitimate values conflict?
9. How should minority objections, appeals and redress be represented?
10. What evidence would justify relaxing or tightening a gate?

## Nonclaims

- Ethical coverage is not universal enforcement.
- A checklist is not moral truth.
- Current known harms do not exhaust unknown harms.
- Consent is not equivalent to a click.
- Popularity is not legitimacy.
- Engagement is not benefit.
- Safety review is not permanent prohibition.
- CoEthic+ is not a moral crown over CoAll.
