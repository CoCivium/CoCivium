## Minimal toy dynamics

```text
V[next] = min(K, max(0, (1 - d) * V[now] + qualified_gain + qualified_input))

qualified_gain  = evidence_current AND consent AND safety
                  ? min(proposed_gain, remaining_gain_budget) : 0
qualified_input = evidence_current AND consent AND safety
                  ? independently validated input : 0
```

`V` here is a synthetic **proxy** for retained useful value, not an objectively measured quantity or moral truth; `d` is decay/obsolescence, `K` is finite capacity. With headroom, `V` grows only where permitted gain plus input exceeds `d * V`. At capacity it plateaus, or the ecology must safely change the kind of contribution, lower costs, or expand actual independently evidenced carrying capacity. Upward numbers from repeat self-generated prompts do not qualify as gain.

**Example only:** begin at `V=10`, `d=0.1`, verified gain of `2` units/cycle, budget `4`, cap `16`. Ten finite mathematical cycles reach `16`; with no gain the same model decays to about `3.49`. The gain case does **not** grow forever; the cap correctly stops it. A 19-case direct arithmetic test suite covers decay, growth, balance, insufficient input, capacity, budget, stale evidence, unauthorized input, unsafe input and malformed values. **19/19 synthetic tests passed; real usefulness not tested.**

## CoHighlight amplification without audience manipulation

1. **CoLivingHighlight+** stores stable asset identity, source, read cursor, version, independently produced outcome, alternative interpretation, predecessor/diff and the current *qualified* front door. Treat a good historical asset as a reproducible seed, not a sacred immutable final version.
2. **CoSubscriptionEcology+** routes only relevant, consented updates to interested receivers. New meaning can reach a specialist audience without a firehose. Count voluntary task success, understanding and corrections, not impressions as adoption.
3. **CoMap+ / CoOrb+** show different scales: `impact`, `confidence`, `cost`, `harm`, `attention`, `currentness`, `dissent`, `uncertainty`. A larger orb or brighter aura is a render choice, never evidence of a larger underlying claim.
4. **CoTurtle+ / CoReview+** asks what *should not* be amplified. Stop unsafe waves, preserve counterexamples, retire duplicated dead ends and welcome criticism even when popularity falls.
5. **CoEnerget+ / CoPressure+** measures actual compute, human attention, money, environmental externalities, latency, reversibility and third-party burden. Pause or sleep where marginal verified gain falls below marginal cost.
6. **CoBoogie+ / CoRegroup+** handles oscillation failure, stale state, outages and reorganization. The objective is resilient continuation after disruption, not endlessly stronger agitation.
7. **CoTime+ / CoRenewal+** preserves snapshots and retests useful work at meaningful wake triggers. A daily freshness check can keep a highlight current without requiring daily new claims.

A bounded cycle: `QUALIFY → GENERATE → CHALLENGE → TEST → OBSERVE RECEIVER → VERSION HIGHLIGHT → COST CHECK → RETIRE/WAKE ↺`. Individual subcycles can shrink or stop while cumulative reproducible learning improves.
