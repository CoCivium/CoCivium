# CoQuestionReservoir+ R0H

State: CANDIDATE_FRONTIER_SEEDED_LAZY_QUESTION_RESERVOIR__NO_RUNTIME_EFFECT

## Why this exists

R0G proved that CoQuestionSpace+ can compile 10,000 synthetic candidates while keeping the active frontier bounded.

R0H binds that scale test back to the actual 50-question CoQuestionFrontier+ and turns the space into a lazy receiver/time-relative reservoir rather than a permanent backlog.

50 frontier cores x 10 perturbation operators x 5 receiver contexts x 4 time horizons = 10,000 deterministic projections.

## Perturbation axes

The ten operators are INVERT_PREMISE, COUNTERFACTUAL_REMOVE, ASSUMPTION_EXPOSE, NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE, MISSING_RELATION, FAILURE_DOMAIN_SHIFT, AUTHORITY_CHALLENGE, SUCCESSOR_RECONSTRUCTION, RETIREMENT_PROOF, and SURPRISE_OUTCOME.

Receiver contexts are HUMAN_OBSERVER, CUSTODY_AUDITOR, LOCAL_OPEN_WORKER, EXTERNAL_PROVIDER_RECEIVER, and PUBLIC_OR_COMMUNITY_RECEIVER.

Time horizons are NOW, NEXT_BOUNDED_WAVE, RECOVERY_AFTER_FAILURE, and LONG_HORIZON.

## Lazy wake model

The 10,000 projections are reproducible possibility objects. They are not all durably materialized.

A projection may wake when new evidence appears, an answer goes stale, a contradiction appears, authority or receiver relations change, currentness changes, a failure/recovery event occurs, prediction diverges from outcome, a dependency disappears, or retirement leverage changes.

## Sharding and pressure

The deterministic projection field is partitioned into 100 processing shards of 100 projections.

The default per-wave materialization budget is 64. This is a pressure/backlog control, not a semantic ranking.

SHARD_NE_SEMANTIC_BOUNDARY
MATERIALIZATION_BUDGET_NE_QUESTION_SUPPRESSION

## Better recurring question operators

- What assumption are we treating as fact?
- What evidence would reverse the next decision?
- Which relation is missing rather than merely unknown?
- Which receiver would interpret the answer differently?
- Which horizon makes the answer stop being valid?
- What looks independent but shares a failure domain?
- What is absent that should have been present?
- What proof retires the most open uncertainty?
- What should remain intentionally unresolved?
- Which question exists only because an old architecture made it seem necessary?

## Nonclaims

R0H does not claim that all 10,000 projections are useful, independent, or worth asking.

It does not execute any question or proof, and it does not change runtime, public, canon, integration, or authority state.
