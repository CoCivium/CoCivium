# CoQ&A+ / CoQuestionCommons+ / CoCore+ Candidate R0

**Date:** 2026-09-24  
**State:** `CANDIDATE__BRANCH_ONLY__NO_CANON_RUNTIME_PUBLIC_OR_AUTHORITY_EFFECT`

## Lead

Most AI use still begins with a question and ends with an answer.

CoAll should treat both as first-class relational objects rather than disposable chat turns.

The target is not merely better answering. It is a reusable ecology in which questions, answers, uncertainty, provenance, currentness, challenge, humour, mystery, learning value, receiver context and observed benefit can survive beyond the chat that produced them.

`QUESTION_NE_PROMPT_TEXT_ONLY`  
`ANSWER_NE_FINAL_TRUTH`  
`CHAT_TURN_NE_DURABLE_KNOWLEDGE_OBJECT`

## Existing substrate

Current CoParticipationEcology already recognizes `QUESTION` as a valid encounter yield and explicitly treats reusable question/answer relations as part of a shared relation commons.

Current CoLivingHighlight supports stable identity, many projections, provenance, challenge, currentness and historical versions.

Current CoTime+/CoAllPulseField keeps observation, prediction, valid time, supersession and currentness distinct.

Therefore CoQ&A+ should reuse these substrates rather than minting a second knowledge system.

`COQA_NE_NEW_GLOBAL_KNOWLEDGE_ROOT`

## CoQuestion+

A question is not just text ending in a question mark.

Candidate relations include:

- stable question identity;
- literal wording;
- normalized intent;
- asker/receiver context when appropriate;
- domain/topic;
- source situation;
- assumptions;
- ambiguity;
- requested depth;
- time sensitivity;
- privacy/confidentiality;
- authority/effect class;
- answerability state;
- open subquestions;
- predecessor/rephrasing relations;
- answer candidates;
- challenge relations;
- learning/scendence affordances;
- humour/mystery affordances;
- observed benefit;
- currentness.

Useful question classes may include:

`FACTUAL | EXPLANATORY | PROCEDURAL | COMPARATIVE | DIAGNOSTIC | CREATIVE | REFLECTIVE | TRAINING | MYSTERY | OPEN_RESEARCH | COUNTERFACTUAL | PREDICTIVE | HUMOROUS`

The classes are descriptive, not a ranking.

## CoAnswer+

An answer is a versioned claim/projection bound to a question and receiver context.

Candidate answer envelope:

`answer_id | question_id | epistemic_class | answer_core | source_refs | observed_at | valid_from | valid_to | confidence | assumptions | receiver_scope | projection_kind | supersedes | contradiction_refs | challenge_refs | uncertainty | next_questions | nonclaims`

Epistemic classes should align with existing CoEvo/CoPulse language where possible:

`OBSERVED | INFERRED | HYPOTHESIS | PREDICTED | PLANNED | PREFERRED | METAPHORICAL | MYTHIC | HUMOROUS | COUNTERFACTUAL | UNKNOWN`

`ANSWER_CONFIDENCE_NE_TRUTH_SCORE`

## Why questions themselves are valuable

A strong question can be reusable even when no answer is stable yet.

Questions may expose:

- hidden assumptions;
- missing evidence;
- unresolved contradictions;
- useful experiments;
- new comparison frames;
- falsification conditions;
- better next questions;
- training sequences;
- public onramps;
- humour;
- mystery;
- participation affordances.

`UNANSWERED_NE_USELESS`

A question can therefore be a CoBenefit-bearing object without an answer.

`CONSENSUS_NE_TRUTH`

## CoTime+ pivot

CoTime+ materially changes the product model.

Legacy Q&A products often behave as:

`question -> one answer -> done`

CoTime+ permits:

`question -> answer at t1 -> evidence changes -> answer at t2 -> prediction calibrated -> contradiction appears -> answer branches -> receiver sees what changed`

A question may remain stable while its best current answer changes.

An old answer may remain historically correct for what was known or valid at the time even when no longer current.

`OLD_ANSWER_NE_FALSE_BY_DEFAULT`  
`LATEST_ANSWER_NE_ONLY_USEFUL_ANSWER`  
`CURRENTNESS_NE_RECENCY_ONLY`

This enables living FAQs, living explainers, living training objects, living product support and longitudinal reasoning.

## CoBenefit+ relation

Do not optimize for engagement merely because questions can be addictive.

The preferred objective is useful relation recurrence and observed benefit.

Candidate benefit envelope:

`beneficiary | intended_benefit | observed_effect | baseline | evidence | counterfactual_confidence | attribution_uncertainty | duration | side_effects`

Examples of useful benefit:

- faster orientation;
- improved understanding;
- better decision process;
- fewer repeated questions;
- detected misconception;
- skill transfer;
- curiosity sustained;
- uncertainty made explicit;
- productive next action;
- reusable explanation;
- successful transfer across receivers.

`ENGAGEMENT_NE_COBENEFIT`  
`TIME_SPENT_NE_VALUE`

## CoScendence / training

CoQ&A+ can support deliberate capability growth without turning every interaction into a lesson.

Candidate path:

`question -> initial answer -> challenge -> alternate framing -> counterexample -> self-explanation -> transfer task -> reflection -> optional next level`

The system may expose difficulty, prerequisite, perspective and transfer relations.

`TRAINING_NE_COERCIVE_ONBOARDING`  
`COSCENDENCE_NE_HIERARCHICAL_SUPERIORITY`

## Mystery

Mystery should be a legitimate projection, not a failure to answer.

A mystery object can preserve:

- what is known;
- what is unknown;
- competing hypotheses;
- clues/evidence;
- dead ends;
- next discriminating tests;
- historical attempted answers;
- humour/poetry projections where appropriate.

`MYSTERY_NE_PERMISSION_TO_INVENT_FACTS`

This can create compelling onramps because unresolved questions often invite participation better than finished explanations do.

## Humour

Humour can operate on the question, answer, mismatch, ambiguity, timing, alternate interpretation or absurdity of the problem.

But humour must remain epistemically typed.

`FUNNY_NE_TRUE`  
`HUMOUR_NE_FACTUAL_OVERRIDE`

Useful forms include:

- playful wrong-answer contrast;
- absurd edge case;
- literal-vs-context mismatch;
- "CoNope+" style bounded error projection;
- memeable question/answer pair;
- receiver-relative joke with exact machine twin underneath.

## Onramp

A public onramp can begin from a question the visitor already cares about rather than from "what is CoCivium?"

Candidate:

`their question -> useful answer -> show evidence/currentness -> expose one related open relation -> optional deeper CoAll surface`

This is likely more natural than demanding ontology literacy before benefit.

`ONRAMP_NE_CONVERSION_FUNNEL`  
`CURIOSITY_NE_CONSENT_TO_DEEPER_ENGAGEMENT`

## CoQ&A as product family

Potential product surfaces:

- **Ask CoAll**: bounded receiver-relative answer with source/currentness.
- **Question Garden**: valuable open questions and challenge paths.
- **Answer Atlas**: multiple answers across time, receiver and evidence.
- **Why Did This Change?**: CoTime answer diffs.
- **CoMystery**: unresolved high-value questions with clues and tests.
- **CoTeach / CoScendence Path**: adaptive question sequences for capability growth.
- **CoHumour Q&A**: playful onramp with epistemic separation.
- **CoFAQ+**: living FAQ objects rather than static support pages.
- **CoAskBack+**: system asks the minimum useful clarifying question rather than guessing.

Names are candidate projections, not separate required subsystems.

`PRODUCT_NAME_NE_ONTOLOGY`

## AI-to-AI and human-to-AI

The same object model should support:

- human asks AI;
- AI asks human;
- AI asks AI;
- human asks group;
- system-generated diagnostic question;
- unanswered question donated to a future receiver;
- question generated from contradiction/currentness drift.

Thus Q&A becomes a coordination primitive, not merely a chatbot feature.

`ASKER_NE_HUMAN_ONLY`  
`ANSWERER_NE_AI_ONLY`

## CI+ / operationalization

First executable proof should remain boring and synthetic.

Candidate matrix:

1. factual answer with source refs;
2. time-sensitive answer with validity/currentness;
3. open mystery with zero forced answer;
4. humorous projection that cannot masquerade as observed fact;
5. training question with next-question relations;
6. answer supersession across CoTime;
7. negative fixture: unsupported high-confidence observed answer;
8. negative fixture: engagement-maximization objective mislabeled as benefit.

CI should validate shape and rails only.

`CI_PASS_NE_ANSWER_TRUTH`  
`SCHEMA_PASS_NE_EPISTEMIC_VALIDITY`

- question clustering;
- duplicate detection;
- source discovery;
- contradiction search;
- alternative answer generation;
- projection generation;
- receiver robustness testing;
- stale-answer detection;
- next-question generation;
- humour/mystery variants;
- answer-diff compilation.

Fan-in must preserve provenance, disagreement and uncertainty before any stronger answer is elected.
