## Thesis
Project management should evolve from a manually maintained task board into an evidence-bearing, recoverable field of intentions, capabilities, work, authority, consequences, relationships and time.

Humans, synths, and automations are starting role classes, not rigid front/middle/back tiers. Each should be elected to the situation where it has the greatest **marginal, complementary, verifiable leverage**, and should actively increase the leverage of the other contributors.

A human may build software; a synth may shape public UX; deterministic tools may deliver accessible creative projections. No role class owns a layer.

## CoLeverage+ selection heuristic
Candidate utility = expected beneficiary value x probability of useful effect x complementarity x timely resource fit
minus coordination overhead, opportunity cost, harm, dependence and recovery debt.

Consent, exact authority, privacy, rights, safety and recovery are hard constraints, **not scores that can be outweighed**.
Optimize team-level *marginal contribution*, not maximal worker utilization.

## Adaptive project graph
Stable IDs and typed edges connect:
- intent, beneficiary and requested outcome;
- participants, capabilities, availability, preferences and permissions;
- work objects, dependencies, conflicts, alternate approaches and resources;
- source evidence, counterevidence, risk, uncertainty and hypotheses;
- time, observed transitions, receipts, correction and recovery;
- predecessors, successors, split and merge relations.

Project and work lifecycle: DISCOVER -> QUALIFY -> ROUTE -> CO-PRODUCE -> CHALLENGE -> VERIFY -> PRESERVE -> EXTEND / RETIRE.

## Dynamic teaming cycle
1. Identify outcomes, affected parties, scope and evidence.
2. Discover capable humans, synths, automations and substitute paths, without assuming all are online.
3. Decompose only as far as it improves real outcomes; aggregate when cross-domain understanding matters more.
4. Assign independent bounded proposal lanes by comparative leverage, capability, availability and authority.
5. Challenge, deduplicate and fan in before electing one writer/actuator per collision domain.
6. Verify receiver-relative effects, track costs and failed alternatives, and write exact receipts.
7. Reallocate roles as evidence changes; release resources and allow work or teams to sleep, split, merge or end.
8. Learn from volunteer feedback without optimizing addiction or silent behavioral profiling.

## Product manifestations
- CoBar / CoOrb / CoMap / CoTime: visually rich human-level purpose, consequences, dependencies and progress.
- CoIndex / CoLex / CoTheoryAll: discoverable IDs, aliases, causal models and source-bound interpretations.
- CoMedia / CoBook / CoRelay: reuse the same source object in videos, web, education and social editions, with channel-specific permission and rights.
- CoOps / CoStead / CoFarm: durable work objects, capability leases, checkable outputs, hash receipts, rollback and resumption.
- BeaxaKitten / CoAvNim: optional, child-appropriate creative guidance; no claim of a cross-app or system-wide companion.

## Project-management successor test
Use CoCivium's public first-use journey as a concrete comparative pilot. Benchmark against a conventional task tracker on time to demonstrable outcome, human attention required, coordination loss, duplicate work, receiver comprehension, recovery and observed beneficiary value.

Measure a full journey: reachable domain -> appropriate beautiful landing experience -> useful voluntary interaction -> optional deeper evidence -> correction feedback.
