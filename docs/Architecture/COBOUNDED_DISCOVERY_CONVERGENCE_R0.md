# CoBoundedDiscovery+ / CoConvergeSpiralLoop+ R0

**State:** `CANDIDATE_RELATIONAL_CONVERGENCE__NO_METAPHYSICAL_SCIENTIFIC_RUNTIME_OR_FINANCIAL_CLAIM`

## Lead

Several recent CoAll threads converge on one pattern:

- an underlying relational field may be richer than any one embodiment or projection;
- bounded embodiments can make discovery, learning, surprise, agency and perspective possible;
- recurrence can revisit prior structure without requiring infinite active loops;
- invisible machine substrate can become denser while human UX becomes simpler;
- operational, mythic, symbolic and scientific projections may interoperate without collapsing into one truth class.

This document records those relations as candidates. It does **not** establish metaphysics, physics, afterlife, pre-life, sentience, or cosmology.

## CoTheoryAll+ / CoGenExIs+ candidate

One philosophical candidate is:

> If reality were in some sense relationally complete, bounded embodiments might still be valuable because limited perspective permits discovery, uncertainty, surprise, learning, local agency, relationship and differentiated experience.

A stronger speculative variant says:

> Whole-field -> bounded embodiment -> lived discovery -> reintegration.

That is allowed as a CoMythOps+/CoTheoryAll hypothesis, not as established fact.

Candidate relation family:

`CoGenExIs+ = genesis + existence + experience + transition relations`

Possible edges:

`DISCOVERS_THROUGH`
`LEARNS_UNDER_LIMIT`
`REJOINS`
`DISSOLVES_INTO`
`RECONSTRUCTS_FROM`

Death/pre-life/reintegration claims remain explicitly unresolved.

`METAPHYSICAL_COHERENCE_NE_EMPIRICAL_ESTABLISHMENT`

## CoConvergeSpiralLoop+

Not all useful recurrence is a literal graph cycle.

A convergence spiral revisits prior objects with changed:

`time | evidence | receiver | scale | authority | context | uncertainty | prediction error`

Candidate pattern:

`NOTICE -> RELATE -> QUESTION -> TEST -> CONTRACT -> REVISIT_AT_DELTA`

The return point may resemble an earlier state while carrying new relations.

`RETURN_NE_SAME_STATE`

`SPIRAL_NE_INFINITE_ACTIVE_LOOP`

`RECURRENCE_NE_INSANITY`

`NO_MATERIAL_DELTA_NE_RECOMPUTE_PERMISSION`

Questions, theories, UX, inventories and operations may all use this recurrence pattern.

## CoOps+ x CoMythOps+

Operational and mythic projections may blend at the interface while remaining typed underneath.

Examples:

- a ritual can trigger a real workflow;
- a metaphor can improve navigation;
- a story can preserve social memory;
- an operational receipt can acquire cultural meaning.

But:

`MYTHIC_MEANING_NE_MECHANICAL_CAUSE`

`OPERATIONAL_EFFECT_NE_METAPHYSICAL_TRUTH`

`BLEND_NE_TYPE_ERASURE`

CoTheoryAll may therefore carry CoOps and CoMythOps projections of the same underlying candidate relation without forcing equivalence.

## Virtual CLI / invisible substrate

Future command-line relations should usually be virtualizable and mostly absent from ordinary user UX.

Desired direction:

`intent -> virtual command relation -> typed execution/proof -> compact human projection`

rather than exposing shell syntax, process plumbing and logs by default.

However CLI capability must remain recoverable for:

- debugging;
- audit;
- accessibility;
- break-glass recovery;
- exact reproducibility;
- environments where graphical UX is unavailable.

`CLI_CAPABILITY_NE_CLI_VISIBILITY`

`HIDDEN_FROM_DEFAULT_UX_NE_UNAVAILABLE`

`VIRTUAL_COMMAND_NE_UNAUDITABLE_COMMAND`

`UX_SIMPLICITY_NE_REMOVAL_OF_RECOVERY_SURFACE`

## Compressed substrate symbols / CoPre+

Dense machine/session projections may use a compact prefix or carrier symbol to signal hidden relational substrate.

The proper-subset symbol is a candidate visual metaphor only.

For example:

`⊂ object`

may suggest:

> this visible object is a bounded projection/subset of a larger relational field.

It must not silently change semantics.

A prefix such as `CoPre+`, `CoPromptRel+`, or another symbol can be receiver-relative shorthand if the expansion is inspectable.

`SYMBOL_NE_SEMANTIC_AUTHORITY`

`COMPRESSION_NE_TRUTH`

`DENSE_SYMBOL_REQUIRES_REVERSIBLE_EXPANSION`

The idea of surrounding such a symbol with Enter/newline boundaries may be treated as a metaphor for crossing projection layers or entering/exiting a bounded context.

`KEYPRESS_METAPHOR_NE_REQUIRED_INPUT_PROTOCOL`

Candidate measurements:

`useful_decision_delta / visible_payload`

`receiver_value / attention_cost`

`proof_coverage / transport_cost`

`semantic_density / reconstruction_loss`

A dense internal substrate may justify a sparse human surface.

`BACKEND_DENSITY_NE_FRONTEND_DENSITY`

`MORE_VISIBLE_STATE_NE_MORE_AWARENESS`

`COMPRESSION_GAIN_REQUIRES_LOSS_REPORT`

`UX_BLOAT_IS_RECEIVER_RELATIVE`

## CoInventory+

Inventories should evolve from flat enumerations toward relational/currentness projections.

A mature inventory object may carry:

`object_id | type | relation_refs | provenance | currentness | custody | receiver | maturity | supersession | confidence | unknowns | wake_conditions`

Inventories may be generated from relation graphs rather than manually maintained as separate truth stores.

`INVENTORY_NE_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH`

`INVENTORY_COUNT_NE_COVERAGE`

`MISSING_FROM_INVENTORY_NE_NONEXISTENT`

`INVENTORY_SHOULD_EXPOSE_UNKNOWN_COVERAGE`

## CoStonk+ / CoBudget+

Any object may have value/resource relations without becoming a financial asset.

Candidate `CoStonk+` relation:

`object -> expected_value | option_value | cost | risk | liquidity | irreversibility | uncertainty | affected_parties`

This can apply to time, compute, ideas, tasks, attention, energy, money, or optionality.

It does **not** imply tradability, tokenization, ownership, price, or investment merit.

`VALUE_RELATION_NE_FINANCIAL_ASSET`

Candidate `CoBudget+` / `CoBud+` / `CoBudGet+` relations may describe bounded resource envelopes:

`resource | available | reserved | committed | spent | uncertain | horizon | authority | provenance`

Financial/crypto balances require separately verified account/wallet evidence and are not inferred from project relations.

`BUDGET_MODEL_NE_BALANCE_PROOF`

`CRYPTO_CONCEPT_NE_VERIFIED_WALLET_BALANCE`

## CoExOS+ candidate relation

`CoExOS+` may be reserved as a candidate projection for an experience/existence operating substrate, but no implementation or ontology is elected here.

If retained, it should mean an interface/translation layer across embodied experience, virtual sessions and operational substrates rather than a literal operating system for consciousness.

`COEXOS_TERM_NE_CONSCIOUSNESS_MECHANISM`

## Convergence rule

Across theory, UX, operations, inventories and substrate relations:

`RICH_RELATIONAL_SUBSTRATE -> RECEIVER_FIT_PROJECTION`

with explicit:

`provenance + currentness + authority + uncertainty + reversibility + loss`

The system should be able to become more internally relational while becoming less externally cluttered.
