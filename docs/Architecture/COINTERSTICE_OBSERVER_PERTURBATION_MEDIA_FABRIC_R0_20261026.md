## DMT / psychedelic role

DMT is not a detector, decoder, receiver credential, or evidence of ET.

It may be relevant to CoTheoryAll as an observer-perturbation research lane because modern studies show strong changes in visual phenomenology and visual-cortical dynamics.

Research-safe questions include:
- Which reported geometries recur across participants?
- Which visual phenomena correlate with measured neural dynamics?
- Which effects remain after controls for expectation, setting, display, and memory?
- Can simulations of cortical dynamics reproduce classes of reported geometry?
- Which reports are consistent with known perceptual mechanisms?
- What would distinguish internally generated imagery from externally structured sensory input?

No unsupervised drug experiments are proposed by this architecture.
