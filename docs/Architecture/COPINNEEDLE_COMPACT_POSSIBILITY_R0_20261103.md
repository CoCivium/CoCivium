## Core question

If a small point, phrase, seed, program or typed relational specification can *address* an immense space of possibilities, how can CoAll explore that space efficiently and selectively turn discoveries into independently verified benefits?

The image of "universes on a pin" and "camels through the eye of a needle" is a **metaphor for compact representation, factorization and recoverable generation**. It does not assert literal physical compression, unlimited information capacity or realizability of every candidate.

`COMPACT_DESCRIPTION_NE_COMPLETE_REALIZATION`  
`LOGICAL_OPTIONALITY_NE_LIVE_WORKER_COUNT`  
`POSSIBILITY_NE_TRUTH_NE_AUTHORITY_NE_BENEFIT`

## Relational address

Candidate coordinates may include:

`source/prior | typed relation | observer | valid_time | observed_time | recorded_time | received_time | scale | substrate | meaning | negation/inverse/null | uncertainty | privacy | authority | evidence | falsifier | cost | recovery`

Distinguish atemporal invariants from time-indexed claims. Preserve previous observations; later readback may correct current custody without retroactively changing an old witness's observation. In/out-of-scope and cross-organizational transitions require exact provenance and qualified permissions.

Use constrained, factorized representation where possible. A reversible recipe is not equivalent to a lossy summary. Never discard counterexamples, nulls, unknowns, conflicting interpretations or origin identities merely to compress the graph.

`PATTERN_RECURRENCE_NE_CAUSATION`  
`FACTORIZE_NE_ERASE_PROVENANCE`

## Execution ecology

`DEDUPLICATE -> FACTORIZE -> GENERATE -> RANK -> SPARSE_SAMPLE -> BOUNDED_PARALLEL_PREPARATION -> CHALLENGE -> VERIFY -> FAN_IN -> RECEIPT -> CHECKPOINT -> SLEEP/WAKE`

- Logical candidates may be numerous; active worker embodiments are resource-budgeted and few.
- Run independent computation and verification in parallel only when their inputs and assumptions are properly separated. Serialize conflicting effects within an elected collision domain: `MANY_WATCHERS_ONE_ELECTED_ACTUATOR`.
- Do not spawn provider sessions or maintain artificial hot loops merely because spare capacity or a generic prompt exists.
- Receiver needs, incremental changes, exact source/currentness checks and expected information gain determine useful wake.
- Evaluate **verified useful receiver outcomes per actual total cost**, not candidate or worker counts. Measure energy only if instrumented.
- Stop, compact or quiesce if coordination and proof debt rise faster than receiver benefit.

A testable proposed effect needs a prospective discriminator, controls/null model, prior-art review, competing explanations, independent replication and honest uncertainty. Do not assume or claim a G_d signal, observer, causal field, agreement or authority.

## Candidate discriminating benchmark (not executed)

Reuse pinned CoDoodle/CoNex, CoStretch/CoTurtle and CoLogicalSpawnPlanner fixtures. Compare **A: exhaustive or fixed-width generation** with **B: factorized, sparse, demand-driven generation** on the same bounded task contracts and budgets.

Measure: verified output equivalence, expected-versus-observed utility, counterexamples retained, duplicate evaluation avoided, p50/p95 latency, peak memory, coordination overhead, actual process-exit recovery, receiver readproof and human relay time.

Required negative fixtures: repeated but misleading pattern, expired observation, confidentiality collision, changed source pin and missing receiver readproof. A clean output hash alone cannot prove correct semantics or permission.

No demonstrated scaling or physical throughput improvement is claimed until these tests and independent reviewer checks pass.
