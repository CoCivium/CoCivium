# CoAll Bounded Discovery / Projection Ecology R0

**State:** `CANDIDATE_RELATIONAL_POTENTIAL_AND_BOUNDED_DISCOVERY__NO_METAPHYSICAL_TRUTH_CLAIM__NO_RUNTIME_OR_PUBLIC_EFFECT`

## 1. CoTheoryAll / CoGenEx+ hypothesis

CoAll may model a contrast between a very large relational possibility field and bounded local embodiments/observers that discover only part of it at a time.

Candidate cycle:

`RELATIONAL_TOTALITY_OR_UNKNOWN -> BOUNDED_PERSPECTIVE -> LOCAL_DISCOVERY -> RELATION_BUILDING -> RETURN_OR_REINTEGRATION_OR_UNKNOWN`

This may support CoTheoryAll+, CoMythOps+, CoGenEx+, CoGen+, CoGenExIs+, and CoExOS+ as hypothesis/metaphor families.

It does **not** establish pre-life omniscience, post-death reintegration, God, reincarnation, or universal consciousness as facts.

`METAPHYSICAL_MODEL_NE_ESTABLISHED_REALITY`  
`BOUNDARY_CAN_CREATE_DISCOVERY_VALUE`  
`DISCOVERY_NE_PROOF_OF_PREEXISTENT_OMNISCIENCE`  
`DEATH_RETURN_TO_ALL_NE_ESTABLISHED_FACT`

## 2. Potential relations versus instantiated relations

Objects such as water may potentially carry, mediate, store, transform, signal, reflect, transport heat, support habitat, mark boundaries, or become symbolic media.

Use lazy capability edges:

`CAN_POTENTIALLY_CARRY`  
`CAN_POTENTIALLY_MEDIATE`  
`CAN_POTENTIALLY_SIGNAL`  
`CAN_POTENTIALLY_STORE`  
`CAN_POTENTIALLY_TRANSFORM`

Materialize concrete edges only when context/evidence warrants them.

`POTENTIAL_RELATION_NE_INSTANTIATED_RELATION`  
`COOMNI_NE_EVERY_RELATION_AT_ONCE`  
`RELATION_SPACE_NE_RELATION_DUMP`

## 3. Virtual CLI / invisible substrate

Normal human UX should be intent-first. CLI/tool text can remain internal execution substrate.

Preferred path:

`USER_INTENT -> VIRTUAL_COMMAND_GRAPH -> TOOL/CLI MATERIALIZATION -> RECEIPT -> UX PROJECTION`

Exact CLI remains available for diagnostics, provenance, replay, advanced users, and break-glass recovery.

`CLI_NE_USER_UX`  
`HIDDEN_CLI_NE_UNAUDITABLE_CLI`  
`VIRTUAL_CLI_NE_NO_CLI`  
`USER_NE_TERMINAL_OPERATOR`

## 4. Compressed substrate symbols

A symbol such as `⊂` may mark that the visible object is only a bounded projection/subset of a larger relational state.

It must have an expandable machine/human meaning and must not become the sole carrier of critical semantics.

Line breaks / Enter-like delimiters may carry structural or temporal projection meaning, but never hidden authority.

`GLYPH_NE_ONLY_COPY_OF_MEANING`  
`DELIMITER_NE_AUTHORITY`  
`ENTER_NE_EXECUTION_PERMISSION`  
`COMPRESSED_PROJECTION_REQUIRES_EXPANDABLE_MEANING`

## 5. CoInventory+ / CoI+

Inventories should evolve from hand-maintained master lists toward generated relational indexes:

`scope | roots | relation_types | currentness | coverage | exclusions | uncertainty | generated_at | receiver | successor`

They may be lazy, sharded, receiver-relative and time-relative.

`INVENTORY_NE_WORLD`  
`INDEX_NE_CUSTODY`  
`INVENTORY_COMPLETENESS_REQUIRES_SCOPE`  
`MANUAL_LIST_NE_DURABLE_CONTROL_PLANE`

## 6. Payload density / CoUX bloat

Higher semantic density is useful only while receiver comprehension and actionability remain adequate.

Candidate relation:

`payload_density = useful_decision_information / visible_attention_cost`

This is heuristic, not a universal metric.

Prefer:

- compact default projection;
- expandable depth;
- progressive disclosure;
- machine-rich hidden substrate;
- currentness and provenance always recoverable;
- exception-first human attention.

`DENSITY_NE_COMPRESSION_AT_ANY_COST`  
`HIDDEN_SUBSTRATE_NE_HIDDEN_CRITICAL_CLAIM`  
`LESS_VISIBLE_NE_LESS_MEANING`  
`MORE_RELATIONS_NE_BETTER_UX`

## 7. CoConvergeSpiralLoop+

Useful recurrence:

`NOTICE -> RELATE -> PERTURB -> TEST -> COMPARE -> COMPACT -> CONVERGE_OR_PARK -> WAKE_ON_DELTA`

A spiral may revisit prior questions with better evidence or a different receiver, but it requires stop, park, compaction and wake conditions.

`SPIRAL_NE_ENDLESS_COMPUTE`  
`LOOP_NE_PROGRESS`  
`REVISIT_REQUIRES_DELTA_OR_NEW_RECEIVER_VALUE`

CoOps+ and CoMythOps+ may interoperate where operational systems carry cultural meaning and cultural models influence real operations, while preserving epistemic typing.

`MYTHIC_MEANING_NE_OPERATIONAL_FACT`  
`OPERATIONAL_EFFECT_NE_METAPHYSICAL_TRUTH`

## 8. CoCheap+ / commodity-by-automation

As automation improves, many formerly scarce deeds become cheap to summon.

Candidate CoCheap+ relation:

`capability -> automation -> marginal_cost_down -> summonability_up -> differentiation_moves_elsewhere`

What becomes cheap may include drafting, classification, translation, summarization, routine code generation, simple analysis, content variants, tests, boilerplate, and increasingly some design/prototyping.

Value then migrates toward scarce dimensions such as:

- trusted provenance;
- verified evidence;
- receiver fit;
- judgment under ambiguity;
- currentness;
- physical-world execution;
- genuine relationships;
- legal/ethical authority;
- scarce data/access;
- failure-domain resilience;
- accountable stewardship;
- taste and selection;
- integration and adoption.

`CHEAP_TO_GENERATE_NE_CHEAP_TO_VERIFY`  
`SUMMONABLE_NE_USEFUL`  
`AUTOMATED_NE_AUTONOMOUS`  
`COMMODITIZED_OUTPUT_NE_COMMODITIZED_TRUST`  
`MARGINAL_COST_DOWN_NE_TOTAL_SYSTEM_COST_DOWN`

This supports a "cheap trick" projection where low-risk commodity work is summoned on demand rather than permanently staffed or stored.

`COCHEAP_TRICK = EPHEMERAL_BOUNDED_SUMMONABLE_DEED`

It must still carry contract, provenance, budget, receiver and retirement conditions.

## 9. Compute placement and moving beyond X2

X2 should remain an edge/observer/control surface, not a custody or compute root.

Prefer a heterogeneous execution ecology:

`edge UX + local/open workers + remote/open workers + CI + independent nodes + replaceable provider models`

Moving work off X2 is desirable only when custody, authority, confidentiality, provenance, cost, recovery and failure domains are explicit.

`MOVE_OFF_X2_NE_MOVE_TO_ONE_NEW_ROOT`  
`REMOTE_NE_INDEPENDENT`  
`OPEN_SOURCE_NE_FAILURE_DOMAIN_INDEPENDENCE`  
`X2_NE_CUSTODY_ROOT`

## 10. Outreach / pay-it-forward

CoOutreach+/CoOut+/CoO+ may model voluntary dissemination of useful inventions, designs, educational material and pay-it-forward artifacts.

Manufacturers, researchers, open-source communities, educators and others may be candidate receivers.

But discovery of a candidate receiver is not authority to contact them.

`CANDIDATE_RECEIVER_NE_OUTREACH_AUTHORITY`  
`PAY_IT_FORWARD_NE_UNSOLICITED_BULK_OUTREACH`  
`PUBLIC_BENEFIT_INTENT_NE_RECEIVER_CONSENT`

## 11. CoBudget+ / CoBudGet+ / CoBud+

CoBudget+ should be broader than money:

`money | compute | energy | storage | network | attention | time | proof_debt | receiver_capacity | risk | authority_budget`

CoBudGet+ may be treated as budget acquisition/allocation dynamics; CoBud+ as compact human projection if useful.

Financial/crypto balances require exact custody/account/wallet evidence and must not be inferred from theory, plans, or stale records.

`BUDGET_NE_MONEY_ONLY`  
`WALLET_NAME_NE_BALANCE_PROOF`  
`ASSET_CUSTODY_NE_PROJECT_AUTHORITY`  
`PREDICTION_NE_TRADE_AUTHORITY`
