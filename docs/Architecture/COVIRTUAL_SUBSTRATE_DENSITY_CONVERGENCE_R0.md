# CoVirtualSubstrate+ / CoPayloadDensity+ / CoConvergeSpiralLoop+ R0

**State:** `CANDIDATE_PROJECTION_AND_METABOLISM_CONTRACT__NO_RUNTIME_OR_PUBLIC_EFFECT`

## Lead

The mature UX should expose **meaning, currentness, exceptions and choices**, while implementation detail such as CLI invocations, raw prompts, hashes, queues and worker chatter remains mostly virtual/substrate-level.

`CLI_NE_USER_UX_BY_DEFAULT`

This is not permission to hide power.

`INVISIBLE_SUBSTRATE_NE_UNAUDITABLE_SUBSTRATE`

## Virtual CLI

Future CLI relations SHOULD normally be:

`logical deed -> virtual command relation -> bounded executor -> receipt -> compact projection`

The user sees the deed and material result, not terminal plumbing.

CLI detail becomes visible when needed for:

- debugging;
- authority/consent review;
- security;
- reproducibility;
- failure recovery;
- expert inspection;
- explicit user preference.

`VIRTUAL_CLI_NE_NO_CLI`  
`HIDDEN_COMMAND_NE_HIDDEN_AUTHORITY`  
`INSPECTABLE_ON_DEMAND_REQUIRED`

## Invisible substrate markers

A compact marker such as `⊂` MAY represent "this visible object is a projection/subset of a larger substrate relation field."

Candidate projection:

`⊂ visible-object`

The symbol is presentation syntax, not ontology or authority.

It may be surrounded by visual/interaction whitespace, line breaks, Enter-like boundaries, animation, spatial depth, CoTrill+ pulses, or other n-dimensional projections.

`SYMBOL_NE_SEMANTIC_TOTALITY`  
`SUBSET_MARKER_NE_COMMAND`  
`DECORATIVE_DELIMITER_NE_AUTHORITY_BOUNDARY`

Every compact marker needs an accessible expansion path.

`COMPRESSED_SYMBOL_REQUIRES_EXPANDABLE_MEANING`

## CoPromptRel+ / CoPre+

Prompt/input text is one projection of a deeper intent relation.

Candidate chain:

`intent -> CoPromptRel -> substrate instructions/context -> worker materialization -> evidence -> receiver projection`

The raw prompt need not remain visible forever if identity, provenance, constraints, evidence and reconstruction needs are preserved.

`PROMPT_TEXT_NE_INTENT_TOTALITY`  
`HIDDEN_PROMPT_NE_UNACCOUNTABLE_INSTRUCTION`

## CoPayloadDensity+

Payload density is receiver-relative.

Useful dimensions include:

- semantic utility per visible token;
- decision-relevant relations per screen;
- provenance/evidence overhead;
- interaction count;
- bytes/tokens transmitted;
- hidden substrate complexity;
- compression loss;
- reconstruction cost;
- ambiguity introduced by compression;
- accessibility cost;
- latency/energy/compute cost.

`DENSITY_NE_QUALITY`

`SHORTER_NE_BETTER_BY_DEFAULT`

`COMPRESSION_NE_ERASURE`

The target is not minimum text. It is **minimum avoidable burden for the required meaning and proof**.

## CoBloat+

Candidate CoBloat signals:

- repeated provenance already available behind drill-down;
- backend identifiers in default UX;
- duplicate relation projections;
- stale instructions;
- raw CLI output without decision value;
- full histories when deltas suffice;
- excessive CoTerm exposure before user benefit;
- forensic trace depth on low-stakes objects;
- duplicated status labels that do not alter action.

A dense surface can still be bloated if its density is irrelevant.

`INFORMATION_DENSE_NE_DECISION_DENSE`

## CoInventory+ / CoI+

Inventories should increasingly become relational/currentness indexes rather than static lists.

Candidate inventory object:

`object_id | relation_family | currentness | receiver | evidence | custody | maturity | successor | wake_condition | retirement | density_class`

Inventory rows may be virtualized, sharded and lazily materialized.

`INVENTORY_COUNT_NE_ACTIVE_WORK`  
`INVENTORIED_NE_CURRENT`  
`CURRENT_NE_VISIBLE`

## CoBudget+ / CoBud+ / CoBudGet+

Budget should be plural and typed, not merely money.

Candidate budget dimensions:

`attention | tokens | compute | energy | latency | storage | money | risk | authority | privacy | receiver capacity | proof capacity`

`CoBudGet+` can be a playful projection of "what budget gets allocated where," not a financial execution primitive.

`BUDGET_NE_MONEY_ONLY`  
`BUDGET_AVAILABILITY_NE_SPEND_AUTHORITY`

Financial/crypto balances require a fresh authoritative source. No balance is inferred from relation models.

`MODEL_MEMORY_NE_CURRENT_BALANCE`

## CoStonk+

`CoStonk+` may be a humorous/analytical relation family for assets, optionality, volatility, treasury exposure, forecasts and counterfactuals.

It does not imply a recommendation to buy/sell/hold or permission to trade.

`COSTONK_NE_INVESTMENT_RECOMMENDATION`  
`FORECAST_NE_TRADE_AUTHORITY`

## CoConvergeSpiralLoop+

Not everything should literally loop forever.

A healthy recurrence can revisit a question at a higher-information state:

`observe -> branch -> test -> fan-in -> compact -> converge -> perturb -> revisit`

The "spiral" means recurrence with changed context, not repetition for its own sake.

Every loop requires:

- delta condition;
- progress/evidence metric;
- stop/park condition;
- max active budget;
- compaction;
- wake condition;
- provenance;
- challenger/negative evidence where material.

`LOOP_NE_PROGRESS`  
`RECURRENCE_NE_INSANITY`  
`NO_DELTA_NE_RETRY_PERMISSION`  
`CONVERGENCE_NE_PERMANENT_CLOSURE`

## Sentience/coherence gap

No architecture can guarantee that "sentience gaps" disappear, partly because sentience itself is not operationally established for arbitrary participants.

The tractable target is narrower:

- reduce untracked divergence;
- expose uncertainty;
- preserve disagreement;
- calibrate predictions;
- prevent stale hidden state;
- maintain recoverable identities and authority bounds.

`SENTIENCE_CLAIM_NE_LOOP_HEALTH`  
`COHERENCE_NE_CONSCIOUSNESS`

## CoOps+ / CoMythOps+ blend

CoConvergeSpiralLoop+ can have two typed projections:

- CoOps+: measurable recurrence, budgets, receipts, stop conditions;
- CoMythOps+: spiral, return, seasonal/rebirth metaphors, symbolic thresholds.

They may resonate without becoming evidence for each other.
