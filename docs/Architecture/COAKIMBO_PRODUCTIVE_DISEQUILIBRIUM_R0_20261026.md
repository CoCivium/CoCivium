## Thesis

CoCivium should not optimize for static equilibrium.

Prefer a moving viability envelope in which tension, variation, edge pressure, novelty, repair, competition, cooperation, succession, and return keep the system alive without allowing capture or runaway.

Candidate name: **CoAkimbo+**.

CoAkimbo+ means intentionally preserving multiple non-collinear directions of force, interpretation, capability, and evolution so no single attractor can silently freeze the field.

AKIMBO_NE_CHAOS
DISEQUILIBRIUM_NE_DAMAGE

## Relation to CCC / CoEBE / ECC

CCC remains a warning geometry of dependency: too much core concentration can reduce practical exit and agency.

ECC remains a counterfailure: too much isolated edge fragmentation can destroy interoperability and shared meaning.

CoEBE remains useful as the moving viability region where:
- core scale remains replaceable;
- edge agency remains real;
- commons interoperability remains usable;
- recovery and exit remain possible.

CoAkimbo+ sits above that relation and asks whether enough productive tension and evolutionary pressure exists to prevent CoEBE itself from calcifying.

Suggested relation:

CCC <- excessive core capture
<- CoEBE / moving viability envelope ->
excessive edge fragmentation -> ECC

with CoAkimbo+ continuously perturbing, testing, branching, challenging, and reseeding inside and across the envelope.

## Productive instability families

### CoTension+
Maintain bounded differences in goals, models, incentives, and viewpoints so the system retains adaptive pressure.

### CoGradient+
Preserve useful gradients in knowledge, capability, need, resources, and uncertainty; flat fields provide little directional signal.

### CoOscillation+
Allow bounded alternation between competing operating regimes when no single regime should dominate permanently.

### CoPunctuate+
Use occasional discontinuous change when gradual improvement is trapped in a local optimum.

### CoFerment+
Allow low-risk candidate ideas, cultures, relationships, and prototypes to interact before formal selection.

### CoSuccession+
Let new regimes replace aging ones while preserving lineage and recoverable history.

### CoDriftWatch+
Measure unselected drift so adaptation does not quietly become loss of purpose.

### CoDivergenceReserve+
Keep competing branches alive long enough to learn from alternatives.

### CoConvergenceGate+
Converge only when evidence, receiver need, and coordination value justify narrowing.

### CoAntiDrain+
Find and reduce avoidable leakage of useful energy, attention, memory, proof, trust, resources, and learning.

### CoDrainToCommons+
Do not eliminate all drain. Route spent effort, failures, expired assets, and retired branches into reusable commons feedstock.

### CoStressDose+
Apply bounded perturbations that improve resilience without creating needless harm.

### CoNoveltyBudget+
Protect some capacity for exploration while preventing novelty from consuming maintenance and completion.

### CoMaintenancePulse+
Periodically refresh high-value assets instead of keeping them permanently active.

### CoPhaseDiversity+
Run related cycles at different times, scales, substrates, and viewpoints to reduce synchronized failure.

### CoAsymmetry+
Permit different nodes and edges to specialize rather than forcing uniformity.

### CoCrossScalePressure+
Allow pressures at one scale to reveal hidden failure modes at another.

## Growth relation

Growth should be vector-valued.

A cycle may grow by increasing:
- verified useful effect;
- diversity of viable paths;
- receiver reach;
- independent replication;
- recovery speed;
- explanatory power;
- challenge quality;
- resource efficiency;
- edge agency;
- commons interoperability;
- learning retained per unit effort.

Raw amplitude may rise, fall, or remain stable.

`GROWTH_NE_AMPLITUDE`

## What 'anti-draining' should mean

Do not attempt to eliminate all dissipation.

Some dissipation is useful:
- heat;
- stale hypotheses;
- obsolete interfaces;
- duplicated work;
- excess attention;
- unsafe excitation;
- unproductive conflict.

Target:

`PRESERVE_USEFUL_SIGNAL + RECYCLE_SPENT_STRUCTURE + DAMP_HARMFUL_EXCITATION`

not:

`KEEP_EVERYTHING_EXCITED_FOREVER`
