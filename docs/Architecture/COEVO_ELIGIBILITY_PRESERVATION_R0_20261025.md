## Core rule

Everything materially touched, observed, related, projected, transformed, challenged, used, failed, retired or newly discovered may become **eligible for evolution review**.

`TOUCHED_OR_RELATED -> EVO_ELIGIBLE`

does **not** mean:

`TOUCHED_OR_RELATED -> SILENTLY_MUTATE`

## Preservation classes

### Frozen witness
Preserve exact bytes/meaning where evidentiary identity matters:
- raw source;
- historical artifact;
- exact receipt;
- original observation;
- signed/hash-bound record;
- historical forecast;
- explicit human statement;
- authority/consent event.

These may gain sidecars, relations, annotations and successors, but are not silently rewritten.

### Versioned state
May change through explicit version/delta:
- structured current state;
- indexes;
- mappings;
- relation weights;
- currentness;
- receiver fit;
- capability availability;
- resource economics.

### Projection
May evolve freely within source/authority constraints:
- CoMap;
- CoOrb;
- CoOrbWare;
- diagrams;
- UI;
- rich media;
- summaries;
- glyphs;
- translations;
- receiver-specific renderings.

### Hypothesis / future
May branch, compete, retire, reopen and calibrate:
- forecasts;
- counterfactuals;
- scenarios;
- theories;
- speculative G_d / ET / nonmaterial lanes.

## Evolution of evolution

Operators, metrics, selectors, stop rules and even this contract are themselves eligible for challenge and replacement.

- authority ceilings do not self-expand;
- safety/privacy/consent rails are not silently rewritten;
- evidence rules require separate governed change;
- historical source identity remains preserved.
