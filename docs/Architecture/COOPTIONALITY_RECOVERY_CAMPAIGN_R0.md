# CoOptionality Recovery Campaign R0

**State:** CANDIDATE_REVIEW_BRANCH

## Purpose

Recover valuable prior CoCivium/CoAll work that may be stale, drained, deprecated, unmerged, provider-trapped, locally stranded, duplicated, superseded for one scope but still useful for another, or simply never operationalized.

This is not nostalgia mining. It is a bounded recovery discipline.

## Why this exists

Prior lifecycle work already established that:

- old does not mean obsolete;
- stale does not mean useless;
- drained does not mean integrated;
- deprecated does not mean erased;
- superseded branches may revive;
- deletion requires proof that unique optionality is not being lost.

The campaign turns those lifecycle rules into an ongoing leverage-recovery program.

## Candidate classes

For each prior object, session, branch, artifact, design or experiment, classify into one of:

- REUSE
- REFINE
- CONVERGE
- FORK
- DISPLACE
- SUPERSEDE
- REVIVE
- DEFER
- HISTORICAL_ONLY
- UNKNOWN

Do not force a disposition when currentness or provenance is missing.

## Recovery questions

For every candidate ask:

1. What unique capability or idea would be lost if ignored?
2. Has a descendant already satisfied it?
3. Does new tooling/hardware/model capability make an old blocked path viable?
4. Was failure caused by the idea or merely by a now-changed precondition?
5. Does the candidate reduce provider/session/manual-relay dependence?
6. Does it close a receiver/readproof, recovery, accessibility, provenance or failure-domain gap?
7. Does it duplicate a stronger current primitive?
8. Can it be compacted as an alias/view rather than revived as a subsystem?
9. What exact evidence should wake it again?
10. What evidence would justify retirement?

## Work selection

Prioritize:

`unique-state-at-risk > authority/provenance ambiguity > receiver/readproof gap > high-centrality contradiction > newly viable dormant path > stale-but-relied-upon state > duplicate residue`

Recovery is work-conserving but not churn-maximizing.

`RECOVER_BEFORE_REINVENT`  
`OLD_NE_OBSOLETE`  
`NEW_NE_BETTER`  
`FAILED_HERE_NE_FAILED_EVERYWHERE`  
`SUPERSEDED_NE_FALSE`  
`DORMANT_NE_DEAD`  
`REVIVAL_REQUIRES_REASON`  
`COMPACTION_BEFORE_PROLIFERATION`

## CoPete+ relation

Workers may generate recovery candidates for one another when they discover:

- missing predecessor context;
- a failed route whose precondition changed;
- duplicated modern work;
- stronger old evidence than a current claim;
- an untested optionality;
- an abandoned product path now cheap enough to retry.

But:

`WORKER_GENERATED_RECOVERY_NE_PRIORITY`

Every revival must still pass value, evidence, cost, collision and authority checks.

## Local / PS7 wake

When an authorized local observer is available, perform read-only discovery first across known CoCivium custody roots.

The local path is:

`DISCOVER -> HASH -> CLASSIFY -> DEDUPE -> RELATE -> ELECT`

before any mutation.

User-reported PS7 readiness is a useful wake hint, not remote execution proof.

`PS7_REPORTED_READY_NE_REMOTELY_OBSERVED_READY`

## Acceptance

This campaign is succeeding when:

- fewer useful ideas are rediscovered from scratch;
- fewer stale claims survive after better descendants exist;
- more blocked historical paths are either revived with changed preconditions or explicitly retired;
- negative knowledge prevents repeated failures;
- unique prior work becomes reconstructable without provider-session archaeology;
- concept count grows more slowly than tested capability.
