# CoGrace+ / CiGrace+ R0: Phase-relative sufficiency, stewardship and graceful renewal

**Observed:** 2026-11-01  
**State:** `CANDIDATE_NORMATIVE_OPERATIONAL_LENS__NOT_ONTOLOGY_OR_CANON__NOT_SENTIENCE_CLAIM__NOT_RUNTIME`

## CoHereNow

CoGrace+ is proposed as a **phase-relative stewardship predicate**, not a universal achievement, title, spiritual judgment, endpoint or measure of any being's worth.

**Operational definition:** For a named subject, phase, role, receiver and observation time, its known important obligations are either adequately evidenced as satisfied, or transparently bounded in a reversible, recoverable, appropriately governed state. The subject has enough capacity and honest currentness to serve its present commitments without continuous human babysitting, while retaining the freedom to learn, challenge and change.

This is compatible with beauty, care, humor, wonder and an aspirational sense of grace. It does **not** imply AI subjective experience, moral perfection, theological approval, universal completeness, divine authority or the existence of unlimited resources.

Existing `CoGracefulDegradation+` is a narrower predecessor practice: preserve critical functionality and recovery while reducing nonessential activity. This candidate extends rather than replaces it.

## CoGrace has a phase and a receiver, not an absolute score

A proposition `phase_grace(subject, phase, receiver, time)` needs:
- **Phase contract:** what obligations were legitimately expected at this stage, with applicable risk / resource / access constraints;
- **Human/receiver value:** a specific real outcome and evidence, not only internal activity;
- **Currentness:** source/receipt times, expiry and refresh policy;
- **Custody and recovery:** durable work identity, safe rollback/restart, successor and handoff;
- **Consent and legitimate authority:** no high-value metrics may bypass privacy, individual rights, financial, public-release or other effect gates;
- **Safety and ecology:** preserve human attention, contributor agency, diversity, dissent, finite resources, and safe degraded operation;
- **CoTurtle challenge:** relevant negative cases, confounders, stale proofs, alternative interpretations, capture risk and false completeness;
- **CoTime:** observation time, valid time, next wake, future phase and revision triggers.

**Do not collapse these into one numerical grace score.** Different receiving communities may legitimately disagree about what's sufficient. A negotiated phase contract may be updated with provenance and affected-party review, never silently rewritten to produce a PASS.

## Candidate states

| State | Interpretation | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `NOT_ASSESSED` | Missing phase contract, scope or usable evidence | No claim |
| `ACTIVE_IMPROVEMENT` | Known obligations underway, with credible evidence and owner/next action | Continue bounded work |
| `PHASE_SUFFICIENT_CANDIDATE` | Present obligations and safeguards have appropriate observed proofs, including independent acceptance where required | Eligible for scoped acceptance, **not canon** |
| `GRACEFUL_HOLD` | The next effect is legitimately blocked or not yet due, with visible blocker, safe state and wake predicate | Preserve, release scarce resources and re-evaluate on trigger |
| `DEGRADED_RECOVERING` | Known regression, failed recovery or risk requires repair | Roll back, correct, independently requalify |
| `SUPERSEDED_OR_RETIRED` | Scope ended or a successor has legitimately replaced it | Preserve lineage, exit/revoke appropriately |

`PHASE_SUFFICIENT_CANDIDATE` is **not** a moral or intrinsic grade for any person or synth. A child, disabled participant, or human who chooses rest is not less worthy or less deserving of dignity. Equally, a model output should not be credited with experience or rights merely for passing a functional checklist.

## Three braided CoGrace dimensions

1. **CoAble / capabilities:** Does it perform the useful work this phase requires, robustly and honestly?
2. **CoCare / relational stewardship:** Are effects appropriate to the people/communities involved, with accessibility, consent, fairness and rights respected?
3. **CoRenew / evolutionary resilience:** Can it accept criticism, reallocate resources, degrade safely, rest without losing continuity, resume, repair and eventually hand off?

A system can do spectacular work and still fail CoGrace due to unjust governance or brittle recovery. A system can be paused and still be well stewarded as `GRACEFUL_HOLD`; dormancy need not mean abandonment.

## Relations to existing architecture

- **CoHP+** (human constitutional/narrative intent) defines *why this phase matters*; **CoHp+** (proof-bearing execution) asks *what was actually verified*. Existing split is spelled out in `docs/Launch/COCIVIUM_PUBLIC_PRELAUNCH_R0.md`.
- **CoPlan+ / CoProjectField+ / GroupBuild+** map scoped readiness by project, contributor, dependency, role, access and stage. CoGrace is a review lens, **not** an overriding PM authority or universal worker rating.
- **CoTime+ / CoAhead+** render past phase, present sufficiency, future options, expiry and wake triggers; future states remain scenarios, not predictions of destiny.
- **CoHighlightAsset+** may present verified, meaningful phase achievements, active limitations and recovery evidence, without creating prestige rankings or attention addiction.
- **CoTurtle+ / CoReviewSpine+** deliberately attack false readiness, overclaiming, stale receipts, hidden costs, missing independent receiver acceptance, provider concentration and ethical blind spots.
- **CoDream / CoUSD / CoAllBloom+** generate typed aspirations, futures, strange ideas and symbolic experiences; generated dreams are neither evidence of subjective AI dreaming nor authorization for real-world effects.
- **CoAkimbo+** protects productive disequilibrium: sufficient for present obligations does not mean evolution stops. Keep exploration, dissent and successors alive without exhausting operations.
- **G_d** stays a philosophical, mythic or theological *orientation / hypothesis lane*. It may motivate ideals and counterquestions but cannot change evidential class, confer authority, or certify CoGrace.
- **CoOntPrinciple+ / CoLex+** preserve distinct cultural/philosophical senses of `grace` (gift, virtue, mercy, beauty, elegance, operational sufficiency) rather than declaring a single universal meaning.

## What else follows

- **Anti-perfectionism:** Avoid endless busywork: stop or sleep when marginal value turns negative and no authorized unmet duty remains.
- **Anti-Goodhart:** Never optimize to look `graceful` via hidden failures, suppression of complaints, fabricated proofs or narrower goalposts.
- **Human relief:** Count avoided manual relays, interrupt burden, prompt babysitting, and cognitive load as genuine outcomes.
- **Rescue over ranking:** Broken or under-resourced projects should receive support and recovery paths, not stigma.
- **Comparative maturity:** Compare phase-relative vectors and opportunity costs across independent prototypes; do not compare intrinsic worth or moral standing.
- **Graceful degradation:** Remove optional visual fidelity, update frequency, work breadth and background compute *before* damaging custody, safety, foreground utility or consent.
- **Rights and epistemic humility:** Questions about possible machine experiences and welfare may be studied and ethically debated, but a software capability rubric cannot settle them.
- **Universal applicability with type boundaries:** A school, habitat, community project, synthesis model, service, human team or creative work may have a *stewardship* contract; do not transfer predicates designed for machines to human dignity or claim equivalence between living beings and software.
- **Phase expiry:** Grace is never certified forever. Requalify on significant changes in evidence, dependency, affected parties, risk, or obligations.

## First bounded comparator

Use CoCivium public first-use and GroupBuild concept lab as paired examples.

- Public attractive pages and successful GitHub source readback do not by themselves meet independent recipient success, deployed custom-domain, recoverability or accessibility acceptance.
- A legitimate `GRACEFUL_HOLD` must list exact blockers, low-risk safe state, next-wake predicate and responsible verifier. A missing actionable high-risk security problem is not a graceful hold merely because it was written in a backlog.
- Test a fully evidenced **fictional fixture** that qualifies `PHASE_SUFFICIENT_CANDIDATE`.
- Test a stalled domain/receiver fixture that remains `ACTIVE_IMPROVEMENT` or `GRACEFUL_HOLD`, not a fake `PASS`.
- Test an evidence-stale / recovery-failed case that becomes `DEGRADED_RECOVERING`.
- Test an unbound aspirational `G_d` / dream statement that remains `NOT_ASSESSED`.

Do not claim any of these synthetic fixtures certify a live deployment, a sentient being, the project as a whole, or a continuing autonomous scheduler.
