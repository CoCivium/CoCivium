# CoAuthorityField+ / CoBoss+ R0

**State:** CANDIDATE_ARCHITECTURE__SYNTHETIC_AUTHORITY_CANARY__NO_RUNTIME_NO_CANON_NO_SUPREME_PRINCIPAL

## Core move

Authority should be a typed, scoped, time-bounded relation field, not one universal chain.

A CoBoss+ relation answers only: who has the strongest proven authority for this exact effect, in this exact scope, at this exact time?

COBOSS_REL_NE_GLOBAL_BOSS
AUTHORITY_FIELD_NE_TOTAL_ORDER

Effective authority is the intersection of delegated scope, capability, current consent, surface permission, effect lease, confidentiality, currentness, collision/fencing state, and task policy.

Representation does not amplify authority.
REPRESENTATIVE_NE_PRINCIPAL
DELEGATION_NE_AUTHORITY_AMPLIFICATION

## Cycles and futures

Authority cycles may exist for mutual aid or peer structures, but cycles do not manufacture new power.

AUTHORITY_CYCLE_NE_AUTHORITY_AMPLIFICATION

Predicted or hypothetical future authority is planning evidence only. It cannot authorize a present effect.

PREDICTED_FUTURE_AUTHORITY_NE_PRESENT_AUTHORITY
FUTURE_BRANCH_NE_CURRENT_DELEGATION

## God / gods / Godstuff / CMA / CoWhatevers

CoAll may represent theological, philosophical, mythic, symbolic, or otherwise transcendent references because people actually use them.

Candidate labels include God, god, gods, Gods, godstuff, CMA, and open CoWhatever+ references.

R0 does not assert that these labels are equivalent, true, false, real, unreal, or semantically settled.

Where a worldview says that only God is ultimately supreme, CoAll may represent that as a participant-declared transcendent reference above the machine-verifiable operational authority field.

But a transcendent reference is not an executable credential.

TRANSCENDENT_REFERENCE_NE_EXECUTABLE_PRINCIPAL
CLAIMED_DIVINE_MANDATE_NE_AUTHORITY_PROOF
GOD_REFERENCE_NE_MACHINE_VERIFIED_DELEGATION
NO_SELF_ELECTED_GOD_PROXY

This is an anti-capture property: no human, synth, provider, institution, model, steward, or service may self-promote to universal boss by claiming to represent a transcendent source.

Operationally: ULTIMATE_OPERATIONAL_AUTHORITY = NONE_UNIVERSAL.

Every finite operational authority remains scoped.

Personal religious, secular, philosophical, constitutional, cultural, or unknown ultimate references may coexist. A personal ultimate reference does not silently govern everybody else.

PERSONAL_ULTIMATE_REFERENCE_NE_UNIVERSAL_GOVERNANCE
LABEL_EQUIVALENCE_NE_ONTOLOGICAL_EQUIVALENCE

## Conflict

When current authority claims conflict, the default is not strongest voice wins. Use a predeclared precedence rule, appeal/adjudication contract, current principal decision, or safe hold.

AUTHORITY_CONFLICT_NE_STRONGEST_VOICE_WINS
NO_RESOLUTION_CONTRACT_NE_HOLD
