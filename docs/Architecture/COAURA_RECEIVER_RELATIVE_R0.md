# CoAuraRel+ Receiver-Relative Relation Envelope R0

**State:** `CANDIDATE_RELATIONAL_PROJECTION__NO_PHYSICAL_AURA_NEUROLOGICAL_OR_METAPHYSICAL_CLAIM`

## Lead

`CoAuraRel+` is a candidate receiver-relative relation envelope:

`CoAuraRel(Object, Observer, Context, Time)`

It is not a claim that a literal physical aura exists.

Its purpose is to preserve a useful class of relations that change with observer, context, time, modality, framing, embodiment, or interpretation without collapsing those differences into either objective fact or mere error.

This is especially useful for:

- embodied or felt experience;
- aesthetic response;
- comfort/tension;
- attention and salience;
- metaphorical projection;
- trust/familiarity impressions;
- semantic resonance;
- receiver-relative UX;
- uncertain or surprising relations;
- transitions between grounded and speculative interpretations.

`COAURAREL_NE_PHYSICAL_AURA_CLAIM`
`OBSERVER_RELATIVE_NE_ARBITRARY`
`EXPERIENCE_NE_MECHANISM`

## Minimal envelope

A candidate relation binds:

```text
relation_id
object_ref
observer_ref
context_ref
event_time
observation_time
modality
epistemic_class
felt_or_interpreted_quality
intensity
valence
mechanism_status
candidate_interpretations
alternative_interpretations
confidence
provenance
calibration_or_discrimination_path
authority_ceiling
```

The relation belongs to the tuple, not to the object alone.

`PROPERTY_OF_OBJECT_NE_OBSERVER_RELATION`

## Receiver-relative non-contradiction

Two observers may produce different CoAuraRel projections of the same object without either projection being automatically false.

Likewise, one observer may produce different projections at different times or in different contexts.

Examples:

```text
same object
observer A / context X / T1 -> WARMER_COMFORT
observer B / context X / T1 -> NEUTRAL
```

or:

```text
same observer
context X / T1 -> TENSE
context Y / T2 -> RELAXED
```

These become contradiction candidates only when the claims assert the same scope, observer, context, time, and epistemic class while remaining mutually exclusive.

`DIFFERENT_OBSERVER_NE_CONTRADICTION`
`DIFFERENT_CONTEXT_NE_CONTRADICTION`
`DIFFERENT_TIME_NE_CONTRADICTION`

## Grounded, metaphorical and speculative projections

The same experience may support several explicitly separated projections:

- `GROUNDED_DESCRIPTION`: what the observer reports or what a sensor records;
- `METAPHORICAL_PROJECTION`: a useful analogy or conceptual donor;
- `MECHANISM_HYPOTHESIS`: a candidate explanation;
- `PREDICTIVE_RELATION`: a future-state claim with calibration path;
- `UNKNOWN`: relation preserved without premature explanation.

A felt asymmetry can therefore remain real as an observed experience while any neurological, hemispheric, material/nonmaterial, paranormal, quantum or causal interpretation remains separately unproven.

`FELT_ASYMMETRY_NE_HEMISPHERE_STATE_PROOF`
`METAPHOR_NE_MECHANISM`
`HYPOTHESIS_NE_OBSERVATION`
`UNKNOWN_MECHANISM_NE_NONMATERIAL_MECHANISM`

## CoInBet+ relation

`CoInBet+` can qualify CoAuraRel when a projection sits between named states or when classification itself remains unsettled.

Examples:

- between tension and relaxation;
- between literal and metaphorical reading;
- between observation and explanation;
- between present state and forecast;
- between competing hypotheses;
- between known and unknown.

The point is to preserve the intermediate relation rather than force a binary label.

`IN_BETWEEN_NE_NOTHING`
`AMBIGUITY_NE_FAILURE`
`COINBET_PROJECTION_NE_NEW_ROOT_ONTOLOGY`

## Predictive overlay

A CoAuraRel may donate a **prediction candidate**, but never silently becomes prediction evidence.

A predictive projection must add:

`claim | horizon | assumptions | discriminating_observation | calibration_due | confidence`

and later receive a calibration disposition.

`PREDICTION_NE_EVIDENCE`
`FORECAST_NE_FUTURE_FACT`
`PREDICTIVE_RELATION_REQUIRES_CALIBRATION_PATH`

For example:

```text
OBSERVED_EXPERIENCE:
  "this interaction feels increasingly tense"

PREDICTIVE_RELATION:
  "if context C persists, reported tension may increase by T2"

CALIBRATION:
  compare later observation at T2
```

The first object is not proof of the second.

## Better-question compiler

Each receiver-relative relation should be able to emit a small discriminating question set rather than merely accumulating descriptions:

1. What exactly was observed, sensed or reported?
2. Which observer, context and time does it belong to?
3. What interpretation is being added beyond the observation?
4. What competing interpretation fits the same evidence?
5. What future observation would discriminate them?
6. What changed between apparently inconsistent projections?
7. Is the relation useful as metaphor even if mechanism remains unknown?
8. What authority or action, if any, actually depends on the interpretation?

`BETTER_QUESTION_NE_MORE_QUESTIONS`

## Cross-CoAll use

This envelope can project into many existing fields without claiming ownership of them:

- CoUX+: receiver-relative comfort, confusion, salience;
- CoSurface+: visible meaning differs by observer/viewport/context;
- CoConsent+: comfort is evidence for inquiry, not inferred consent;
- CoTime+: changing relations and forecast calibration;
- CoResource+: perceived versus measured pressure;
- CoRelation+: observer-relative or contested relation interpretations;
- CoScendence+: expansive/grounded metaphorical projections without neurological claims;
- CoWarChest+: sentiment or confidence may be recorded but never substitutes for financial evidence or authority.

`FELT_CONFIDENCE_NE_FINANCIAL_EVIDENCE`
`EXPERIENCE_NE_CONSENT`
`RELATION_PROJECTION_NE_EFFECT_AUTHORITY`

## R0 executable canary

The fixture proves:

1. two observers may differ without contradiction when observer scope differs;
2. one observer may differ across time/context without contradiction;
3. an observed experience cannot be typed as a proven mechanism;
4. a metaphorical projection cannot silently become a mechanism claim;
5. a surprising relation may remain UNKNOWN_MECHANISM without paranormal/quantum promotion;
6. a predictive projection requires a calibration path;
7. a CoInBet state may remain unresolved without binary collapse;
8. effect authority remains unchanged across every relation projection.

## Current boundary

R0 makes no claim about literal auras, hemisphere state, neurological diagnosis, quantum/nonlocal mechanisms, paranormal effects, nonmaterial causation, predictive accuracy, financial merit, runtime adoption, canon, or CoEx.
