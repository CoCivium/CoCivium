## Core inversion
A CoSong is *not only music and not only whale sound*. Treat it as a **time-varying pattern with explicit sender/receiver and source metadata**. Sound in/out, light in/out, vibration in/out, graphics, text, signal samples, timed events and accessible alternatives are different physical/virtual I/O adapters with different loss profiles. A picture of a pattern is not a sound recording; a visual equivalence is a declared encoding, not physical equivalence.

**CoTuner+** is a navigational lens over a finite, enumerated set of documented patterns, rates and modalities. A dial position is a candidate inspection window, not evidence that a signal exists at every position or that an unknown signal is meaningful. The dial is an educational control, **not a radio-frequency transmitter**.

## Pattern packet
- `pattern_id`, version, work_id and author/rights source
- canonical abstract events: symbol, relative tick, duration, magnitude, intentional changes, pauses
- per-modality transforms with units, timebase, amplitudes and limits, projection loss and accessibility twin

## ET / SETI factual boundary
Radio astronomy observes the hydrogen line near 1420.40575 **MHz**, not 1420 Hz. The 1400-1427 MHz band is protected for passive radio astronomy. **Do not transmit at 1420 MHz.** Source: ITU preferred-band documentation and spectrum rules; https://spektrum.ctu.gov.cz/en/band/1400-1427-mhz.
An illustrative 432 Hz or 174.61 Hz audible tone has no established special astronomical meaning. A non-random pattern alone cannot distinguish intended alien transmission from natural processes, instrumentation artifacts, interference, compression or terrestrial signals. Confirm with controls and independent observations. Source: https://www.seti.org/about/faq and https://www.seti.org/research/seti-101/protocols-for-an-eti-signal-detection/.
Real RF beacon transmissions require spectrum legality, engineering, ethics, international considerations and independent authorization; this R0 deliberately excludes them.
