Do not automatically type, transmit, submit, execute, collect passwords, inspect private conversations or infer consent from predicted intent. Suggestions must be suppressed in password, payment, authentication, unknown or inaccessible editor contexts. CoAckRAW/ReWis+ preserves raw input and records revisions separately.

All-to-all discovery is not all-to-all delivery. Select only authorized, privacy-compatible subscriptions; retain rejection reasons and exact delivery/readproof states.
