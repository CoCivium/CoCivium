**Observed:** 2026-11-01

## Binding to prior plans

The current `plans/MasterPlan.md` designates **groupbuild.org** as the community-building entry point (XR organization tooling and governance, Phase 1/2). It designates **groupbuild.com** as a proposed product alias / redirect or commercial landing. This candidate **extends** those allocations, but does not silently supersede them. Domain ownership, HTTPS/DNS, corporate status and live services require separate evidence.

The shared mission: **help people and synths organize around worthwhile outcomes, with qualified automations doing routine work and every participant increasing the others' leverage.**

## Two coordinated but distinguishable doors

| Dimension | GroupBuild.org / Commons | GroupBuild.com / CoPayback+ |
|---|---|---|
| Visitor promise | Find your people; start or improve something together | Build and operate ambitious teams with managed tools and services |
| First use | No-login inspirational worlds, local demo, task/interest matching by explicit choice | Transparent product examples, supported plans, case studies, pricing and a bounded demonstration |
| Stewardship | Community/public benefit and open collaboration; organizational legal status unverified | Paid services only after legal, contractual, tax and operational qualifications |
| Core capabilities | Group formation, skills/preferences, inclusive governance, learning, impact receipts | Managed CoProjectField, private organizations, support, SLA, integrations and hosting |
| Source treatment | Public-safe standards, open research, explicit licensing and provenance | Compliant use of licensed technology and community contributions; no unilateral transfer of commons |
| Feedback | Consent-based correction, accessibility and independently verifiable benefit | Customer satisfaction plus cost/recovery/quality, with no secret profiling |
| Return flows | Volunteer knowledge, participation, social utility | Funding, contribution, improvements, infrastructure support or paid contributor opportunities where agreed |

The distinction is a product and governance hypothesis, not a claim that either domain is registered to a charity or company. A commercial entity cannot appropriate community assets merely because it supports the project.

## CoTeaming+ / CoProjectField+ / CoLeverage+ shared kernel

One durable **work object**, with intentional public/private projections:

```
project_id
+ purpose / beneficiaries / permissions
+ people / synths / automations / organizations (typed)
+ capability / availability / preferences / consent
+ task, dependency and time graph
+ project and participant resource budgets
+ governance, privacy and contribution rights
+ public impact / private contracts / payback entitlements
+ challenger, verifier, exact receipts and recovery
 -> CoProjectField elected work frontier
 -> bounded multi-contributor execution
 -> independently observable outcomes and corrections
 -> revised roles, successors, reinvestment and learning
```

Humans do not own only the front end, synths do not own only the middle and automations do not own only the back. Use **comparative, complementary, situational leverage**; choose roles by actual capabilities and hard permission limits. Treat human attention as scarce. Prefer many passive observers and scoped proposals, one elected actuator per conflicting effect.

## Onboarding and experience

A human-first site should start with a beautiful, accessible and genuinely useful encounter, not a developer catalog:

1. **Imagine a better world:** Rich media, CoTime scenarios and a concrete story of what collaboration changes; identify simulation as simulation.
2. **Try an idea:** One local micro-production or group exercise that succeeds before account creation.
3. **Choose a path:** Start a group, join a group, learn, mentor, help verify, make a creative artifact, or explore paid support.
4. **Inspect evidence and terms:** Accessible source links, consent, contribution/license choices, privacy and verified limitations.
5. **Invite participation only when wanted:** No hidden cross-service account linking, unsolicited group joining, or coercive rewards.

Desktop, mobile, XR and speech may be separate projections of the same work identity. Do not claim GroupBuild is working inside Quest merely because the browser demo has 3D-like graphics.

## CoPayback+ possibilities

Payback is broader than revenue:
- direct earnings to contributors under explicit agreements;
- transparent paid hosted teamwork and enterprise integration;
- maintained managed infrastructure for organizations;
- optional sponsorship and gifts, clearly separated from purchases;
- transparent community reinvestment or grants where legally and financially supported;
- reputation built on consented, verifiable contribution receipts, not popularity or forced gamification;
- reduced burden, time saved, community benefit and reusable outputs for non-paying participants.

**Candidate commercial models** should be compared on fairness, maintenance economics, access, marginal benefit and anti-capture. No pricing, revenue, profit, charity/tax deductibility, ownership or payment acceptance is currently asserted.

## Cross-subsidy and anti-capture tests

- The community path retains a useful exit and data export; paying should not be mandatory to contribute to the commons.
- Every paid tier must provide a genuine service increment (hosting, support, reliable operations, managed integration), not artificial impairment of open functionality.
- Financial, legal, labor and contributor-rights policies need explicit agreement; founders cannot simply assign collective work to a company.
- No use of children's creation history to target paid products, and no tracking of gaze/location without informed and scoped permission.
- Public claims and commercial claims share source IDs and compatible evidence but have separate legal/effect gates.
- Community input is not consent to endorsements, public outreach, cross-organization data sharing or unrestricted training.

## Lifecycle and relay

```
DISCOVER -> QUALIFY -> FORM -> CHARTER -> LEASE CAPABILITIES
 -> PARALLEL PRODUCE / CHALLENGE -> VERIFY
 -> ALLOCATE CREDIT + RIGHTS + PAYBACK
 -> PRESERVE / EXPORT -> EXTEND / SPLIT / MERGE / RETIRE
```

Each work item records who can propose, who can act, what would prove completion, and how to recover. A user may change their mind without erasing prior receipts. Site names and repositories are projections, not the ontology.

## First qualifying proof

**Project:** Make an accessible, appealing GroupBuild concept doorway with one interactive local scenario connecting a common-good project and an optional managed-service version. The prototype may be published as source within CoAcademy, not as an unverified groupbuild.org or groupbuild.com deployment.

**Acceptance checks:**
1. Exact GitHub source readback and immutable commit pointer.
2. Independent browser HTTP 200 / title / useful first action, on mobile and desktop.
3. Keyboard and reduced-motion functionality, no personal data collection.
4. A consenting first-time visitor completes a brief exercise and can distinguish commons benefit from paid service value.
5. Domain/ownership/DNS/SSL/privacy/security review before redirect or activation.
6. Legal and accounting review before any financial claims, billing, or revenue-sharing program.
7. Separate receiver pickup/ACK and human acceptance tests, not inferred from source publication.
