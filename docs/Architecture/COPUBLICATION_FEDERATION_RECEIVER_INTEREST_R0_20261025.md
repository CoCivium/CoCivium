## Thesis

Do not make one publication carry every audience, tone, depth, offer and use case.

Use one source-bound publication graph with many receiver-relative editorial projections.

```
SHARED SOURCE GRAPH
-> PUBLICATION FEDERATION
-> RECEIVER-CHOSEN ROUTES
-> READ / WATCH / LISTEN / BUILD / CHALLENGE
-> CONSENTED / AGGREGATE RETURN SIGNALS
-> SUCCESSOR PROJECTIONS
-> SOURCE GRAPH
```

The federation may include Substack publications, websites, books, videos, podcasts, GitHub/dev surfaces, academic notes, commercial explainers, educational tracks, community surfaces and public-safe civic information.

`MANY_PUBLICATIONS_NE_MANY_TRUTHS`

## Receiver interest model

Prefer explicit and low-risk signals:
- topic subscription;
- selected reading mode;
- followed series;
- saved chapter;
- chosen depth;
- explicit question;
- requested format;
- language;
- accessibility preference;
- voluntary challenge / contribution.

Aggregate behavioural signals may improve editorial planning when privacy-respecting and disclosed.

Do not silently infer or target sensitive traits such as:
- political affiliation;
- religion;
- medical state;
- sexual orientation;
- financial distress;
- vulnerability.

`INTEREST_NE_IDENTITY`
`CLICK_NE_CONSENT_TO_PROFILE`

## CoMarketing 101+

The useful marketing primitives are:

1. **Relevance**
   - show the right doorway to the right self-selected interest.

2. **Clarity**
   - tell the reader what they will get and why it may matter.

3. **Curiosity**
   - open a real unanswered question, not clickbait detached from content.

4. **Proof**
   - make evidence and source lineage inspectable.

5. **Continuity**
   - let readers follow a series / book / argument without re-orienting every time.

6. **Identity**
   - recognizable visual and conceptual grammar.

7. **Participation**
   - let readers challenge, remix, test, contribute or build.

8. **Return**
   - reader feedback changes future projections when justified.

9. **Portability**
   - content survives platform change.

10. **Trust**
   - no fake scarcity, fake countdowns, hidden targeting, or manufactured consensus.

`RELEVANCE_BEATS_SPAM`
`CURIOSITY_NE_CLICKBAIT`
`URGENCY_NE_DECEPTION`

## Honest urgency

Time pressure may be legitimate when it reflects reality:
- live event;
- expiring external source;
- changing regulation;
- current election / civic deadline;
- limited pilot capacity;
- current software/API state;
- time-bounded challenge;
- active experiment;
- real price / inventory window.

If a live link or capability may disappear, show:
- observed currentness;
- checked-at time;
- archival/source pointer if lawful;
- replacement/successor link;
- reason for expiry.

Do **not** deliberately rot links merely to manufacture engagement.

`EXPIRY_NE_MARKETING_TRICK`
`DEAD_LINK_NE_URGENCY`

## Living publication without historical erasure

A reader may encounter a current successor rather than the old front-door version, but the version actually read should remain addressable where lawful.

Use:

```
CURRENT PROJECTION
-> VERSION ID
-> SOURCE ID
-> PREDECESSOR
-> SUCCESSOR
-> WHAT CHANGED
-> WHY
-> ARCHIVE / TOMBSTONE / REDIRECT
```

The past may fade from the **foreground**, not from provenance.

`FOREGROUND_FADE_NE_HISTORY_ERASURE`

## R0.1 | Substack platform qualification and honest reader-choice gate

**Checked 2026-10-25 against current public Substack help.** This section qualifies existing federation R0 and [draft CoBook PR #272](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoCivium/pull/272). No Substack account settings, sections, audience data, sends or published posts were changed by this source edit.

- **Sections ≠ automatic opt-in.** Substack lets publishers create newsletter sections, control `Add new subscribers by default`, and optionally copy their existing list. Its subscriber help says new subscribers receive all sections *by default*. To preserve the proposed `default_subscribe: false` values in PR #272's `federation.json`, the owner must **uncheck Add new subscribers by default**, **leave Copy your email list unchecked**, then verify with a consenting new-reader route. A JSON value is not a provider setting. [Substack Sections help, updated 2026-09-24](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360060687771-How-can-I-create-multiple-newsletters-or-podcasts-under-one-publication) · [reader controls](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/8914938285204-How-do-I-subscribe-to-or-unsubscribe-from-a-section-on-Substack).
- **App ≠ web.** Substack's September 2026 guidance says sections are **not separately browsable inside the app**, while web section URLs can be distinct; app notifications are configurable. Use a pinned, accessible *Choose your path* index and deep links, not a false claim of per-topic app isolation. [Official app limits](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/4531775625492-Can-I-see-one-section-on-its-own-on-the-Substack-app).
- **Portability.** A publication-level `/feed` exists, and Settings → Exports supports post/subscriber export. Do not invent live per-section RSS endpoints, automatic API sync or rights to replicate customer data into CoAll. [RSS](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038239391-Is-there-an-RSS-feed-for-my-publication) · [Exports](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037466012-How-do-I-export-my-posts).
- **Segments ≠ consented interests.** Substack supports dynamic subscriber segments and metrics, but inferred activity, payment, location or account categories are not permission to infer religion, politics, vulnerabilities or preferred philosophies. [Segments](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/52113249525140-What-are-subscriber-segments-on-Substack) · [Metrics](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/5320347155860-A-guide-to-Substack-metrics).
- **Time and evolution.** The current edition may become the default reading surface while the version actually read stays citeable, exportable and visibly dated. Reading a page may generate a **consented feedback proposal**, not silently rewrite its source. Let genuine news or software deadlines create real urgency; no artificial link expiry, vanishing citations or FOMO countdowns.

## CoMegaScroller+ resilient constellation and fragment recovery R0

**2026-11-09 | PUBLIC-SAFE CANDIDATE DESIGN, NOT A MULTI-HOST DEPLOYMENT.** Reuse CoPublication Metabolism, CoLivingHighlight, CoMap/CoNav/CoOrb, CoSourceGraph and the already-public dated [October 19 megascroller edition](../Public/Snapshots/2026-10-19_CoMegaScroller_CC_CoTheoryAll_R0.md). Do not turn a new megascroller domain, GitHub copy, newsletter or cached CDN page into a separate canonical source.

### Publication object and nested navigation

```text
publication_id, edition_id, source_work_id, source_commit, source_sha256,
section_ids[], fragment_anchors[], lineage_refs[], relation_refs[],
rights_scope, publication_authority, generated_at, valid_time,
evidence_class, correction_refs[], successor_id, predecessor_id,
formats[], accessibility_fallback, expected_mirrors[],
observed_mirrors[], recovery_recipe_ref, reader_feedback_gate
```

A section or fragment must preserve parent identity, edition and predecessor/successor anchors. Nested diagram, summary, text, video, audio and machine-twin views are **loss-aware projections** of a source object. A fragment's embedded pointer enables bounded context reconstruction; it is not a complete stored hologram or implied global lookup permission.

### Distinguish redundancy classes

- **Content redundancy:** exact-byte copies and hash-addressed revisions.
- **Hosting redundancy:** independent failure domains for DNS, web host, storage, deployment, account, CDN, TLS and network route. Distinct URLs may still be a single failure domain.
- **Temporal redundancy:** immutable reader editions plus current successor pointers.
- **Format redundancy:** static HTML / accessible text / offline export, with optional dynamic megascroller, CoOrb, audio and diagram.
- **Institutional redundancy:** rights to export/migrate, independent keys, documented recovery recipe, qualified custodians; never assume hosting company survival.
- **Epistemic redundancy:** independent evidence, corrections and dissent; identical media copies add no independent verification.

**Publication release fence:** producer signs/identifies exact immutable edition manifest; at least one separately administered recovery location independently fetches and hashes the exact edition before any multi-mirror acceptance; each mirror emits `mirror_id, failure_domain, source_sha, fetched_sha, fetched_at, HTTP/outage evidence, availability_status, recovery_test_id`. A DNS record or hyperlink alone is not receiver readproof. No credential or sensitive source is placed in the public edition.

### Progressive first-use

The public front door shows **one readable headline, one currentness/version cue and one route into the work**. CoNav offers a table of contents with stable anchors, a compact mini-map and one-context-at-a-time evidence expansions. Fractal exploration should not impose endless scrolling on keyboard, screen-reader, mobile or low-bandwidth users. Search engines and synth consumers receive static semantic HTML plus a deliberately public machine manifest; do not assume crawlers discovered or consumed it.

### Recovery acceptance exercise (PROPOSED, NOT EXECUTED)

1. Freeze a public-safe snapshot and exact source digest; test fragment anchors in static HTML and text.
2. Produce a new edition with one corrected claim, one diagram and one preserved predecessor; compare fragment lineage without redirecting away historical meaning.
3. Publish to two genuinely independent administered domains only after permissions and rights checks; read back each exact artifact and manifest.
4. Disable the primary route; independently retrieve the same edition and accessible text from another failure domain.
5. Inject stale CDN content, wrong hash, absent fragment, broken external URL, split DNS and failed private-data filter; display DEGRADED / HELD rather than a false synchronized green.
6. Let one new consenting reader or synthetic agent retrieve a specific claim, evidence, correction and historical edition without copying the entire document.
7. Record real recovery time, version parity, source availability, accessibility, cost and independent reader understanding. Do not count clicks, mirror URLs or generated media as useful outcomes without qualification.

**CoMegaScroller is a reader-oriented *view*, not the source of truth or the only backup.** Local/offline copies may remain private until explicitly rights-cleared. CoLUE outreach may link an eligible public fragment but must not automatically send draft emails or publish private relationships.
