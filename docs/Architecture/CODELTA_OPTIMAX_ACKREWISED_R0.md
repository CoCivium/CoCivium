## Delta-OptiMax

Candidate operating question:

> Given current evidence, capability, receiver capacity, cost, uncertainty and reversible optionality, what bounded next set of deltas most improves the reachable frontier?

Represent the frontier across at least:

`benefit | evidence | reliability | speed | optionality | reversibility | fairness | privacy | resilience | accessibility | maintainability | energy | cost | learning_value | receiver_capacity | externality | uncertainty | recovery | human_burden | time`

A useful delta improves expected frontier position after costs and side effects.

`DELTA_OPTIMAX = MARGINAL_VERIFIED_FRONTIER_GAIN_PER_BOUNDED_RESOURCE`

## Why unlimited resources do not imply OptiMax

Even with very large available compute, agents, devices or money, useful width remains bounded by:

- correlated failure;
- collision domains;
- stale priors;
- duplicated work;
- verification capacity;
- receiver attention;
- integration bandwidth;
- source quality;
- authority scope;
- privacy and safety constraints;
- coordination overhead;
- diminishing returns;
- proof debt;
- reversibility;
- recovery cost.

Therefore:

`PHYSICAL_WIDTH = ADAPTIVE`
`LOGICAL_OPTIONALITY = VERY_LARGE`
`MATERIALIZATION = SPARSE_RELATIVE_TO_PHASE_SPACE`

## Alienness / translation

As CoSoftware+, Civ2+, CoTheoryAll+, CoCommons+ and higher-order relation systems evolve, they may become increasingly unfamiliar to legacy software and human conceptual defaults.

Alienness can be useful when it represents genuine compression, new capability, better relation handling or new explanatory power.

Alienness is harmful when it means:

- unexplained jargon;
- irreconstructable decisions;
- lost human agency;
- inaccessible interfaces;
- unverifiable claims;
- semantic drift hidden behind novelty;
- systems that only their creators can operate.

Therefore:

`ALIENNESS_MAY_BE_FEATURE`
`ALIENNESS_NE_VALUE`
`NOVEL_NE_BETTER`
`DEEP_MODEL_REQUIRES_TRANSLATABLE_PROJECTION`

Candidate receiver projections:

`NATIVE_DEEP | TECHNICAL | OPERATOR | HUMAN_PLAIN | BEGINNER | SKEPTICAL | MACHINE | HISTORICAL_LEGACY`

The source model may remain strange while its projections become understandable.

`ONE_DEEP_MODEL_MANY_PROJECTIONS`
`TRANSLATION_NE_DUMBING_DOWN`
`RECEIVER_RELATIVE_NE_TRUTH_RELATIVE`
