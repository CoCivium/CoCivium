## Why many, not one

One real exemplar is a demonstration; it is not a general test of CoGrace+ or CoProjectField+. A credible `CoOmniPie` should sample **different independent contexts, methods, people, failure modes, scales, languages, rights arrangements, costs and degrees of maturity**, alongside counterexamples.

CoOmniPie is a **working label for the comparative method**, not a new operating root, a privileged ontology, a claim to infinite resources, or a registered CoTerm. Reuse CoReviewSpine, CoExperimentSelector, CoAllBloom, CoLeverage, CoOntPrinciple and the existing GroupBuild architecture.

## CoGrace+ and CoTime+ cross-case protocol

Every case receives its own **phase contract**. Evaluate neither people's intrinsic worth nor software by one magic scalar.

```
SCOPED INTENT / PHASE / BENEFICIARY
 -> PRIOR ART AND EXTERNAL REFERENCE
 -> EVIDENCE / FAILURE HYPOTHESES
 -> CONTROL OR COMPARATOR DESIGN
 -> CONSENT / RIGHTS / DATA AND EFFECT GATES
 -> MULTIPLE INDEPENDENT BOUNDED OBSERVATIONS
 -> CO-TURTLE CHALLENGE AND COUNTEREXAMPLES
 -> CROSS-CONTEXT FAN-IN (WITH TRANSLATION-LOSS)
 -> SOURCE-CURRENTNESS / RECEIVER ACCEPTANCE
 -> PHASE-SUFFICIENT CANDIDATE OR EXPLICIT HOLD
 -> CONTINUE / SLEEP / RETIRE / REQUALIFY
```

Track a **vector**: task outcome, beneficiary value, accessibility, cost, human attention, contributor rights, privacy, recovery, independent evidence and limitations. Distinguish improvement from mere additional activity. Before/after comparisons need explicit source and temporal scope.

## Sampling and anti-cherry-picking

- **Independent contexts before duplicate examples.** Distinct organizations using different governance, funding, user populations and technical substrate matter more than ten pages on one hosting account.
- **Stratify, do not assume representativeness.** Six named references are a deliberately diverse starting set, not a random or statistically representative sample.
- **Hold out cases.** After using an initial group to design the method, test an unfamiliar comparator without retrofitting the rules.
- **Include negative controls.** Outages, abandoned work, unavailable volunteers, unclear contributor rights, children/guardian boundaries, stale evidence and cross-provider failures.
- **Look for inversions.** A tiny group may outperform a complex federation; a less visually dazzling interface may be more accessible; a dormant project may be more responsible than an always-active one.
- **Reweight selection, not truth.** A changed project phase may legitimately favor a different method; it may not retroactively fabricate success.
- **No outsider endorsement.** Published material from these organizations is prior art, not permission to test users, connect their systems, resell their work or claim partnership.

## Multi-team comparative execution

- Many bounded proposal/review workers can inspect **distinct** cases concurrently when actual resources exist.
- One elected coordinator consolidates the cross-case schema; one elected actuator per mutation target.
- Use CoOmni resource leases, currentness, timestamps and hashes. Avoid duplicating the whole source universe in every worker's context.
- A case with no independent receiver effect stays `NOT_MEASURED`; source existence cannot promote it to CoGrace.
- Cross-domain generalization requires repeated discriminating outcomes, independent challenges, and explicit applicability limits.

## CoHP / CoHp / CoHighlight / CoDream / G_d

- CoHP frames desired human-facing outcomes; CoHp records actual proof-bearing effects.
- CoHighlight can show one success, one active contradiction and one recovery/failure comparator **without** hiding incompleteness.
- CoDream and symbolic G_d lanes can generate alternative visions and ethical questions. Neither is test result, authority or claim of sentient AI experience.
- CoTime captures phase, observed/valid times, age of evidence, future scenarios and next requalification.
- CoOntPrinciple prevents copying contextual meanings of `success`, `participation`, `free`, `fair`, `care` or `grace` across incompatible projects.

## First practical comparative proof

Compare **CoAcademy first-use, GroupBuild concept lab and one independent external reference from each of two unrelated domains** on an identical *public-document review* protocol. Keep the external cases as sourced precedents, not user-acceptance tests. Use a separate consenting newcomer task test for CoAcademy/GroupBuild. Gather baseline and negative evidence before changing anything to win the comparison.

**Acceptance:** repeatable source/currentness and public URL checks; legible differences between simulation and operation; one independently observed beneficiary task for each qualified internal pilot; no unauthorized external effects.
