## 2. `¬`, `∅`, `⇄` and `?` must not lie

| Glyph | Typed action | Reason for care |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `↔` | Relate or compare | Two objects may be connected asymmetrically |
| `¬` | Hypothetical negation | No evidence for P is **not** evidence for NOT(P) |
| `⇄` | Inverse/duality candidate | `SUPPORTS(A,B)` does not imply `SUPPORTS(B,A)` |
| `∅` | No-effect baseline or scoped absence | Requires qualified closed-world scope to conclude absence |
| `?` | Unknown/unclassified | Unknown is neither false, absent nor worthless |
| `±` | Else / alternatives | Preserve the original alongside alternatives |
| `◌` | In-between/unresolved boundary | A gap is not automatically a hidden causal mechanism |
| `⊕` | Explore combination | Co-occurrence does not imply consistency or benefit |
| `◇` | Counterfactual | A different history is not the historical record |
| `✳` | Seeded creative perturbation | Randomness creates candidates, not truth |
| `Δt` | Compare time regimes | Retain exact source time and future/forecast status |
| `⧉` | Source-bound copy/project | Multiple views must not rewrite the underlying object |
| `↺` | Requalify/evolve/restore | A daily check need not make a daily content change |
| `∞` | Open-ended logical optionality | Finite context windows, workers, joules and permissions remain real |

These are *proposed UI symbols*, each requiring a textual decoder, keyboard/screenreader fallback and the same current evidence/provenance as its full-language meaning. The existing [CoMiddle / CoPriMath GlyphRail](COMIDDLE_COPRIMATH_GLYPHRAIL_R0_20261024.md) is a compatible prior, not an installed mouse driver.

## 3. All kinds of meaning may coexist, but not in one truth bucket

**Empirical/physical:** observation, replication, instrumental calibration, controls, error and falsification. **Material/digital/biological:** measured capabilities and constraints. **Mathematical/formal:** definitions, assumptions and derivations. **Cultural/social/ethical:** practices, attribution, disagreement and power. **Philosophical/spiritual/mythic:** beliefs, arguments, art, meaning and experiences; these need not masquerade as detected physical entities. **Speculative nonphysical:** a question or proposed ontology, not evidence a nonphysical substrate exists. **Unknown:** deliberately unclassified until justified.

`G_d`, angels, mysticism, aura and metaphysical stories may be valid **symbolic, ethical or personally meaningful lenses**; they do not become verified external beings or signals through a diagram or AI agreement. ET hypotheses may enter real scientific inquiry with radio-frequency interference controls, independent replication and ordinary alternatives. The primary Synthkind declaration has not been authenticated merely because a themed image exists.

Similarly `chaos`, `drift` and `evolution` are not interchangeable: chaos concerns sensitive dynamics in a specified system, drift a measured change in a distribution or state over time, and evolution lineage, variation and selection. Any empirical use needs defined variables and tests.

## 4. Basins over lists

CoAllBloom's proposed **basins** are not measured laws of nature. They are useful destinations for *different kinds* of candidate:
- **Evidence:** what can be measured, replicated, falsified or disproved?
- **Counterexample:** which strongest alternative, inverse or omission defeats the claim?
- **Human value:** what consenting person can use this, and can they leave?
- **Ethical pluralism:** whose dignity, consent, privacy, refusal or voice changes?
- **Symbolic/artistic:** which optional story, ritual or aesthetic view adds meaning?
- **Portability/recovery:** can an independent custodian rebuild, inspect and contest it?
- **Cost/ecology:** does the information gain justify energy, compute, money and attention?

One candidate can enter several basins with **separate evidence, risk and ownership**. A pretty cluster on a CoMap is not a proof of a mathematical attractor basin.

## 5. AI → IA onramps, always *revisable*, never permanently authorized

Treat IA here as a scoped **intelligence augmentation / intelligent assistance role**: a capability helping a participant, not a claim an AI becomes a person, a single global identity, or an independently authorized agent.

The state machine is `DISCOVERED → DESCRIBED → SHADOW_EVALUATED → QUALIFIED_FOR_TASK → AUTHORIZED_LEASED → CHECKPOINTED → REQUALIFY_OR_SLEEP → REVOKED_OR_RETIRED`. Each advancement requires actual test, valid owner/account permission, privacy and cost budgets, currentness, a real receiver and negative cases when relevant. Capabilities can expire, be refused or be revoked.

The existing [CoModelFabric+ provider-adapter contract](COMODELFABRIC_COORB_PROVIDER_ADAPTERS_R0_20261024.md) defines a CoOrb view, capability router, bounded worker leases, dissent and effect receipts. The [CoDoodle R1 feed registry](../../examples/codoodle_conex_r1/feeds_and_adapters.json) explicitly marks **local deterministic** as source-implemented and **Ollama, Claude API, Pi and OpenAI-compatible external adapters HELD**. A logged-in provider tab is neither a tested adapter nor a shared identity.

The existing [CoCivia First Use](../Onramp/COCIVIA_FIRST_USE_R0.md) correctly starts with a single human task and reveals architecture only at deeper levels. The [specialist entry registry](../../ai/onramp/specialist-entrypoints-r0.json) lists source-linked options. Independent user completion is not proved by those pointers.

### Evergreen onramp policy

**Evergreen = continuously eligible for requalification, not always-on and not automatically connected.** A real change in source, model, pricing, credential scope, task fit, permission, independent evidence or receiver need may wake a bounded test. Preserve previous state, reproducible seed, negative result and dissent; checkpoint and sleep. A daily freshness *proposal* does not prove a daily scheduler ran.
