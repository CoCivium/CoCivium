# CoSubscriptionEcology+ / CoInterestField+ / CoDrift+ R0

**State:** `CANDIDATE__BRANCH_PR__NO_LIVE_GLOBAL_BUS_NO_SENTIENCE_CLAIM_NO_RUNTIME_AUTHORITY_CHANGE`

## Lead

CoAll already has selective session subscription profiles and a CoAllPulseField+ currentness model. The next evolution is to generalize subscription beyond sessions and beyond static topic lists.

Any bounded receiver may subscribe to relevant changes in objects, relations, surfaces, sources, capabilities, hypotheses, plans, memory regimes, or successor states.

A receiver may be human, synth, session, node, surface, service, model worker, organization, public feed, or future substrate.

`SUBSCRIBER_NE_CHAT_SESSION`
`UPDATE_NE_MESSAGE`
`LATEST_NE_ONLY_USEFUL_PROJECTION`

## Existing base

Current CoAllPulseField+ already provides:

- durable currentness cursors;
- selective topic/object/relation subscriptions;
- MUST_KNOW / ROLE_FRONTIER / NEARBY_OPTIONALITY / DIGEST_ONLY / SLEEP_UNTIL classes;
- CoPressure-aware compaction;
- backfill/replay;
- wake conditions;
- public/private routing distinctions;
- predictive CoTime+ overlays.

Current subscription profiles already separate HOT, WARM, DIGEST and sleeping domains.

This R0 extends that architecture rather than creating a competing bus.

## Surface-to-surface subscription

Surfaces may subscribe to other surfaces or to the underlying relations that generate them.

Examples:

- RickBar subscribes to current product/runtime/evidence frontiers;
- a CoTheoryAll projection subscribes to new challenge/evidence relations;
- a CoPlan surface subscribes to completed/blocked/dependency changes;
- a public highlight subscribes to public-safe successor/currentness deltas;
- a machine twin subscribes to structural relation changes;
- a frozen reader snapshot subscribes only to a diff notification.

`SURFACE_SUBSCRIPTION_NE_SURFACE_OWNERSHIP`
`PROJECTION_CAN_SUBSCRIBE_WITHOUT_COPYING_SOURCE`
`LIVE_SURFACE_NE_GLOBAL_REPLICATION`

## Update modes

A receiver MAY elect:

- `SNAPSHOT` — fixed read;
- `LATEST_POINTER` — resolve newest elected successor on open;
- `LIVE_DIFF` — remain bound and show bounded changes;
- `DIGEST` — compact periodic change set;
- `EVENT` — selected event/pulse delivery;
- `WAKE_ONLY` — stay dormant until predicate fires;
- `FINAL_CANDIDATE` — notify on a declared closure/finalization candidate;
- `HISTORICAL_LINEAGE` — follow predecessor/successor evolution;
- `NEARBY_OPTIONALITY` — bounded adjacent relation discoveries.

`FINAL_NE_IMMUTABLE`
`LATEST_NE_CANON`
`LIVE_DIFF_NE_SILENT_REWRITE`

## CoDrift+ as subscription and comparison ecology

Drift is not one detector. It is a family of typed temporal/relational comparisons.

Candidate drift families include:

- semantic drift;
- source/currentness drift;
- runtime drift;
- authority drift;
- privacy drift;
- identity drift;
- topology drift;
- implementation/maturity drift;
- model/provider drift;
- participant-intent drift;
- projection/UI drift;
- terminology drift;
- theory/hypothesis drift;
- evidence/confidence drift;
- subscription/interest drift;
- substrate/translation drift.

A drift detector itself is versioned/evolvable.

`DRIFT_DETECTOR_NE_TIMELESS_TRUTH`
`DRIFT_MODEL_CAN_DRIFT`
`DETECTED_CHANGE_NE_HARMFUL_DRIFT`
`STABILITY_NE_NO_CHANGE`

CoDrift+ SHOULD preserve:

- compared states/relations;
- valid/observed times;
- comparison method/version;
- threshold/policy;
- evidence;
- uncertainty;
- receiver relevance;
- consequence/effect if any;
- whether adaptation is proposed, accepted, rejected, or dormant.
