## Useful dimensions

Candidate work may vary across:

- domain / object family;
- required capability;
- observer / receiver class;
- stakeholder class;
- time horizon and scale;
- epistemic status;
- challenge method;
- projection/media surface;
- failure mode;
- provider/substrate/failure domain;
- evolution/perturbation operator.

The combinatorial address space can be extremely large. That is an indexable
possibility space, not a claim of equally many valid tasks or active workers.

## Materialization governor

Prefer work-conserving but not compute-maximizing behavior.

Parallelize independent read-only research, comparison, simulation, challenge
and preparation. Serialize shared mutation, authority/promotion decisions,
public effects, security/privacy/financial effects and destructive operations.

Throttle generation when verification or compaction falls behind.

## Freshness / renewal

Fresh work can arise from new evidence, changed source/currentness, new
contradictions, failed predictions, receiver gaps, capability/provider changes,
resource changes, stale dependencies, uncovered observer classes, distribution
shift, new failure modes, new media derivatives, new standards or new user
intent.

Completed work may emit successor Needs, experiments, falsifiers, derivatives
or retirement conditions. Failed work should emit negative knowledge and
changed-precondition wake rules.

## Roles

Logical roles may include currentness observers, provenance verifiers,
authority checkers, contradiction challengers, failure-domain analysts,
receiver-readproof checkers, recovery planners, accessibility challengers,
resource-fit analysts, dependency analysts, negative-knowledge keepers,
counterfactual challengers, compaction/dedupe roles and restore verifiers.

A role is not necessarily a process.
