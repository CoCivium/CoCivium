## Four coupled loops
1. **CoFront+:** identify the current, source-bound domain and its exact roles, custody, consent and last-known-good state.
2. **CoEcho+:** follow predecessor pointers backward (including dormant, declined, and failed alternatives), then forward again to test whether current descendants actually satisfy prior needs. Historical work is not silently upgraded.
3. **CoTime+:** retain event, observed, qualified, landed, and independent receiver-read times separately. Late discoveries never rewrite when an event originally occurred.
4. **CoEv+:** generate fresh, bounded hypotheses from uncovered history; preserve diversity, contradiction and useful independent challenge. A historical echo is not itself a new worker or newly completed effect.

## The coverage cell
For each bounded source item store or reference:

`WorkID | source_ref | exact_source_digest? | event_at? | observed_at? | qualified_at? | privacy_scope | evidence_class | predecessor[] | successor[] | dissent[] | intended_receiver | receiver_receipt? | disposition | last_known_good? | next_wake`

If unavailable, record null or UNKNOWN; never backfill guesses from neighboring filenames or chat summaries. Keep provenance and claim validity separate from byte integrity.

## Disposition before retirement
- **LIVE_REFERENCED:** qualified current work, source discoverable.
- **PRESERVED_ALTERNATIVE:** competing source or unresolved hypothesis retained.
- **SUPERSEDED_WITH_LINK:** exact successor and acceptance reason visible.
- **DELIBERATELY_RETIRED_WITH_EVIDENCE:** evidence, authority, and recovery policy recorded.
- **ORPHAN_RISK:** found but no reliable current owner or downstream lineage.
- **UNKNOWN_REQUIRES_RECOVERY:** source may exist but scan or receiver access is insufficient.

**No entry is equivalent to "all prior work is covered."** Negative knowledge is preserved. A private source pointer is never copied to a public graph without an independent privacy and authority decision.

## Traversal
```
known anchor -> bounded prior-source search -> source qualification -> predecessor/successor comparison
    -> coverage disposition -> candidate better or deliberate hold -> receipt -> next wake
```

Use per-source cursors and role-filtered retrieval. Work backward by known lineage and time windows, not by replaying every historical chat. Keep parallel read-only discovery independent of any elected shared-state actuator. One authoritative merge writer per collision domain. Favor independent verification over source-count inflation.

**Retro-predictive inversion:** ask whether an older ignored warning could have changed a later decision, and what observation today would discriminate competing explanations. This is a clearly labeled counterfactual experiment, not evidence that the future sent a message to the past. G_dSide / CoTheoryAll philosophical hypotheses remain separate from operational proof.

## Failure and completion conditions
A coverage pass is qualified only for its **declared scan roots and interval**, with counts for substantive objects, image evidence, transients, sidecars, semantic reviews and unresolved links. Flag missing exact source bytes, permissions, ambiguous hashes, split identities, invalid successors, currentness conflicts, privacy and external receiver gaps.

**Break Glass:** never auto-publish private data, infer AGI/sentience, overwrite an ancestor, merge incompatible source scopes, declare an independent ACK from a local upload, or treat a UI screenshot as X2 deployment.
