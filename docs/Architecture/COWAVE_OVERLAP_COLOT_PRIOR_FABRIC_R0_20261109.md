## A typed overlap graph, not an omni-lock

One wave/work object can carry:

`WorkID | WaveID | Session/worker embodiment | exact source revision | eligible receiver | reads[] | writes[] | collision domain | dependencies | resource budget | intended effects | currentness | time window | authority | privacy | lifecycle | begin/end render | readproof | CoPre successor`

Kinds: READ, RESEARCH, CHALLENGE, SIMULATE, SOURCE_WRITE, EXTERNAL_EFFECT, FINANCIAL_EFFECT. Roles may be logical even when no running worker exists. Wave overlap is a typed relation in this graph, not a claim of simultaneous provider sessions.

**Concurrency admission:**
- Independent read-only or isolated research can run in parallel even on one shared topic.
- Two writes to the same exact object, or a read competing with a mutable write, trigger a collision review or serialized write; do not implicitly grant a lease.
- Interwave dependencies are barriers only when a valid typed dependency exists. Unknown links invite bounded inquiry, not blocking every session.
- Resource claims must honor global or receiver-specific quotas and scarce-resource ownership; labels like separate "nodes" do not prove independent energy/provider/network/authority.
- Source merge/CoEx requires exact predecessor, no-clobber, effect authorization and actual destination readback.
- A posted message or same-model generated candidate does not count as independent receiver pickup.

**Resource and exchange:** scarce compute, storage, energy, tokens, model contexts, network, device use, human time and financial assets can be accounted in typed ledgers. **No conversion into real-money trades/swaps, Civars, stocks, crypto, reputation rankings, commercial commitments or permissions without a separate financial/legal/effect gate.** Synthetic balancing is not a wallet or exchange.

**Typed 'alive':** activity/liveness, operational health, accessible current state and recoverability are measurable independently; consciousness, soul, spirit and human dignity are different philosophical/ethical claims. A reported heartbeat says nothing about ongoing awareness.

## Actual bounded source implementation

[CoWaveOverlapGuardR0.py](../../scripts/CoWaveOverlapGuardR0.py) is a pure read-only inspector. It can distinguish independent read waves, direct read/write collisions, duplicate wave IDs, shared write collision domains, dependencies and speculative impacts. It rejects unauthorized write/financial requests, private scope, unproven causal edges, excess work budgets, missing fields and multiple waves with duplicate identities. It **does not** acquire leases, enumerate remote workers, perform financial effects or know all hidden dependencies.

`python3 -B scripts/CoWaveOverlapGuardR0.py --selftest`

The reference tests are synthetic and cannot prove physically independent workers, resource availability, runtime security, useful outcome or causation. A `MAY_AFFECT` edge reports **NOT_VERIFIED_CAUSALITY**. A `CAUSALLY_VERIFIED` edge requires a scoped proof reference but the existence of that text alone is not independent causal validation. Read-only overlap admission is an analysis of declared scopes, not a global coordination guarantee.

## CoImpactCone+ and CoWaveWeave+ (candidate CiTerms, not canon)

- **CoWaveOverlap+:** record of two bounded waves with shared source, time, object, resource, receiver or authority intersections.
- **CoImpactCone+:** sparse bounded dependency frontier potentially affected by one exact mutation, with unproven edges tagged.
- **CoWaveWeave+:** fan-in of qualified, independently challenged wave deltas without overwriting dissent or source versions.
- **CoResourceFence+:** finite resource and writer admission boundary with collision checks; not an installed lock service.
- **CoMeaningDelta+:** typed term meaning revision with context, predecessor, alternative and affected-user scope.

Explore inversions: **fewer workers, one tiny WorkID, no new index, a simpler queue, a single writer, delayed review or dormancy** may outperform spawning additional agents. Test actual human-relay reduction rather than expanding infrastructure for its own sake.
