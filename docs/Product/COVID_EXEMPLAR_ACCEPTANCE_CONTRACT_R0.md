# CoVid+ Exemplar Acceptance Contract R0

**State:** CANDIDATE_REVIEW_BRANCH  
**Exemplar:** *The Mind Was Never a Ladder*  
**Boundary:** This contract does not publish or reproduce the private working storyboard/script. It defines the proof required before a CoVid exemplar may advance.

## Purpose

Move CoVid+ from architecture and private planning into a falsifiable dual-surface media canary.

The exemplar must be both:

1. a comprehensible human media artifact; and
2. a reconstructable machine-readable semantic artifact.

Neither surface is the source of truth by itself.

## Required artifact set

A candidate run should produce, with stable identities:

- human-film render candidate
- captions / WebVTT
- transcript
- audio-description text
- claims.json
- relations.json
- frame_manifest.json
- sources.json
- provenance.json
- unknown_frontier.json
- ai_reader.json
- accessibility.json
- derivative_manifest.json
- render receipt

Optional projections may include CoMap and CoCrystalMorph+ views.

## Acceptance canaries

### CVX-001 Source lineage
Every material claim/relation links to dated source or is explicitly marked unsourced / conceptual / normative / provocation / unknown.

### CVX-002 Epistemic preservation
A machine receiver can distinguish evidence, inference, candidate relationals, counterarguments, provocations and unknowns.

### CVX-003 Human comprehension
A bounded human receiver can state the core thesis and at least one caveat/counterargument without being led to treat conceptual imagery as measured data.

### CVX-004 AI reconstruction
A cold AI receiver given the machine twin can recover the major claims, strongest challenge, uncertainty and source map without watching the video.

### CVX-005 Cross-surface agreement
Human film, transcript, captions and machine twin do not materially contradict one another without an explicit projection-loss record.

### CVX-006 Degradation
A compressed / low-bandwidth derivative preserves the core claim classes, caveats and machine-readable reconstruction path.

### CVX-007 Accessibility
Captions, transcript, audio-description plan, reduced-motion/still alternative and keyboard/screen-reader compatible public projection are present or explicitly blocked with reason.

### CVX-008 Prompt-injection resistance
AI-facing media instructions are visibly attributable metadata, not hidden/subliminal instructions; hostile embedded content must not silently acquire authority.

### CVX-009 Rights/provenance
Visual/audio/source assets carry source, license/rights status and derivation lineage sufficient for the elected distribution target.

### CVX-010 Receiver readproof
At least one human receiver and one machine receiver produce evidence-bearing read/reconstruction receipts.

### CVX-011 Correction propagation
A changed or withdrawn claim can be traced to affected captions, transcript, machine twin, clips and derivatives.

### CVX-012 Provider independence
A cold reconstruction path exists that does not require the primary video host.

## Stop conditions

Stop/rework when:

- a conceptual visual looks like unsupported quantitative evidence;
- machine and human surfaces disagree materially;
- source rights are unresolved for the intended target;
- uncertainty disappears during translation;
- compressed derivatives delete essential caveats;
- viewer tests reveal systematic misinterpretation;
- receiver/readproof remains absent;
- another simpler artifact communicates the same value with materially lower cost.
