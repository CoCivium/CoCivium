`SOURCE_NE_PROJECTION`  
`MORPH_NE_TRUTH`  
`TRANSLATION_NE_EQUIVALENCE`  
`PLATFORM_NE_CUSTODY_TOTALITY`  
`GLOBAL_REACH_NE_MAXIMUM_PLATFORM_DEPENDENCE`  
`REDUNDANCY_NE_DUPLICATE_CHAOS`  
`PUBLICATION_NE_CANON`  
`VIDEO_NE_TRUTH`  
`VIRALITY_NE_VALIDITY`  
`PROJECTION_LOSS_MUST_BE_DECLARED`

Every CoMap/CoCrystalMorph-compatible initiative should preserve, where applicable: source identity/version, provenance, claim grade, uncertainty/contradiction, omitted details, changed metaphors, audience, language, risk class, currentness and projection-loss receipt.

The 500 entries are **candidate work cells**, not 500 live agents and not 500 commitments. Materialize by value, evidence, cost, collision and authority.
