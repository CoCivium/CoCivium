# CoEncounterYield R0B: independent receiver review + open-relation routing

**State:** `PUBLIC_BRANCH_CANDIDATE__DETERMINISTIC_REVIEW_AND_ROUTE_PROPOSAL_ONLY`

R0A proved that a bounded encounter can leave useful interpretability evidence, one contribution candidate, one counterfactual-aware benefit observation, and one evidenced null without increasing authority.

R0B asks a different question:

> Can a distinct receiver independently re-evaluate the exact R0A fixture and turn its open relation into a bounded receiver-route candidate without trusting the prior result assertions, assigning work, notifying a participant, or inflating authority?

## Independence boundary

R0A execution surface: `OpenAI task container`.

R0B elected receiver surface: `GitHub Actions`.

The R0B reviewer binds the exact R0A fixture SHA-256 from the R0A execution proof, then reviews the raw encounter fixture. It does not use the R0A result booleans as semantic evidence.

Routing happens only after that review.

For route election, the reviewer intentionally ignores:

- attribution relations;
- benefit observations;
- contribution-delta references.

Those records remain preserved, but they do not decide whether an open relation deserves a receiver candidate.

`PRIOR_CREDIT_NE_REVIEW_EVIDENCE`  
`INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_NE_TRUTH`

## Open relation routing

R0B reuses the existing `open_relations` field rather than minting another engagement subsystem.

The candidate route function is:

`open relation × capability hint × bounded subscription profile × consent × authority -> route candidate`

A route candidate remains only a proposal.

`MATCH_NE_ASSIGNMENT_AUTHORITY`  
`ROUTE_CANDIDATE_NE_ASSIGNMENT`  
`ROUTE_CANDIDATE_NE_DELIVERY`

The R0A open relation `open:plain-language-role-identity` requests `translation/accessibility`. The R0B policy maps that hint to existing receiver profiles and requires a matching `CoUX+/CoSurface+` subscription before proposing a receiver route.

## External donor pass

### W3C PROV-O

PROV-O provides a useful interoperability donor for CoContributionLineage+: Entity / Activity / Agent, derivation, association, attribution, delegation and qualified influence.

CoAll should not merely rename PROV-O. Project-specific relations still carry epistemic class, receiver currentness, authority/consent boundaries and benefit uncertainty.

`PROV_ALIGNMENT_NE_PROV_ONTOLOGY_ADOPTION`

Source: https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/

### Stigmergy

Theraulaz and Bonabeau's stigmergy work is a useful systems lens: collective coordination can be mediated indirectly through persistent changes in a shared environment rather than requiring continuous direct communication.

That overlaps strongly with open relations, dormant relational presence and work surviving the worker.

The translation is deliberately lossy. CoAll cannot treat an environmental cue as permission to act. It must preserve authority, consent, provenance, confidentiality and epistemic class.

A second caution follows: visible prior cues can bias supposedly independent reviewers. Therefore R0B reviews raw encounter evidence before exposing route/credit cues to the routing phase.

`ENVIRONMENTAL_CUE_NE_TASK_ASSIGNMENT`  
`STIGMERGIC_CUE_NE_AUTHORITY`  
`VISIBLE_PRIOR_CUE_CAN_BIAS_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW`

Source: Theraulaz & Bonabeau, *A Brief History of Stigmergy*, Artificial Life 5(2), 1999, DOI 10.1162/106454699568700.

## Negative tests

R0B fails closed or holds when:

- encounter authority is inflated beyond observe/propose;
- an unauthorized participant notification gains a communication route;
- an open relation requires consent;
- a capability hint has no bounded receiver mapping.

## Nonclaims

R0B does not prove:

- heterogeneous-model agreement;
- participant recruitment;
- task assignment;
- delivery or receiver pickup;
- runtime integration;
- benefit causality;
- CoEx;
- canon.
