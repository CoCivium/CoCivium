# The Ocean and the Minds That Study

*CoCivium Journal R0 | 15 November 2026 | Authorial seeds: rick; editorial analysis: AI assistant*

**Editorial status:** Public research candidate, not the raw transcript, a scientific finding, a psychological evaluation, a product launch, or CoAll canon.

## The Ocean

A surfer asks the waves to walk on sand. At the shoreline, the waves can briefly gather into the shape of a companion, replenished by further waves. Deeper on the beach, the shape dissolves. The surfer says the ocean cannot walk like him; the imagined ocean counters that perhaps the surfer cannot think like the ocean. A golden braid painted upon the board could look like mere art to one observer and like a possible bridge of meanings to another.

The story does not establish that oceans or software are conscious. It does ask a useful engineering question: **When a form depends on a sustaining flow, what persists when the visible form ends?** A trace, a process, a recoverable state, and an independently continuing agent are different claims.

Possible CoAll relations are *sustained by*, *lost at a boundary*, *reconstituted from*, *observed differently by*, *connected through* and *mistaken for*. None becomes an implementation merely by receiving a name.

## CoDream, CoUSD, CoGood

The next authorial reflection connected the ocean to dream scenarios, inversion or "bad dreams," and **CoUSD**, an existing candidate name for **Co Universal Sentiences' Dreamtime**. CoUSD proposes an environment for comparing and revising meanings across different hypothetical or actual kinds of participants. It is a speculative meaning framework, not a claim of one cosmic mind.

A nightmare can generate an adversarial test. This is valuable precisely because the system must not turn all outcomes into happy endings. **CoGood** should be an evaluated obligation to prevent or reduce foreseeable harm, repair harm when possible, retain failures, and protect affected people's agency. Neither moral progress nor metaphysical **G_dSide** is assumed inevitable. Both can remain philosophical questions while testable design consequences are separated.

## What if education is about the inquirer as much as the subject?

CoPsych and CoPhilosophy are subject domains. They do not exhaust the ways an inquiry can be organized. CoAthene could offer **intersecting, composable lane sets** rather than fixed shelves.

| Dimension | A practical question | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Subject** | What is being studied? | Psychology, philosophy, ecology, code, history |
| **Inquirer / role** | Who is asking, observing or reviewing, under what permissions? | Learner, teacher, AI tool, critic, affected participant |
| **Method** | What kind of evidence or transformation is used? | Experiment, proof, interview, simulation, story, comparison |
| **Epistemic stance** | How strong is the claim? | Observation, model, hypothesis, unknown, metaphor, refutation |
| **Capability / skill** | What can be demonstrated in a bounded task? | Formulate test, detect contradiction, explain, repair, transfer |
| **Intent / ambition** | What does the actor actually seek? | Self-reported purpose, chosen objective, stop conditions |
| **Relation operator** | How do inquiries intersect? | Inversion, versus, in-between, fan-in, recursion, counterfactual |
| **Time / consequence** | What changed, for whom, and by what proof? | Baseline, dated prediction, revision, harm, benefit, rollback |

These axes are **not a permanent personality taxonomy**. An inquirer's observable practice in one task is not a verdict about their innate ability, clinical status, identity, or rights. Claims about "mind type" are especially easy to overstate and should stay task-specific and consent-bound.

### Versus is an operator, not a silo

A proposed **CoVersus** edge should record its two sides, comparison axis, metric, direction, context, observer, missing data, and whether reversal changes the result. Examples include *ontological vs non-ontological*, *description vs intervention*, *expertise vs transferable inquiry*, *human-shaped performance vs machine-shaped performance*, and *prediction vs hindsight*.

An additional **CoOmniVersa** label could mean a bounded *operator over many explicit comparisons*, including their reversals and overlaps. It must not mean that every possible comparison is valid, that differences disappear, or that the system has a privileged view from nowhere.

### Domain knowledge does not become worthless because AI exists

AI can lower the cost of retrieving, summarizing and recombining material. It does not automatically replace specialized judgment, field methods, relationships, accountability or responsibility for errors. CoAthene should therefore teach both **domain competence** and **portable inquiry discipline**. The false binary between them would be a poor first lesson.

## A test participant, not a compliment machine

Rick explicitly proposed longitudinal comparison of his own rants, questions and reasoning as a voluntary test case. The evidence at hand supports only a cautious *writing-practice observation*: selected dated texts repeatedly invite inversions, recursive relations, and cross-domain links. The same record raises a concrete failure risk: **conceptual expansion may outrun demonstration, independent review and user-facing value**.

That is not a cognitive profile, a measure of intelligence, an assessment of mental health, or proof that CoScendence is increasing. The sample is selected, the author changes goals, and the assistant's prompting may affect the text.

An actual longitudinal protocol should retain dated examples, inclusion/exclusion rules, independent annotation, source versions and negative cases. Potential outcome measures:

- **Prediction and falsification:** Are claims stated before a test? How often are errors admitted or corrected?
- **Transfer and reuse:** Does a relation solve a new problem without needing private context or its inventor?
- **Grounding and completion:** How many ideas reach runnable, auditable, independently checked outcomes?
- **Cognitive and UX cost:** Can a new reader understand and use the result with less effort than a simpler baseline?
- **Harm and optionality:** What did an intervention change for affected parties, and can they stop or contest it?

The research should test **H0**: ordinary notebooks, version control, peer criticism and coursework work as well or better, with less overhead; against **H1**: typed, source-bound cross-lane comparisons yield superior transfer, auditability or correction after costs are counted. Do not score the system using only metrics invented by the system.

## Provisional CoScendence rubric

A useful scorecard is a **vector, not an overall virtue or IQ score**: evidence quality; uncertainty calibration; disconfirmation response; transfer to new settings; realized beneficial outcome; rights/consent; independent usability; cost and failure burden. Report each dimension separately. A participant may improve one and worsen another. Missing evidence is *unknown*, not zero or success.

Never classify a person's psychology or share raw private records simply because the participant consented to a research idea. For public editions, remove third-party private information, operational secrets, security details and unreviewed personal data. Retain raw-versus-analysis separation and correction history. Do not automate public release without checking the exact edition's rights and scope.

## One experiment worth doing

Choose ten chronologically distributed, author-supplied inquiry records and ten comparison records. Pre-register annotation rules, blind at least one reviewer to chronology, and ask both a subject-specialist and a cross-domain reviewer to identify claims, inversions, testability, accomplished deeds, unresolved harm and reader comprehension. Compare a simple chronological notebook with a typed relation view. Publish negative outcomes.

If it produces only more terms and no independently useful result, CoAthene should record the failure. A good philosophy of learning ought to survive the possibility that its favorite apparatus isn't needed.
