# CoSpiff+ / CoSurviveAll+ | Not too big to fail, but safe to fail and too useful to lose

**R1 · October 24, 2026 · Public design and proof index, not a launch certification**

## CoHereNow

**Status: PUBLIC R&D, NOT FIREWORKS-READY.** CoCivium has source-linked demos, reproducible bounded experiments, a human-readable starter path and a growing specialist constellation. It does not yet have evidence that an outsider has independently used it successfully, that essential state can be cold-recovered after GitHub and X2 are unavailable, or that multiple independent stewards can keep it useful without its present builders.

The question is not whether CoCivium becomes essential enough to deserve rescue from others. The better test is whether **each failure is containable, useful capabilities survive, and other people can leave, fork, restore, replace and improve without coercion**.

- The [R2 source-currentness record](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoAcademy/blob/master/docs/relay/READPROOF_R2_RECEIPT_20261024.json) records 18/18 deterministic CI tests and Pages workflow success. It explicitly **does not** claim that its seven exact-byte public endpoint comparisons completed after deployment.

**FAIL or incomplete:**
- One fresh public observation found the main custom-domain apex returning 404 and its www host showing a legacy metadata-style index instead of a human first-use page. An independent second-vantage check and site privacy review remain required. Details of possible sensitive paths belong in a private security issue, not in marketing copy.
- A root listing of **CoAcademy** contains no top-level LICENSE. This does not establish that no individual file has a grant or who owns the rights. It prevents us from asserting unrestricted FOSS reuse across that repository without a rights-holder review. The CoCivium core root has GPL-3.0.
- Independent first-use comprehension, audience attribution, source-to-off-GitHub-custodian readproof, cross-power-domain restore, second-maintainer reproduction, actual CoOrbWare runtime and provider-substitution task parity have not been proved in this assessment.
- The [CoRelay failure-domain contract](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoAcademy/blob/master/docs/relay/resilience.json) explicitly groups GitHub Pages, Raw, repo and Issues into **one failure domain**, despite multiple addresses. A Git clone elsewhere is a candidate recovery artifact until a separate custodian proves readback and recovery.

## CoSpiff+: not prettiness; a trustworthy first useful moment

CoSpiff is the measurable combination of low attention cost, clear purpose, predictable behaviour, reversible choice, accessibility and immediate real task value. The moustache graphic can identify a public R&D family, but a logo does not create usability, rights or credibility.

Every specialist entrance should answer in ordinary language: **Why should I care? What can I do in 30 seconds? What actually worked? What remains unknown? How do I stop, correct or leave?**

One shared identity/evidence fabric can support multiple independently chosen brands. A `.node`, `.clone`, persona, game or domain alias is a typed candidate, not a new legal owner, public DNS domain, sentient entity, installed product or account permission.

Third-party software needs independent checks of exact revision, licence, security, model dependencies, network behaviour, maintenance and risk before installation. No covert WiFi sensing or other sensors should be activated as an architectural shortcut.

## What else is missing: the 14 gates

Read the [machine assessment](../../ai/resilience/cospiff-fireworks-r1.json) for exact current state, test and proof boundary. Priority groups:

**P0 | Avoid harm and repair the doorway:** Public-site routing and potential metadata disclosure, rights/licence review, one stable human front door. Do not use more marketing to compensate for a broken destination.

**P1 | Survive a real loss:** Independently powered/off-GitHub custody, cold restoration into another host, recovery keys and a receiver-produced exact hash, with no dependency on the X2 session or primary provider credentials.

**P2 | Demonstrate real use:** Five consenting first-time task successes as a proposed pilot threshold, two voluntary returns, a simple route-vs-specialist comparator, accessibility and correction/exit measures, bot/self-excluded opt-in aggregate counting. These thresholds are **proposals**, not achieved metrics.

**P3 | Avoid founder and vendor capture:** A second real steward can rebuild and contribute; licence grant and third-party notices are verified; governance and dispute/correction processes function; security, financial and legal responsibilities are held by authorised people. No unverified war-chest balance, patent status or trading result counts as a public readiness proof.

**P4 | Useful evolution:** Transparent provider-neutral adapter tests, typed transfer of evidence and uncertainty, bounded leased resource allocation, independent challenge/hypothesis lanes and measured cost per accepted result.

## Fireworks: staged rather than detonated

1. **Public R&D:** Supported by source publication and bounded tests. **Current qualified class.**
2. **Useful first-use pilot:** A safe front door and one independently completed task. **HELD.**
3. **Independently survivable service:** An off-provider cold restore and non-originator recovery. **HELD.**
4. **Credible public fireworks moment:** Five first-use successes, two returns, accessible/privacy-safe route, verified licence rights, two proven failure domains, real independent rebuild, and one independent counterexample/correction route. **HELD; deliberately testable.**
5. **Self-sustaining ecosystem:** Repeated community contributions, independent stewards and restorations over time. **HELD.**

No stage is inferred from repository count, generated tokens, public posts, follower counts, project names, 10¹² logical session descriptors, the number of bots, finances or a branded conference announcement.

## Smallest safe next deed

**First the owner should qualify and repair the existing public-domain privacy/routing issue, without copying its metadata to new venues. In parallel, an authorised receiver can accept one public-only source bundle on a second non-GitHub provider and return a fresh exact-hash plus cold-rebuild receipt.** These are independent, bounded lanes. A receiving device/host is not independent merely because it has a new path or is on a different screen.

Once the safe public entrance exists, measure a real human first-use task against the existing plain first-use guide before expanding the specialist constellation.

**Evergreen invariant:** Fail small. Preserve original source. Restore elsewhere. Invite criticism. Reward real usefulness. Let people leave freely. Never turn evidence into a crown.
