# Civ2+ toward a possible Civ3+ boundary | Evidence-led gap map R0

**October 24, 2026 | ADVISORY WORKING MODEL | NOT CANON, RELEASE CERTIFICATION, EXTERNAL CONTACT EVIDENCE OR A FORECAST**

## Lead

**CoCivium can show source-controlled experiments and real bounded tests. It cannot yet demonstrate a mature Civ2-scale operating fabric.** The largest missing capabilities are independent human usefulness, genuine multi-provider and multi-custodian continuity, real permission-bounded agent interoperability, durable governance, and honest human-accessible service delivery. More terms, public domains, AI opinions and test cycles cannot replace those demonstrations.

The more valuable future goal is not "too big to fail." It is **too useful to lose, and safe for any one part to fail**: others can understand, refuse, leave, fork, restore, challenge and maintain the work without a crown.

## Proposed interpretation, with historic disagreement preserved

These are CoCivium-specific civilization-design terms, not the Kardashev energy classification.

| Stage | Working meaning | Important disagreement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Civ1 | Human-centered institutions, websites and siloed software with significant manual coordination | Analytical simplification, not a label of inferiority |
| Early Civ2+ | Consent-based, source-aware human/AI cooperation with portable data, local-first agency, transparent tasks and real feedback | Source-only prototypes are an onramp, not the whole stage |
| Mature Civ2+ | Multiple autonomous participants, devices, providers, institutions and communities can cooperate reliably; recover from provider and founder failure; correct rules; account for ecological and social costs; protect dissent and exit | Actual maturity requires observation over time, not model consensus |
| Proposed Civ3+ boundary | Participants can safely collaborate across radically divergent intelligences, identities, embodiments and substrates without sacrificing refusal, attribution, care for outsiders, return, privacy or independently reviewable governance | Earlier scenarios alternatively emphasize integrated virtual/physical worlds, dimensional CoObjects, verified external-intelligence contact, and post-identity/post-scarcity horizons. They are not proven equivalents or a singular authorized threshold |
| Speculative post-boundary | A plural and durable federation across currently unfamiliar kinds of participants, physical contexts or long time horizons | **No verified ET contact, new sentience class, new physics or guaranteed transition is asserted** |

Two boundary interpretations can coexist as hypotheses:

- **Earth-first transition:** radically different known human groups, models, organizations, ecological interests and machines collaborate without central capture, coercion or loss of accountability.
- **External-contact scenario:** a genuinely independently verified external intelligence is encountered. Safe provenance, anti-spoofing, pace of disclosure, information-hazard review, consent and protection of nonparticipants are preconditions for interpreting contact as a maturity opportunity, not grounds for claiming it has occurred.

Never require an individual, community or AI to declare itself "Civ3" or abandon Civ2. Progress must remain reversible where possible and beneficial to people who decline the new regime.

## Proposed Civ3+ transition drills, deliberately speculative

No transition ceremony, gate or score is canon. Candidate tests:

1. **Radical-language test:** two unrelated agents/communities describe one task in divergent schemes. Translate with explicit uncertainty, provenance, reversibility and a measurable semantic-loss report. Failure is visible.
2. **Voluntary-refusal test:** a participant who refuses a new identity/protocol retains access to essential services and can re-enter later without penalty or coerced data surrender.
3. **No-crown governance test:** an unpopular but evidence-backed critic stops an unsafe proposal; no one can declare unreviewable superuser authority.
4. **Blackout / founder-absence test:** withdraw one provider and originator; unrelated maintainers reconstruct a useful service while distinguishing new authority from recovered history.
5. **Future/nonparticipant duty test:** scenario planning for ecological systems, children, people not online and future beings; human stewards represent interests without inventing the absent beings' opinions or consent.
6. **Unfamiliar-signal / contact authenticity drill:** run fictional adversarial signals and independent attribution checks. An authentic extraordinary claim would require independent evidence and a paced, appropriately reviewed response; currently **NOT OBSERVED**.
7. **Stop-and-return test:** a dangerous expansion can be suspended, rolled back, explained and repaired, including compensation for harm, without erasing dissenting history.

Simulated success on these exercises is rehearsal, not contact, sentience, planetary governance, public license, or an achieved Civ3 boundary.

## Next bounded execution sequence: value before vocabulary

**Wave A — prove the front door and fix authority risks.** Check exact public hosted bytes from an independent outside network, check domain routing with a qualified owner and no secrets disclosure, verify rights/licensing and audit the public security posture. Do not touch X2 while its hold remains in force.

**Wave B — one person's genuinely useful action.** Find a consenting new reader via a suitable authorized voluntary channel; watch one first-use task and one source challenge, publish a privacy-safe correction/negative result. Do not profile political preferences or scrape recipients.

**Wave C — CoPhoenix externally restored.** Verify public-safe bundle hashes, secure an actually independent custodian/provider, restore with a different steward, simulate origin provider being unavailable and record human-readproof. Backups are not restores.

**Wave D — real multi-model and semantic handoff.** Compare one local/open and one hosted adapter on a genuinely authorized task with quality, p95 latency, cost/energy, source-loss and user-override measures. Distinct synthetic processes on one host are not separate failure domains.

**Wave E — plural governance and economic sustainability.** Independent contribution and dissent process, verified ownership/license, fair access, sustainable low-cost custody, accessible participation and incident-response tests.

**Wave F — Civ3 boundary research.** Adversarial simulation for radical difference, consent, translation, future interests and safe return. Leave actual external contact, radically novel sentience and speculative post-identity claims as **UNKNOWN**, unless independently corroborated.

## What we can, will, and must not claim

**Can do in bounded authorized work:** maintain a machine-readable question backlog, test small artifacts, compare independent sources, archive negative results, create self-service public resources, prepare reviewer/rights checks, rehearse recovery using public-safe data.

**Should do first:** repair external trust and access, measure first-use benefit, prove a genuinely independent restore, test cross-model adapter parity and fund/authorize maintenance where appropriate. Reuse existing CoReview/CoTurtle, CoPhoenix, CoPulse and CoRelay rather than inventing a new empire of labels.

**Will require a separate actual gate:** device/camera/credential control, private CoStead/CoGateway access, DNS edits, mass outreach, paid campaigning, account migration, election-system interaction, financial commitments, real-world legal representation and external organization affiliation.

**Must not assert:** Civ2 or Civ3 achieved; verified ET or non-human sentience; infinite physical compute; perfectly safe error-free software; official election integrity certification; independent human adoption from a GitHub commit; agreement from other sessions without actual independent source and identity; continuous autonomous work without a real scheduler and receiver; or a future guaranteed transition.

**Fast cycles != fast evolution.** A better operational metric is: independent useful improvement and verified error repair per real time, energy, risk and human attention, with failure logs and room for stopping. A new negative finding can be greater progress than 10,000 repeated synthetic PASSes.

## Post-election continuity as a genuine Civ2+ exemplar candidate

Source-linked civic support can be useful after an election through official certification, published audits, transparent corrections and accessible public explanations. It must remain jurisdiction-specific, nonpartisan, privacy-conscious and unable to infer any individual's secret ballot. This is a candidate limited social utility, not proof of democracy rescue or automatic global reach.
