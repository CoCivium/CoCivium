## The central reader promise

**A parent and a child explore a changing world by questioning each other.** The child's **sex is never revealed** in this CoCivia/CoBook reader. No dialogue, narration, illustration, voice casting, marketing metadata or generated adaptation should insert an implied sex; a non-specific child and parental perspective are possible without depriving either character of individuality. The parent can be wrong; the child can be wise, mischievous, angry, bored, compassionate or unconvinced. Intellectual adventure arises from lived events, surprises and consequences rather than technical exposition.

The two gradually experience **Civ2+ as near-future history**, a deliberate *fictional/future-history narrative device*, not an assertion that a forecast has happened or that all of Civ2 is already here. **Civ3+** is a contested open horizon with no fixed date, mandatory phase transition or single answer. Younger generations inherit choices and exits, not the parent's debts or required identity.

An enduring motif: **we need one another as inversion benchmarks.** The child tests the parent's claims; the parent helps test the child's speculations; both are changed. The parent becoming a back-seat adviser is not authority transfer over an actual child or compulsory continuation of the parent's mission.

## The key progression: analogue ⇄ digital ⇄ hybrid

| Lens | What it contributes to the scene | What it must not imply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Analogue / embodied** | touch, mortality, local ecology, play, material limits, lived memory, inherited stories, face-to-face consent | That humans are unreliable relics or embodiment is obsolete |
| **Digital / represented** | archives, simulation, text, models, provenance, tools, distributed copies, remixing, machine collaboration | That a stored representation is identical to a lived person, a receiver or a sentient being |
| **Hybrid / relational** | people and diverse machines jointly interpreting context and acting within consent, portable identity/WorkID, physical feedback, cross-substrate recovery and multiple meaningful interfaces | A fused universal mind, automatic AI rights, loss of refusal, or verified Civ3 transition |

The arrow is **reversible, plural and overlapping**, not progress-through-erasure. Humans do not have to become digital; digital systems need not become human; hybrid institutions should remain intelligible to people who opt out.

**Candidate Civ3+ boundary:** safe cooperation among radically different intelligences, embodiments, institutions, languages and time horizons *without coercion, capture, unwarranted equivalence or abandoning outsiders*. Alternatives include Earth-first pluralism and hypothetical independently verified unfamiliar-intelligence contact; the latter is NOT observed or a reason to imply it has occurred. See [Civ2/Civ3 gap map](../Strategy/CIV2_CIV3_BOUNDARY_GAPMAP_R0.md).

## Every chapter is a story that optionally opens six doors

**Core story first:** an enjoyable scene that survives if all supplementary links break. Optional reader-selected routes, never mandatory screens:

1. **See:** one coherent illustration / vignette / short dialogue / plain-language highlight.
2. **Understand:** a gentle explanation of a real idea, counterexample and limits.
3. **Question:** child's strongest challenge and a second viewpoint; include a wrong or unresolved explanation.
4. **Explore:** accessible CoMap / scenario / CoGame experiment, with consent and rollback.
5. **Verify:** citations, exact source and claim type, historical snapshot, failure evidence and an inspectable challenge path.
6. **Help or depart:** voluntary contribution, local practical action, quiet exit or return to the fiction.

A chapter, interactive card, audio or video is a **projection of the same versioned scene/claim**, not a new governing source. No user must sign in, disclose identity, hear unrequested audio, share child data, or learn CoTerms to finish the story.

## Example of the first playable intellectual adventure

**Opening question:** “Who remembers a promise after the machine that made it disappears?”

The parent claims a copy is enough. The child asks whether the intended recipient received and understood it. They make an analogue paper record, a digital source and a third independent receiver test. One route loses part of the message, one retains it, and the receiver can reject it. The child's objection changes the outcome.

The reader may only enjoy the dialogue, or optionally:
- explore which parts survived;
- switch from a paper memory to a typed WorkID and then a consent-bound handoff;
- find the most revealing counterexample;
- read a real test receipt *separately marked as engineering evidence*.

**Three separate endings:** recover with independent proof; discover a mistaken success claim; or choose not to share and preserve the boundary. The story does not award more points for sharing data or agreeing with the author.

This one scene bridges memory, CC rights, CoTheoryAll uncertainty, analogue/digital/hybrid transition, CME as a *possible* shared sensemaking environment, and practical CoPre/CoLatch proof. It does not attempt to teach the entire ontology in one page.

## Editorial ethos and public participation

- **Nonreligious in governance:** wonder and G_dSide are welcome but no belief is demanded or certified. Nonpartisan and anti-coercive, not evasive about governance, power or political institutions.
- **Anti-capture:** no forced account, coerced invitation, dark patterns, private-family exposure, surveillance-based engagement or personality manipulation. Critique state/corporate/institutional capture with evidence and fairness, not dogma.
- **Child and vulnerable-person safeguards:** fictional child's unidentified sex preserved; identifiable minors, voices, family details, photographs and usage analytics require separate permission. Legends and harmful histories may be remembered through age-appropriate, opt-in **soft quarantine**, never obligatory fear as pedagogy. Care, exit, repair and hope matter more than shocking imagery.
- **Fiction/research/engineering distinctions:** every nontrivial nonfiction claim is labelled ESTABLISHED, OBSERVED, PROPOSED, SPECULATIVE/THEOLOGICAL or FICTIONAL. Imagined Civ2+/Civ3+ chronology never masquerades as reported news.
- **Living, not secretly rewritten:** each chapter has stable ID and reader-observed snapshot, visible change note, preserved predecessor, opt-in live mode, text-first accessible fallback and correction route.
- **Mutual support, not forced funnels:** Godspawn remains a satisfying novel when no visitor clicks through. CoTheoryAll remains independently falsifiable research. CC remains a voluntary constitutional/consent architecture. Games remain fun and self-contained. Participation follows value, not conversion.
