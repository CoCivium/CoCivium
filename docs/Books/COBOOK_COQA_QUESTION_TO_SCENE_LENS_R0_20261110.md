## One useful relation

A genuine question can turn into a moving story **when the answer encounters a worthy challenge**.

`QUESTION → ANSWER (attributed, optionally private) → INVERSION → CHARACTER CHOICE → CONSEQUENCE → READER QUESTION → LEARNING / CORRECTION`

The question itself may remain unresolved. An unresolved question is not a failed story. CoQ&A already has a candidate question/answer commons and versioned answer schema; this is one **optional creative projection** of that existing relation model, not CoQA R1, a competing database or a replacement for the book source.

## Capture-to-scene rule

1. **Ask one non-leading, voluntary question.** The reader or participant may pass, defer, correct the question, answer hypothetically, or keep the answer private. No divine, political or scientific premise is imposed.
2. **Keep source boundaries.** Preserve the exact consenting person's words separately from third-party generated text, assistant paraphrase, uncertain attribution and fictional speech. Assign a stable Q&A identity; retain observation time and original context. A chat excerpt is not consent to public publication.
3. **Extract a tension, not a doctrine.** A fear collides with a value; a cherished answer fails one counterexample; two people love each other but want different futures. Admit uncertainty where it matters.
4. **Find the child's independent inversion.** Child sex remains undisclosed. The child may refuse, disagree, misunderstand, laugh, walk away, or ask a stronger question. Do not make the child recite CoTerms or inherit the parent's life purpose.
5. **Write one original scene** with physical setting, specific stakes, an action, a change of direction, and an ending that preserves optionality. No detached theology lecture or synthetic tragedy merely to maximize emotional reaction.
6. **Offer an optional evidence lens.** Classify each underlying claim: fiction, source-submitted belief, empirical evidence, proposal, unanswered metaphysics. An analogue prop, digital record and hybrid receiver can illustrate real mechanisms but cannot verify a fictional event.
7. **Verify before release.** Source attribution, privacy, identity-neutral character wording, permission, rights, uncoached reading, accessibility, comprehension, reader exit, emotional effects and versioned correction must pass separately.

## Guardrails against an attractive failure

- **G_dSide, ETs, revelation, damnation, afterlife and alleged external rules** are characters' concerns, metaphors or speculative questions unless independently supported. A novel is not evidence of their existence or instructions.
- **Never use death, self-sacrifice or vanishing to gain privileged knowledge or deserve love.** In this kind of scene, the person deciding to stay, seek help, repair harm or make room for another person's future is consequential. No child is responsible for keeping an adult alive.
- **Faith, skepticism and refusal deserve live options.** Asking a question never authorizes a claimed deity, parent, model or institution to answer on someone else's behalf.
- **The shared mouse is not magical provenance.** An actual utterance has an author/actor, a gesture has a device and consent scope, a model draft is marked as a model draft. Literary ambiguity must not erase real provenance.
- **No covert capture.** No recording minors, personal beliefs, family vulnerabilities or traumatic history into public training/marketing assets solely because a response could make powerful prose.
- **Do not optimize for tears, time-on-page or conversion.** Optimize for story credibility, independent character agency, voluntary return, transferable understanding and honest evidence separation.
- **No invented universal sync or audience proof.** A readable candidate is not a deployed book or independent reader acceptance.

## Repeatable scene identity, not endless scene generation

A candidate scene may carry `qa_id`, `scene_id`, `source_visibility`, `answer_authorization`, `adaptation_provenance`, `dramatic_inversion`, `child_agency_test`, `epistemic_labels`, `reader_feedback_ref`, `rights_review`, `revision`, `nonclaims`. Source fields with unverified permission stay `PRIVATE_OR_HELD`, not published verbatim.

A positive test requires an uncoached reader to state **what the parent believed, what the child challenged, what materially changed, and whether each could refuse**. The scene must also work if the reader chooses not to answer a personal question.

Run one meaningful scene experiment at a time; return feedback to the existing CoBook and CoQA lineages. No new Seed, forced heartbeat, secret unlock or background questioning.

## Miniature illustrative inversion (original fiction, not submitted testimony)

> Child: “If you had every answer, would you give them to me?”
>
> Parent: “I used to think that was what love required.”
>
> Child: “And now?”
>
> Parent: “Now I'm afraid I'd leave you nowhere to stand.”
>
> Child: “I didn't ask for the whole staircase. I asked whether you'd stay while I built a step.”
>
> Parent: “Yes. Even if you build in another direction.”
>
> Child: “Then maybe that's the answer I needed.”
>
> They left the mouse between them. No one clicked.

The exact private words that inspired any future scene must **not** be pasted into a publicly readable GitHub draft merely because an AI adapted them in chat. A publicly posted scene may be an original, reviewed and consent-cleared fictional derivative with the source held privately.
