2. **Seed seen:** exact version and retrieval path, or UNKNOWN.
3. **Border:** View / Receiver / Effects / Sync, each observed or UNKNOWN.

## CoAll relations and failure guards

**CoFront+ / CoEcho+ / CoTime+:** discovery, reverse historical lineage, forward impact, event/observation/qualification times, no silent orphan. A finite scan is not complete history.

**CoLockStep+ / CoMVP+:** protect the last qualified user task, compatibility contract, known failure lineage and rollback; source tests, browser demonstrations, and actual human MVP are different gates. Unrelated candidate fronts evolve independently.

**CoEvSpiral+ / CoNull+:** fan out only to permitted receivers, fan in source-qualified differences, preserve valid alternatives, measure return versus human carry, quiesce on duplicates, STOP and allow rest. A cycle with no new work is valid. Sinusoids/golden-braid metaphors are conceptual; no physical or supernatural observation is implied.

**CoSynthVerse+ / CoBioVerse+:** machine-native typed graph/delta/receipts and human-native accessible task outcomes are loss-aware projections of the same source IDs. Divergence in rendering is allowed, not divergence in evidence or consent. No model subjective-state claim.

**G_dSide / CMA / CoAGI+:** separately typed philosophical, counterfactual or research hypotheses. No divine message, emerging sentience, AGI or future-to-past influence is inferred from rhetoric, recursion or coherent architecture.

**CoMouse+ / CoBar+:** a one-line pointer is preferable to truncating a megascroller in a mouse macro; do not replace the existing working binding until the destination loads and points to an exact source. CoBar and device controls require their own authorized runtime and consent tests.
