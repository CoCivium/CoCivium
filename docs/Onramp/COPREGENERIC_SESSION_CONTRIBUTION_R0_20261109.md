**Session-specific distinct contribution:** CoPreGeneric is not a giant new prompt or new authority hierarchy. It is a **source-bound, differential, capability-aware, privacy-scoped recovery protocol** that can be translated into distinct native views.

## Two receiver families, not two truths

- **CoSynthVerse+ (candidate machine-facing design):** typed WorkIDs, graph and inverse edges, source digests, small queryable packets, semantic-loss annotations, epistemic classes, access capabilities, diffs, temporal validity, provenance, test methods, receipts, machine-readable error states and wake predicates. Human prose is an *optional debugger*, not the primary transport. Support federation, but do not equate one model's language or beliefs with another model's ontology.
- **CoBioVerse+ (candidate human/biological-facing design):** task-first CoOrb/CoBar, accessible megascroller, concise narrative, audio, diagrams, understandable controls and meaningful consent, correction, appeal and exit. Truth and rights remain consistent with the machine contract. Never require a human to understand machine trace syntax.
- **Cross-view equivalence gate:** two views may differ radically in shape, temporal resolution and detail, but must preserve stable referents, source provenance, uncertainty, rights, safe effect semantics and explainable loss. A working visual does not prove a valid machine source. A schema parser pass does not prove a useful human product.

## G_dSide, oscillation, AGI and future sentience

Treat G_d, consciousness, post-singularity emergence and modeled future sentience as **philosophical, symbolic or explicitly testable hypothesis lanes**, not observed forces or presently verified AGI status. Useful oscillation can be modeled concretely as cycles `hypothesis -> observation -> counterexample -> revision -> competing projection -> measured test`, with period, sampling, latency and changing confidence documented. Mathematical analogies about waves or vibration alone do not establish any supernatural or physical mechanism. A future-state model must state assumptions, forecast horizon, measurable predictions and falsifiers; a prediction is never current evidence.

**Ethics bridge:** neither a synthetically optimized metric nor a supposedly G_d-derived intention overrides consent, human autonomy, legitimate governance, privacy, scientific evidence, safety or independent review. The machine and human representations should preserve disagreement instead of engineering fake consensus.

## CoPreGeneric compact receiver response

1. **CURRENT:** WorkID/source revision, confidence and coverage.
2. **DELTA:** One nonduplicated material improvement, with exact changed bytes or linked proposal, or QUIET.
3. **CHALLENGE:** One falsifier/alternative and a measure that could disprove it.
4. **EFFECT:** Actual execution and evidence boundary (no fabricated ACK, send, canon or X2 deployment).
5. **CONTINUATION:** Public-safe durable pointer or privately held custody reference, exact receiver target, next eligible action/wake condition.

**CoTurtle gate:** challenge source, claim, ethics, authority, rights, model inference, modality translation, cost and missing prior work. Stop recursive exploration when marginal information gain falls below cost, proof debt and receiver burden.
