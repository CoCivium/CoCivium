## One small falsifiable demonstration

Take an original abstract motif: three short events; a pause; three short events with one controlled change. Encode it in text, diagram and consented quiet audio. Optionally add a standards-checked tactile representation.

Provide two independent readers with the rule, and a third reader without it. Ask each to reconstruct:
1. the number of events and pauses,
2. which event changed,
3. the intended relation (repetition-with-variation),
4. which interpretations they inferred but cannot actually justify.

Include scrambled motifs and misleading similarities as negative controls. Score **structure recovery**, **semantic agreement** and **false analogy** separately.

The result might be that structural patterns transfer reliably while meanings do not. That would be a better, more honest result than claiming universal communication.

## The speculative branch, carefully marked

An ET-oriented analogy asks whether sufficiently unfamiliar intelligences could detect some artificial structure. Nothing above is evidence of extraterrestrial intelligence, and no RF transmitter is proposed. A signal that resembles a pattern does not prove an intentional sender.

G_dSide is a philosophical and theological reflection on listening, humility and self-reference. It is not a measurable communication endpoint, empirical explanation, or authority that overrides independent tests.
