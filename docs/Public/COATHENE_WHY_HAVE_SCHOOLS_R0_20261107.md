# Why have schools at all? | CoAthene+ founding-question R0

**PUBLIC R&D CANDIDATE · 2026-11-07 · NOT A SCHOOL, ADMISSIONS PROCESS, ACCREDITED CURRICULUM, OR OPERATING SERVICE.**

## Start before a syllabus

**What is education for?** A good answer must include more than tests, jobs or certificates. It must include children growing safely, deep understanding, human relationships, creativity, practical capability, civic judgment, and the freedom to learn and change over a lifetime.

**Why schools, rather than apps?** A school can provide trained teachers, responsible supervision, peers, social services, belonging, accessible places, specialist knowledge, mentoring, protection and community. None of those can be presumed supplied by an AI chat box. An optional tool or visiting lab can support a legitimate institution without impersonating one.

**Who should participate?** Education should be accessible throughout life, including people who need different modes, more time, lower connectivity, non-headset alternatives and support. For children, legal schooling obligations, safeguarding and legitimate family/school oversight remain binding. No person must join a fictional House, accept a ranking, buy hardware or adopt a philosophy to access core learning.

**What do we teach first?** Begin from day one with observing, questioning, learning, testing, correcting and cooperating, **alongside** literacy, mathematics, science, history, arts, culture and appropriate subject depth. Neither thinking techniques nor domain facts can replace the other. Work in the language the learner understands; private jargon is not a prerequisite.

**Who pays?** Publicly funded schooling is a public responsibility. Proposed supplements might combine properly licensed open learning materials, grants, community support, funded public institution contracts, optional paid professional experiences and transparent cross-subsidy for free access. A financial sponsor must not acquire authority over student data, truthful content, assessment or other institutions. An open lesson is not a promise of unlimited free teachers, hosted compute or personal services.

**How long can people return?** The aspiration is lifelong re-entry, not a never-ending account or a forever-retained childhood record. The useful output is transferable learning, original creations, portable references and the ability to change providers. Specific classes, mentors and hosted tools have finite budgets, retention rules and terms.

**Who decides?** Real educators, institutions, families, learners and public authorities have distinct responsibilities and legitimate powers. AI assistance does not confer teaching certification, custody, policy authority or learner safety oversight. Learners should be able to challenge instructions and hear alternative evidence.

## What might be distinctive about CoAthene?

The proposed contribution is an **integrated pathway from a learner's own question to reliable knowledge, a reason to challenge it, a grounded test, an improved explanation, and a transferable result**, with rights and recoverability throughout.

CoAthene's provisional names for this include **CoWays+** (optional ways of inquiry), **CoMoves+** (things to try), **CoPraxis+** (responsible practice), and **CoScendence+** (growth across contexts while retaining the ability to be corrected). None names a verified universal primitive, cognitive rank or prerequisite belief. Optional **CoClans+ / Houses** could make collaboration playful without sorting children into permanent types.

Example: *Can two things look like mirrors and still behave differently?* In particle physics, parity can be violated in weak interactions. In a distinct software analogy, mirroring an icon does **not** reverse the holder's permissions. The transferable habit is **test the assumed symmetry**; the physical mechanism emphatically does **not** transfer. A useful lesson must teach the actual domain, not declare victory because an analogy sounds clever.

This is not an assertion that today's schools do not teach inquiry or agency. They do, often impressively. CoAthene should be judged against existing practice and a neutral explanation for subject accuracy, comprehension, accessibility, independent transfer, child well-being, honest correction and actual cost. **That evaluation has not yet been completed.**

## First credible demonstration to seek

One consenting independent adult educator compares a CoAthene original lesson, a good neutral lesson and an established approach on the **same meaningful subject question**. Measure accuracy, ability to challenge a misleading analogy, genuine transfer, learner agency and accessibility. Publish favorable and unfavorable findings only with proper consent and exact outcome evidence. An adult-reviewed prototype must not be described as an approved child curriculum.

This is an open research invitation to evaluate **an idea**, not an invitation to enrol children or an announcement that a school or program exists.
