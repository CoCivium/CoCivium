## Core public doorway

**CoTheoryAll: A Map of the Questions We're Exploring**

Public aspiration:

> **Upgrade the world. Keep the rollback.**

Interpretation: iterative, evidence-seeking improvement with revision and rollback. Not a claim that CoCivium or CoTheoryAll already knows the correct final form of society, science, or reality.

## Why this might matter

Modern knowledge is highly specialized, but the relations among explanations are often informal, lossy, stale, or invisible.

CoTheoryAll is a candidate research and representation framework for keeping competing models, assumptions, evidence, contradictions, predictions, failures, revisions, observers, scales, and unknowns explicitly related without forcing premature unification.

## What it is not

- not a validated theory of everything;
- not established physics;
- not proof of cosmic sentience, telepathy, FTL signalling, or nonmaterial substrates;
- not proof that all theories are equally credible;
- not a governance, financial, clinical, or security authority;
- not canon.

## One-sentence press description

CoCivium is developing an open, provenance-aware framework for mapping competing explanations and evolving complex systems while preserving uncertainty, challenge, rollback, and public inspectability.
