### The story in one minute

If an AI assists someone for months, what happens to the work when that service changes or disappears? Can a second agent recover the *task*, not merely a transcript, without inventing facts, exceeding permissions, or sending its human operator back to the beginning?

CoCivium is one open-source research attempt to make that possible. Its proposed **CCC** describes the risk of Core Controlled Coercion: dependence created when one platform controls memory, tools, identity, distribution, permissions and practical exit. Its proposed **CoEBE** describes a changing Core/Edge balance with portable state and independent checks. These are **risk models and goals, not proof of a vendor conspiracy or completed federation**.

The journalism is a test of an uncomfortable question: can federation preserve agency and useful work better than convenient centralization, or merely make the system harder to operate?

### How a newsroom could use this proposal

**At the pitch desk:** Choose a question you can independently test. The main one is whether a user can switch AI providers without losing the operative *task*, not just their chat log. The four articles are possible installments, not a fixed order or a request to publish four stories.

**Before assigning a reporter:** Inspect the public code and identify a qualified outside user, a competitor or open-standards expert, and one critic of CoCivium's approach. Check who currently controls project repositories, trademarks, domains and hosting. Do not assume a GPL license prevents service-level capture.

**Before publishing:** Confirm a real demonstration in the stated environment, compare it with a simpler alternative, obtain permission for any human example, and independently verify numbers, quotations and technical effects. A passing synthetic test cannot be described as a fully functioning product or independent receiver proof. The reporter should determine whether the correct conclusion is favorable, mixed or negative.

**After publication:** Corrections should identify an exact claim and source version, preserve the historical article, and explain the substantive change. A reply from CoCivium is an attributed source response, not a newsroom-approved correction. A reporter may decline additional contact; no automatic follow-up or default exclusivity is requested.

### The four installments

| Article | Editorial opening | The reporting test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. The AI Future Nobody Should Own** | The right to leave an AI service may be more consequential than its subscription price | Measure real switching costs and compare established open standards |
| **2. The Memory That Survives Its Maker** | Exporting chat history is not the same thing as resuming a task | Have an independent agent recover a bounded task and its STOP rule |
| **3. A Republic of Different Minds** | Human experts, different models and deterministic verifiers can contribute without sharing identity or authority | Test disagreement, usability and costs against a simpler single-agent baseline |
| **4. The Frontier Between Wonder and Evidence** | Ambitious AI projects also attract claims about awareness, communication and prediction | Require consent, operational definitions, blind controls, negative cases and independent replication |

### Why a reporter might investigate

An inspectable public codebase and source-tested claims make it possible to distinguish ambition from achievement. The [CoCivium repository](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoCivium), [contribution guidance](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoCivium/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md), and [truth-gate browser example](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoCivium/tree/main/examples/codemow) are public. [CoPre research PR #401](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoCivium/pull/401) and [narrower MVP candidate #406](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoCivium/pull/406) have code and CI tests. [Issue #403](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoCivium/issues/403) explicitly holds first-use MVP acceptance without an independent receiver.

Other open-source agent interoperability efforts already exist, including [A2A](https://a2a-protocol.org/) and the [Linux Foundation Agentic AI Foundation](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/agentic-ai-foundation-announces-four-new-projects-agent-skills-open-responses-openenv-and-opensandbox). A reporter should ask exactly what CoCivium adds to, contradicts or duplicates in these efforts.

**No independent product adoption, live multi-provider service, noncapture governance, editor interest, or other extraordinary abilities are claimed by this treatment.**

### Scenes to seek, not invent

One consenting outside reader attempts a five-minute first-use task. A second worker tries to continue after the original session and primary store are unavailable. A reviewer finds a failing test, then checks whether the criticism actually changes the next source version. A governance specialist checks who can revoke access or change rules. For speculative CoTele or consciousness ideas, an independent scientist asks for a preregistered protocol before discussing results.

No scenes, interviews, demonstrations or quoted replies are represented as already having occurred.

### Why journalism, not marketing?

A serious journalist may conclude the project is too immature, too complex, insufficiently distinctive, or technically wrong. Those are valid possible outcomes. The reported facts, interviews, headline, article order and publication timing belong to the journalist. A contributor can invite an examination without buying a favorable conclusion.

A single human introduction should link to **this treatment** first, not dump the whole repository on a stranger. Full sample articles and a separate machine research pack can be offered only as optional depth. Do not assume an exclusive, automatically send further pitches, or infer interest from a mailbox acknowledgement.
