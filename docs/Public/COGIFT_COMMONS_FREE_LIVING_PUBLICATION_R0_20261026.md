# CoGiftCommons+ R0 | Free access, voluntary support, and living-publication trust

**26 October 2026 · PUBLIC-SAFE DRAFT ONLY · NOT A LIVE DONATION SERVICE, RELEASE, ENDORSEMENT, MASS EMAIL CAMPAIGN, OR AUTOMATED EDITOR**

## One-sentence public promise

**Read freely. Challenge openly. Improve together. Give voluntarily. Leave freely.**

This is a thin ethical offer layered over existing [CoBook+](../Architecture/COBOOK_LIVING_RICH_MEDIA_SYSTEM_R0_20261025.md), [CoPublication Federation](../Architecture/COPUBLICATION_FEDERATION_RECEIVER_INTEREST_R0_20261025.md), [CoPublication Metabolism](../Architecture/COPUBLICATION_METABOLISM_R0_20261025.md), [CoParticipationEcology](../Architecture/COPARTICIPATION_ECOLOGY_R0.md), [CoOutreach](../Architecture/COOUTREACH_INFLUENCE_FABRIC_R0_20261025.md), and [Public Prelaunch](../Launch/COCIVIUM_PUBLIC_PRELAUNCH_R0.md). It does not create a competing publication root or authority system.

## Free, by default

- Free public reading, public research questions, correction submission, source inspection and public-safe downloadable educational material where rights and capacity allow.
- Open-source software and remix rights **only** for assets bearing an actual compatible license. A public link alone is not permission to fork proprietary or contributed material.
- No payment, donation or recruitment required to access free material, submit a criticism, dispute a claim, opt out or leave.
- Finite infrastructure, private data, hosted compute, physical goods and professional services may carry real costs and published constraints. Never claim unlimited compute or perpetual zero-cost service without funding/proof.
- No preferential scientific, editorial, governance, search or queue status in exchange for money, fame, referrals, spiritual identity or personal access.

## Voluntary support for partners and helpers

Donations **may be proposed**, but do not enable collection without identifying a lawful recipient, ownership, payment provider, privacy terms and accounting responsibilities.

- Prefer direct, clearly identified recipient links when a partner/helper has consented to be listed and has independently verified payment details; distinguish third-party support from CoCivium income.
- If CoCivium collects and redistributes funds, publish the recipient, jurisdiction, fees, allocation policy, reconciliation period, conflict-of-interest controls, consent and a way to correct errors **before** enabling payments. No claimed charitable-tax status without verification.
- No implied endorsement, co-ownership or obligation when someone contributes, receives support, appears in a contributor list, or is invited to review.
- Support is voluntary, nonexclusive and never buys authority over conclusions, researchers, contributors, volunteers or recipients.
- Display a zero-dollar path beside any donation path, not behind it.

## Living publications with a reliable past

Publication evolves through **consented feedback → qualified candidate change → factual/rights/privacy review → versioned successor → diff and archive**. Source identity, citations, disputes, falsifiers, authorial attribution and the text a reader actually saw remain recoverable.

A reader interaction alone is not editorial authorization. Source evidence must not be silently strengthened by popularity, contributor status or generated agreement. Corrections should be easy; retractions and disagreement must remain visible. Publish machine-readable twins only with source and rights boundaries.

## Community and recognition

- Voluntary question, critique, accessibility fix, translation, experiment, issue report, alternative explanation, demonstration or remix.
- Attribution is opt-in, editable and withdrawable where feasible; a pseudonym or anonymous contribution route may be offered where safe.
- A public contributor profile is **not** consent to receive marketing, permit training on private materials, accept an affiliation, donate or join a board.
- Fame, leaderboards, badges or credits should reward demonstrable useful contributions, not engagement farming; do not imply independent verification.
- Protect people from harassment, impersonation, spam and inadvertent sensitive-data exposure. Review before publication.

## Spiritual, G_d and nonmaterial perspectives

May be available as an **explicitly self-selected ethical, philosophical, cultural, literary or testable-hypothesis lens**. The benevolent motive is not hidden: helping people and expanding choices should be stated plainly. No claimed divine endorsement, privileged revelation, supernatural effect or religious profiling. All readers, including nonbelievers, can participate without adopting a worldview.

## A credible academic first wave

A possible future pool of **up to 1,000 scholars** is a research *candidate registry*, not 1,000 messages, permissions, partnerships or endorsements.

Start with a small, individually justified, nonspam batch only after stable public evidence links exist. Each invitation should carry the sender's real identity, one concrete relevant question, sources, uncertainty, voluntary response, and a respectful no-contact path. Dedupe and honor opt-outs. Keep actual `DRAFT`, `SENT`, `DELIVERED`, `ACKNOWLEDGED`, `UNDERSTOOD`, `CHALLENGED`, `CONTRIBUTED` states separate. Do not claim mass academic acceptance from a contact list.

## Public first-use test

One invited, consenting newcomer can:

1. understand one offer in under 90 seconds;
2. open a free item without a required account, donation or religious pledge;
3. inspect its version, evidence, rights and known limitations;
4. submit a bounded critique or choose to do nothing;
5. return later and see what changed without losing the prior version;
6. leave or export permitted data without retaliation, manipulation or artificial scarcity.

Track first-use success, comprehension, accessibility, actual corrections, recipient-observed benefit, privacy cost, operating cost and unsuccessful or declined interactions. **A favorable GitHub review or CI test alone is not first-use proof.**

## Next bounded implementation

Test it with a consenting person. Keep the current publication and donation surfaces unchanged until their real provider settings, rights and accounting are verified.
