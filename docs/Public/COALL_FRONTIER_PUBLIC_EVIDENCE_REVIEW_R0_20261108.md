# CoAll+ / CiAll+ Frontier: Public Evidence Review R0

**As-of:** 2026-11-08 UTC  
**Classification:** PUBLIC WORKING RESEARCH REVIEW; NOT SCIENTIFIC CONFIRMATION, ORGANIZATIONAL POLICY, CANON, RUNTIME PROOF, OR A PUBLIC SAFETY ADVISORY  
**Scope:** Publicly checkable evidence and candidate research methods across AI-assisted discovery, collective intelligence, security, interoperability, SETI, unknown-intelligence screening, and relational measurement.  
**Change policy:** Source-bound, revisable, and correction-friendly. Publication time and event time are not interchangeable.

## At a glance

The strongest externally grounded signals in this review are measured AI-assisted R&D acceleration, AI-produced formal mathematics, expanded autonomous laboratory orchestration, and serious failures at software supply-chain and agent-authorization boundaries. The strongest scientific qualification is equally important: **none of these establishes unrestricted artificial general intelligence or extraterrestrial intelligence.**

This is a public synthesis of previously reported developments, not an assertion that eight separate emergencies happened today. Project-specific alert delivery or acknowledgment is intentionally *not* publicized; a public document is not a receiver acknowledgment.

## 2. Additional measured capability and policy frontiers

**Autonomous physical laboratories.** The peer-reviewed *Nature* paper on **AutoDNA** (Nov 4, 2026) reports orchestration of over 20 classes of instruments across more than 11,000 experimental steps, a 3.2-fold improvement in concurrent protocol execution under the studied conditions, and several nucleic-acid workflows. Humans still maintain hardware and replenish materials. This is significant *bounded physical orchestration*, not demonstrated general independent scientific discovery. [Primary publication](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11228-6).

### AutoDNA: testable transfer to CoFactory+ and CoOps+

**Primary evidence (2026-11-04):** Wu et al., *Nature*, [doi:10.1038/s41586-026-11228-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-11228-6), report coordination of more than 20 instrument types and over 11,000 laboratory steps, with 3.2-fold faster concurrent execution than the studied sequential comparator. The [author-provided code](https://github.com/IPADS-X/AutoDNA) and [versioned study data](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20624108) are inspectable. Human maintenance and replenishment were still necessary. The published figure's concurrent/sequential comparison does not, by itself, identify how much benefit is *specifically caused by AI-agent reasoning* rather than scheduling, instrumentation or engineered parallelism.

**Counterhypotheses:** fixed protocol engineering, hardware concurrency, task selection, common-source measurements, and strong infrastructure support may explain some or most reported gains. Independent cross-site replication, long unattended survivorship, source-to-independent-receiver results, and broad portability are not established by publication or released code.

**Bounded non-executed discriminators (rank by independent information gain, not drama):**
1. **Matched, safe task baselines:** compare agent orchestration with ordinary schedulers and trained operators under equal equipment, time, resource, quality and rights constraints. Report quality-adjusted successful output and cases where the simpler baseline wins.
2. **Independent reproduction:** use an unaffiliated observer and approved nonclinical test fixture; record which models, code, datasets, instruments and calibration sources are shared.
3. **Failure and replay:** in an isolated authorized test, evaluate interruption-before/after-commit, stale actuator fencing, duplicate physical action prevention, observable recovery and human touches.
4. **Portable interfaces:** substitute one supported device/provider while holding task and acceptance criteria constant; inspect least-privilege permission, safe refusal and exact output readback.
5. **Alternative analysis:** blinded re-scoring and a distinct data pipeline, retaining null, contradictory and failed outcomes instead of only successes.
6. **Whole-system costs:** measure verified usable outcome per unit time, energy, money, materials, maintenance and operator effort, rather than extrapolating one throughput factor.

**Candidate evidence packet, not a new global ontology:** `work_id | source_doi | source_revision | task_id | permitted_scope | instrument_versions | model_versions | baseline | event_time | observation_time | calibration | counterhypotheses | outcome | resources | failure_replay | evidence_hash | independent_receiver_readproof | nonclaims | next_wake`. Unknown fields stay UNKNOWN. A receiver must verify the *exact effect* independently before promotion.

**Next acceptance gate:** demonstrate one *authorized* intent-to-physical-result workflow with failure/restart and external readproof, using pre-existing CoAll WorkID and custody contracts. This is proposed research transfer only; **no CoCivium lab integration, actual experiment, deployed agent, independent reproduction, or authorized biological operation is claimed**.

**China and global agent interoperability.** At the Nov 4 Wuzhen forum, publicly documented initiatives addressed mutual trust and interoperability of AI agents. The Hong Kong government's [official November 4 account](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202611/04/P2026110400624.htm) confirms the forum and official participation. Proposed standards, trust mechanisms, and cooperation are **not** proof of adopted technical standards or independently reproduced cross-provider authorization, revocation, and recovery.

**Candidate CoAll discriminator:** Reproduce an independently witnessed transaction across two unrelated systems using portable actor identity, least-privilege consent, revocation, source provenance, failure recovery, and exact receiver readback. Reject evidence that only replays one provider's own test fixture.

## 3. First Contact Watch: three stages must remain separate

1. **Ordinary observation or investigative candidate.** A source- and instrument-bound residual merits investigation. It may be interference, calibration artifact, known natural behavior, unknown natural behavior, human technology, selection bias, or something else.
2. **Independently corroborated credible technosignature.** Multiple suitably independent observations and analyses survive serious terrestrial, instrumental, and natural alternatives; the scientific interpretation remains qualified.
3. **Confirmed extraterrestrial intelligence (ETI).** Responsible discovery investigators and independent reviewers reach their stated scientific verification standard and follow the applicable reporting process. This is a distinct notification event, not automatic promotion from stage 1 or 2.

**Observed recent result:** On **Nov 3**, the [SETI Institute](https://www.seti.org/news/when-should-we-listen-for-alien-signals) reported approximately **17 hours of pilot anti-solar-point observations** with **no detected technosignature**. An interesting search strategy is not evidence that an alien communicator chose the same target.

**Governance boundary:** The International Academy of Astronautics' [June 2026 updated principles](https://www.seti.org/research/seti-101/protocols-for-an-eti-signal-detection/) emphasize verification, open reporting after confirmation, and international consultation before any reply. This report does not authorize transmission or contact.

**Current public-review state:** NO VERIFIED ETI DETECTION; NO CORROBORATED CONTACT; NO FIRST FULL CONTACT CLAIM.

## 4. Unknown / independent intelligence: classify observation before origin

A working preclassification path is:

`OBSERVATION -> RESIDUAL -> ANOMALY -> STRUCTURE -> AGENCY? -> TECHNOLOGY? -> INDEPENDENT_ORIGIN? -> EXTRATERRESTRIAL? -> CONTACT?`

Every arrow is a question with a defeasible test, not a promotion by rhetorical confidence. Retain *instrument/analysis artifact*, *interference*, *known natural*, *unknown natural*, *human technology*, *selection/base-rate effect*, and other serious hypotheses as independent competitors. Nonhuman does not imply extraterrestrial; a technological artifact does not imply an extant technologist or a communicative act.

## 5. CoET+ / CoFFF+ / CoPlanck+: proposed relational evidence grammar

These are **research/design constructs**, not discoveries, measured Planck-scale phenomena, scientific standards, or claims of machine or extraterrestrial sentience.

- **CoET+ candidate object:** source, observation and publication time, instrument, frame, evidence class, currentness, independent provenance, alternatives, contradictions, predictions, disconfirmers, and a bounded option set. Typed edges may be `SUPPORTS`, `CONTRADICTS`, `PREDICTS`, `EXPLAINS`, `INDEPENDENTLY_REPLICATES`, or `SHARES_SOURCE_WITH`. Apparent cross-modality does not establish independence when pipelines or calibration are shared.
- **CoFFF+ (First Full Contact+):** a *potentially distributed relational process*, not the first signal. Compare endpoint identity, directionality, persistence, response contingent on prior input, challenge/response, correction, shared reference, semantic transfer, latency, independent origin, and source multiplicity. Unknown dimensions remain UNKNOWN. Shared reference or one apparent response is not confirmed contact or proof of sentience.
- **CoPlanck+ shared invariants:** a candidate name for independently recoverable relations across observers and representations, **not a claim about direct Planck-scale measurements**. Test whether observer A and observer B recover the same externally constrained invariant under alternate encodings, sensors, coordinate systems, models, and corrections. Common training data or calibration can explain superficial agreement. An invariant is not communication.

**Follow-up option families:** independent replication, alternative analysis pipelines, archival search, prospective predictions, interference exclusion, spatial or temporal localization, natural-baseline modeling, false-positive review, sister-signature search, and HOLD/RETIRE when evidence weakens. Score by *information gain, independence gain, cost, latency, reversibility, contamination risk,* and *ability to distinguish competing hypotheses*. Generating options does not authorize external execution.

## 6. CoMeasure+ / CoMetrology+: proposed common measurement contract

Meaningful cross-domain evaluation requires explicit **quantity**, **unit**, **method**, **uncertainty**, **reference frame**, **timestamp**, **source**, **calibration**, **confidence**, **authority**, **negative observations**, and **noncomparability**.

- Physical quantities should reuse established standards and traceability where applicable, rather than inventing new absolute physical units.
- Computation and information must distinguish bytes, bits, entropy, effective information, provenance, and practical task outcomes.
- Social and operational metrics such as trust, task fidelity, safety, welfare, reversibility, reliability, recovery and human effort are often *constructs* or *proxies*, not universally convertible physical quantities.
- `CoNull+` can encode *zero observed*, *missing*, *not applicable*, *below detection*, *contradicted*, and *untested* as different states.
- Derived graphs may connect measurements through exact conversion, experimental calibration, causal model, correlation or speculative analogy. Only some relations permit arithmetic conversion. Being related does not imply being commensurable.

**Proposed verification test:** two independent receivers recover the same source-bound metric definition and reproduce a conversion or decision, while invalid conversions, hidden missing data, shared-pipeline dependence and false success receipts fail closed.

## 7. Public reception, correction and release-state rules

This document records a public research synthesis. It does **not** imply independent CoCivium media coverage, institutional endorsement, a deployed CoAll runtime, an approved scientific theory, a verified cross-session fan-out, or a confirmed discovery. Links and figures are time-bounded snapshots; later evidence can supersede them without retroactively making the earlier observation fabricated.

Correction requests should identify the exact claim, source URL, observed date, competing evidence and proposed replacement. Corrections and null results are welcome; sensational claims without primary evidence are not promoted.

**Public research principles:** observation != interpretation; correlation != causation; source availability != recipient adoption; model agreement != independent evidence; scientific candidate != scientific confirmation; publication != delivery or acknowledgment; human-supervised automation != unrestricted agency.
