## Projection contract

A projection MAY vary:

- opening hook;
- terminology density;
- examples;
- emphasized relations;
- call-to-action;
- length;
- technical depth;
- citation density.

A projection MUST NOT silently vary:

- project status;
- evidence strength;
- nonclaims;
- provenance;
- authority;
- whether contact/read/ack/adoption is proven;
- licensing facts;
- currentness state.

## Receiver projections

### Agentics / multi-agent systems

Emphasize:

- coordination across agents and sessions;
- bounded autonomy;
- handoff/currentness;
- machine-readable provenance;
- relation-of-relations;
- dedupe and successor semantics.

Compact hook:

> CoCivium is testing a provenance-aware relational fabric for multi-agent coordination, bounded authority, currentness, and challengeable evolving objects across providers and sessions.

### AI safety / governance

Emphasize:

- explicit authority;
- challengeability;
- nonclaims;
- provenance;
- observable state transitions;
- separation of sent/read/accepted/adopted/executed.

Compact hook:

> CoCivium explores governance primitives where authority, evidence, currentness and adoption stay distinct, inspectable, and challengeable instead of collapsing into one opaque agent state.

### Local-first / offline-first

Emphasize:

- provider independence;
- local custody;
- recoverable state;
- session disposability;
- portable relational objects;
- graceful degradation.

Compact hook:

> CoCivium explores relational continuity that survives provider/session loss by keeping identity, provenance and currentness portable rather than trapping them inside one app.

### Knowledge graph / semantic web

Emphasize:

- typed relations;
- relation-of-relations;
- temporal/currentness semantics;
- provenance;
- supersession;
- observer-relative rendering.

Compact hook:

> CoCivium treats relations as versioned, evidence-bearing objects that can themselves be related, challenged, superseded and rendered differently without deleting lineage.

### HCI / human-AI interaction

Emphasize:

- observer-first surfaces;
- progressive disclosure;
- explainable currentness;
- human/machine twins;
- low-friction participation;
- explicit uncertainty.

Compact hook:

> CoCivium experiments with interfaces where humans and AI systems see different useful projections of one shared provenance spine, with changes and uncertainty made visible.

### Civic tech / digital governance

Emphasize:

- consent;
- distributed participation;
- challenge paths;
- transparent authority;
- non-coercive coordination;
- public inspectability.

Compact hook:

> CoCivium is testing public, challengeable coordination patterns that keep consent, authority, evidence and participation explicit across human and AI actors.

### Provenance / reproducibility

Emphasize:

- source lineage;
- evidence receipts;
- currentness;
- transformations;
- machine twins;
- reconstruction paths.

Compact hook:

> CoCivium treats every important public object as something that should expose where it came from, what changed, what supports it, and what superseded it.

### Resilience / continuity

Emphasize:

- failure-domain independence;
- durable checkpoints;
- successor state;
- recoverability;
- currentness evidence;
- redundant but non-duplicative surfaces.

Compact hook:

> CoCivium is exploring continuity primitives where work can survive session/provider loss without confusing a duplicate copy with independent resilience.
