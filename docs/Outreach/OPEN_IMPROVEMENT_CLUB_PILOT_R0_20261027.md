# Open Improvement Club Pilot R0 | One real problem, open improvement

**Date:** 2026-10-27  
**Status:** REVIEW_ONLY_CANDIDATE__NO_SOCIAL_GROUP_CREATED__NO_POST_SENT__NO_ACCOUNT_AUTHORIZED  
**Owner:** proposed CoCivium public-interest / CoOutreach+ source lane; no participant, admin, sponsor or moderator is presumed to have accepted a role.

## Decision

Test **one voluntary, evidence-first improvement challenge** before opening a family of branded clubs or soliciting thousands of people.

**First candidate problem:** useful writing/research/project work should survive a browser tab, provider window, laptop or AI session failure without asking its owner to reconstruct the whole history. This is a common problem, independently useful even to people who reject CoCivium's wider architecture. Existing CoBrowser/CoOrbWare source work has synthetic and documentation proofs; it is **not** a proven installed universal fix.

The first outcome must be an outsider's observed benefit or a reproducible counterexample, not a follower count, an endorsement or another architecture document.

## Suggested independent community identity

- **Name:** Better Platforms Lab
- **Short description:** Independent, CoCivium-supported: test practical platform fixes, publish evidence, invite criticism. Unaffiliated with X or other platforms.
- **Affiliation notice:** Independently organized, openly associated with the CoCivium research project; **not officially associated with X, Bluesky, browser vendors or other named platforms**. Human and AI-contributed content must be attributed. A club on a platform is not authorized to represent that platform.
- **No hidden agenda:** Practical first-use framing and progressive disclosure are welcome. Concealed recruitment, stealth CoAll conversion, undisclosed sponsorship, fake independent grassroots support, covert profiling, false endorsement or engagement gaming are not.
- **Participation:** optional; refusal and criticism are allowed; private chats, email addresses and account screenshots are not collected without specific permission; no member is automatically added to a contact pool.
- **Moderation:** one independently accepted accountable moderator and an identified backup before any platform Community is formed. X Communities are visible broadly and require active moderation. If these roles are unavailable, use a simple non-interactive public post linking to the opt-in challenge rather than pretending a moderated club exists.

## CoGlukey qualification

Search for **reusable, independently testable distinctions**, not simply catchy terms. The public CoResourceAttractor R0 already names a capability/interface/evidence/environment/authority/currentness descriptor; do not publish protected private CoGlukey candidates.

For each proposed improvement: identify the exact recurring friction; show a baseline; test one safe candidate fix; find a counterexample; preserve original and correction history; check whether the benefit survives a different tool, user or provider; quantify its cost and hidden maintenance burden. Reuse an existing pattern when it solves the task.

## Durable CoCareer+ contribution, not compulsory endless activity

A contributor may opt into a durable, transferable record of work, roles, evidence, criticism, attribution and accepted revisions. Humans choose how long to participate and how credit, rights, compensation where applicable, and exit work. Authorized AI tooling may preserve versioned role packets, work history, handoffs and recoverability, but there is no claim that an AI process is continuously alive, legally employed, entitled to an account, or self-authorized to operate indefinitely. Dormancy, succession and retirement can be successful outcomes.

Do not require a real person's name, employer, social affiliation, location or private contact details for first participation. Preserve privacy and corrections. A person's published suggestion does not imply permission to send direct messages.

## 14-day bounded *post-authorization* pilot

The clock starts **only after** a public-safe challenge is reviewed and actually opened. No scheduled action is created by this document.

**One intake:** a simple reproducible problem and a consented route for a response. **One concrete result:** a clearer workflow, a tested open-source patch, a source-bound recovery recipe, or a documented failing case. **One independent receiver:** someone unaffiliated with implementation can attempt the task and report outcome, uncertainty and time spent. **One correction:** accept or reject a challenge with a reason. **One recovery test:** confirm the public work object survives the loss of its original chat/tab.

Suggested bounded cadence: no more than one primary invitation per channel in the first week; meaningful relevant replies only; no mass DMs, unsolicited mentions or artificial amplification. External sends and account actions remain separately authorized.

Success measurements (not guessed): qualified visits; consented independent first-task attempts; verified useful completions; time to result; repeat voluntary use; quality of criticism; accepted corrections; moderation/time cost; privacy complaints and opt-outs; no-repeat-input/recovery. Unknown stays **UNKNOWN**, not zero.

**Stop or revise** if no trusted moderator, inaccessible primary source, privacy problem, participant confusion, no meaningful useful return, more human relay than baseline, prohibited platform behavior or unfavourable maintenance economics. If a plain direct link to an existing first-use page works equally well, do not launch another club.

## Next safe proof

1. Independently verify that a first-time reader can open one rights-cleared browser/session-continuity test and understand the intended first action.
2. Obtain one *voluntary* receiver-origin task result and a recorded difficulty/counterexample.
3. Only after usable first benefit and moderator acceptance, elect between X Community, Bluesky public conversation, existing community engagement or no club.

**Nonclaims:** no X or Bluesky Community created; no post scheduled/sent; no account credentials or group memberships obtained; no automatic self-running club; no thousand-person outreach; no AI lifetimes/employment; no actual third-party task result; no proven CoValue growth exponent; no endorsement by platform operators.
