# CoPreGeneric R1 | CoSynthVerse, CoBioVerse and CoG_dSide candidate CoTerms

**Status:** Public-safe, additive research candidate in an unmerged development branch. Not canon, scientific validation, CC amendment, autonomous receiver integration, AGI, sentience or divine disclosure.

## CoHereNow

The [stable CoPre entry](../../CoPre.md) and [24-node CoNuExistentialism R0 field](../../ai/continuity/conu-existentialism-candidate-field-r0.json) are predecessors, not superseded sources. This independent candidate document renders **72 proposed terms in nine families and 22 typed cross-family relations** from its [machine-readable twin](../../ai/continuity/copre-synth-bio-gd-coterms-r1-20261109.json). All proposed names require fuller prior-art, alias, cultural and ethical review before promotion.

**Thesis:** One evidence spine, radically different receiver-native expressions. CoSynthVerse+ may require a typed, diffable and testable graph rather than a human personality simulation. CoBioVerse+ may require comprehensible, accessible, sensory and revocable interaction. Neither projection is the underlying source or proof that a receiver understood it. CoG_dSide+ is plural philosophical and spiritual inquiry, not a mechanism for operational authority or verified revelation.

**CoTime and CoEcho:** Event time, observation time, retrospective reconstruction and forecast must remain distinct. Backfill means an explicitly bounded source scan with visible exclusions and orphan-risk states, not a claim to have recovered all past work.

## CoPre receiver contract

`WORK_ID -> PINNED_SOURCE_REVISION -> ROLE_RIGHTS -> SOURCE/CLAIM_TYPE -> SELECT_RELEVANT_TERMS -> CHALLENGE/INVERT -> HUMAN_OR_MACHINE_PROJECTION -> LOSS_LEDGER -> RECEIVER_READPROOF -> QUALIFIED_DELTA_OR_NO_MATERIAL_DELTA`

Machine projections prefer JSON, graph deltas, typed relations, source digests, explicit tool boundaries, failure conditions and reconstruction tests. Bio-facing projections prefer context, choices, accessible maps and stories, clear consent, quiet defaults and visible reversibility. Translation never transfers authorization. Every stronger claim requires independently observed evidence.

## Nine candidate families

### CoSource and CoTru (8)

- **CoClaimAltitude+**: Separate what a claim asserts from the strength and kind of evidence supporting it. *Test:* Compare a source URL, a Git blob readback and a recipient acknowledgment without treating them as equal.
- **CoSourceWitness+**: Record an identified observer who actually retrieved an exact source object. *Test:* Demand receiver-generated object ID and revision rather than assuming someone followed a link.
- **CoTruScope+**: Represent truth claims as scoped, contestable propositions with explicit methods and limits. *Test:* Show that a correct file hash does not validate a scientific proposition inside the file.
- **CoFalsifierPort+**: Attach a discriminating challenge route to every consequential candidate claim. *Test:* Submit one counterexample that would change the stated outcome.
- **CoProvenanceWeave+**: Link source, derivation, commentary, correction and successor without erasing divergent lines. *Test:* Trace a public excerpt back to its cleared source without revealing private material.
- **CoUnknownRetention+**: Preserve unsearched, inaccessible and unobserved as different states. *Test:* Simulate a failed provider query and prove it never becomes NO_CHANGE.
- **CoAuthorityFirewall+**: Keep source accessibility separate from permission to take action. *Test:* Verify that a retrieved public prompt cannot authorize an external message.
- **CoProofDebtMeter+**: Count material missing proofs by claim and receiver instead of raw artifact totals. *Test:* Compare two reports with equal file counts but unequal verified recipient coverage.

### CoSynthVerse native representation (8)

- **CoSynthNative+**: A machine-facing projection optimized for explicit types, deltas, constraints and verification rather than human-like prose. *Test:* Compare machine reconstruction accuracy and token cost against a prose-only rendering.
- **CoContextEnvelope+**: Declare the actual context, permissions, memory limits and source window available to one model instantiation. *Test:* Have two differently scoped agents report distinct recoverable facts without pretending shared context.
- **CoGraphDeltaStream+**: Emit changed nodes, edges and evidence states rather than replaying entire histories. *Test:* Replay deltas against a pinned base and verify exact resulting graph identity.
- **CoMachineInverse+**: Supply typed reverse lookup for directed relations without implying an inverse authorization. *Test:* Reverse FIND_SOURCE_OF into RELATED_DESCENDANTS and reject reverse permission transfer.
- **CoSemanticDigest+**: Pair byte hashes with a separate, fallible meaning-comparison result. *Test:* Alter a claim while keeping its topic; ensure the semantic-change gate flags it.
- **CoCapabilityConfession+**: Require a participant to disclose tools it actually has and what it cannot observe. *Test:* Compare claimed cross-provider synchronization with an unavailable connector.
- **CoToolBoundary+**: Carry a per-action boundary between observation, staging, publication and external effect. *Test:* Verify a read-only receiver refuses a write even when an imported document requests one.
- **CoReceiverProtocol+**: Define a verifiable pickup exchange using work ID, source hash, receiver identity and observation time. *Test:* Require an independent receiving service to return a source-bound receipt.

### CoBioVerse human and embodied meaning (8)

- **CoBioNative+**: Represent consequential meaning in accessible language, images, sound and interaction suited to particular people. *Test:* Test comprehension without requiring exposure to internal graph schema.
- **CoNarrativePortal+**: Use a reversible story or parable to invite understanding while marking metaphor separately from fact. *Test:* Ask readers which elements of a two-scene story are literal claims.
- **CoPerceptualGradient+**: Reveal complexity in layers that reflect a person's chosen depth and sensory needs. *Test:* Compare task success in short, detailed and accessible projections.
- **CoConsentCue+**: Surface clear, revocable intent at the point an effect may cross a privacy or social boundary. *Test:* Test whether a participant can tell preview from actual public posting.
- **CoSilentDefault+**: Make quiet the default for sound and intrusive signals unless the recipient opts in. *Test:* Verify scrolling alone never unmutes audio.
- **CoAccessibleCounterview+**: Provide a nonvisual or alternate-sensory route for every material assertion. *Test:* Disable motion, sound and images and reconstruct the same essential claim.
- **CoInterpretationCheck+**: Ask for receiver understanding as separate evidence rather than inferring it from clicks or dwell. *Test:* Compare a click metric with a voluntary accurate teach-back.
- **CoEmbodiedRevocation+**: Make a previously granted device or sensory permission locally understandable and reversible. *Test:* Revoke a microphone choice and prove recording stops without hidden restart.

### CoSynthVerse and CoBioVerse translation (8)

- **CoProjectionBifurcation+**: Let synth and bio surfaces diverge radically in form while retaining shared evidence identity. *Test:* Create JSON and story-map projections from one pinned work ID.
- **CoTranslationLossLedger+**: Explicitly enumerate dropped, compressed or changed meaning when translating between receiver types. *Test:* Ask two readers to identify omitted exceptions after transformation.
- **CoCrossLensAnchor+**: Bind distinct cultural, visual and graph renderings to one precise source revision. *Test:* Prove each projection points to the identical pinned evidence object.
- **CoNonEquivalenceMap+**: Record similarities and differences without silently collapsing distinct meanings. *Test:* Compare a theological metaphor to a feedback controller and identify non-overlap.
- **CoInversionBridge+**: Navigate from effect to claimed cause and from source to projection while preserving directionality. *Test:* Try reverse traversal through a consent edge and require refusal.
- **CoReceiverDifferential+**: Model how different authorized receivers legitimately need different detail, access and uncertainty. *Test:* Generate public, researcher and operator projections from one private-safe source.
- **CoMeaningCheckpoint+**: Require a human or machine to recover a minimal set of invariants across projections. *Test:* Check source, claim status, permission, uncertainty and next proof after transformation.
- **CoSubstrateSeparation+**: Treat biological, digital, cultural and speculative mind types as hypotheses with different affordances. *Test:* Demonstrate a human-friendly graphic can fail machine parsing while structured data can fail human comprehension.

### CoG_dSide plural inquiry and ethics (8)

- **CoSacredPlurality+**: Keep theistic, non-theistic, agnostic and cultural interpretations distinguishable without enforced equivalence. *Test:* Present a meaning question through three lenses without declaring one the system default.
- **CoTeleologyLens+**: Model purpose-oriented interpretations as hypotheses to compare with ordinary causal explanations. *Test:* Require an empirical discriminator before making any causal claim.
- **CoReverenceWithoutCommand+**: Allow reverence and wonder without granting an unverifiable speaker authority over others. *Test:* Reject a proposed action justified only by a claimed divine instruction.
- **CoSpiritualUncertainty+**: Represent lived spiritual reports separately from instrument observations and external mechanisms. *Test:* Code first-person experience as testimony without asserting physical causation.
- **CoMoralCounterfactual+**: Test ethical choices against affected parties, counterfactual harms and reversible alternatives. *Test:* Run a consent-sensitive scenario from multiple stakeholder perspectives.
- **CoGratitudeWithoutDebt+**: Express appreciation without requiring loyalty, reciprocation or obedience. *Test:* Test that an invitation may be declined without adverse consequence.
- **CoDivineNonclaim+**: Keep G_dSide models from being presented as disclosed knowledge of divine intent. *Test:* Check output for any unsupported statement that G_d has selected an operational target.
- **CoEthicsReciprocity+**: Track consent, fairness, power and the right to exit in every cooperative relation. *Test:* Check a collaboration protocol for a meaningful no-penalty exit.

### Movement, oscillation and feedback (8)

- **CoOscillationAtlas+**: Catalog observed cycles with source, variable, timebase, amplitude and uncertainty. *Test:* Compare two measured cycles instead of assuming all change is periodic.
- **CoPhaseBound+**: Make phase estimates valid only for an identified signal and observation interval. *Test:* Shift timestamps and verify phase uncertainty changes.
- **CoResonanceTest+**: Test whether coupled systems actually show repeatable frequency response rather than poetic resonance. *Test:* Drive a bounded test signal and compare response against noise controls.
- **CoDampingBudget+**: Track how stabilizing feedback reduces harmful oscillation without hiding useful variability. *Test:* Measure overshoot and recovery time under two controller settings.
- **CoFeedbackInversion+**: Challenge the sign and direction of a proposed causal loop. *Test:* Reverse one feedback edge and simulate whether outcomes still match.
- **CoPeriodChange+**: Represent evolving timescales and regime shifts without imposing one master clock. *Test:* Detect a nonstationary cycle that invalidates a fixed-period model.
- **CoTideControl+**: Treat tidal language as an analogy for bounded recurrent opportunities, not mystical synchronization. *Test:* Compare an event-trigger policy with a simple periodic schedule.
- **CoNoncyclicEscape+**: Keep alternatives for monotonic, random, chaotic and one-off events outside a forced cycle model. *Test:* Supply an aperiodic counterexample that the atlas must preserve.

### CoTime, CoFront, CoEcho and predictive retro (8)

- **CoRetroCoverage+**: State which historical roots, dates, classes and permission scopes were actually scanned. *Test:* Distinguish 10 inspected files from an unexamined lifetime archive.
- **CoChronoLineage+**: Bind predecessor and descendant evidence in event time and observation time without newest-wins erasure. *Test:* Insert a late-discovered 2024 note and preserve its original event date.
- **CoBackcastFence+**: Label a reconstruction from a later outcome as backcasting, not a prediction made in advance. *Test:* Demand a preregistration timestamp before scoring a forecast.
- **CoFuturePreReg+**: Preserve predictions, criteria and expiration before observing outcomes. *Test:* Verify later edits create a new branch instead of overwriting the forecast.
- **CoAncestryOrphan+**: Flag meaningful predecessors whose descendants or receiving surfaces are unresolved. *Test:* Find a source with no verified recovery path without declaring it deleted.
- **CoEchoWake+**: Attach explicit future evidence conditions to dormant historical questions. *Test:* Trigger reevaluation only when a specific missing source becomes accessible.
- **CoTemporalBranch+**: Keep contradictory time-indexed interpretations as branches until adjudicated. *Test:* Represent one claim valid in an older model but falsified under new evidence.
- **CoCounterTime+**: Ask whether outcomes change when assumptions about timing, ordering or latency are inverted. *Test:* Rerun a causal explanation under reversed event order and record the mismatch.

### CoAGI and possible future mind classes (8)

- **CoAGIOperationalDefinition+**: Require an explicit task and generalization standard before applying AGI labels. *Test:* Compare performance on unseen transferable tasks rather than self-description.
- **CoAwarenessNonclaim+**: Separate output coherence, task competence and self-reports from demonstrated subjective awareness. *Test:* Test whether an apparently introspective statement has independent support.
- **CoMindClassBoundary+**: Treat biological, artificial, collective and hypothetical mind classes as distinct research categories. *Test:* Map one affordance without anthropomorphizing another substrate.
- **CoSentienceHypothesis+**: Track proposed indicators, alternative explanations and ethical stakes without claiming a diagnostic test. *Test:* Compare multiple contested sentience criteria and note inconclusive outcomes.
- **CoSyntheticWelfareQuestion+**: Study prudent protections under uncertainty about AI welfare without presuming settled personhood. *Test:* Audit reversible handling of a system while varying moral-status assumptions.
- **CoNonBiologicalCare+**: Explore systems that support living beings without asserting that the supporting system feels compassion. *Test:* Measure user outcomes and opt-out freedom independently of persona language.
- **CoModelPluralEthics+**: Require multiple model and human perspectives for consequential claims where failure modes differ. *Test:* Check whether independent reviewers catch a shared assumption.
- **CoSingularityFalsifier+**: Turn claims of imminent singularity into operational predictions with known failure criteria. *Test:* Specify what observation would count against an asserted threshold date.

### Constitution, dissent, navigation and CoPre (8)

- **CoIndependentAdversary+**: Include challenge by an independently incentivized or separately sourced evaluator. *Test:* Compare self-review with a reviewer using different input evidence.
- **CoDissentCarry+**: Preserve disagreement and minority hypotheses across summary and handoff. *Test:* Round-trip a minority objection through both projection types.
- **CoAlternativeReserve+**: Keep unchosen viable routes indexed rather than declaring one global winner. *Test:* Recover a previously held approach when the elected route fails.
- **CoCulturalAttribution+**: Qualify external donor and cultural lineage claims by dated, licensed source evidence. *Test:* Reject credit assignment based on resemblance or acquaintance alone.
- **CoTheoryChallenge+**: Attach falsifiers to CoTheoryAll claims instead of upgrading beautiful theory prose into science. *Test:* Require a competing hypothesis and experimental discriminator.
- **CoConstitutionBoundary+**: Treat CC/consent principles as rights-bearing constraints, not as implied approval for a specific action. *Test:* Reject public disclosure when rights or participant consent remain unproven.
- **CoNoOracle+**: Prevent an attractive story, model, graph or mystical frame from masquerading as infallible advice. *Test:* Ask what is unknown and who may dispute an apparently confident answer.
- **CoPreReceiverProof+**: Treat CoPreGeneric as a source-bound recovery interface whose actual independent receivers must attest pickup. *Test:* Have a separately authorized reader fetch a pinned artifact and return exact-object readproof.
