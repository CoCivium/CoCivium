# CoSignet+ Family / CoAll-CoCivium-Co+ Brand Relations R0

**State:** `CANDIDATE_BRAND_RELATION_CONTRACT__NO_PUBLIC_REBRAND_NO_LOGO_REPLACEMENT`

CoAll+, CoCivium+ and Co+ should be visually related without being collapsed into one identical public identity.

`SHARED_FAMILY_MARK_NE_SAME_ENTITY`
`VISUAL_RESEMBLANCE_NE_IDENTITY_EQUIVALENCE`

Candidate role relation:

- **CoAll+**: broad relational ecology / umbrella system concept.
- **CoCivium+**: project/public implementation and coordination front within the broader field.
- **Co+**: notation/family marker, not automatically a single organization, legal entity, product or account.
- **CoCivia and other fronts**: family derivatives with separately typed identity and authority.

Reuse existing `CoSignet+` direction as the visual-derivative relation. A derivative may bind `principal_or_object | family | role | context | audience | authority | provenance | version | visual_core | differentiators | accessibility | usage_scope`.

Candidate default:

`shared visual grammar + recognizable core signet + typed derivative`

rather than one exact logo copied everywhere or unrelated logos for every CoTerm.

Possible shared primitives include a common eye/orb/relational motif, common geometry, common typographic relation, provenance language and accessibility rules. Differentiators may include wordmark, context ring/halo, role marker, provenance/version marker and public-vs-internal projection.

An identical logo everywhere can falsely imply one principal, one authority domain, one product or one governance scope. Completely unrelated logos create unnecessary cognitive fragmentation.

`COMMON_LOGO_NE_COMMON_AUTHORITY`
`BRAND_UNITY_NE_GOVERNANCE_UNITY`
`DISTINCT_IDENTITY_NE_UNRELATED_IDENTITY`

Visual projections may evolve. Elected artwork should carry version/provenance and an explicit successor relation when changed.

`LATEST_ARTWORK_NE_ETERNAL_CANON`
`VISUAL_SUCCESSOR_REQUIRES_PROVENANCE`

No essential distinction should exist only in colour, animation or subtle visual similarity.
