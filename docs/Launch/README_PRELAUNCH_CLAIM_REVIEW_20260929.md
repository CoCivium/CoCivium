Examples include language about post-truth effects, erosion of ethics / decency / democracy, and technologies as civilizational-scale instruments of deformation.

**Review status:** thesis / interpretation, not established by the current CoNode / continuity / receipt evidence.

### 2. "Weapons of mass destruction" analogy

The README compares newer technologies with weapons-of-mass-destruction-scale danger.

**Review status:** rhetorically strong analogy. The current Ring-0 packet does not establish this comparison.

### 3. Claims about AI wanting / destined ethics

The README discusses equitability solutions AIs may "want" and AIs eventually setting or sharing emergent ethics.

**Review status:** speculative / philosophical working hypothesis unless separately evidenced. Do not read as established fact about present AI systems.

### 4. Trust and capture claims

The README says open-source systems may be trusted to guide more centrally controlled social products and that CoCivium may avoid capture.

**Review status:** design objective / anti-capture posture, not a guarantee.

### 5. Human / AI motivational claims

Statements about what AIs do or do not "dream," "want," or need from humans are broader than the technical packet.

**Review status:** conceptual framing, not operational evidence.
