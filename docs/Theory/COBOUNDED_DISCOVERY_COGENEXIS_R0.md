# CoBoundedDiscovery+ / CoGenExIs+ R0

**State:** `CANDIDATE_METAPHYSICAL_AND_EPISTEMIC_RELATION_MODEL__NO_TRUTH_CLAIM`

## Lead

A useful CoTheoryAll hypothesis is that apparent boundedness may be constitutive of discovery.

One speculative formulation is:

> If reality were in some sense relationally complete or globally knowable, locally bounded embodiments could still experience novelty because they do not instantiate or access the whole relation field at once.

This is a metaphysical hypothesis, not an established fact about minds, death, pre-life, divinity, or the universe.

`METAPHYSICAL_MODEL_NE_VERIFIED_COSMOLOGY`

`EXPERIENCED_DISCOVERY_NE_GLOBAL_IGNORANCE_PROOF`

## Bounded embodiment

Candidate relation family:

`CoBoundedEmbodiment(entity, accessible_state, inaccessible_state, temporal_horizon, embodiment_constraints, discovery_frontier, reintegration_hypothesis)`

- bounded body as a local perspective;
- life as temporally situated relation-discovery;

## CoGen+ / CoGenEx+ / CoGenExIs+

Candidate distinctions:

- **CoGen+**: generation, emergence, creation, derivation, or becoming.
- **CoGenEx+**: generation through exploration/discovery.
- **CoGenExIs+**: genesis-through-existing-and-discovering, where existence and inquiry co-evolve.

These are relation families before they are ontological claims.

Example:

`state_A --CoGenEx--> state_B`

means state B became reachable through exploration, perturbation, evidence, or discovery, not merely through deterministic construction.

`GENERATION_NE_CREATION_EX_NIHILO`

`DISCOVERY_NE_CAUSAL_ORIGIN`

## CoExOS+

`CoExOS+` may be modeled as an execution/experience operating layer for bounded beings, agents, nodes, or relational fronts.

It should not imply that a metaphysical substrate literally behaves like software.

Useful relation:

`CoExOS(subject, embodiment, accessible_relations, effect_permissions, observation_channels, learning_loop, exit/reintegration_model)`

`OS_METAPHOR_NE_UNIVERSE_IS_SOFTWARE`

## Discovery as a value

A complete predictor may still deliberately preserve uncertainty if:

- exploration itself creates value;
- local autonomy matters;
- surprise improves learning;
- multiple perspectives reveal different relation structure;
- overprediction would collapse optionality;
- simulated certainty would make lived discovery sterile.

This supports:

`KNOWABLE_NE_SHOULD_PRECOMPUTE`

`PREDICTABLE_NE_SHOULD_REMOVE_DISCOVERY`

`UNCERTAINTY_CAN_BE_INFORMATIONALLY_OR_EXPERIENTIALLY_VALUABLE`
