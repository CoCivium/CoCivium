## One question, many reader doors

**Story:** Can applications become capabilities people may lawfully compose without surrendering data or freedom?

**Engineering:** The public [Great ReFact+ source](../../../Architecture/COGREAT_REFACT_R0_20261025.md) describes a proposal, not a vendor override. [CoPreCursor+](../../../../examples/coprecursor/README.md) is a small offline preview/accept/undo demonstration.

**Evidence and challenge:** The [CoSpiff survivability assessment](../../../Strategy/COSPIFF_FIREWORKS_SURVIVABILITY_R1.md) tracks still-unproved human utility and independent recovery. [CoLens+](../../../../examples/colensmedia/README.md) offers source-bound perspective changes over a synthetic theme, not a complete CoOrbWare runtime.

The reader prototype's Story/Evidence/Challenge switch changes its explanation, never the underlying source; source links and current limitations remain visible.

## Distribution model, not yet activated

1. Canonical, versioned open-source manuscript with source and rights checks.
2. A mobile-accessible book site and accessible fallback text.
3. Explicitly approved RickPublic/Substack chapters, excerpts, and optional audio/video. **Do not bypass the existing per-article approval queue.**
4. Offline HTML/EPUB and optional later print-on-demand gift edition.
5. Reader questions, feedback, source correction, visible versions and independent receiver acknowledgements.

**Rights:** Publicly inspectable text is not blanket permission to republish third-party material. This pilot contains original editorial text and links to already-public CoCivium sources, but the intended giftware/reuse licence requires owner and third-party-rights review before redistribution promises are made.
