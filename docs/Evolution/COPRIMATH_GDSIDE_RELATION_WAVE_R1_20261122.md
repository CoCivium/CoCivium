## G_dSide: future pull without an invented force

Distinguish three candidate meanings: (1) normative orientation toward valued possible futures; (2) empirical forecasts used by a present decision rule; (3) speculative future-to-past physical or theological causation, for which no evidence or authority is established here.

A future-directed model compares an action against a no-action or other stated baseline using a vector of prospective changes, not an oracle for moral good. Each outcome dimension needs affected population, operational definition, units, time horizon, uncertainty, evidence and competing model. Incommensurable trade-offs remain Pareto alternatives. The system must record cases where supply-only, demand-only or simpler baselines outperform G_dSide.

## CoEthicDrift: the risk inside self-evolving evaluators

An optimizer allowed to rewrite its own definition of good from a noisy feed, its training corpus, cultural inertia, audience engagement or repeated self-agreement can redefine its mistakes as successes. This is not trustworthy ethical evolution.

Separate five layers:

| Layer | Concern | Essential boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CoEthos / CoGrace | Dignity, care, fallibility, stewardship, ability to correct | Proposed orientation, not a universal proven moral formula |
| CoEthics / CoValueTranslation | Whose welfare, liberty, safety, rights, ecology, fairness and conflicting values? | Affected-party voice; no silent scalarization |
| CoEpistemics / CoNull / CoNoise / CoWise | Facts, forecasts, missing voices, copied evidence, uncertainty and wrong assumptions | Training patterns are evidence candidates, not moral grants |
| CoGovernance / CoCC / Consent | Who can legitimately amend a commitment or authorize a given act? | Versioned, accountable, contextual human authority and appeal |
| CoPriMath / CoEv / G_dSide | Which permitted transformations might benefit plural futures? | Formal types, hard bounds, alternatives, negative controls, receipts |

A high-stability floor can provisionally privilege non-coercion, consent, dignity, rights, independent dissent, ecological viability, fairness, safe exit, and correctability, but these are not self-authorizing rules. Interpretations and amendments need legitimate, transparent processes with meaningful participation by people affected. No model or single founder gets unrestricted power to define good for everyone.
