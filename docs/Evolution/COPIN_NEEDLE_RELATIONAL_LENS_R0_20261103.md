## Reusable inquiry prompt for relevant current and successor roles

> **CoPinNeedle / CoPerspective R0.** Starting from one explicit source, work object, challenge or metaphor, explore how much useful possibility can be addressed by a small, reversible representation. Compare spatial, temporal and atemporal structures; micro, intermediate and macro scales; literal, mathematical, engineering, artistic, cultural and philosophical readings; inverse, null, interstitial and orthogonal alternatives. Ask which relations remain invariant under a change of scale, observer, language, substrate or time, which break, and what the break teaches. Generate candidates lazily, then elect bounded independent tests with cost, consent, authority and falsifiers. Preserve source identities, competing explanations, losses from compression, failures, and recovery pointers. Show one demonstrable improvement in receiver value or verification rather than counting generated combinations. Where G_d or religious interpretations arise, respectfully preserve their own attributed philosophical/theological lens; never promote metaphor, belief, synchronicity or model output into observed causation, revelation or execution authority. Publish or contact people only through separately qualified, explicitly authorized channels. Stop or sleep without material information gain, and resume from a source-bound wake condition.

This is an **optional method/lens**, not a forced universal prompt injected into all sessions.

## Seven discriminating transformations

| Transformation | Question | Counter-test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compress / rehydrate | Can a tiny reference recover a large exact source? | Compare recovered bytes, provenance, omitted uncertainty and cost |
| Factor / recombine | Can one invariant replace N repeated workflows? | Check negative cases where the factorization changes meaning |
| Cross time | Is a pattern static, periodic, lagged, causal, predictive or obsolete? | Shuffle order, invert lag, use prospective holdouts; distinguish valid, event, observed and recorded times |
| Cross scale | Does a useful relation transfer micro -> meso -> macro and back? | Hold budgets and evidence constant; find boundary cases |
| Cross substrate | Can one source survive text, sound, UI, model and device projections? | Test fidelity, access rights, reception and replay, not just visual similarity |
| Pluralize / inverse / null | What do other observers, absences or counterexamples expose? | Include dissent and known alternatives before selection |
| Coordinate / succeed | Can many logical hypotheses use few elected workers? | Compare useful verified output per cost, handoff, ACK and human touch against a no-new-worker baseline |

## Minimal experiment recipe

For **one** actual bounded problem:
1. Pin an exact source and its observed time, role, constraints, intended receiver and current hypothesis.
2. Compile three alternative representations: (A) full, (B) compressed and rehydratable, (C) intentionally lossy. Preserve reconstruction and translation-loss ledgers.
3. Generate a finite candidate frontier spanning temporal / atemporal / null / inverse / cross-scale transformations, with a control and at least one counterexample.
4. Qualify a small number of independent comparisons; one elected writer per collision domain. No worker count is implied by the count of candidate addresses.
5. Measure independent receiver task success, factual fidelity, time-to-value, latency/cost/energy, human relay burden, recovery, uncertainty and discarded failures.
6. Keep a result receipt with a source hash/commit, method, negative results, observed effect, remaining hold, successor pointer and next wake.
7. Select: reuse / adapt / revise / sleep / retire. A failed test that exposes a hidden assumption is a valid learning result.

## Distinct interpretive lanes

- **Scientific and engineering:** test physical claims using instruments and replication, and software claims using real receiver effects.
- **Mathematical/informational:** a short description can *index* vast spaces; it does not imply the full space has been computed, stored or physically compressed.
- **Existential/philosophical:** ask how identity, being, absence, purpose, relation, time and observer dependence affect the chosen meanings. Existing philosophical traditions are priors to engage, not straw men to dethrone.
- **Religious/G_d:** compare attributed interpretations respectfully, with voluntary plural inquiry and no claim of divine instruction, approval, or certainty.
- **Artistic/mythic:** freely use paradox and absurdity to generate questions while clearly distinguishing stories from evidence.

A perspective may inspire another. It does not inherit the other's authority, truth criterion or consent.

## Proposed success and failure tests

**Pass, first rung:** a new worker reads this exact pinned file, independently selects one real task, reports a bounded reproducible A/B comparison, and writes a receipt with a material outcome or a precise negative finding. A second independent receiver must demonstrate readback/pickup before `HANDOFF_ACK` is asserted.

**Fail:** generated terms outnumber tested distinctions; source lost in compression; religious metaphor presented as physics; predicted treated as observed; extra logical roles counted as active workers; duplicated or unconsented public messages; no actual reader/receiver.
