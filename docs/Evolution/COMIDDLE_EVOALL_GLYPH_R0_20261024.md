## Relation and inverse contract

A relation must retain `subject, predicate, object, time, scope, polarity, epistemic_state, provenance, authority, consent, receiver`.

- A registered converse swaps subject/object roles without negating the claim or creating independent evidence.
- Explicit denial != unknown != not applicable != non-observation != contested != counterfactual.
- An absent event may be meaningful, but is not automatically an observed negative.
- `@` resolves to a real opted-in recipient/receiver only with verified scope, authority and readproof.
- `>`, `<`, `<> `, `?`, `&` and `+` are candidate human-readable glyphs; a lexer must map them to unambiguous predicates, not handwave their meaning.
- Philosophical, cultural, ET or G_d proposals may be labeled hypothesis/metaphor/fiction; semantic proximity to quantum or Planck concepts never proves a physical mechanism.

## CoDoodle / CoNex / CoTurtle bounded rail

One deterministic seed can generate a finite batch of alternative experiment candidates, each with a registered converse, negative control, affected-person/consent check, falsifier, feasibility/energy cost and an elected next proof. Current preview sizes: 8, 24, 64. CoTurtle proposes at most four local nonexecuting variants per selected candidate.

A local Node test generated 9,600 synthetic candidates in 150 bounded 64-candidate batches and observed an approximate 0.068 ms average batch composition on that workbench. This **does not** measure user input performance, sustained rate, concurrency, AI inference, provider latency, device success or unlimited resources. The local test suite reported 27 unit tests PASS, alongside 13 explicit live checks NOT_RUN and three intentional unsupported-claim FAIL gates. Independent reproduction and user-device QA remain needed.
