## CoDot

CoDot is a candidate smallest visible locus for state, attention, signal, anomaly, or invitation-to-inspect.

A CoDot may remain tiny and unobtrusive, then expand into an icon, button, bar, map, or richer surface when attention is warranted.

CoDot is a surface projection, not the intelligence itself and not a claim of consciousness.

## CoCloud

CoCloud is a candidate many-dimensional discovery space containing terms, relations, inversions, alternatives, temporal states, models, evidence states, metaphors, and cross-domain projections.

Clouding is intended to increase combinatorial discovery while preserving provenance and uncertainty.

**CLOUDING != TRUTH**

## CoNulla and CoQuestion

CoNulla represents a candidate moving middle or unresolved state rather than a forced binary.

CoQuestion represents durable questions that can outlive particular answers.

Candidate loop:

CoDot -> CoNulla -> CoQuestion -> CoTest -> CoEvidence -> new CoDot.

## CME

CME is retained as a stable candidate name for a persistent field of collective mind, meaning, questions, perspectives, and inquiry.

This document does not claim that CME is the mind of God or any other metaphysical entity.

## Co-Or Theory

Co-Or Theory is an Einstein-inspired candidate research and visualization framework for exploring evolving relations among fields, observers, oscillations, space-time, uncertainty, and thought experiments.

It may use historical reconstruction and synthetic dialogue, but must clearly distinguish:

documented history
synthetic reconstruction
modern interpretation
simulation
speculation
empirical evidence.

It is not a replacement for established physical theory without independent evidence.

## CoSpiralConvergence
