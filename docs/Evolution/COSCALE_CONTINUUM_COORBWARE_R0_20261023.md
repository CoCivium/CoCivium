## CoHereNow

A logical population can be very large without making the active worker population large. The new offline package simulates a **1,000,000,000,000-descriptor address space**, **at most two leased simulation slots** and **zero actual workers**. Orb/Map/Text/Ambient are projections of one event-sourced state; no accounts, sockets, daemons, emails, node fanout, CoStead receipts or external sends were produced.

**Reduction should be reversible while the source survives.** Reduction of a *view* means show less detail, and a work item can later wake from `SLEEP` to `READY` to a newly budgeted lease. Reduction by *destruction* or lossy summarization is not generally reversible. A content hash, thumbnail, compressed display or model summary is not a replacement for unretained source bytes.
