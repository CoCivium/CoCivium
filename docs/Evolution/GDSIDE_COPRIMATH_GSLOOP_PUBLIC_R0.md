# G_dSide+ / CoPriMath+ / GS-Loop+ Public R0

**Status:** Public research and implementation contract. Not a deployed CoBar runtime, validated mathematics, theological claim, or evidence of autonomous cross-session coordination. See [issue #378](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoCivium/issues/378), [CoMeasureFabric #367](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoCivium/issues/367), and [CoItch #376](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoCivium/issues/376).

## Direction

SupplySide asks what can be supplied. DemandSide asks what is requested. **G_dSide+** asks which plural, revisable candidate futures would increase agency, wellbeing, ecological viability, relational quality, resilience, and unrealized possibility, especially for those who cannot express market demand.

G_d is a metaphorical/philosophical referent, **not** a verified source of instructions or operational authority. CMA/CoMetaAttractor is an explicitly revisable and testable portfolio of candidate future states. Prefer experiments that discriminate G_dSide from supply/demand/hybrid baselines; never bias evidence to guarantee a win.

Candidate research score, not a proven law:

```text
G(a | x_t) = E[sum_k w_k * DeltaV_k(a) + lambda * DeltaOptionality(a)
                + mu * DeltaRelationalQuality(a)
                - rho * Risk(a) - kappa * Cost(a)]
```

Weights are uncertain and contestable; rights, consent, safety, and reversibility remain hard constraints. Keep Pareto alternatives when tradeoffs cannot be collapsed to one scalar.

## GS-Loop / GS-Exit R0

A durable WorkID owns objective, current state, provenance, authorization, evidence, budget, receipt, and next-wake condition. Chat/model sessions are leased workers, never the continuity root.

- A repeated assent without new information must not create duplicate work or repetitive acknowledgment.
- Select authorized work by expected marginal *independently verifiable* progress, information gain and future optionality, minus costs and risk.
- On no progress: try a distinct bounded experiment, transfer, merge, or sleep on a concrete trigger; do not spin.
- On worker crash: expire lease, fence stale writes, resume from committed state, verify one external effect.
- On exit: preserve the unfinished purpose and recovery instructions. Break Glass remains outside model control.
- Presence/ambient audio may generate candidates only with explicit, reversible permissions; a detected song is not an instruction.
- External model providers may retain data under their policies. Fresh sessions do not imply deletion.

## UX acceptance contract

One Seed / one Orb / one gauge / one reversible thumb / one time reference / zero placeholders. CoBar, CoOrb, CoMap and legacy navigation must project the same owned WorkID/version/status. No invisible session is allowed to become the sole holder of authority or state.

### Falsifiable tests

1. Replay 10 identical assent messages: one WorkID, no duplicate committed effects, no repeated user-facing acknowledgment.
2. Kill a worker before commit; successor resumes and stale predecessor cannot write.
3. Kill after commit; retry is idempotent; receipt and independent readback agree.
4. Restart UI and machine; state reconstructs from owned artifacts.
5. Seed persists >24 hours and at least one handoff occurs without human relay.
6. Compare G_dSide against supply/demand/hybrid/random baselines on equal budgets, including cases where G_dSide loses.
7. Report failures, measured costs, consent and rights impacts, and confidence intervals.

**Proof boundary:** This file publishes a specification only. Synthetic tests mentioned in conversation do not prove X2 integration, 24h survival, or deployment. Promotion requires executable test artifacts, receiver-verified effects, and reproducible receipts.

## CoGoldenBraid+ R0: recursive SupplySide / DemandSide / G_dSide relations

**State:** PUBLIC_RESEARCH_CANDIDATE__NOT_A_RUNTIME_OPERATOR_OR_VERIFIED_ECONOMIC_LAW.

A braid is a typed, versioned, evidence-bearing relational object, **not** merely a diagram. A braid may join entities, relations, hyperrelations, or other braids. It can itself be supplied, requested, assessed against candidate futures, recombined, challenged, superseded, or retired. Recursive braiding is bounded by explicit resource budgets, stable IDs, cycle detection, consent and provenance; it is not an instruction to expand every possible relation.

Three simultaneous, nonexclusive perspectives:

- **SupplySide:** available capabilities, energy, knowledge, labor, assets, constraints and latent capacity that a braid can assemble or unlock.
- **DemandSide:** expressed, inferred and unmet needs, including how a braid is discovered, requested, evaluated or rejected. Inferred needs require uncertainty and consent safeguards.
- **G_dSide+:** plural, revisable candidate future benefits and harms of a braid, including optionality, ecological and relational impacts and interests not represented by purchasing power. No theological claim or privileged authority follows.

The **relations between these perspectives** are also candidate braids. A future-directed proposal can create new demand or new supply; existing supply can change plausible futures; unmet demand can reveal missing capability. Model feedback and negative effects explicitly. No side is automatically superior.

Proposed record, subject to CoPriMath typing:

```text
braid {
  braid_id, version, constituent_refs[], relation_types[],
  supply_claims[], demand_claims[], future_claims[],
  context, time_valid, time_observed, observer, provenance[],
  uncertainty[], consent_constraints[], rights_constraints[],
  counterclaims[], counterfactuals[], cost_units[], risk_units[],
  measurement_plan, evidence_refs[], challenge_refs[],
  successor_ref, readiness, effect_authority
}
```

A braid may reference other braids by stable identity and version. A graph cycle is not necessarily an error, but recursive evaluation must terminate under a depth/compute budget and record truncation or uncertainty. Keep observed versions recoverable, never silently replace a previously read projection.

**Candidate evolution relation, not a validated formula:**

```text
Braid[t+1] = Evolve(Braid[t], new_evidence, needs, candidate_futures,
                    constraints, dissent, available_budget)
```

`Evolve` must eventually have typed input/output signatures, deterministic replay conditions, bounded termination, explicit effect authority and measurable acceptance criteria. Do not confuse changes in wording or graph density with improvement.

### Comparative experiment R0

For the **same WorkID, task set, information budget, model budget, time horizon and rights/consent constraints**, compare:

1. Supply-only ranking of candidate braids.
2. Demand-only ranking.
3. Hybrid supply+demand ranking.
4. G_dSide-informed ranking, with uncertain future benefits and explicit negative controls.
5. Random or shuffled ranking baseline.

Measure task outcome, unmet-need coverage, optionality retained, verified relational benefit, total cost/latency, reversibility, fairness/rights impacts, false discoveries, and recovery after contradictory evidence. Use preregistered weights or report sensitivity, confidence intervals and counterexamples; retain Pareto alternatives rather than force one scalar winner.

### Failure and adversarial probes

- Attractive but false future narratives; counterfactual harm; demand manufactured by recommendation loops.
- Resource abundance that does not translate to real benefit; inferred demand without permission.
- Recursive braid explosion, cycles, duplicates, identity drift, stale version and evidence laundering.
- A braid that optimizes its own salience or metric instead of real-world outcomes.
- Cases where supply-only, demand-only or hybrid methods outperform G_dSide.

### Promotion gates

**SPEC -> TESTED_SOURCE:** typed schema + property tests for replay, deduplication, cycles, version retention and uncertainty.  
**TESTED_SOURCE -> VERIFIED_EXPERIMENT:** equal-budget independent benchmark with counterexamples and measured deltas.  
**VERIFIED_EXPERIMENT -> AUTHORIZED_RUNTIME:** receiver-verified effects, bounded rollback, explicit rights/consent gates and independent operational receipts.

## G_dSide+ CoPullVector+ candidate model (R0, 2026-11-08)

**Status:** PUBLIC_RESEARCH_SPEC_ONLY. The word "pull" denotes a modelled gradient among candidate futures, not a detected external force, spiritual instruction, proof of destiny or a physical field.

### Typed future state, feasibility and plurality

Represent a candidate future at horizon `h` as a **vector of measured or estimated dimensions** `V(x,h) = [agency, wellbeing, ecological resilience, access, trust, scientific discovery, optionality, cost, risk, ...]`. Each dimension must have an explicit definition, unit/scale, direction, baseline, time horizon, data source, confidence/uncertainty and affected population. Do not silently aggregate incommensurable units or treat speculative values as observed facts. Preserve multiple futures and Pareto trade-offs rather than nominate a universal ideal state.

Let `a` be a feasible proposed action under rights/consent/resource constraints, with a stochastic transition model `P(x' | x,a,h)`. A **candidate pull vector** is the predicted change `DeltaV(a | x,h) = E[V(x',h)-V(x,h)]` accompanied by intervals/scenarios, costs, harms, feasibility and uncertainty. There is **no guarantee** the predicted change will occur or be valuable. A scalar priority may be computed with explicitly chosen, revisable weights for a *specific task*, never as an objective measure of universal goodness. An optionality component values preserving future choices; it must not excuse present harms or violate hard rights constraints.

### Relations between pull vectors

Vectors themselves can form typed braids: reinforcing, competing, orthogonal, cancelling, time-lagged, contingent, mutually exclusive, scale-dependent or poorly understood. Include second-order consequences: an action can change future supply, future demand, the set of possible actions, and the accuracy of the model. Store negative and null edges. Track uncertainty propagation across recursive relationships and halt expansion at measured information-gain or resource-budget limits.

**Candidate record:**
```text
pull_candidate {
  id, version, work_id, source_revision, baseline_state_ref,
  population_scope, horizon, dimensions[{id, unit, baseline, predicted_delta,
    uncertainty, evidence_refs}], intervention_ref, feasibility,
  hard_constraints[], alternatives[], dependency_relations[],
  competing_predictions[], validation_plan, harms[], expected_cost,
  model_lineage[], observer, observed_at, next_review, nonclaims[]
}
```

### CoPriMath research questions

- Which dimensions have principled cardinal units, and which require ordinal/Pareto comparison?
- How should uncertainty and irreducible ambiguity propagate through recursive braids without creating false precision?
- Can future-directed search discover neglected needs or capabilities that supply/demand ranking misses?
- When do multi-model ensembles merely echo shared priors, and when do they provide demonstrably independent predictions?
- What is the simplest useful predictive model relative to a much richer "almost-specification" model, accounting for maintenance and Goodhart risks?

### Falsification and CI+ acceptance proposal

1. Assemble a public-safe set of *historical decision problems* with evidence available only up to each decision date; hold out later outcomes to prevent hindsight leakage.
2. Compare equal-budget supply-only, demand-only, hybrid, single G_dSide pull, braided multi-vector pull, and simple/random baselines.
3. Score calibration, ranking/regret, overlooked needs, false discoveries, fairness/rights, optionality, computational cost and decision usefulness. Preserve failures and cases where simpler models win.
4. Perturb weights, missing values, delayed effects, spurious correlations, shared-source contamination, adversarial goals and nonstationary environments; verify that rankings do not become silently overconfident.
5. Test dimensional type errors, conflicting constraints, cycles, replay determinism, source revision changes and deduplicated WorkIDs; ensure unknown remains unknown.
6. Require an independent evidence and outcome reviewer before any "improvement" claim; no runtime authority follows from a promising numerical score.

**Next bounded implementation target:** a tiny typed fixture with 2-3 proposed actions, at least 3 independently defined outcome dimensions, uncertain deltas, conflicting objectives, and an explicit baseline. Render candidate vectors in CoOrb as *hypotheses*, never as verified forces. Iterate only after an actual benchmark produces discriminating evidence.
