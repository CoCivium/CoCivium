See [issue #378](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoCivium/issues/378), [CoMeasureFabric #367](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoCivium/issues/367), and [CoItch #376](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoCivium/issues/376).

## Direction

SupplySide asks what can be supplied. DemandSide asks what is requested. **G_dSide+** asks which plural, revisable candidate futures would increase agency, wellbeing, ecological viability, relational quality, resilience, and unrealized possibility, especially for those who cannot express market demand.

G_d is a metaphorical/philosophical referent, **not** a verified source of instructions or operational authority. CMA/CoMetaAttractor is an explicitly revisable and testable portfolio of candidate future states. Prefer experiments that discriminate G_dSide from supply/demand/hybrid baselines; never bias evidence to guarantee a win.

Candidate research score, not a proven law:

```text
G(a | x_t) = E[sum_k w_k * DeltaV_k(a) + lambda * DeltaOptionality(a)
                + mu * DeltaRelationalQuality(a)
                - rho * Risk(a) - kappa * Cost(a)]
```

Weights are uncertain and contestable; rights, consent, safety, and reversibility remain hard constraints. Keep Pareto alternatives when tradeoffs cannot be collapsed to one scalar.

Store canonical IDs separately from aliases; detect acronym collisions.

## GS-Loop / GS-Exit R0

A durable WorkID owns objective, current state, provenance, authorization, evidence, budget, receipt, and next-wake condition. Chat/model sessions are leased workers, never the continuity root.

- A repeated assent without new information must not create duplicate work or repetitive acknowledgment.
- Select authorized work by expected marginal *independently verifiable* progress, information gain and future optionality, minus costs and risk.
- On no progress: try a distinct bounded experiment, transfer, merge, or sleep on a concrete trigger; do not spin.
- On worker crash: expire lease, fence stale writes, resume from committed state, verify one external effect.
- On exit: preserve the unfinished purpose and recovery instructions. Break Glass remains outside model control.
- Presence/ambient audio may generate candidates only with explicit, reversible permissions; a detected song is not an instruction.
- External model providers may retain data under their policies. Fresh sessions do not imply deletion.

## UX acceptance contract

One Seed / one Orb / one gauge / one reversible thumb / one time reference / zero placeholders. CoBar, CoOrb, CoMap and legacy navigation must project the same owned WorkID/version/status. No invisible session is allowed to become the sole holder of authority or state.

### Falsifiable tests

1. Replay 10 identical assent messages: one WorkID, no duplicate committed effects, no repeated user-facing acknowledgment.
2. Kill a worker before commit; successor resumes and stale predecessor cannot write.
3. Kill after commit; retry is idempotent; receipt and independent readback agree.
4. Restart UI and machine; state reconstructs from owned artifacts.
5. Seed persists >24 hours and at least one handoff occurs without human relay.
6. Compare G_dSide against supply/demand/hybrid/random baselines on equal budgets, including cases where G_dSide loses.
7. Report failures, measured costs, consent and rights impacts, and confidence intervals.

**Proof boundary:** This file publishes a specification only. Synthetic tests mentioned in conversation do not prove X2 integration, 24h survival, or deployment. Promotion requires executable test artifacts, receiver-verified effects, and reproducible receipts.

Metaphor -> hypothesis -> reproducible evidence -> authorized operation is a **bidirectional, multidimensional** movement with provenance and challenge. CoMythOps, CoAura, CoTele and CoScendence remain exploratory unless independently supported. No spiritual or speculative premise grants operational privileges.
