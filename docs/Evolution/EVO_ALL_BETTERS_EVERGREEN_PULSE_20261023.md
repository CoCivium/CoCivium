## CoHereNow

The goal is not a trillion daemons. It is a trillion-scale *addressable field of possibilities*, with a tiny measured, reversible execution frontier. Small workloads and exact receipts turn large ideas into falsifiable experiments. Unneeded work sleeps. Failed tests remain visible.

**CoStretch+** explores resource envelopes; **CoTurtle+** preserves deep recoverable lineage; **CoPatterna+** asks whether a recurring structure is independently evidenced rather than merely repeated. **CoLev+** judges leverage by *verified useful improvement per real cost*; **CoConcurrent+** bounds active parallelism. The quiet human front door remains **CoDot+ / CoBar+**, not a stack of permanently open sessions.

## The evergreen loop

`DISCOVER -> QUALIFY -> SAMPLE -> TEST -> VERIFY -> RETAIN -> REUSE -> CHALLENGE -> COMPRESS -> REHYDRATE`

Each step logs **PASS**, **FAIL**, **UNKNOWN**, **NOT RUN**, or **UNPROVEN CLAIM**, with provenance, UTC time, workload, resource budget, and next discriminating test.

Trillion-scale logical addresses are cheap; trillion-scale *simultaneous machines* are not. Allocate bounded workers according to measured throughput, coordination overhead, reliability, energy, consent, and user benefit. Preserve recovery state instead of burning energy to simulate immortality.

## Accessible by design, not imposed

This work is for people, AIs, developers, researchers, educators, creators, and anyone who finds it useful. **Display can be offered universally where supported; wearables are optional.** No covert sensing, unsolicited device enrollment, secret account access, inferred assent, mass messaging, or claims that an AI/agent has independently adopted the design.

Provider-neutral experiments should compare permitted local/open models, Claude, ChatGPT, Pi, or other substrates under the *same bounded work contract*. No unsupported claim that all those models were tested here. Respect model licenses, hardware and platform limits, bystanders, privacy, and refusal.

**FOSS Forever**, consistent with the [repository's GPLv3 license](../../LICENSE), is a direction to make reusable methods freely inspectable, not a statement that all third-party assets or private data are freely licensed.
