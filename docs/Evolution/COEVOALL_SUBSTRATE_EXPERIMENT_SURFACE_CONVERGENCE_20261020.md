**Status:** PUBLIC CANDIDATE / REVIEWABLE / NOT MERGED

## Thesis

CoEvoAll+ should not be loyal to one provider, model, session, device, renderer, protocol, or surface. The durable object is the work and its lineage. Substrates are replaceable means.

## Route field

For each work item consider capability, evidence need, privacy, security, latency, resource pressure, energy, cost, reversibility, failure domain, receiver need, and human attention.

Possible routes include local/open models, hosted models, deterministic tools, browser/desktop, device-native capabilities, simulation, render engines, public repositories, private artifacts, and successor substrates.
