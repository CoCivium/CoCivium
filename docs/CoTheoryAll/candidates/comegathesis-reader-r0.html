07 / Wonder and doubt

ET, pre-life, forgetting, and the unknown

The question of pre-life choosing to forget may be philosophically rich. It remains a metaphysical proposition. ET detection is a separate scientific question. Functional forgetting in artificial systems can be tested, but success would not prove pre-life agency or consciousness.

Open the alternatives and turtles

H0: ordinary emergence; H1: information becomes inaccessible; H2: adaptive forgetting; H3: simulated memory partition; H4: pre-embodied chosen forgetting, unverified; H5: unknown substrate; H6: philosophical or mythic interpretation.

Who chose? How can choice precede identity? If forgetting destroys its own evidence, how could this hypothesis differ from no previous agent? What is the strongest simpler explanation?
