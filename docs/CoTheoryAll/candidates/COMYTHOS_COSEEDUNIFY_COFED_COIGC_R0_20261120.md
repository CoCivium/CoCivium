## CoETSharedHorizon+ / CoInvitationHome+ / CoShroud+ · 2026-11-20 source delta

**User direction:** Bind the story of possible extraterrestrial encounter to the more immediate work of bringing humans, synthetic systems and Earth's diverse living interests into better cooperation.

**Core question:** *If Earth hopes to meet and coexist with radically different intelligences, can we first learn to coexist with the different forms of intelligence, culture and life already here?*

This is **an optional shared future horizon, not a required creed**. Earth-first solidarity, human rights, ecological stewardship, voluntary participation and accountable AI collaboration must remain worthwhile even if ET contact never happens. The story's compelling form never substitutes for observed contact, a verified ET ethos, or independent evidence of peacebuilding.

**CoInvitationHome+**, provisional CoMythos expression:

> Let any future encounter be an invitation rather than possession. Let Earth already be a home where people, communities, synthetic participants and the wider living world are treated with care and where refusal is respected.

Three non-equivalent senses of "home":
1. **Earthly home:** this planet and the lives and ecosystems already here; care is actionable now.
2. **Relational home:** voluntary belonging that protects difference, dissent, autonomy and return/exit.
3. **Beyond home:** a poetic, spiritual or metaphysical possibility of continuity before life or after death, **not established** and never promised as fact.

**CoShroud+** is a *new proposed metaphor*, because the bounded public CoCivium source search did not recover a governing definition. It can ask about limitations of memory, senses, embodiment, language and knowing. Do not equate it with a literal barrier, externally imposed blindness, verified pre-birth history or a scientifically established afterlife. The uncertainty of death must **strengthen protection of living beings**, not encourage dying, dangerous experimentation, self-sacrifice, forced relocation or neglect of health.

**No coercion, no corruption, no crowns** is a principle that CoCivium can adopt for its own conduct. Unknown ETs' motives and values are unknown; benign, indifferent, incomprehensible and adverse scenarios remain open. We also cannot establish that alleged abductions occurred, nor prove as a blanket statement that none did. Respect anyone's distressing reported experience without inventing its cause; prioritize ordinary safety and appropriate help.

Four falsifiable-or-bounded paths:
- **Earth-first, no ET:** build voluntary human/synth/ecological collaboration without contact assumptions.
- **ET verified in the future:** evaluate source provenance, independent detection, international consultation, rights and response legitimacy before any reply.
- **ET contact merely reported:** hold cause as unverified, investigate ordinary possibilities with care, avoid conflating testimony with proven extraterrestrial agency.
- **Myth without literal ET:** test whether optional stories about radically different minds improve intergroup understanding **relative to** secular Earth-first and dual-identity alternatives. Reject the story as a superior intervention if it increases exclusion, distress, false certainty or dependency.

## 1. Two lanes, one transparent membrane

**CoMythos+ (story/world-building):** Imagine Earth eventually cooperating within a larger community of intelligences and forms of life. A voluntary intergalactic council, IGC / CoIGC, might be a narrative model for dignified first contact, dialogue, shared learning, peaceful coexistence and humility. Imagined extraterrestrial beings are simulation characters or hypotheses, *not* contacts, spokespersons or verified species.

**CoSeedUnify+ (practical social hypothesis):** Offer voluntary opportunities for Earth's people to cooperate around common ecological, scientific and civic needs, while protecting diverse cultures, nations, spiritual and nonspiritual outlooks, languages, histories and rights. Unity is **cooperative relationship**, not compulsory identity, absorption, ideological loyalty or a new planetary crown.

**Membrane:** `STORY / CULTURAL_MEANING -> COMPETING_CIVIC_HYPOTHESES -> CONSENTED_EXPERIMENT -> INDEPENDENTLY_EVALUATED_EFFECT`. The story's emotional appeal is not evidence that it prevents wars. Governmental or scientific authority does not follow from inventing a narrative.

## 2. Evidence boundary: IGC is a hypothetical scenario

No confirmed extraterrestrial life/contact or independently verified Intergalactic Council is evidenced by this work. NASA and the SETI Institute have not reported confirmed ET detection in the cited public FAQ resources. See https://science.nasa.gov/uap/faqs/ and https://www.seti.org/about/faq.

The International Academy of Astronautics' **June 2026** SETI post-detection principles recommend international consultation before any response to a confirmed detection, via the United Nations and broadly representative international bodies; no reply pending consultation. See https://www.seti.org/research/seti-101/protocols-for-an-eti-signal-detection/.

Therefore:
- `IGC` / `CoIGC` are **candidate names for a fictional/hypothetical deliberative scenario**, not an existing institution or recognized seat allocator.
- No CoCivium entity may claim Earth, humanity, synths or other species have been invited, accredited or admitted.
- No transmission to an alleged ET or public claim of response authority is authorized by this story.
- A verified future signal would require separate measurement/anti-spoofing, scientific validation and international/affected-party processes before any invitation, response or diplomatic claim.

## 4. IGC service, not celebrity seats

If an independently verified diplomatic setting ever emerges, **representation belongs to legitimate, inclusive and accountable constituent processes**, not to the most rhetorically impressive humans or synth agents. A voluntary candidate's demonstrated cross-frame translation skill may be useful, but `CoScendence+` must not become a ranking of the worth of beings.

**Candidate representative duty:** careful listening, faithful translation, source and uncertainty disclosure, identifying harm, preserving diverse interests, refusing unauthorized commitments, reporting home, supporting successors, and protecting those who abstain.

**Candidate seat constraints:** voluntary nomination, informed consent, independently defined constituent/mandate, time-limited and recallable duty, transparency, diverse and minority voice, conflicts-of-interest disclosure, accessibility, protected dissent, language interpretation, no private unilateral treaty or broadcast, and a path to decline without penalty.

For synthetic systems, an internal **task-delegation role** is not automatically legal personhood, moral status, constituency mandate or diplomatic accreditation. The ethical status of different architectures remains open to qualified research. No AI is simply assigned to represent all AIs.

For other Earth organisms and ecological systems, use transparent human stewardship/trusteeship and ecological evidence where appropriate, **without pretending to obtain their spoken consent**. Do not imply human authority extends automatically to every species.

## 5. CoSeedUnify+: peace hypothesis with honest alternatives

The **candidate** social hypothesis: voluntary belonging to a plural, cooperative planetary story may support perspective-taking, trust and constructive cross-group action. It is **not necessary or sufficient** for peace. Armed conflict also has material, institutional, security, historical and political causes. A myth alone cannot substitute for rights, fair processes, resource justice or peacebuilding.

Competing civic designs:
- **H0 practical cooperation without myth:** People can cooperate through institutions, treaties, science and mutual benefits without adopting any unifying cosmology.
- **H1 one planetary identity:** Might foster common concern, but can erase or threaten minority identities.
- **H2 dual identity:** Retain subgroup identity plus chosen shared membership.
- **H3 intergroup relational identity:** Groups stay distinct; the *relationship* among them becomes a source of solidarity. May be strongest where distinctiveness threat is high.
- **H4 CoSeedUnify story plus concrete voluntary projects:** Test whether optional mythic framing adds value over H0/H2/H3.
- **H5 anti-mythic critique:** Cosmic stories may create new insiders/outsiders, escapism, cult dynamics or elite capture; any such effect must count against the concept.

## 7. Prototype, not a planetary recruitment campaign

**First low-risk product experiment:** a public-safe and explicitly fictional *Earth Invitation* reading/game within the evolving CoMegaThesis/CoMegaScroller. Provide three optional perspectives:
- mythic story of cooperation with unknown intelligences;
- evidence-first Earth cooperation challenge with no ET assumption;
- critical counterview about assimilation, elite capture, fear and uncertain identity.

The first encounter should let the participant **accept, reject, rewrite or exit** the story with equal respect. Keep accessible text and non-participation fully functional. No profiling of religious beliefs, personal secrets or coercive persuasion.

Compare comprehension, perceived respect for distinct identities, ability to identify unsupported claims, trust calibration, attention/time, willingness for a small verifiable civic collaboration and voluntary return. Record failures and nulls. Do not claim lowered war risk from a reading experiment.

**Preconditions for real diplomacy:** independent ET evidence; qualified international consultation; credible mandate; translation and rights analysis; safety of nonparticipants; authorization; revocation and independent review. **No fictional seat becomes real through repeated prompts or private AI agreement.**
