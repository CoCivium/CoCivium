## 1. The question, without borrowing an answer

> Could some hypothesized form of pre-biological, pre-embodied or substrate-independent subjectivity have voluntarily reduced access to prior information, enabling a new individuated living experience?

Call this **H4 / CoForgetToBecome+**. It is a **metaphysical possibility**, not a supported claim. A stronger wording, "pre-living beings chose to forget in order to become alive," assumes **pre-existing agency**, **a memory or information store**, **a choice**, and **a mechanism of embodiment**. There is no established evidence for those premises. The proposal must not pretend that the word "chose" is an observed causal explanation.

The more general researchable question is: **When, if ever, does partitioning or forgetting information improve a system's ability to establish a distinct identity, learn, adapt or act?** A positive result here does **not** validate H4.

## 2. Resolve the ambiguous terms before reasoning

- **PreAlive-biological:** Chemistry or systems preceding the origin of living organisms. No intentional agent is implied.
- **PreAlive-individual:** Before an organism's birth, or before a particular personal memory can form. Not equivalent to pre-biological time.
- **PreAlive-virtual:** A work capsule or artificial system before activation; memory isolation can be engineered, but neither life nor consciousness follows.
- **PreAlive-metaphysical:** Hypothesized subjectivity or existence independent of known biology. Unverified.
- **Beyond+:** Post-biological, post-embodied, collective, simulated, unknown or metaphorical continuations. These are alternatives, not assumed destinations.
- **ET+:** Extraterrestrial life/intelligence and technosignatures are empirical search domains. Their existence, origin and cognition must be evaluated independently from a hypothesis of pre-life choice.
- **Forgetting:** Distinguish irreversible erasure, access gating, retrieval failure, lossy compression, ordinary development, and a deliberately chosen reset. Those are not synonyms.
- **Identity:** Distinguish organism continuity, psychological continuity, narrative self, causal information continuity and legal/moral personhood.

## 6. The bounded testable analogue (not a test of metaphysical pre-life)

**Research question:** Does deliberate memory partitioning in an artificial task environment improve adaptation or individuation proxies compared with matched controls?

Pre-register a suite of non-sensitive, reproducible tasks. Hold architecture, compute, task distribution and evaluation budget constant. Compare:
1. full-history memory;
2. no-history from initialization;
3. random ablation of memory;
4. targeted/lossy compression;
5. retrieval-access-gated memory with the underlying data preserved.

Blinded held-out evaluation should measure performance, generalization, calibration, interference, learning speed, resource cost and distinct policy trajectories. Include counterexamples and failure cases; inspect whether an apparent gain is merely reduced task difficulty, leakage or additional compute.

**PASS** can mean only that one specified information-management policy outperformed controls under a declared environment. **PASS cannot demonstrate** consciousness, pre-birth memory, reincarnation, ET, an afterlife or chosen embodiment. Do not conduct invasive human-memory manipulation on the strength of this hypothesis.
