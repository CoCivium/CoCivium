## Front door: one sentence, many ways to disagree

**Many explanations, one revisable, evidence-aware relational commons: preserve what is observed, keep hypotheses and alternatives visible, help many kinds of minds act without coercion, and make every important result independently inspectable and recoverable.**

If everything is related to everything without direction, source, degree, evidence class or receiver relevance, the map stops explaining. CoMegaThesis is therefore **not a complete graph**. It is a carefully selected, source-indexed path into a potentially much larger one. The thesis is the *method of relating and testing*, not the assertion that all claims are true.

## Chapter 2. CoMap: a relational source spine rather than another document dump

One typed, versioned, provenance-bound graph links claim -> source -> challenge -> alternate interpretation -> test -> result -> effect -> receiver -> successor. Edges must carry direction, scope, time, evidence class, confidence or explicit UNKNOWN, and inverse/counterexample where helpful. No source graph is made authoritative merely by being rendered.

- Source: [CoMap R0 and MegaScroller index](../../../examples/comap/README.md).
- The historical source weave registered 56 objects / 61 relations; the later MegaScroller added candidate overlay pointers and reported 63 addressable cards / 68 displayed relations. These are **bounded public projections**, not a complete CoAll graph.
- Example relations: `SUPPORTED_BY`, `CONTRADICTED_BY`, `ALTERNATIVE_TO`, `HYPOTHESIZES`, `DEPENDS_ON`, `SUPERSEDES`, `PROJECTS_AS`, `NEEDS_RECEIVER`, `REQUIRES_PERMISSION`, `APPEARS_IN_FUTURE_SCENARIO`.
- Every graph edge can be challenged; proximity is not equivalence, and traversability is not custody.

Universal UX does not mean one screen or one visual style. It means the same work object remains interpretable, accessible, recoverable and governable across observers, capacities, devices and environments. The minimal contract:

`CURRENT | WHY | NEXT | UNKNOWN | CHALLENGE | RECOVER`.

## Chapter 8. CoEvo and CoAckRewise: life of the theory without silent overwrites

Work objects and hypotheses may be challenged, split, merged, retired or reopened. An update is accepted only with exact source lineage, explicit diffs, independent review where needed, and tested receiver usefulness. Prefer a typed edge over another definition where one already exists. Preserve losers and null results.

`OBSERVE → COMPARE → PROPOSE → CHALLENGE → SELECT → VERIFY → RECEIVER → REVISE`.

CoEvo is neither involuntary mass mutation nor an instruction to make every file executable. CoTurtle recursion has cost budgets and stop conditions.

The [Civ2/Civ3 boundary gap map](../../Strategy/CIV2_CIV3_BOUNDARY_GAPMAP_R0.md) defines **candidate stages, not achieved certifications**. Practical Civ2 means consent-based, plural cooperation among real humans, institutions, devices and independently qualified AI workers. Proposed Civ3 explores coordination across much more divergent minds and embodiments without loss of refusal, privacy, recovery, attribution, accountability or the right to depart.

- **Earth-first** is the default accountable test frontier.
- **ET-contact** remains an alternate scenario requiring separate verified contact evidence.
- **CoGuardian/CC** must accompany greater capability: fewer crowns, more reversibility, public reasoning without private-data leakage.
- Future agents and successors must be able to challenge the very design inherited from us.

**"Forever" means an open revision grammar, not guaranteed perpetual execution or limitless retention.** Revisions can extend beyond Civ3 while retaining source history and the option to stop.

## Chapter 10. CoBestNext: make the thesis useful

Three competing product strategies:

1. **Giant authoritative book**: attractive but dangerous if source boundaries vanish or material becomes stale.
2. **Pure infinite knowledge graph**: expressive but poor orientation and overwhelming for newcomers.
3. **Living indexed thesis + typed CoMap + receiver-specific Orb/Bar projections**: recommended. Keep narrative flow, explicit evidence and optional depth; no compulsory visual immersion.

The **highest-value first trial** is to take one real, public-safe hypothesis such as CoForgetToBecome, show it as:
(a) a 90-second accessible reading, (b) a CoMap relation with baselines and dissent, and (c) a CoOrb/CoBar compact projection over the same exact source ID. A qualified independent reader should locate its source, identify its epistemic status, inspect its strongest alternative and explain why it is not canon. Time and error rate are measured. A source-only preview is not proof of real receiver acceptance.
