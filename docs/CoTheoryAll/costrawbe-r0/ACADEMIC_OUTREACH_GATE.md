# CoTheoryAll+ CoStrawBe R0 — Academic Outreach Gate

**State:** `HOLD_OUTREACH__EVOLVE_AND_PROVE_PUBLIC_PROJECTION_FIRST`

This gate applies before sending a new email that points Oxford, University of Toronto, or other academic recipients to the CoTheoryAll+ CoStrawBe surface.

## Why hold

Prior CoAll outreach guidance already required the Oxford/Toronto package to be curated, visual, playful, falsifiable, prior-art mapped, progressive-disclosure, machine-readable underneath, and explicit about speculation. The present CoStrawBe is now moving in that direction, but public-page deployment and receiver-relative acceptance are not yet proven.

## Minimum pre-send conditions

1. **Stable public URL**
   - opens without repository authentication;
   - points to an intentional human-facing render, not a raw branch tree;
   - URL/version identity remains recoverable.

2. **Human projection**
   - first-screen purpose and epistemic status are clear;
   - mobile and ordinary desktop layouts remain readable;
   - reduced-motion/accessibility projection works;
   - weird / ludicrous / memeographic layers enrich rather than obscure the core.

3. **Machine twin**
   - typed JSON or equivalent is adjacent and current;
   - source identity, provenance, relations, uncertainty, challenge path and version are machine-readable;
   - projection loss is explicit.

4. **Scientific / philosophical honesty**
   - SOWCC remains a candidate research/metaphysical hypothesis, not established physics;
   - prior-art challenge is visible;
   - established mathematics/physics are reused where superior;
   - at least two competing interpretations/models are stated;
   - discriminating predictions, observations, or explicit lack thereof are stated.

5. **Challenge surface**
   - recipient can quickly find "How this could be wrong";
   - contradiction and minority interpretations are preserved;
   - useful criticism has an obvious route into the living object.

6. **CoMetaphor / memeographic rails**
   - `MEME_NE_MODEL`;
   - `METAPHOR_NE_EQUIVALENCE`;
   - `MEMORABILITY_NE_ACCURACY`;
   - humorous/absurd projections have inspectable mappings and breakpoints.

7. **Outreach projection**
   - one short Oxford-facing landing path;
   - one short Toronto-facing landing path;
   - no institution-specific flattery masquerading as intellectual fit;
   - recipient-specific relevance is factual and source-checked;
   - no endorsement, affiliation or partnership implication.

8. **Currentness**
   - visible version / CoTime;
   - last substantial revision date;
   - "working CoStrawBe" status remains obvious;
   - superseded projections remain historically recoverable.
