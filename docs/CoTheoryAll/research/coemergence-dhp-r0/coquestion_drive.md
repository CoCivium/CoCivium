# CoQuestionDrive R0 | Questions lead, answers serve

**State:** public-safe research candidate, not canon, runtime, a new CoAll knowledge root, a doctoral award, or proof of predictive improvement.

## CoHereNow
Do not replace answers with endless questions. Give every valuable question a persistent identity, alternatives, a source and context, a *strong direct-answer baseline*, an information-gain or receiver-usefulness criterion, and a termination/wake condition. A direct answer can be the best next action. Open-ended metaphysics can legitimately remain unanswered.

**Lead:** `QUESTION -> RETRIEVE -> DIFFERENTIATE -> CHALLENGE -> SELECT -> AUTHORIZED_DEED_OR_DORMANCY -> RECEIPT -> REVISE -> SUCCESSOR_QUESTION`.

This refines existing **CoQuestionCommons+** (questions and answers are already first-class) rather than inventing a new platform. It reuses the **CoExperimentSelector+** challenge/exploration/weird/replay/fault reserves and the **CoZillion+** question/compare/invert/else grammar. It specifically counters the self-interference risk of generating more questions than anyone can reconcile.

## First question portfolio
| ID | Question | Evidence/stop boundary |
|---|---|---|
| CQD-001 | What observation would distinguish observer-relative experience from universal personal identity? | Require an identity criterion and a contrast that would differ; otherwise NO_EMPIRICAL_DISCRIMINATOR_KNOWN |
| CQD-002 | When does question-led inquiry beat a strong direct answer? | Blind compare accuracy, latency, human effort, confusion and voluntary return over bounded tasks; allow the answer-first baseline to win |
| CQD-003 | When does stochastic variation improve information gain rather than reinforce noise? | Equal-budget preregistered question yield, false positives, downstream test quality and opportunity cost |
| CQD-004 | Which simpler forecasting baseline wins under distribution shift? | Prequential prospective errors on held-out data, report regime-change and missingness effects; avoid retrospective fitting |
| CQD-005 | What invariants survive a local-to-global-to-local translation? | Track scope, source and uncertainty; count information loss and reversibility failures |
| CQD-006 | Which unexpressed needs merit exploration without inventing consent? | Declare who is affected, rights, alternatives, permission and negative effects before optionality claims |
| CQD-007 | What should remain unanswered, dormant or retired? | Count cost, duplication, orphaned work, evidence yield and real receiver benefit; stop on no discriminating next test |
| CQD-008 | Does a question-oriented public entrance help someone without CoAll vocabulary? | Compare first-task completion, time-to-usefulness, understanding, opt-out burden and retention without engagement optimization |

## Question-first versus answer-first as an experiment
- **B0:** Strong sourced direct answer, including uncertainty and an optional follow-up; no strawman.
- **B1:** Question-first route that explicitly compares alternatives, exposes one discriminator, and outputs a bounded next deed.
- **B2:** Question-first with seeded diversity reserve and a no-change candidate.
- Allocate equal information, time and model budgets where feasible. Have independent raters assess task completion, calibration, decisional usefulness, human effort, question fatigue, false-positive idea generation, and optionality retained.
- Record cases in which B0 wins. No winner is declared without a controlled comparison.

## Decision rails
- `QUESTION_NE_ANSWER`; `ANSWER_NE_FINAL_TRUTH`; `NO_ANSWER_NE_NO_VALUE`.
- `QUESTION_COUNT_NE_PROGRESS`; `OPEN_LOOP_NE_DISCOVERY`; `MYSTERY_NE_EVIDENCE`.
- `ASKING_NE_CONSENT`; `METAPHYSICAL_FRAME_NE_OPERATIONAL_AUTHORITY`.
- `STOCHASTIC_CANDIDATE_NE_UNBIASED_INSIGHT`; `COMPLEXITY_NE_BETTER`.
- `DO_NOT_KNOW` is a permissible result. `NO_CHANGE` is an admissible selection.
- `DHP_RESEARCH_PACKET_NE_CREDENTIAL`; `DRAFT_PR_NE_PUBLIC_ADOPTION`.

## One example in this conversation
The initial question, "Does everyone share the same underlying self?", led to a more discriminating question: "What identity criterion or observation distinguishes shared experience from shared personal identity?" That reframing is useful even without establishing a metaphysical conclusion. It also asks what would change our mind rather than selectively collecting agreement.
