## CoHereNow

Patterns can improve prediction only when deltas are typed and time-anchored, counterexamples remain visible, and future performance outperforms a strong, simpler benchmark. **Counterpatterns are features.** Never reverse an arrow from association to causality merely because the diagram is pretty.

## Eight independently reviewable cards

### DHP-EM-019: CoDeltaBasis+
**Class:** `FORMALIZATION_AND_DATA_QUALITY` | **Status:** `CANDIDATE_NOT_VALIDATED`

**Proposal:** Delta is meaningful only across comparable units, populations, time windows and measurement definitions.

**Strong baseline:** A trusted plain-text change log with explicitly named denominator and version.

**Challenge / experiment:** Reject a purported delta if units/denominators/scopes differ; measure reconstruction accuracy and time.

**Nonclaim:** No universal numerical delta between ethics and physics; incomparable dimensions remain incomparable.

### DHP-EM-020: CoInverseType+
**Class:** `LOGIC_AND_CAUSAL_DISCIPLINE` | **Status:** `CANDIDATE_NOT_VALIDATED`

**Proposal:** Use different type tags for inverse conditional, logical converse, temporal replay, counterfactual intervention, backcasting and symmetry; none implies another.

**Strong baseline:** Ordinary logic/causal inference notation and clearly annotated Markdown.

**Challenge / experiment:** Reject the claim P(X|Y) = P(Y|X); require evidence of reversibility before reversing causal arrows.

**Nonclaim:** Statistical association does not establish causal effect; inverse model does not imply an inverse physical process.

### DHP-EM-021: CoDirectionAtlas+
**Class:** `REPRESENTATION_AND_INTEROPERABILITY` | **Status:** `CANDIDATE_NOT_VALIDATED`

**Proposal:** A forecast packet may encode directional lenses: future forecast, past explanation, counterfactual, local-to-global, global-to-local, cross-domain and inside/outside CoAll.

**Strong baseline:** Existing multidimensional data cubes, causal DAGs and time-series dashboards.

**Challenge / experiment:** Verify named direction/scope/observer and freeze information cutoff per view; compare view comprehension to ordinary dashboard.

**Nonclaim:** Views may disagree and may be non-equivalent, non-composable or not predictive.

### DHP-EM-022: CoScaleInversion+
**Class:** `MATHEMATICAL_COUNTEREXAMPLE` | **Status:** `CANDIDATE_NOT_VALIDATED`

**Proposal:** Aggregate trends can oppose all within-group trends when population composition changes (Simpson-style reversal).

**Strong baseline:** Simple spreadsheet grouped counts with matched rates and denominators.

**Challenge / experiment:** Supply the finite two-period example in the executable fixture; require aggregated and group-wise comparisons and a fixed-mix counterfactual.

**Nonclaim:** The synthetic example says nothing about real-world causality or prevalence.

### DHP-EM-023: CoRetroPredict+
**Class:** `TEMPORAL_EVALUATION` | **Status:** `CANDIDATE_NOT_VALIDATED`

**Proposal:** A forecast, a retrodiction and a backcast are different tasks with different evidence-cutoff and success criteria.

**Strong baseline:** Ordinary version-controlled prequential evaluation, historical control series and explicit goal-seeking planning.

**Challenge / experiment:** Prohibit any input observed after issuance for forecasts; keep revised historical knowledge separate; compare future-only error and planning feasibility.

**Nonclaim:** Reconstructing the past after seeing outcomes does not establish foresight.

### DHP-EM-024: CoContextTransfer+
**Class:** `TRANSFER_AND_EXTERNAL_VALIDITY` | **Status:** `CANDIDATE_NOT_VALIDATED`

**Proposal:** Local, global, inside-CoAll and outside-CoAll transfers require explicit invariants, boundary conditions and target-domain holdouts.

**Strong baseline:** Standard stratified analysis and domain adaptation with a well-maintained comparison table.

**Challenge / experiment:** Require pre-registered source-to-target mapping; evaluate on unseen target domain; report mapping losses and group harms.

**Nonclaim:** Global validity is not inferred from local success; CoAll graph inclusion grants no authority over outside systems.

### DHP-EM-025: CoStrategicPareto+
**Class:** `NORMATIVE_DECISION_RESEARCH` | **Status:** `CANDIDATE_NOT_VALIDATED`

**Proposal:** Select robust, consent-respecting actions from multiple feasible futures; preserve incomparable good options rather than crown a scalar winner.

**Strong baseline:** A strong ordinary decision table of cost, harm, reversibility, alternative, and explicit stakeholder constraints.

**Challenge / experiment:** Small synthetic target-feasibility example plus a hard-gated rights scenario; compare human decision quality and effort to simple baseline.

**Nonclaim:** A G_dSide framing is a revisable value lens, not a measurable divine command or automatic action authority.

### DHP-EM-026: CoEchoDamp+
**Class:** `ROBUSTNESS_AND_ADVERSARIAL_TESTING` | **Status:** `CANDIDATE_NOT_VALIDATED`

**Proposal:** Reinforcing a repeated pattern without independent evidence can amplify error; each boost needs a dissent, negative-control and expiration path.

**Strong baseline:** Append-only contradiction ledger and automated expiry using off-the-shelf version control.

**Challenge / experiment:** Inject duplicated reports, synthetic feedback loops and later contradictory evidence; verify confidence cannot rise from repetition alone.

**Nonclaim:** No claim that recursively braided output improves truth, forecasting, or returns by itself.

## Finite synthetic proof obligations

The adjacent pure-Python fixture tests: (i) typed unit mismatch; (ii) inverse conditional vs converse; (iii) within-group/aggregate reversal with a fixed-mix counterfactual; (iv) forecast information-cutoff; (v) strict preservation of a feasible lower-cost action; (vi) rights and consent hard gate; (vii) result determinism; (viii) no credential or authority promotion. These are tests of software behavior and well-defined counterexamples, not of predictive accuracy in the world.

## Baselines and stop rule

Compare a well-written, sourced plain-text table and conventional stratified spreadsheet models with this structured representation. Primary endpoints: valid inference reconstruction, held-out forecast error and calibration, harmful false transfer rate, human time and error correction cost. Pause or retire the extra Co-prefixed wrappers if simpler tools win. Avoid selective metrics and Goodhart loops.

## Synthetic-scale counterexample

| Time | Group A successes/total | Group B successes/total | Pooled |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| t0 | 90/100 = 90% | 10/100 = 10% | 100/200 = 50% |
| t1 | 80/100 = 80% | 0/20 = 0% | 80/120 = 66.7% |

Both group rates **decrease**, while the pooled rate **increases**. At t0 group weights, the t1 rate would be 40%, not 66.7%. Composition changed. This does not identify a cause or imply similar real-world behavior.
