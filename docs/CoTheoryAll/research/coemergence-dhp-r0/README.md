# CoEmergence+ × CoDhP+ | Evidence-First Research Cards R0

**WorkID:** `COEMERGENCE_DHP_GOLDENBRAID_RESEARCH_PACKET_R0_20261109`  
**State:** Public-safe *draft candidate* for review; not a credential, proven discovery, canon, runtime, or active academic research cohort.  
**Purpose:** Produce bounded, challengeable research contributions from CoTheoryAll+ / CoGoldenBraid+ without pretending a more impressive number of cards proves progress.

## What CoEmergence means here
A candidate synthesis counts as useful only if it yields a **new, independently reviewable question, counterexample, testable hypothesis, or clearer representation** beyond its inputs. Simple paraphrasing, longer lists, or several AIs agreeing is not evidence of emergence.

## DhP boundary
DhP and the proposed `CoDhP+` research facility are **not** PhD, an awarded degree, a professional credential, a title of doctor, a nomination, or an institutional endorsement. The earlier private DhP prototype is not a public directory and is not reproduced here.

## The first 12 cards

### DHP-EM-001: Reality, observer, and model are different
**Lane / claim type:** CoTheoryAll / `EMPIRICAL_MODELING_HYPOTHESIS`

**Proposal:** A model can be observer-conditioned without the external world being unreal.
**Strong rival:** Strictly world-independent perceptions or metaphysical idealism.
**Strong baseline:** Well-sourced introductory psychology and ordinary measurement model.
**Challenge or test:** Specify shared stimulus, record observer variation and interventions; reserve ontology as a separate question.
**Stop / fail:** No prediction or explanation beyond ordinary observation models.
**Potential benefit:** Prevent model-to-reality conflation.
**Nonclaim:** Observer-relative does not establish world-as-illusion.
**Source keys:** cotheory, costrawbe

### DHP-EM-002: Universal relatedness does not imply one personal identity
**Lane / claim type:** CoTheoryAll / `LOGICAL_NON_ENTAILMENT`

**Proposal:** Entities can be connected by shared relations yet remain numerically distinct.
**Strong rival:** Open individualism positing a single underlying subject.
**Strong baseline:** Two distinct observers in a shared communication network.
**Challenge or test:** Construct a two-observer countermodel where connectedness holds and distinctness also holds; open individualism requires a separate identity argument.
**Stop / fail:** Countermodel impossible under explicit defensible identity axioms; otherwise maintain both branches.
**Potential benefit:** Separate empathy from identity speculation.
**Nonclaim:** No accepted empirical discriminator for shared self is claimed.
**Source keys:** cotheory

### DHP-EM-003: God-as-totality does not logically imply divine intention
**Lane / claim type:** CoGodstuff / `LOGICAL_NON_ENTAILMENT`

**Proposal:** A stipulated equation God := Universe says nothing alone about what the totality wants.
**Strong rival:** Personal/intentional deity with independent purpose-bearing claims.
**Strong baseline:** Compare an impersonal pantheistic model with an agentive model.
**Challenge or test:** Write interpretations in which God=U is true and Wants(God,x) is undefined/false; demand independent premises for intention.
**Stop / fail:** Independent persuasive agentive premises are supplied and reviewed.
**Potential benefit:** Explore spirituality without inventing commands.
**Nonclaim:** No revelation or instruction from God has been observed.
**Source keys:** public primary source needed

### DHP-EM-004: Contingency and observer-selection
**Lane / claim type:** CoTheoryAll / `PHILOSOPHICAL_AND_STATISTICAL_QUESTION`

**Proposal:** A strikingly improbable personal history alone does not prove a guided pathway.
**Strong rival:** Intentional selection of a particular outcome.
**Strong baseline:** Condition on an observer being able to report a history; compare unconditional and conditional likelihoods.
**Challenge or test:** Build a finite sample-space toy that shows observer selection and discuss limits of extrapolating to cosmology.
**Stop / fail:** A selected model provides distinct prior predictions and supported evidence.
**Potential benefit:** Reduce hindsight teleology.
**Nonclaim:** A toy selection effect does not disprove a deity.
**Source keys:** public primary source needed

### DHP-EM-005: Typed nothing, unknown, absence and zero
**Lane / claim type:** CoPriMath / `SOFTWARE_INVARIANT`

**Proposal:** A typed evidence system can prevent UNKNOWN, NOT_OBSERVED, ABSENT, and MEASURED_ZERO from being silently exchanged.
**Strong rival:** A carefully designed plain-text evidence table already suffices.
**Strong baseline:** Strong Markdown table with explicit four-value definitions and source links.
**Challenge or test:** Feed renderer malformed status mappings; require loss logging and rejection for status upgrades; compare Markdown + automation.
**Stop / fail:** Baseline is clearer or lower cost at equal accuracy.
**Potential benefit:** Avoid fictional certainty in CoNull and CoTime.
**Nonclaim:** A typed field is not a new mathematical foundation.
**Source keys:** public primary source needed

### DHP-EM-006: Hypothesis identity survives view changes
**Lane / claim type:** CoPriMath / `SOFTWARE_INVARIANT`

**Proposal:** A claim ID, evidence type, and revision can remain referentially stable across text and graph views.
**Strong rival:** Existing W3C PROV / standard linked documents or version control suffice.
**Strong baseline:** Well-structured Markdown plus exact Git history and source links.
**Challenge or test:** Render then decode the identifier and type; inject missing provenance and record which transformations fail.
**Stop / fail:** Reference baseline matches reliability at lower burden.
**Potential benefit:** Cross-surface reconstructibility and auditability.
**Nonclaim:** Projections are not proof of ontology.
**Source keys:** cotheory

### DHP-EM-007: Emergent insight requires more than recombination count
**Lane / claim type:** CoEmergence / `EMPIRICAL_RESEARCH_PROTOCOL`

**Proposal:** Cross-domain synthesis may yield useful, independently challengeable questions not proposed by source strands alone.
**Strong rival:** Concatenation, generic prompting or random association yields similar quality.
**Strong baseline:** Equal-budget strong plain-text brainstorming with separate expert-informed source summaries.
**Challenge or test:** Preregister novelty, falsifiability and utility rubrics; blind judges to origin; include negative controls and cost.
**Stop / fail:** No gain after accounting for reviewer agreement, effort and confounds.
**Potential benefit:** Improve useful research question generation rather than asset count.
**Nonclaim:** Emergence is not yet a measured novelty mechanism.
**Source keys:** cotheory

### DHP-EM-008: Golden braid preserves rivals instead of averaging them
**Lane / claim type:** CoGoldenBraid / `SOFTWARE_INVARIANT`

**Proposal:** A typed, bounded braid can retain incompatible models with context, uncertainty and lineage.
**Strong rival:** Simple indexed Markdown notes and a standard knowledge graph provide equivalent value.
**Strong baseline:** A conventional model comparison table with versioned links.
**Challenge or test:** Inject cycles, stale references and competing claims; ensure termination, no implicit merge, and exact version retention.
**Stop / fail:** Braid loses information or consumes substantially more work than baseline.
**Potential benefit:** Retain minority views and preserve alternative futures.
**Nonclaim:** No proof of operational superiority or runtime self-coordination.
**Source keys:** goldenbraid, wisdom

### DHP-EM-009: G_dSide as revisable value inquiry, not divine command
**Lane / claim type:** G_dSide / `NORMATIVE_RESEARCH_PROTOCOL`

**Proposal:** Compare plural candidate futures against demand/supply baselines using agency, wellbeing, rights and reversibility.
**Strong rival:** Demand/supply or a simple hybrid can outperform G_dSide proposals.
**Strong baseline:** Supply-only, demand-only, combined, and random policies under matched budgets.
**Challenge or test:** Create synthetic decision records, preregister axes and report Pareto incomparability, harms, cost and no-gain cases.
**Stop / fail:** Alternative methods dominate or rights/consent costs outweigh benefits.
**Potential benefit:** Represent interests of future and unrepresented stakeholders.
**Nonclaim:** G_dSide is not a detected spiritual force or an authority source.
**Source keys:** goldenbraid

### DHP-EM-010: DhP research dossiers are not credentials
**Lane / claim type:** CoDhP / `INSTITUTIONAL_DESIGN_NORM`

**Proposal:** A public-safe, challengeable research dossier can document work without conferring any title.
**Strong rival:** Conventional research portfolio, ORCID, institutional repositories and peer review already cover the need.
**Strong baseline:** A citation-backed research page without a new designation.
**Challenge or test:** Compare reviewer comprehension and attribution errors; require independent issuer, consent and policy before any recognition scheme.
**Stop / fail:** Dossier confuses readers about qualifications or lacks added utility.
**Potential benefit:** Clearer attribution and constructive criticism.
**Nonclaim:** Zero DhP awards, endorsements, nominations or doctorate equivalences asserted.
**Source keys:** public primary source needed

### DHP-EM-011: Open research cards, not an invented cohort
**Lane / claim type:** CoDhP / `RESEARCH_PROTOCOL`

**Proposal:** Finite research cards with source, alternative, testability and loss labels may lower effort to independently evaluate a proposal.
**Strong rival:** A well-written ordinary scholarly abstract and registered protocol already suffice.
**Strong baseline:** Strong plain-text protocol that names prior work, claims, countercases and data.
**Challenge or test:** Give independent readers equal tasks in random order; compare correct inference, time, accessibility and refusal/exit friction.
**Stop / fail:** No benefit or greater misunderstanding than baseline.
**Potential benefit:** Auditable contribution without credential inflation.
**Nonclaim:** The number of cards is not the number of scholars or PhDs.
**Source keys:** public primary source needed

### DHP-EM-012: CoStrawBe first contact should expose disagreement
**Lane / claim type:** CoStrawBe / `HUMAN_UX_HYPOTHESIS`

**Proposal:** A provocative public front door may invite inquiry if alternatives and limitations remain one click away.
**Strong rival:** A conventional clear, neutral summary is more comprehensible and trustworthy.
**Strong baseline:** Equal-length academically responsible prose with source, alternative and caveats.
**Challenge or test:** Randomize reader exposure with opt-in consent; test comprehension, trust calibration, correction and return interest.
**Stop / fail:** Provocation reduces accuracy, excludes readers or attracts unearned confidence.
**Potential benefit:** More informed, willing participation without persuasion tricks.
**Nonclaim:** Memorability does not establish scientific validity.
**Source keys:** costrawbe, cotheory

## Comparative evaluation before promotion
1. Publish a **strong plain-text baseline** containing the same sources, alternatives, tests, limitations, and outcomes; do not use an intentionally weak straw man.
2. Run schema/property tests on JSON. Record what failed and the time/cost of maintaining both views.
3. Have consented, independently recruited readers assess comprehension, accurate uncertainty, correction effort and time-to-useful-question, blinded when possible.
4. Compare B0 and B1 at matched time and information budgets; preserve no-gain, loss, and cases where B0 wins.
5. Require an independent receiver report before claiming improved usefulness. Credential and governance claims remain separately gated.
