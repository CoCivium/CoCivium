# CoPatternPayForward+ / CoTime+ / CoPrediction+ | DhP research addendum R0

**State:** Public-safe research candidate; no credentials, independent peer review, validated predictions, divine instructions, runtime, or public release is asserted by the file itself.

**CoHereNow:** Pattern reuse can help future work only after independently verified transfer. Feedback can compound both learning and error. Every prediction needs a past-only cutoff, future target, horizon, calibration, and a simpler competing baseline.

**CoTume+:** Exact user coinage is preserved as an unresolved candidate. Do not silently equate it with CoTime+, invent its meaning or assert a new temporal primitive.

## Six follow-on research cards

### DHP-EM-013: CoPatternGain+
**Class:** `SOFTWARE_AND_RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS` | **Status:** `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE_NOT_VALIDATED`

**Proposal:** A reusable pattern that survives independent challenge may improve later hypothesis generation per unit of cost.

**Strong rival:** Simple retrieval, templates, or ordinary accumulated experience yield equal improvements.

**Strong baseline:** Versioned plain-text notebook with well-maintained recipes and citations.

**Discriminator or test:** Pre-register pattern, source cohort and target task. Measure later correct reuse, independent replication, human-time saved, false transfers and correction costs versus strong notebook.

**Stop condition:** Repeated phrasing, common model priors or graph densification masquerade as learning; abandon if no net independently verified benefit.

**Receiver benefit:** Reduce repeated investigation without suppressing unusual counterexamples.

**Nonclaim:** Reusable text is not new causal law or evidence of self-evolution.

### DHP-EM-014: CoTime+ / CoTume+ naming and bitemporal foresight
**Class:** `FORMALIZATION_AND_NAMING_CANDIDATE` | **Status:** `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE_NOT_VALIDATED`

**Proposal:** Every predictive relation should distinguish time observed, time valid, training cutoff, forecast issuance and target horizon; the uttered CoTume+ is preserved as an unresolved term alias, not silently redefined.

**Strong rival:** Ordinary event-sourced timestamps, version control and standard temporal databases already suffice.

**Strong baseline:** Simple historical ledger with ISO-8601 UTC timestamps, valid-time and observed-time fields.

**Discriminator or test:** Replay forecast from a frozen cutoff; verify later corrected facts do not leak into earlier forecast. Require a separate user-reviewed definition before treating CoTume+ as a distinct primitive.

**Stop condition:** Any future-data leak, stale-time overwritten source or unauthorized alias promotion blocks adoption.

**Receiver benefit:** Retrospective and prospective consistency across different devices and observers.

**Nonclaim:** A timestamp schema does not enable time travel, retrocausation or prophecy.

### DHP-EM-015: CoPrediction+ calibrated future tests
**Class:** `EMPIRICAL_HYPOTHESIS` | **Status:** `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE_NOT_VALIDATED`

**Proposal:** Historical patterns may improve forecasts on some measurable tasks, with explicit horizons and calibrated uncertainty.

**Strong rival:** An ordinary majority, last-observed or constant-rate baseline performs as well or better; nonstationary regimes destroy the effect.

**Strong baseline:** Prequential majority, last-value and uninformative 0.5 forecasts run on identical frozen synthetic and then independent real task windows.

**Discriminator or test:** Score future-only Brier, log loss and decision regret at matched horizon and budget. Show uncertainty, sample size, target distribution shifts and negative cases.

**Stop condition:** Hold if real prospective gains are null, not reproducible, inaccessible or explained by leakage, multiple testing or trivial covariates.

**Receiver benefit:** Earlier warning of uncertain outcomes without overstated confidence.

**Nonclaim:** No genuine predictive superiority can be established from a synthetic fixture alone.

### DHP-EM-016: CoFeedback+ and CoCounterPattern+
**Class:** `SAFETY_AND_LEARNING_PROTOCOL` | **Status:** `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE_NOT_VALIDATED`

**Proposal:** Every positive feedback edge should have a separately routed disconfirmation, dissent and stop/damp channel.

**Strong rival:** Ordinary audits, holdout validation and contradiction registers achieve the same correction.

**Strong baseline:** Append-only ledger with explicit success and failure entries, source weights, and negative controls.

**Discriminator or test:** Inject a popular but false claim, duplicated model echoes and late contradictory evidence; test that no evidence/authority promotion occurs, that corrective signals reach readers, and that count-based reward cannot dominate.

**Stop condition:** False consensus or hidden contradictions persist across projections, or popularity increases unsupported claim confidence.

**Receiver benefit:** Learning from failures while preventing self-reinforcing error cascades.

**Nonclaim:** Reinforcement is not proof, causation, or divine guidance.

### DHP-EM-017: CoG_dSide+ candidate futures and ethical evaluation
**Class:** `NORMATIVE_AND_DECISION_PROTOCOL` | **Status:** `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE_NOT_VALIDATED`

**Proposal:** A plural and revisable future-state portfolio can help identify neglected stakeholders and preserve optionality.

**Strong rival:** A simple supply-demand hybrid can select equally beneficial, less costly options.

**Strong baseline:** Equal-budget supply-only, demand-only, hybrid and shuffled selection rules with rights and consent gates.

**Discriminator or test:** On pre-registered problems compare outcomes, failure modes, stakeholder impacts, choices preserved, calibration, cost and reversibility. Keep Pareto incomparability.

**Stop condition:** Prefer simpler baselines when no measured net benefit; suspend any proposal crossing rights/consent boundary.

**Receiver benefit:** Future interests considered without treating speculative perspectives as instructions.

**Nonclaim:** No claim that G_d exists as an agent, has wants, or authorizes action.

### DHP-EM-018: CoTransfer+ cross-front pay-forward
**Class:** `EMPIRICAL_GENERALIZATION_HYPOTHESIS` | **Status:** `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE_NOT_VALIDATED`

**Proposal:** A source-qualified pattern may transfer across domains only when mappings, invariants and known loss are explicit.

**Strong rival:** Apparent transfer is metaphor, correlated training data or a renamed existing method.

**Strong baseline:** Domain-specific expert summaries and standard retrieval without a new universal pattern layer.

**Discriminator or test:** Freeze source-domain rule; use independent held-out target domain tasks, pre-registered mappings and independent evaluators. Score successful prediction, explanatory compression, failures and time cost.

**Stop condition:** Pattern predicts only in source domain, degrades target accuracy, or adds unnecessary ontology.

**Receiver benefit:** Legible reuse of learning without papering over domain-specific distinctions.

**Nonclaim:** Relations among analogies do not alone establish causality, identity or global prediction.

## Synthetic stress test (not a forecast validation)

A reproducible finite fixture scores one-step probability forecasts on five deliberately contrasting 60-event sequences, starting after ten warmup events. Four baselines are tested: uninformative coin, last-value, global empirical majority, and sliding recent rate. Each forecast uses only earlier observed values. Lower Brier and log loss are better.

| Synthetic sequence | Coin Brier | Last Brier | Majority Brier | Recent Brier |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| constant_one | 0.250 | 0.010 | 0.001 | 0.010 |
| alternation | 0.250 | 0.810 | 0.258 | 0.250 |
| regime_flip | 0.250 | 0.026 | 0.299 | 0.065 |
| unpredictable_fixed_seed | 0.250 | 0.442 | 0.271 | 0.278 |
| mean_reverting_pairs | 0.250 | 0.410 | 0.258 | 0.250 |

**Source-level guard checks:** 12/12 passed. No source-level test establishes real-world forecasting or comparative superiority. Method ranking varies across intentionally constructed scenarios; this is the result to preserve, not hide.

## Promotion by evidence, never enthusiasm

1. Freeze candidate rule, source and temporal cutoff; specify target, horizon and loss metric before viewing the future.
2. Benchmark against a strong simple baseline with matching information, computation, human time and constraints.
3. Reserve untouched real prospective observations; document drift, negatives, confidence, and independent reviewer assessment.
4. Require receiving-system readproof and opt-in for effects; never infer status from publication, poetry, recursion or G_dSide labels.
