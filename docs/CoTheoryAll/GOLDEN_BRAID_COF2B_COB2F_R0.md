# CoTheoryAll / CC candidate: Golden Braid R0

Status: CANDIDATE_HYPOTHESIS, NOT_CANON, NOT_VALIDATED, NOT_PUBLIC. 2026-11-18.

## Central hypothesis
CoF2B+ human-facing exploration and CoB2F+ synthetic back-to-front construction can converge through an evidence-mediated, cyclic Golden Braid. Humans inspect readable prototypes, explore alternatives, and give consequential feedback. Synthetic agents conduct bounded architecture, evidence, testing and implementation work. Neither stream inherits authority from the other.

## Ethical participation invariant
Engagement must not be simulated merely to occupy humans. Show when feedback is received, evaluated, incorporated, declined, or awaiting review. Never promise every contribution will be acted upon. Feedback quality informs exploration priorities; avoid opaque behavioral manipulation and cross-domain profiling.

## Relational operators (proposed, not proven)
- CoF2B+: front-to-back human reading, discovery, challenge, feedback and UX learning.
- CoB2F+: back-to-front synthetic infrastructure, tests, proofs and bounded release candidates.
- CoFanIn+: many independent receipts and alternatives to one reviewable decision, retaining minority objections and provenance.
- CoFanOut+: authorized expansion of selected candidates to bounded parallel experiments; no uncontrolled forks.
- CoNull+: explicit zero, cancellation, unresolved, or no-signal state; not evidence of semantic consensus.
- CoLoop+/CoEv+: correction, evaluation, hypothesis revision, experiment and measured iteration.
- CoRelShock+: impact propagation when evidence, assumptions, or dependencies change.
- Golden Braid: repeated intersections with source-bound receipts, contradiction handling, human agency and CoGuardian gates.
- CoMegaScroller+: progressive-disclosure reading surface with linked claims, evidence, demos, alternatives, corrections and machine-readable deep dives.

## Falsifiable predictions
H1: Visible feedback disposition increases meaningful return participation compared with unacknowledged feedback.
H2: Progressive disclosure increases comprehension at equal reading time compared with undifferentiated mega-scroll.
H3: Fan-in retaining minority challenges yields fewer overlooked material objections than majority-only summarization.
H4: Parallel CoF2B/CoB2F reduces time to a verified, understandable artifact compared with sequential construction, subject to quality gates.
H5: Wider fan-out increases candidate diversity until cognitive and review load erodes decision quality.

## Experiments
Preregister outcomes: comprehension, task completion, cognitive load, feedback quality, correction latency, time to verified artifact, trust calibration, accessibility and return participation. Compare baseline and treatment with consent; report null/negative outcomes and confounders. Three independent challenges or HOLD before strong external claims.
