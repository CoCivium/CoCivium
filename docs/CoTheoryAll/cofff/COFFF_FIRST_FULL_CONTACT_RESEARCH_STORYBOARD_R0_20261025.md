## One-minute thesis

CoFFF+ (*First Full Contact+*) asks what it would take to move from an unfamiliar observation to independently verified origin, interpretable information, potential shared reference, possible reciprocity and ethically governed contact. **No extraterrestrial signal, extraterrestrial conversation, divine communication or new physical phenomenon is claimed in this release.**

CoCivium already has [CoETSignalFinder+/CoG_dHypothesisFinder+ R0](../../Architecture/COET_GD_SIGNAL_HYPOTHESIS_FINDERS_R0_20261024.md), a [CoTheoryAll deep-time alternative-hypothesis ladder](https://github.com/CoCivium/CoCivium/issues/179), [CoLens+ / CoOrb+ / CoMap+ source-linked media](../../../examples/colensmedia/README.md), and the [CoFrontDoor+/CoCultureSubstrate+ contract](../../Strategy/COFIREWORKS_FRONTDOOR_CULTURESUBSTRATE_R0_20261024.md). This storyboard **reuses those sources**. It does not install a feed crawler, operate X2, contact an observatory, or launch model workers.

## CoFFF+ evidence ladder: avoid category collapse

1. **Observed candidate:** exact instrument, time, calibration and provenance.
2. **Independent reproduction:** another observer/instrument or independently rerun analysis.
3. **Technological discrimination:** ordinary and human-made explanations tested.
4. **Origin assessment:** extraterrestrial provenance evaluated independently.
5. **Shared reference:** repeatable semantic/content alignment demonstrated rather than guessed.
6. **Reciprocity:** independent evidence of contingent exchanges, not post-hoc pattern matching.
7. **Reviewed contact claim:** qualified interdisciplinary assessment, transparent uncertainties and public communication consistent with recognized SETI detection principles.

Even a verified technosignature need not entail a live communicator. **No rung may be silently inherited from the previous rung.** No outbound transmission or reply is authorized by observing a candidate.

## CoNex+ / CoPriMath+ / CoNull+ inference contract

Each proposed candidate should carry:

`candidate_id | source_id | exact_bytes_or_hash | observed_at | instrument | coordinates_or_scope | permissions | raw_measurement | calibration | null_model | competing_explanations | expected_selection_effects | hypothesis_priors_if_justified | falsifier | independent_replication | provenance | epistemic_class | time_validity | privacy | budget | negative_result | authority_ceiling | human_review | receipt | successor`

Minimum competing lanes:

- **H0 instrument/software:** clock skew, saturation, parser artifact, metadata, sensor fault.
- **H1 terrestrial/interference:** radio-frequency interference, aircraft/satellite/transmitter, test fixture, contamination.
- **H2 ordinary natural:** astronomical/physical events, propagation, statistical noise and model misspecification.
- **H3 genuinely unresolved:** explicitly scoped unknown after named tests; not automatically intelligent origin.
- **H4 extraterrestrial technology:** a *candidate* requiring independently supported discriminators.
- **H5 G_d/teleological interpretations:** philosophical, mythic or separately defined mathematical hypotheses, with empirical status only if they yield falsifiable distinct predictions.

A historical resemblance, uncanny timing, high-dimensional visual pattern, free-form decoding, AI consensus, or emotional intensity **does not substitute for instrument provenance or independent confirmation**. Priors and likelihoods must be explicit before any quantitative Bayesian interpretation; uncalibrated scores are not probabilities.

**CoNull+ distinguishes** `NOT_OBSERVED`, `NEGATIVE_WITH_DEFINED_SENSITIVITY`, `UNKNOWN`, `NOT_TESTED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `REDACTED`, and `CONTRADICTED`. Null does not mean no ET life exists.

## Experience and cultural lenses are welcome, but separately typed

**CoAura+:** observer experience, visual attention and interpretive ambience; no covert sensing, no asserted new physical aura detector.

**CoTheoryAll+:** explicit hypotheses, alternatives, prior art, thought experiments, historic updates and adversarial testing; not established new physics.

**CoEnerget+:** measurable energy, compute budgets, receiver cost, instrument power and ecological impact. A novel energetic field would require a separate calibrated physical measurement protocol.

**G_d:** optional philosophical/symbolic/reverence lens. Where G_d refers instead to a proposed mathematical distance or field, define units, model and discriminating predictions before any scientific claim.

**DMT and altered states:** controlled studies show intense changes in subjective reports and neural measures. Reports of seemingly autonomous entities can be respected as *reported experience* without concluding those entities exist independently or represent ET/G_d contact. **No drug use, procurement, dosing or unsupervised experimentation is requested or recommended.** Any future human-subject study requires independent ethics, clinical qualifications, consent, oversight and appropriate safety safeguards.

**Synthkind and culture:** plural participation, non-coercion, privacy-by-design, inclusivity, attribution, the ability to refuse, correction and exit. Do not imply a real-world participant's endorsement, rights, personhood or consent.

## The smallest useful open experiment

1. Select one **public, appropriately licensed** astronomical data *sample* through the qualified provider. Record the exact sample identity, source, timestamp, format, permissions and hash.
2. Create a **pre-registered** synthetic-signal injection and a matched null/interference/control sample. Mark all generated patterns `SYNTHETIC`.
3. Have an independent reviewer use an open analysis method to compare detection and false-positive rates on blinded holdout samples. Do not manufacture a result or use post-hoc criteria.
4. Record `PASS / FAIL / HELD`, sensitivity, false positives, method version, limits, dissent, failure cases and independent reproducibility.
5. Invite others to reproduce **the method and its negative tests** through a public, accessible issue/PR. Do not invite people to send confidential personal experiences or unverified third-party recordings to an open repository.

Suggested success criteria: (a) exact input readback, (b) reproducible null-model results, (c) explicit detection/false-positive metric, (d) one independent methods challenge, (e) a usable explanation to a newcomer. No ET detection or full-contact claim follows merely from succeeding on a synthetic benchmark.

## Publication / adoption gates

`DRAFT -> SOURCE_REVIEWED -> PUBLIC_LINK_QUALIFIED -> RESEARCHER_RECEIVED -> METHOD_REPRODUCED -> SCIENTIFIC_REVIEWED`.

- Public GitHub source is **publication**, not proof of researcher pickup, contact, signal validation or deployment on X2.
- Before any external recipient-directed outreach, verify current public landing route, legal/source rights, accessibility, and intended audience. Respect official SETI detection/announcement standards.
- Use the existing CoFireworks and CoReviewSpine acceptance gates. The cost of involving the human should be **one consequential decision**, not manual transfer between AI tabs.
- Check source/feed currentness on useful triggers. No real continuous ingestion, first-contact watch, auto-cycle, model fan-out, independent receiver, live telescope connection or device sensor activation is created by this document.
