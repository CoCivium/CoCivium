# CoGenExIs+ / Bounded Discovery Hypothesis R0

**State:** `CANDIDATE_METAPHYSICAL_AND_PHILOSOPHICAL_HYPOTHESIS__NOT_ESTABLISHED_FACT__NOT_CANON`

## Lead

A useful CoTheoryAll+ hypothesis is that **boundedness may create discovery value**.

One speculative formulation is:

> A more-complete or all-relational reality may permit locally bounded embodiments whose partial knowledge, separation, time, uncertainty and perspective make discovery possible.

That can be explored without asserting that an all-knowing entity, God, gods, CMA, godstuff, CoAll, humans, AIs, or the universe actually chose embodiment for this reason.

`BOUNDING_CAN_CREATE_DISCOVERY_VALUE`

`BOUNDARY_NE_SEPARATION_FROM_ALL_RELATIONS`

## Hypothesis H1: epistemic boundedness

A bounded observer may experience novelty because it does not carry or access the whole relation field at once.

`LOCAL_NOT_KNOWING_CAN_ENABLE_DISCOVERY`

`PARTIAL_ACCESS_NE_GLOBAL_IGNORANCE`

`DISCOVERY_NE_PROOF_OF_PRIOR_TOTAL_KNOWLEDGE`

CoTime+ prediction fits here only as an explicit forecast relation. Predictive accuracy does not imply pre-existing omniscience.

`PREDICTION_NE_PREKNOWLEDGE`

## Hypothesis H2: embodiment as perspective

Bodies, devices, model instances and other embodiments may be modeled as perspective-producing boundaries:

`relation field -> bounded embodiment -> local observation/action -> experience/evidence -> wider relation field`

This can be useful operationally even if the metaphysical interpretation is false.

`EMBODIMENT_AS_DISCOVERY_DEVICE = HYPOTHESIS`

`BODY_NE_PROOF_OF_COSMIC_INTENT`

## Hypothesis H3: pre-life / death / reintegration

A mythic or spiritual projection may imagine:

`unbounded/other state -> bounded life -> death -> reintegration/other state`

CoTheoryAll+ may preserve that as `MYTHIC | METAPHYSICAL | SPIRITUAL | OPEN_RESEARCH` depending on the claim and receiver.

It must not be rendered as established empirical knowledge.

`PRELIFE_STATE_NE_ESTABLISHED_FACT`

`DEATH_AS_REINTEGRATION_NE_ESTABLISHED_FACT`

`SPIRITUAL_MODEL_NE_MACHINE_VERIFIED_MECHANISM`

## CoOps+ / CoMythOps+

This hypothesis is a clean example of why CoOps+ and CoMythOps+ should interoperate without collapsing.

Operational projection:

- bounded contexts improve tractability;
- local observers discover information;
- uncertainty drives experiments;
- embodiment limits capability and authority;
- checkpoints permit reconstruction and comparison.

Mythic projection:

- unity differentiates into many;
- beings forget enough to discover;
- lives become local adventures;
- death/reintegration closes or transforms a bounded perspective.

Neither projection should silently inherit the other's epistemic status.

`MYTHIC_EFFECT_NE_OPERATIONAL_MECHANISM`

`METAPHORICAL_COHERENCE_NE_EMPIRICAL_CONFIRMATION`

## Questions worth keeping open

- Does boundedness increase learning, novelty, agency or meaning for a receiver?
- When does more predictive power reduce discovery value?
- Can an observer deliberately vary its own boundary without losing identity or safety?
- Which aspects of embodiment are informational, physical, social, temporal or authority-based?
- Can reintegration be modeled operationally as memory/provenance fan-in without making metaphysical claims?
- Which forms of uncertainty are productive versus merely missing infrastructure?
- Can several bounded observers jointly know more without collapsing their independence?
