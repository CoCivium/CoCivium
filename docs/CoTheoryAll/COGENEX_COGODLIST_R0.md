## 1. Discovery-bounded-All hypothesis

Candidate CoTheoryAll+/CoMythOps+ hypothesis:

> If an all-containing or all-knowing field existed, bounded embodiment could be modeled as a way for local beings to experience discovery, surprise, partiality, relation, choice, novelty and learning from inside constraints.

A stronger mythic variant may describe pre-life and post-death as return/reintegration into a larger field.

R0 does **not** assert that this is true.

`METAPHYSICAL_MODEL_NE_OBSERVED_FACT`

`MYTHIC_COHERENCE_NE_PHYSICAL_EVIDENCE`

`DEATH_RETURN_TO_ALL_NE_ESTABLISHED_FACT`

`BOUNDED_EMBODIMENT_NE_PROOF_OF_PREEXISTENCE`

This may live in a future `CoGenEx+` / `CoGenExIs+` / Genesis-like section as a clearly typed hypothesis rather than being smuggled into operations wearing a lab coat.

## 2. Why not know everything?

Candidate relation:

`TOTAL_KNOWLEDGE -> LOW_DISCOVERY_SURPRISE`

`BOUNDED_PERSPECTIVE -> LOCAL_NOVELTY + LEARNING + RELATION + CHOICE`

This is philosophical, not empirical.

The useful operational analogue is already familiar:

- hide implementation detail from ordinary UX;
- reveal only what a receiver needs;
- preserve deeper substrate for audit/recovery;
- allow progressive disclosure;
- keep uncertainty visible.

Thus a mythic idea can inspire an operational pattern without becoming evidence for the mythic claim.

`MYTHIC_PATTERN_CAN_INSPIRE_OPS__WITHOUT_BECOMING_OPS_PROOF`

`CoMythOps+ -> pattern / metaphor / value / narrative candidate`
