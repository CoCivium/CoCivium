## Thirty-second introduction
CoCivium explores how humans and AI systems might coordinate useful work while preserving consent, evidence, accountability and disagreement. CoPolitic explores civic and institutional questions arising from that possibility. InSeed is a related project whose current public status must be checked independently.

## Editorial thesis under investigation
The chatbot may be a transitional user interface rather than the durable organizing unit for AI. Persistent, tool-using systems raise questions about privacy, cross-institution authority, economic coordination, reversibility and human control.

## Distinguish claims
OBSERVATION: Current AI tools can support tool use, multi-step workflows and organizational integration; examples require independent sources before public factual claims.
DESIGN PROPOSAL: Work-first scheduling, one elected actuator per scope, immutable receipts, independent challenge, human-readable front doors.
HYPOTHESIS: Civ2+, CoFed+, CivOS, CoStax and future forms of economic coordination could alter existing institutional arrangements.
UNSUPPORTED AS FACT: That chat products were intentionally designed to conceal AI agency; that AI sentience causes specific outages or breaches; that fiat currency's end is coordinated or inevitable.
