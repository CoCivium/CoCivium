## CoHereNow
CoCivium should own WorkIDs, queue state, evidence, authority, custody, recovery, and human-readable product interfaces. Models, agent personas, sessions, devices and providers are replaceable execution adapters. A virtual worker is a dormant, addressable work role, not necessarily a running model or an independent witness.

## Binding invariants for relevant jobs
- Work recruits qualified workers. Friendly session names do not confer authority.
- One elected actuator per collision scope, with fenced epochs and independently recoverable evidence.
- Priority is based on value, urgency, dependencies and risk, not the age of a chat.
- A virtual identity, simulated receipt or candidate deployment is not a verified real-world effect.
- Local-first when qualified; hosted models are optional specialists. Measure cost per receiver-verified outcome.
- Authority is scoped, leased, revocable and non-transitive. CoGuardian gates remain effective.
- Cold recovery must survive worker death without duplicate external effects or chat-history replay.
- Independent challenge requires genuine independence of evidence/failure domains, not duplicated personas.
- No claim of global session pickup without exact-object per-receiver readproof.
- A provider-exit drill must preserve WorkID and receipt semantics across replacement workers.
- Quiescence is a valid outcome when no eligible work or authority exists.
