# CoCommons+ and InSeed | Separate open research from commercial operations

**15 November 2026. Design clarification R0. Public-safe candidate, not an adopted legal charter, financial disclosure, payment endpoint, contract, or tax opinion.**

## CoHereNow

CoCivium's steward identified **InSeed (inseed.com) as the commercial partner**, reporting that InSeed has established accounts and accountants. This is a direct project-steward report; this document has **not** inspected company registration, accountant engagement letters, bank records, tax filings, existing contractual relationships, ownership, or authority to receive payments.

The preferred **two-lane** relationship is:

| CoCivium / CoAll public R&D commons | InSeed commercial partner |
| --- | --- |
| Publishes rights-cleared, source-bound, freely accessible research and FOSS | Offers explicitly identified paid products, contracts, hosted services and professional work where authorized |
| Accepts voluntary code, criticism, translation, replication and community participation | Maintains its own invoices, ledgers, payment channels, bookkeeping, applicable taxes and professional accounting |
| Maintains open-source licenses, community exit/fork rights, provenance, corrections and independent review | Uses open-source work under its actual licenses, and secures separate permission for restricted materials |
| Does not imply charity, tax exemption, registered NPO status, paid work obligations or a revenue share | Does not imply that commercial revenue automatically belongs to CoCivium, or that paying makes a claim true |

**Commercial support of the commons is compatible with free access.** It is not equivalent to saying InSeed is an NPO, that CoCivium is incorporated, or that no commercial activities take place in the wider project ecology.

## Operational separation and routing

1. **Visitor task first.** CoCivium public experiments remain openly usable without commercial purchase where rights allow. A voluntary contributor may improve a site without becoming an InSeed client or supplier.
2. **Identity at each boundary.** Any sales page, invoice, subscription, sponsorship, donor form, contributor payment, or support offer names the actual legal contracting or receiving entity, purpose, price/consideration, applicable terms, and applicable privacy controller.
3. **Money goes to the real counterparty.** Do not describe a payment to InSeed as a gift to a tax-exempt CoCivium charity. Do not describe an ordinary purchase as a donation, and do not route funds through unnamed personal accounts.
4. **Licenses and IP remain intact.** The CoCivium repository currently displays GPLv3. A commercial partner may use licensed FOSS under its applicable terms, but commercial association does not confer ownership of community work or rights to relabel third-party media.
5. **Funding the commons is an explicit transaction.** If InSeed pays hosting costs, hires contributors, sponsors experiments, or transfers funds to an authorized commons recipient, record type, amount/value, purpose, approval, counterparties, restrictions, rights and receipts. Avoid silent commingling.
6. **Conflicts and independence.** InSeed or a donor cannot buy research results, peer-review votes, CoScendence scores, canonical status, user referrals or priority disclosure. State related-party interests where they materially affect recommendations.
7. **Contract and risk ceilings.** No presumed promise of technical SLA, warranty, employment, agency, fundraising entitlement, token redemption, securities value or paid rewards from a public prototype.
8. **Contributor optionality.** Recognition, volunteered time and accepted patches do not automatically become paid labor, transferable tokens, IP assignment, or future profit claims.
9. **Node and site separation.** Reusing hosting, domains or interfaces does not make private source, credential scope, billing authority or production service contracts interchangeable. A public subsite is not a proxy for unpublished commercial service acceptance.

## Funding and recognition options, in preferred order

- **Lane A, volunteer FOSS:** no project payment mechanism required; free access, credit, community feedback, rights-cleared public releases.
- **Lane B, commercial sponsorship by InSeed:** when agreed and documented, InSeed may fund infrastructure, accessibility, creative work or maintenance with its own accounting and transparent relationship disclosure.
- **Lane C, optional independently identified fund recipient:** only with an actual receiving entity, donation/sponsorship classification, bookkeeping, controls and verified legal/tax posture.
- **Lane D, optional separate paid InSeed services:** customers contract with InSeed, not by implication with a charity or every public site.
- **Civar/reputation:** recognition-candidate only, not transferable money, investment, wage, redeemable credit or governance purchase. A future economic token requires its **own** legal, tax and securities review.

## Canadian regulatory cautions, official references

- [CRA: registered charity versus NPO](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/charities-giving/about-registered-charities/what-difference-between-a-registered-charity-a-non-profit-organization.html). Ordinary NPO status does not authorize official charitable tax receipts.
- [CRA: GST/HST donations, grants and sponsorships](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/rc4081/gst-hst-information-non-profit-organizations.html). Consideration and benefits can change a payment's characterization; an exchange need not use cash to have tax effects.
- [Ontario not-for-profit corporation guide](https://www.ontario.ca/page/guide-not-profit-corporations-act-2010). Business activity can coexist with qualifying not-for-profit purposes, but legal conditions and applicable corporate requirements must actually be met.
- [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd/). Proper open-source licenses allow commercial use by recipients. A steward may operate a noncommercial commons without imposing a noncommercial-use restriction on FOSS recipients.

Before activating a fund, sponsorship contract, token, claimed tax receipt, or related-party payment, review the real facts with InSeed's accountants and appropriately qualified counsel. Their existence is helpful; **their approval of a specific arrangement has not been observed.**
