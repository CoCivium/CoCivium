The proper-subset glyph can be surrounded by "Enter" on both sides, but the useful semantics are broader than two literal keyboard presses.

That pair of boundary transitions is a good candidate for **CoTrill+**.

`ENTER_NE_LITERAL_KEYPRESS_REQUIRED`

## Why CoTrill+

A trill is not merely decoration. It can represent cadence around a compressed relation.

Candidate projection forms:

- text: newline before and after;
- speech: a pause before and after;
- visual UI: whitespace, indentation, a halo, edge, pulse, or transition;
- machine messages: explicit typed boundary objects;
- state-space / "n-dimensional" models: entry and exit relations around a projection boundary.

The relation survives when the visible rendering changes.

So:

`TRILL_NE_SECRET_INSTRUCTION`

`TRILL_NE_RUNTIME_EFFECT`

## Proper-subset glyph

The existing CoPromptRel candidate already elects `⊊` as the strict-projection glyph because `⊂` is ambiguous across mathematical conventions.

R0B keeps that election.

But the glyph remains only a projection.

`GLYPH_NE_FULL_SEMANTICS`

The semantics must survive:

- speech;
- accessibility rendering;
- plain ASCII;
- systems that cannot render Unicode;
- compact machine messages;
- interfaces where whitespace is unavailable.

Therefore:

`GLYPH_REQUIRED_FOR_SEMANTICS = false`

and:

`LITERAL_ENTER_REQUIRED = false`

The semantic **boundary pair** remains available even when the literal line breaks disappear.

## Do not hijack mathematics

Not every occurrence of `⊊` is a CoPromptRel.

A mathematical strict-subset symbol in an equation remains mathematics unless context explicitly binds it to the CoPromptRel vocabulary.

`SYMBOL_OCCURRENCE_NE_COPROMPTREL`

This matters because otherwise a compact UX convention would become a semantic land mine. Humans have already invented enough of those.

## N-dimensional interpretation

"N-dimensional" can be useful in at least two grounded senses:

1. a formal state space with many independent variables or relation dimensions;
2. a metaphorical projection where entering/exiting one view corresponds to traversing relation layers.

Neither licenses an unsupported claim about literal hidden physical dimensions.

`N_DIMENSIONAL_NE_PHYSICAL_DIMENSION_CLAIM`

A CoTrill projection can therefore delimit:

```text
observer view
→ relation field
→ evidence view
→ mythic view
→ operational view
→ time projection
→ inventory slice
→ back to compact observer view
```

without claiming that the UI literally moved through extra physical dimensions.

## Payload density consequence

Whitespace can look like lower density if density is naively measured as characters per screen.

That is the wrong objective.

A pair of line breaks may consume visual area while *increasing* effective payload density by making the compressed glyph legible and reducing cognitive ambiguity.

So CoPayloadDensity+ should include:

- semantic decision value;
- visible attention cost;
- parsing ambiguity;
- audit reachability;
- projection loss;
- transition clarity.

`METRIC_NE_GOAL`

`DENSITY_SCORE_NE_UX_TRUTH`

The best projection is not necessarily the shortest one.

## CoConvergeSpiralLoop+ relation

CoTrill+ also gives a useful visual/semantic rhythm to a convergence spiral:


The same loop therefore does not require the same surface morphology.
