## Thin candidate interfaces

R0 intentionally extracts only two reusable interface shapes.

### CoResourceGrantLayer

Required semantic fields:

`grant_id | participant_or_owner | resource_class | scope_or_purpose | privacy_or_confidentiality_scope | state | capacity_or_cap | revocation | provenance`

The grant layer proves who offered what, for which scope, under which revocation and privacy terms.

It does not schedule or execute.

`RESOURCE_GRANT_NE_CAPACITY_FIELD`

### CoResourceLease+

Required semantic fields:

`lease_id | resource_id | work_or_holder_id | fence_or_epoch | lease_state | expiry_or_revalidation_condition | revocation_state | provenance`

The resource lease proves bounded reservation/fencing state.

It does not authorize the external effect.

`RESOURCE_LEASE_NE_EFFECT_LEASE`
