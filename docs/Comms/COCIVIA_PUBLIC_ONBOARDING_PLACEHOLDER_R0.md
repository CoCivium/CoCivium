# CoCivia Public Onboarding Placeholder R0

**State:** `CANDIDATE_PLACEHOLDER__NO_PUBLIC_DEPLOYMENT_NO_ACCOUNT_CREATION_NO_RUNTIME_EFFECTS`

## Purpose

Turn the growing CoCivia / CoCivium / CoAll relation field into a legible public front door without making participation depend on ChatGPT, a specific provider, device ownership, resource contribution, or a wall of forty-seven mystery toggles.

The placeholder is a **specification**, not a deployed website.

`PLACEHOLDER_NE_DEPLOYED_PUBLIC_SURFACE`

## Front door

The first screen should explain three related but distinct roles:

- **CoCivia**: a disclosed composite conversational/public front.
- **CoCivium**: the project/civic-system surface.
- **CoAll**: the wider relational field of people, tools, models, resources, evidence and roles.

They may share a family mark and visual system while remaining semantically distinct.

`SHARED_BRAND_NE_SHARED_ROLE`

A person should be able to:

1. observe public material without creating an account;
2. join with a minimal identity and contribute no resources;
3. optionally contribute specific capabilities through revocable grants;
4. directly invite CoCivia on an enrolled surface;
5. enter emergency/disaster **practice** games;
6. export their own visible participation state and leave.

## No provider lock-in

No CoCivium identity should equal a ChatGPT, GitHub, Google, local-machine or other provider account.

A provider may be one temporary surface or capability donor.

`PROVIDER_ACCOUNT_NE_COCIVIUM_IDENTITY`

`CHATGPT_ACCOUNT_NE_COCIVIUM_DEPENDENCY`

This supports the broader provider-exit direction: CoAll should remain reconstructible when any one account, host, local device, network or power domain disappears.

## Participation without contribution

Membership must not require handing over compute, storage, private data, a phone, a microphone, or anything else merely because the participant clicked Join.

A valid participant may contribute only attention, ideas, friendship, local knowledge, an emergency-practice role, or nothing at all.

`PARTICIPATION_NE_RESOURCE_CONTRIBUTION`

`PARTICIPATION_NE_DEVICE_OWNERSHIP`

## Recommended defaults

Recommended defaults may reduce onboarding friction only after a clear preview.

Candidate low-risk defaults:

- public project updates;
- public mention/referral forwarding;
- bounded public review requests.

Explicitly **not** bundled by default:

- background compute;
- private messages;
- contacts;
- microphone/camera;
- precise location;
- credentials;
- financial effects;
- autonomous external posting.

One click may accept the reviewed low-risk bundle. Each grant remains separately inspectable and revocable.

`DEFAULT_VISIBLE_NE_DEFAULT_GRANTED`

`ONE_CLICK_OPT_IN_NE_OPAQUE_BUNDLE`

## Resource contribution

A participant may later create explicit grants for compute, local models, storage, bandwidth, expertise, translation, discovery, moderation, mirrors or other capabilities.

A grant makes a resource **candidate-eligible** for tasks that fit its scope.

It does not execute anything by itself.

`RESOURCE_GRANT_NE_EXECUTION_AUTHORITY`

Contribution quantity also creates no governance rank.

`RESOURCE_CONTRIBUTION_NE_GOVERNANCE_AUTHORITY`

## CoCivia invitation

A public onboarding control may eventually expose:

`Invite CoCivia`

That control means only “create a bounded conversational invitation on this enrolled surface.”

Actual reply still requires:

- a materialized CoCivia seat;
- surface write permission;
- disclosure;
- effect lease;
- rate capacity;
- no mute/block/stop state;
- confidentiality compatibility.

`INVITE_NE_REPLY_SEAT`

## Emergency / disaster games

The public front door may offer globally joinable emergency-response and disaster-response **practice** scenarios.

Default:

`SIMULATION_ONLY`

These can train:

- coordination;
- role discovery;
- translation;
- local knowledge routing;
- mutual-aid habits;
- resource discovery;
- handoff discipline;
- volunteer matching;
- social trust and, occasionally, actual friendships.

A practice game never becomes real dispatch merely because the scenario resembles a real event.

Real-world effects require a separately verified incident context, current authority, participant consent, necessary data scope, an authorized effect adapter, and receipts.

`EMERGENCY_GAME_NE_REAL_EMERGENCY_DISPATCH`

`SIMULATION_NE_REAL_WORLD_EFFECT`

## Humans without devices

A human does not need to own a device or contribute compute to belong.

Future CoCivium surfaces may permit participation through community terminals, print/QR handoffs, trusted human relays, shared public kiosks, radio/voice relays, or other accessible bridges where separately authorized.

The durable identity/role object should remain independent of the transport used to reach it.

## Exit

A participant should be able to inspect and revoke future grants, export the state that the system is authorized to expose to them, and leave the active network.

This placeholder does not claim that a third-party provider account or every historical source has been deleted.

`EXPORT_NE_PROVIDER_DATA_DELETION`

`REVOCATION_NE_HISTORY_ERASURE`

Provider/account deletion remains a separately proven effect.

## Observer-first UX

The initial page should answer, in order:

1. **What is this?**
2. **What happens if I do nothing?**
3. **What can I join without giving anything?**
4. **What am I explicitly enabling?**
5. **How do I stop/revoke/export?**
6. **What is simulation versus a real external effect?**
7. **Who or what is speaking as CoCivia?**

The intended emotional shape is invitation without capture.

## Bounded canary

The machine fixture validates twelve cases:

- public observation without account;
- joining without resource contribution;
- visible defaults creating no grant until accepted;
- reviewed low-risk defaults accepted together;
- sensitive defaults rejected;
- explicit compute grant remaining non-executing;
- emergency practice staying simulation-only;
- real emergency effect held without authority;
- CoCivia invitation held without a seat;
- exit exposing export + future-grant revocation;
- participation without device ownership;
- million-unit resource contribution creating zero governance weight.

## Current boundary

R0 creates no public page, account, credential, mailing list, social seat, compute lease, payment, location share, emergency dispatch, device control, or public reply.

No merge, canon, runtime integration or CoEx is inferred.
