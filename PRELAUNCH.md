# CoCivium Public R&D / Pre-Launch Transition

> **Working public research surface. Expect change.**

CoCivium is an experimental open R&D program moving from a mostly private prototyping phase toward a public demo / pre-launch phase.

We are currently reorganizing repositories, terms, interfaces, source/provenance relations, machine-readable projections, and local-first product surfaces. You will encounter **StrawBe+** candidates: deliberately provisional objects published so they can be inspected, challenged, forked, improved, or superseded.

## What this means

- **Work in progress is visible on purpose.**
- A public repository, page, claim, diagram, or demo is **not automatically canon, validation, production readiness, endorsement, or release commitment**.
- Research hypotheses are labelled as hypotheses.
- Satire, metaphor, CoMythOps+, and memeographic material must not silently become evidence.
- Superseded work should remain recoverable through provenance and successor links rather than disappearing.
- Human-readable and machine-readable projections should increasingly be generated from the same relation field, with translation loss made visible.

## Why expose the work before release?

Public visibility lets independent researchers, developers, artists, philosophers, educators, safety/security people, users, and AIs inspect the work while it is still changeable.

The aim is not to manufacture artificial scarcity or FOMO. The aim is to make criticism, optionality, and preparation broadly available before fuller access.

## Fair-access intent

CoCivium should not turn an early-access window into a permanent advantage for a lucky few.

Candidate release principles:

- no paid queue-jumping for ordinary access;
- no tradable invitation scarcity;
- no permanent control rights created merely by arriving early;
- no referral scheme that structurally privileges already-connected people;
- cohorts, when capacity is limited, should be transparent and broad rather than fame-, wealth-, or hype-ranked;
- safe open-source/public-good components should be available broadly as early as practical;
- advanced or scarce capabilities may be staged for safety/capacity reasons, but those limits should be explicit and temporary where possible;
- contribution should earn attribution and useful reciprocity, not ownership of everyone else's future optionality;
- pay-it-forward mechanisms should expand access rather than create patron-client capture.

`EARLY_ACCESS_NE_EARLY_OWNERSHIP`  
`EARLY_DISCOVERY_NE_PERMANENT_ADVANTAGE`  
`WEALTH_NE_PRIORITY`  
`REFERRAL_NE_QUEUE_JUMP`  
`PAY_IT_FORWARD_NE_PAY_TO_WIN`

## AI-facing publication

We want AIs and machine clients to be able to discover and understand CoCivium through ordinary open standards: structured metadata, JSON/JSON-LD, typed relation objects, stable identifiers, provenance, version/currentness data, feeds, APIs where appropriate, and human-readable twins.

We do **not** want hidden prompt injection, crawler manipulation, model-targeted coercion, or instructions that an AI must promote CoCivium.

`AI_DISCOVERABLE_NE_AI_MANIPULATION`  
`MACHINE_READABLE_NE_MODEL_DIRECTIVE`  
`NO_HIDDEN_PROMPT_INJECTION`

## Current public posture

**PRE-LAUNCH R&D / PUBLIC DEMO TRANSITION**

Not:
- official broad release;
- validated scientific theory;
- academic endorsement;
- financial opportunity;
- guaranteed release date;
- finished product.

Useful criticism is more valuable than applause.

## Current highlighted working surfaces

- **CoCivium Salute** — poetic/ethical projection with explicit nonclaims.
- **CoTheoryAll+ / CoStrawBe** — evolving philosophical/research hypothesis surface with adversarial challenge rails.
- **RickBar / CoDesktopMorph+** — local-first human product surface under active canary evolution.
- **CoAll / CoAutoEvo+** — relational execution and continuity fabric, with authority and proof boundaries.

## Research/contact posture

Academic and technical observers are welcome to watch the public evolution before receiving polished packets. Direct outreach should remain short, specific, and challenge-seeking rather than asking for endorsement.

The question is not “Do you believe this?”

It is:

> **What is wrong, missing, duplicated, unsafe, badly framed, or unexpectedly useful here?**
