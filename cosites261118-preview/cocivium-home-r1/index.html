Begin with a question, not a verdict

  1. State a question and what a useful answer would change.
  2. Map plausible alternatives, including objections.
  3. Connect claims to sources and distinguish observations from hypotheses.
  4. Test what could disprove each important conclusion.
  5. Record the decision, its uncertainty and the next review condition.

CoF2B turns readable experiences into questions and feedback. CoB2F brings verified research, testing and corrections back into the experience. Neither direction is automatically authoritative.

What remains unproven?

  • Public deployment and external uptime.
  • Independent scientific or governance validation.
  • Operational public feedback or submission endpoints.
  • Authority to publish, merge or act on behalf of visitors.

Questions worth asking: Who supplied the evidence? What could falsify it? Who can authorize an action? What happens when reviewers disagree?
