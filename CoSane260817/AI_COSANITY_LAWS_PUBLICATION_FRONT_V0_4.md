## Lead editorial decision

Do not publish the prior `$600T committed AI capital` phrasing as an established fact until its calculation is reconstructed and independently checked.

Preserve it as a **$600T AI-relational exposure hypothesis**: a possible direct + indirect long-horizon capital surface affected by AI if the accounting basket includes compute, power, digital infrastructure, enterprise software, automation, logistics, industrial retooling, labor substitution/augmentation, finance, and downstream infrastructure. The current corpus proves we previously used the number; it does not yet prove the number.

Use the immediately verifiable scalars first:

- McKinsey: about $6.7T global data-center capital requirements through 2030, around $5.2T for AI workloads.
- Gartner: about $2.59T worldwide AI spending forecast for 2026.
- WSJ analysis: roughly $3T off-balance-sheet commitments across nine major technology companies, mostly AI-related, plus roughly $600B of latest-year reported capex.
- PwC: $151.1T cumulative global infrastructure spending forecast through 2050, driven by multiple forces including AI, electrification, urbanization and modernization. This is NOT all AI spend.

These figures overlap and must never be added together as though they were one non-overlapping capital total.

## Opening V0.4

We already know this is a trillion-dollar bet.

McKinsey estimates that the global data-center build-out required through 2030 could approach **$6.7 trillion**, including roughly **$5.2 trillion for AI workloads**.

Gartner forecasts **$2.59 trillion of worldwide AI spending in 2026 alone**.

A Wall Street Journal analysis now identifies roughly **$3 trillion of off-balance-sheet commitments** across nine major technology companies, mostly AI-related, before counting about **$600 billion of their latest-year reported capital spending**.

And PwC forecasts a **$151.1 trillion global infrastructure cycle through 2050**, with AI among the forces reshaping power, digital, transport and industrial systems.

Those numbers overlap. They are not a sum.

They are something more useful:

**a scale warning.**

The directly measurable AI bet is already in the trillions. The economic surface that AI may alter is vastly larger.

In earlier RickPublic work we used an even more explosive estimate: **$600 trillion of direct and indirect AI-relational capital through roughly 2040**.

We are not asking you to accept that number here.

We have not yet reconstructed a publication-grade derivation that separates direct commitments, indirect infrastructure, capital turnover, economic exposure and double-counting.

So treat `$600T` as a hypothesis we intend to either prove, revise, or kill.

The argument below does not need it.

Because even the verified trillions expose the same missing question:

> **What if intelligence is scaling faster than sanity?**

Not sanity as emotion.

Sanity as the ability of an increasingly autonomous machine system to establish what is real, what is current, what a symbol means, where evidence came from, who has authority, which future was predicted, what action actually occurred, what changed in the world, and whether another intelligence can independently reconstruct the chain.

If that substrate fails to scale, smarter AI does not automatically create a smarter system.

It can create a faster incoherent one.

That is why we propose the AI CoSanity Laws.

## The mnemonic: STAY SANE

The public mnemonic should be **STAY SANE**.

### S — State must be reconstructable.

Consequential objects need recoverable identity, provenance, currentness, dependency lineage and governing status.

### T — Time must be typed.

Observed is not historical. Historical is not current. Current is not predicted. Predicted is not planned. Planned is not authorized. Counterfactual is not real.

### A — Authority must be explicit.

Capability is not permission. Recommendation is not approval. Approval is not execution authority. Execution authority is not acceptance of the result.

### Y — Your output is not the outcome.

A model response, tool-call success flag, emitted message or completed computation is not proof that the intended world-state change occurred.

### S — Sending is not receiving.

Emission is not receipt. Receipt is not understanding. Understanding is not acceptance. Acceptance is not execution. Execution is not successful outcome.

### A — Action must stay bounded.

Autonomy should expand first through observable, lease-bounded, checkpointed and reversible effect domains with explicit blast-radius limits.

### N — Natural language is not the hard gate.

Human language can propose, negotiate and explain. High-consequence meaning and authority should bind to machine-recoverable semantic objects rather than silently inheriting ambiguity from prose.

### E — Evidence must survive independence.

No model, verifier, log, memory or proof system gets universal authority. Material actions should survive independent reconstruction from durable evidence.

## One-line compression

**STAY SANE: reconstruct the State, type the Time, bind Authority, observe the Yield, separate Sending from receipt, bound Action, keep Natural language outside the hard gate, and make Evidence independently recoverable.**
