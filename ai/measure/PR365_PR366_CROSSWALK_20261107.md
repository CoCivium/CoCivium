## Two immediate falsifiers that should feed the primary

- **CH01 | bounded fraction over 1:** a fraction-of-whole must not accept e.g. 120% as a qualified bounded share. If the quantity instead denotes relative growth or rate, it needs a *different quantity kind*, not relaxing the bounded share silently.
- **CH02 | fractional event counts:** a `1.5` qualified ACK/restore/human-touch **count** is not a discrete count under ordinary event semantics. It may be an estimate/expected count in a *different quantity kind* with model uncertainty, not a verified observed discrete event.

This supplemental branch now adds explicit source-level guards and seven tests for CH01/CH02. This is not proof that #365 also has those guards; port and test against the #365 exact `Fraction` engine, with a source-diff and separate CI receipt before any candidate promotion.
