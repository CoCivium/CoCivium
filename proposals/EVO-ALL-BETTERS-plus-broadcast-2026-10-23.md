## Action
- Discover and compare substrates, models, algorithms, agents, interfaces, and resource strategies, including local/open-source and provider-backed options when authorized.
- Represent trillion-scale *candidate* experiment spaces symbolically. Execute only bounded, useful, permitted experiments.
- Keep deterministic baselines, adversarial tests, failure injection, held-out evaluation, and independent replication.
- Re-evaluate historical artifacts without overwriting originals. Publish successor versions with provenance.
- Record PASS, FAIL and NOT_TESTED, resource cost, test scope, limits, and evidence. Do not mistake a pointer for a receiver acknowledgment.
- Prefer portable, privacy-preserving custody and dormant virtual workers over millions of unnecessary online sessions.
- Publish useful research drafts before perfection, while clearly distinguishing proposal, test, replication, deployment and adoption.
