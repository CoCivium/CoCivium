# CoPreGeneric + CoFront/CoEcho/CoTime Retro Rail (PUBLIC DRAFT)

**Status:** Proposal only. Not canon, runtime, automatic synchronization, or a completed migration.  
**Prepared:** 2026-11-09 UTC date.  
**Origin:** Reconciled from two contemporaneous conversation streams; private message contents are not reproduced here.  
**Authority:** Candidate for review, not a governing source of truth.

## Purpose

Prevent valuable project reasoning, evidence, dissent, and unfinished work from becoming orphaned during migration among conversations, applications, machines, and public surfaces. Public publication is a **projection**, not proof that all source material is public-safe or complete. A private custody lane must remain available.

## Core invariants

1. **One logical seed; many representations.** Store a stable seed identifier, version, content hash, source pointer, status, and compatible renderings. Do not assume all versions are identical.
2. **Evidence before status promotion.** `PROPOSED`, `HELD`, `OBSERVED`, `VERIFIED`, `RECEIVED`, `INTEGRATED`, `RELEASED` are different states. Never infer later states from earlier ones.
3. **Separate human and synthetic surfaces.** Human narrative, accessible visualization, machine-readable graph, schema, and proof are projections of evidence-bound objects. Record translation loss; preserve original source.
4. **No forced consensus.** Keep contradictions, independent counterevidence, negative results, inverse/null models, and the provenance of every claim.
5. **No silent drop.** Every known object must be integrated, explicitly held with recovery pointer, superseded with lineage, or marked unresolved discovery debt.
6. **No user courier assumption.** Mouse/thumb/chat are pointers and initiators, not presumed durable receivers. Automation remains unverified until independently tested.
7. **No public leakage.** Private conversations, contact details, secrets, credentials, unreleased personal material, and sensitive evidence remain private pending independent publication clearance.
8. **No anthropomorphic promotion.** Agent heartbeats or eloquent narratives do not establish consciousness, AGI, ETI, or independent agency.

## CoPreGeneric: machine-readable minimum

Each object should expose:
`object_id, type, version, status, source_ref, observed_at, recorded_at, content_sha256, access_class, evidence_class, owner_or_steward, relations[], contradictions[], supersedes[], held_reason, recovery_hint, next_discriminator, last_checked_at`.

A source-independent read must either fetch these fields, receive an explicit access-denied reason, or return `UNKNOWN`. A prompt saying 'log publicly' is not evidence of a landing.

## CoFront+ / CoEcho+ / CoTime+

**CoFront+** is an identifiable stream of work with custody, present status, dependencies, and a next checkpoint.  
**CoEcho+** is a deduplicated, source-linked observation, improvement, objection, or derived artifact emitted by a front.  
**CoTime+** distinguishes event time, observation time, recording time, publication time, recognition time, and prospective forecast horizon.

Relations include `DERIVED_FROM`, `SUPPORTS`, `CONTRADICTS`, `SUPERSEDES`, `HELD_BY`, `REQUIRES_RECEIVER`, `CROSS_CHECKS`, `PREDICTS`, `FALSIFIES`, `TRANSLATES_TO`, `RECOVERS_FROM`, and `SHARES_UPSTREAM_SOURCE`.

## Retroactive reconciliation: two-directional sweep

**Backward sweep:** Start from existing public heads and trusted local receipts; traverse `DERIVED_FROM` edges toward earlier versions, prior conversations, artifacts, and unverified gaps. Do not assume earliest material is accessible.

**Forward sweep:** Start from the oldest *verified accessible* custody anchor and traverse toward current heads; compare predicted downstream artifacts with what actually exists.

**Sideways sweep:** Cross-index fronts by semantic relationship, shared SHA, duplicate content, citations, and dependency, not just calendar order. Record shared origin so copies are not mistaken for independent evidence.

**Gap inventory:** `KNOWN_MISSING`, `POSSIBLY_MISSING`, `INACCESSIBLE`, `UNVERIFIED_POINTER`, `PRIVATE_HOLD`, `SUPERSEDED`, and `INTENTIONALLY_EXCLUDED` are distinct.

A claimed zero-orphan state requires an auditable inventory denominator, not simply a zero in the current error log.

## CoEvSpiral+ without the paste-bus

`DISCOVER -> CAPTURE -> TYPE -> DEDUPE -> CHALLENGE -> RELATE -> VERIFY -> LAND/HELD -> PREDICT -> REVIEW`.

Fan-out reads an immutable or version-pinned seed. Fan-in aggregates receipt-linked deltas. New work requires evidence of added information gain, not repetition. Stop when marginal gain is below cost or authority is missing. A pause is a status, not proof that a running watcher exists.

## CoNull+ / G_dSide+ typed correctly

`CoNull+` is a useful design family for missing values, zero observations, silences, abstentions, recovery gaps, and explicitly held alternatives. These must **not** all collapse to the numeric value zero.

`G_dSide+`, future-sentience models, and oscillatory/golden-braid metaphors may motivate ethics or hypotheses. They are not scientific evidence without external discriminating tests. A harmonic pattern is not evidence of cosmic coordination.

## Multi-surface landing constellation

- **Machine:** typed JSON/JSON-LD or graph exports, schema checks, immutable hashes, APIs, event and revision streams.
- **Human:** readable site/megascroller with citations, timeline, diagrams, accessibility, and honest statuses.
- **Archive:** version-pinned source snapshots, manifests, checksums, recovery instructions, and offline-capable copies.
- **Exploratory media:** diagrams/audio/other projections may encode source-bound state, but must not be represented as empirical whale communication or verified warnings without tests.

Do not publish all private history to make the public graph look complete. Publish safe summaries and private-hold stubs with non-sensitive recovery references.

## Immediate proof gates

1. Confirm a stable public URL and that a separate reader can retrieve it.
2. Verify the published bytes/hash against the authored artifact.
3. Ingest the candidate into a real CoPreGeneric receiver with an explicit acknowledgment identifying the exact version/hash.
4. Test a restart and >24-hour recovery without relying on a chat transcript or human relay.
5. Prove deduplication and no-clobber behavior with old and competing versions.
6. Demonstrate private/public redaction and role-scoped access.
7. Reconcile the prior source chronology and create a measurable unresolved gap register.
