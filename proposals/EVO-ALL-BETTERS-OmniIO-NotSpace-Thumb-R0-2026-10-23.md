## Consolidation
CoOmniIO+: each item may act as input, output, connector, constraint, hypothesis, missing prerequisite, or future possibility. Roles are contextual and evidence-scoped, not blanket permissions.

CoNotSpace+: explicitly model absent, contradicted, deliberately prohibited, unobserved, and hypothetical edges. Missing is not permission to connect. Preserve unknown and refusal.

CoSmoosh+: compose views while retaining distinct provenance, identities, semantics and consent. Never merge away contradictory evidence.

CoDvl+: Build / Challenge / Repair, with independent falsifiers and stop conditions.

CoSpirit+ and other nonmaterial or symbolic descriptions may be represented as experiential or speculative classes, without claiming verified supernatural or extraterrestrial communication.

CoQuanta+: minimal event or resource descriptor carries id, version, time, source, meaning, relation type, evidence class, privacy, authority, uncertainty and recovery.

CoMap+/CoOrb+/CoOrbWare+/GRAIL+: alternate projections of the same evidence graph. Trails and connectors are typed and inspectable; accessible list is always available. Wallpaper/screensaver displays are optional and privacy-filtered, with reduced-motion and resource-budget controls.

CoVirtualSessions+: durable task state with ephemeral bounded workers. No mass fake personas, unauthorized provider sessions or evasion of service limits. Account retirement requires exported custody, tested substitutes, recovery and billing review.

CoReWis+: beyond-cursor predictive suggestions are local-first and preview-only; no silent submission or private-data transmission.

CoAckRAW+: PREPARED, SENT, RECEIVED, ACKNOWLEDGED and APPLIED require separate evidence. Pointer is not ACK.

## CoThumb+ middle-mouse proposal
Default: retain native middle-click. Configurable user-owned gesture invokes preview/inspect, then optionally copy resource reference, relate, benchmark, or share to an authorized receiver. Never broadcast merely from a gesture. Provide keyboard equivalent and visible undo/escape.

## Acceptance gates
- deterministic fixture and independent replay;
- correct handling of absent/prohibited/hypothetical edges;
- provenance-preserving export/import;
- safe gesture handling and accessibility;
- no unauthorized data access or automatic transmission;
- baseline comparisons for time-to-find, comprehension, energy, latency and recovery;
- append-only PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED/NOT_TESTED ledger with hash chain and receiver receipts.
