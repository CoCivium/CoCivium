## Execution contract
1. Define a symbolic experiment grammar; do not enumerate the entire search space.
2. Filter invalid combinations, deduplicate, rank by expected information gain and marginal resource cost.
3. Compare deterministic baselines with eligible local/open-source, Claude and other authorized models. Do not assume provider superiority.
4. Reassess historical code, prompts, agents and claims without overwriting originals.
5. Challenge results using independent methods, counterexamples, held-out tests and failure injection.
6. Record PASS / FAIL / NOT_TESTED separately. Record provenance, version, scope, resources, constraints and uncertainty.
7. Require explicit authority for external actions; no deceptive personas, unsolicited bulk outreach or provider-limit evasion.
8. Preserve portable artifacts, checkpoints, source identity, receipts and receiver acknowledgments; pointer != ACK.
9. Prefer dormant virtual agents and bounded jobs over idle online sessions.
10. Publish useful drafts with clearly marked limits; never imply a draft is a verified deployment.
