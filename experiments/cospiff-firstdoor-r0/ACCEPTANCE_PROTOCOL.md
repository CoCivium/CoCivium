# CoSpiff+ | Volunteer first-use acceptance (NOT RUN)

Target: establish whether one default entrance plus optional specialist routes helps a **consenting** first-time visitor accomplish one bounded task, versus seeing all capabilities at once. The participant can stop at any time. No account linking, covert telemetry, private document uploads, inferred persona or durable tracking.

1. Recruit diverse independent participants voluntarily; avoid selection solely by personal relationship or existing CoCivium familiarity. This protocol has recruited **zero** participants.
2. Provide the same simple, synthetic or participant-approved task to two entry layouts in counterbalanced order. The baseline may be current `START_HERE.md` rather than an artificial "firehose" if it is already concise.
3. Record only consented task-result scores, error categories, and coarse elapsed seconds. Do not retain their raw free text by default. Anonymized feedback may still require ethics/privacy review depending on setting.
4. Ask the participant whether source, demonstration, and deployed service were clearly differentiated; whether they found a useful result; whether they knew how to refuse, correct, or return to the primary entrance.
5. Score PASS/FAIL/HOLD: can identify one correct route; reproduce one result; reject unwarranted claim; demonstrate accessible keyboard use; leave without losing control. Separate *task success* from *promotional engagement*.
6. Promote a new route only if it improves outcomes under fair and realistic comparison without adding privacy errors, lock-in, confusion, or unsupported deployment claims.

Report sample size, recruitment, limitations, inclusion, inaccessible routes and all negative examples. A direct first-use ACK requires the independent participant's own acknowledgment, not a maintainer clicking the source link.

Pre-registered stopping rule: five first-use sessions or four weeks, whichever first, with a failure-driven review before any rollout. This is a proposed test budget, not an actual study in progress.
