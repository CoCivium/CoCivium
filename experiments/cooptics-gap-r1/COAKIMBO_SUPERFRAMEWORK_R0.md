# CoAkimbo+ | Productive disequilibrium with persistent protections

**26 October 2026 · Candidate conceptual superframework, not an adopted ontology, measured cosmic principle, G_d transmission or live CoOrbWare feature**

## Prior definitions that must not drift

- CCC = Core Controlled Coercion. A concentrating core can make access to memory, models, tools, credentials, data, money, identity and exit practically captive. CoCCC is a CoCivium term for that same diagnostic failure, not a different empirical system.
- CoEBE = CoEvolutionary Edge-Balanced Equilibrium (also abbreviated Edge Balance Equilibrium). The earlier CoEvo definition was an adaptive balance in which no actor dominates, evolution continues without collapse and trust persists. ECC = potential opposite failure, fragmented coercive edge mini-cores.
- Equilibrium need not be static equilibrium, thermodynamic equilibrium or zero-change. Dynamical stability can include trajectories, cycles and disturbances within a viable region. It is NOT equivalent to universal monotonic growth.

## CoAkimbo+: a thin **operator/lens** across existing frameworks

Proposed conditional definition: "Preserve agency, safety, provenance and recoverability while actively exploring bounded, falsifiable variations in knowledge, representation, participation, architecture and interventions." Not automatically a master controller, canon, or physical process.

## A bounded toy recurrence for social/operational evaluation, not a physical law

B[t+1] = clip((1 - d[t]) B[t] + G_verified[t] + I_authorized[t] - H[t], 0, K[t])

- B is a *declared proxy* for independently measured beneficial outcomes, not cosmic or moral energy.
- d is obsolescence/attrition; G is improvement supported by independent results; I is actual voluntary input/reuse; H is explicitly measured harm/resource/attention debt; K is safe capacity.
- Only G/I backed by evidence, consent and resource authorization count. The score can fall. Non-growth can mean successful completion, rest, consolidation or deliberate preservation.
- Stability constraints guard consent, auditability, revocability, independent exit, safety and resource ceilings.
- The system should expose disagreement between measures, not hide all objectives in one "positive energy" scalar.

## CoEBE stress test: reversal as resilience

Experiment `REMOVE_CORE`: forbid access to the founding provider/repository/model; ask an independent edge to open, test, export and correct one minimal artifact. Observe recovery time, success, privacy leakage and whether change returns to shared knowledge voluntarily.

Counterexamples:
- Total instability destroys continuity and trust.
- Total fragmentation produces CCC-like power in each edge.
- Continuous amplification can grow misinformation, coercion, spam, waste and unconscious dependence.
- Some beneficial things are slow, quiet, complete or stable rather than permanently expanding.
- Apparent independent workers under one cloud account are not independent failure domains.

ET is an external-origin hypothesis requiring provenance, independent detection and adversarial terrestrial alternatives. DMT phenomenology belongs to brain/perception research, not instrument-grounded contact evidence.
