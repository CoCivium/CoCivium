# CoCivium Commons Offer R0 | Free knowledge, optional reciprocity

**Status: proposed public-benefit policy for review, not a live donation program, tax-registered charity, partnership agreement, installed service, or unlimited free-hosted-AI promise.**

## The short offer

CoCivium aims to make public research, human-readable learning materials, and rights-cleared open-source tools free to read, inspect, challenge, learn from, and fork where their licenses allow. A person should not have to donate to test a public demonstration, offer criticism, obtain ordinary public information, or participate in an accessible conversation.

The goal is a **commons that becomes more useful when challenged**, rather than a funnel that sells artificial scarcity. Operating hosted services, research, accessibility, moderation and paid inference still have finite costs and may require explicit capacity limits. Nothing here promises unlimited free compute, human service, commercial rights, or perpetual availability.

## Voluntary support without hidden obligations

Contributions may eventually support qualified maintainers, educators, accessibility work, collaborators, fiscal hosts, or project infrastructure. **No donation channel or recipient has been approved by this document.** No payment link will appear until a lawful receiver, processing fees, currency, refund rules, use of funds, recipient consent, conflict disclosures and any tax-receipt eligibility have been checked and published.

Support must not buy governance votes, editorial conclusions, academic endorsement, evidence promotion, queue-jumping for ordinary access, or preferential handling of criticisms. Contributors may be thanked **only with their opt-in**, and an identifiable partner may be named as a partner only after they agree. Financial transparency should report actual funds and expenses, not invented generosity. No charitable tax status is implied. A genuine fiscal sponsor or host may be considered only after its eligibility and terms are confirmed.

## Living does not mean silently rewritten

Every released edition should have a stable citation/version, original publication date, source and licensing references, and a recoverable predecessor where lawful. New evidence, user questions, alternative explanations and corrections generate **candidate changes**. Candidates undergo source verification, adverse review, privacy/licensing checks and appropriate human publication approval. Accepted changes become new editions with visible diffs and reasoned changelogs, while material errors may be clearly corrected or retracted. Historical content is not made to say something it never said.

`READ -> QUESTION -> CANDIDATE -> TEST -> REVIEW -> NEW_VERSION -> CONTINUITY` is a **design contract**. No continuously running public editor, subscription engine or receiver feedback loop is demonstrated by this document.

## Community, recognition and welcome disagreement

Participation is voluntary. People may submit a bug, a falsification, independent reproduction, accessibility finding, translation proposal, prior-art reference, or useful alternative without joining an identity system. Public GitHub issues currently offer a source-controlled route for voluntary feedback. A correction has value even when its author does not seek publicity. Contributor names, portraits, stories, quotations and affiliations require appropriate permission; withdrawing future recognition must be practical.

Use transparent credits and evidence, not fame as a substitute for truth. Independently reproducible tests, fair dissent, clear attribution and the ability to decline further involvement are more useful than a leaderboard of agreeable people.

## Academia and expert invitations

Begin with **one recipient and one specific research question**, invite criticism without presuming endorsement, and offer a short relevant public source. Any later small cohort should be deduplicated and evaluated against jurisdiction-specific communication law, permission, recipient burden and objections. There is no standing authorization in this document to email 1,000 academics, auto-follow up or claim an institution is collaborating. Commercial or fundraising content in outreach requires additional review.

## Interpreting generosity and G_d

People can be moved by faith, secular ethics, curiosity, compassion or many other reasons. CoCivium need not assert hidden or divine motivation to show practical generosity. Let publication terms, free access, transparent spending, honest setbacks, explicit consent, fair treatment, safe exits and independently observed benefit carry that evidence. A G_d interpretation may be discussed voluntarily as philosophy, mythology or hypothesis; it is not a source of technical permission, measured physics or institutional endorsement.
