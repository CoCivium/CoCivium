# G_dSide R0A | Meaning is a projection, not a universal transport authority

**State:** source-only adversarial research amendment in existing draft PR #379, under existing G_dSide issues #377/#378. **Not** CoLex canon, a new ontology, a verified social theory, a validated future-attractor model, cultural consent, receiver ACK, runtime or evidence of a metaphysical force.

## The correction

The earlier CoSubstrate / CoMeaning direction remains useful for translation, lineage and human explanation. It is **not sufficient** as the invariant that moves a future-oriented proposal across cultures, models, generations, institutions, physical devices or unknown substrates.

A shared word can hide incompatible measurement practices. A translated word can preserve a practical agreement. A culturally borrowed symbol can preserve its public description while changing control, benefit, consent, refusal and credit. Those are different transitions.

**Candidate carrier:** a versioned, situated bundle of *observed interactions, affordances, constraints, permissions, affected parties, source rights, possible transitions, alternative futures, counterfactual consequences, uncertainty, and independently observed effects*. Meaning is an important, revisable, observer-relative projection **of** this bundle, not a guaranteed common root.

This is **not** a meaning-free graph. The choice of edges, populations, metrics, horizons and desirable futures is itself normative and culturally situated. No algorithm may infer consent or a universal good from shared metric labels, graph connectivity or numerical superiority.

## Separate layers

1. **Observation / relation:** who did what, where, under what conditions, with which source and uncertainty. Description is not endorsement.
2. **Situated interpretation:** which community, person, model or institution assigns which senses, purposes, analogies or refusals; record nontranslation, contradiction and history.
3. **Normative proposal:** whose preferred future, affected population, rights, consent, ecological limits, distributional harms, benefits and vetoes. A high forecast score never supplies authority.
4. **Transition and effect:** feasible intervention, causal assumptions, null/no-action alternative, rollback, receipt, receiver-origin readproof, revision and successor.

A future "pull" is an explicit comparison of *candidate transitions*, not a force, prophecy, divine instruction, or authority. Preserve plural Pareto alternatives, contested dimensions and the possibility that a simpler supply/demand policy wins.

## Cross-substrate transfer packet (proposal, not a new schema)

Reuse CoOntPrinciple, CoSubstrateField, CoMeasureFabric, CoPriMath, CoFuturePull and CoSource. Candidate fields:

```text
work_id, revision, context_id, origin_and_affected_parties,
source_refs_and_rights, observer_and_valid_time,
observed_relations, possible_actions, constraints_and_refusals,
measurement_contracts_by_axis, translation_loss_and_nontranslations,
competing_interpretations, consent_and_benefit_obligations,
counterfactuals, uncertain_outcome_distributions,
causal_assumptions_and_falsifiers, evidence_and_challenge_refs,
rollback, receiver_readproof, successor_refs, authority_ceiling
```

The packet can be **partially intelligible** without pretending the other party shares its ontology. Unknown/withheld/untranslatable values remain typed unknowns, not zeros. When translation fails, preserve a loss-marked branch and a refusal route.

## Inversions, in-betweens and turtles

- **Same word, different measurement:** HOLD comparison. "Flourishing" may denote incompatible practices, units or populations.
- **Different words, same situated contract:** labels alone must not alter an outcome comparison.
- **Same symbolic artifact, changed rights:** HOLD cultural transfer until source-community provenance, consent scope, benefit obligations and revocation are genuinely reviewed. A synthetic string pointer is not that review.
- **Same outcomes, different beneficiaries:** do not declare equivalence; distribution, control, extraction and ecological externalities matter.
- **Same relations, changing values:** keep future preferences, horizons and dissent versioned; optimization itself may change what participants value.
- **No shared language:** coordinate first on bounded observations, safe experiments, refusal and correction, not an invented universal translation.
- **Recursive pull over pull:** use stable IDs, bounded depth/compute, cycle detection, explicit information-gain stopping, and retain unresolved edges. A recursive graph is not an infinite work mandate.
- **Reverse challenge:** ask what evidence would disprove the claimed benefit, whether a no-action path is better, and whether the alleged beneficiaries would reject the intervention.

## Source-level adversarial gate in this draft

The existing `model.py` Pareto comparator previously accepted the same six metric **names** as sufficient for comparing vectors. That was a semantic equivalence assumption. R0A requires an explicit per-axis measurement contract, affected-population scope and rights context. It rejects comparisons across mismatched contracts rather than flattening them. A culturally transferred artifact requires explicit source-community, consent and benefit-return references even to enter this **synthetic** comparison, while a rights violation remains a hard veto.

`test_model.py` adds six negative cases: identical words with different contracts, affected-population mismatch, rights-context mismatch, missing cultural provenance, cultural transfer with rights veto, and absent situated scope. Existing tests still require that G_dSide can lose to supply and that a label never creates dominance.

**Limit:** these tests validate structural refusal only. They cannot verify that a community exists, that its consent is authentic, that a benefit is delivered, that a metric is morally sound, or that any future prediction is calibrated. A real transfer would require affected-party review and independently verified authority and effects.
