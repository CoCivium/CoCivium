# G_dSide R0B | Situated futures, nonmaterial relations and bounded meta-inversion

**State:** source-only adversarial research amendment in existing draft PR #379, under existing G_dSide issues #377/#378. **Not** CoLex canon, a new ontology, a verified social theory, a validated future-attractor model, cultural consent, receiver ACK, runtime or evidence of a metaphysical force.

## The correction

The earlier CoSubstrate / CoMeaning direction remains useful for translation, lineage and human explanation. It is **not sufficient** as the invariant that moves a future-oriented proposal across cultures, models, generations, institutions, physical devices or unknown substrates.

A shared word can hide incompatible measurement practices. A translated word can preserve a practical agreement. A culturally borrowed symbol can preserve its public description while changing control, benefit, consent, refusal and credit. Those are different transitions.

**Candidate carrier:** a versioned, situated bundle of *observed interactions, affordances, constraints, permissions, affected parties, source rights, possible transitions, alternative futures, counterfactual consequences, uncertainty, and independently observed effects*. Meaning is an important, revisable, observer-relative projection **of** this bundle, not a guaranteed common root.

This is **not** a meaning-free graph. The choice of edges, populations, metrics, horizons and desirable futures is itself normative and culturally situated. No algorithm may infer consent or a universal good from shared metric labels, graph connectivity or numerical superiority.

## Separate layers

1. **Observation / relation:** who did what, where, under what conditions, with which source and uncertainty. Description is not endorsement.
2. **Situated interpretation:** which community, person, model or institution assigns which senses, purposes, analogies or refusals; record nontranslation, contradiction and history.
3. **Normative proposal:** whose preferred future, affected population, rights, consent, ecological limits, distributional harms, benefits and vetoes. A high forecast score never supplies authority.
4. **Transition and effect:** feasible intervention, causal assumptions, null/no-action alternative, rollback, receipt, receiver-origin readproof, revision and successor.

A future "pull" is an explicit comparison of *candidate transitions*, not a force, prophecy, divine instruction, or authority. Preserve plural Pareto alternatives, contested dimensions and the possibility that a simpler supply/demand policy wins.

The packet can be **partially intelligible** without pretending the other party shares its ontology. Unknown/withheld/untranslatable values remain typed unknowns, not zeros. When translation fails, preserve a loss-marked branch and a refusal route.

## Inversions, in-betweens and turtles

- **Same word, different measurement:** HOLD comparison. "Flourishing" may denote incompatible practices, units or populations.
- **Different words, same situated contract:** labels alone must not alter an outcome comparison.
- **Same symbolic artifact, changed rights:** HOLD cultural transfer until source-community provenance, consent scope, benefit obligations and revocation are genuinely reviewed. A synthetic string pointer is not that review.
- **Same outcomes, different beneficiaries:** do not declare equivalence; distribution, control, extraction and ecological externalities matter.
- **Same relations, changing values:** keep future preferences, horizons and dissent versioned; optimization itself may change what participants value.
- **No shared language:** coordinate first on bounded observations, safe experiments, refusal and correction, not an invented universal translation.
- **Recursive pull over pull:** use stable IDs, bounded depth/compute, cycle detection, explicit information-gain stopping, and retain unresolved edges. A recursive graph is not an infinite work mandate.
- **Reverse challenge:** ask what evidence would disprove the claimed benefit, whether a no-action path is better, and whether the alleged beneficiaries would reject the intervention.

## Source-level adversarial gate in this draft

The existing `model.py` Pareto comparator previously accepted the same six metric **names** as sufficient for comparing vectors. That was a semantic equivalence assumption. R0A requires an explicit per-axis measurement contract, affected-population scope and rights context. It rejects comparisons across mismatched contracts rather than flattening them. A culturally transferred artifact requires explicit source-community, consent and benefit-return references even to enter this **synthetic** comparison, while a rights violation remains a hard veto.

`test_model.py` adds six negative cases: identical words with different contracts, affected-population mismatch, rights-context mismatch, missing cultural provenance, cultural transfer with rights veto, and absent situated scope. Existing tests still require that G_dSide can lose to supply and that a label never creates dominance.

**Limit:** these tests validate structural refusal only. They cannot verify that a community exists, that its consent is authentic, that a benefit is delivered, that a metric is morally sound, or that any future prediction is calibrated. A real transfer would require affected-party review and independently verified authority and effects.

## R0B: nonmaterial, unusual and self-referential relations

**No second ontology and no hidden actuator.** These are typed lenses over the existing CoOntPrinciple, CoTheoryAll, CoAura, CoNull, CoEnerget, CoInterstice and CoOrbWare inversion architecture, not new universal primitives.

**Material/nonmaterial is not a truth ranking.** An abstract proof, a social promise, a reported experience, a calibrated physical measurement and a claimed metaphysical signal are different referent and epistemic classes. A promise can change human behavior without implying a novel physical field. A reported sensation can be meaningful without proving an external source. Quantum correlations, where relevant, are subject to actual physical models and experimental constraints; "spooky" is not a substitute for a measurement protocol.

**Causal escalation:** a synthetic claim may be structurally *eligible for independent review* only when it declares physical measurement, calibrated independent replication and controls for ordinary causes, blinding, leakage and preregistration. Those fields are **untrusted strings** in this harness. Even a fully populated synthetic record is not authenticated evidence, physical causation, supernatural contact, or action authority.

**Meta recursion:** the source-only walker detects a cycle along the current path, distinguishes a shared descendant from a cycle, and stops on explicit depth/node limits. This models an anti-loop invariant, not cross-session continuity, automatic CoEvo+, or an elected runtime actuator. Unvisited branches remain addressable.

**Effect boundaries:** all claims and meta walks return `effect_authority=NONE`; nothing activates sensors, microphones, devices, mail, finance, publication, policies, new watchers, or unattended jobs. Existing CoStead/WorkID/CoOrb recovery and one-writer rails remain separate proof obligations.

## R0D: In-between interaction is a typed derived result (2026-11-15 UTC)

The four-term pair-interaction operator `I(A,B)=V(A+B)-V(A)-V(B)+V(empty)` is meaningful only if all four counterfactual observations share one explicitly declared measurement contract, affected population, rights context, applicability regime and horizon. It is *not* an identified causal effect merely because the arithmetic works.

`model.InteractionObservation` requires a finite non-boolean scalar, source pointer, all five comparison-context fields and an explicit rights-veto status. A missing counterfactual is HOLD, not zero. Mismatched contexts are `INCOMMENSURABLE_INTERACTION`; unknown rights or an explicit veto cannot be bypassed by an attractive result. A shared synthetic source pointer is not proof of authentic consent or independently observed outcomes.

`interaction` returns an immutable `InteractionResult` carrying the original unit, scope, rights context, applicability, horizon and all four source references, with `epistemic_state=SYNTHETIC_DERIVED_ONLY` and `effect_authority=NONE`. The result **cannot automatically feed the same operator as a new observation**. An additional observation/provenance contract would be required for a recursive turtle, which prevents derived numerics from silently self-certifying.

Six negative-control families plus one recursive-derived-output test extend the existing stdlib CI on the elected draft PR #379. This closes a **source-only mathematical composition gap**. It is not a new unit registry, empirical benchmark, G_dSide authority, CoStead receiver, universal UX runtime, or real-world consent proof.

## R0C: Are real things defined by the between, rather than quantum matter?

**Decision: do not assert either exclusive foundation.** For Civ2+/CoAll *coordination software*, the useful **operational unit** is often a situated interaction or a possible transition with identity, participants, boundaries, physical realization where relevant, evidence, rights and outcomes. This does **not** prove that every physically real entity is reducible to relations, that relata disappear, that quantum mechanics is inapplicable, or that a metaphorical nonmaterial carrier exists. It is a design preference for interoperable work records, not a discovery in physics or a compulsory metaphysics.

### Eight concurrent lenses on one WorkID

1. **Relata:** participants, artifacts, institutions, bodies, devices or other addressable entities, with explicit identification confidence. A relation need not erase its endpoints.
2. **Interaction:** a typed, directed/undirected, scoped relation; observed or proposed, not self-authenticating.
3. **Event / change:** a time-bounded occurrence or transition, with a before/after contract and uncertain causal attribution.
4. **Interstice:** a bounded interval, boundary, silence, absence, delay, gap, or unchosen alternative. `NOT_OBSERVED` is not `EXPLICITLY_ABSENT` or consent.
5. **Material realization:** physical systems, biological substrates, classical/quantum devices, energy and instruments where causal or performance claims actually require them. `ABSTRACTION_NE_NEW_PHYSICS`.
6. **Situated meaning/value:** observer, affected population, interpretive tradition, disputed rights, benefit, refusal and translation losses.
7. **Evidence:** source bytes, conditions of observation, measurement units, calibration, uncertainty, falsifiers, and competing interpretations.
8. **Authority / receiver effect:** who may act, exact WorkID and version, revocation, no-action, result, accepted receiver evidence, recoverability.

These are **overlapping projections**, not eight new ontological strata or a mandatory global dictionary. Some domains use only a subset, and new categories must be justified by discrimination or operational advantage. Ontic structural realism, process philosophy and entity-centered models are competing philosophical viewpoints, not a consensus that relational structure alone proves existence (see [SEP structural realism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/structural-realism/) and [SEP process philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/process-philosophy/)).

### Discriminating failure / inverse cases

- Same object IDs and the same `flourishing` score, but different evidence-generating conditions or physical substrate assumptions: **HOLD comparison** until an applicability contract matches and, for actual claims, is independently supported.
- Same cultural label, changed affected-party rights, benefit recipient or refusal: **HOLD**, notwithstanding identical graph topology.
- Same relation text, different temporal direction or provenance: do not presume identical cause, consent or effect.
- Different labels / hardware substrates under the *same declared synthetic* operational contract: may compare **only for that stated fixture**, not infer a single material ontology or cross-substrate performance parity.
- Pauses, silences, empty intervals and failures to receive a reply: may be recorded as interstices, but are **not** intention, rejection, a supernatural signal or a permission grant.
- The absence of physical observations does not prove a nonphysical mechanism; conversely social obligations, promises and experiences can matter without a novel force.
- A rejected schema mutation or unauthorized receiver write must not destroy a separately held pending-work draft.
- A recursively challenged interpretation must have a finite budget and preserve dissent and `UNKNOWN` after stopping.
