# CoRegenerative+ R0 | Growth with control, not endless agitation

**State:** Offline synthetic challenge / public R&D candidate. **Not** a deployed CoCivium control plane, evidence of adoption, physical energy engine, scientific definition of `G_d`, or actual promotional campaign.

## What the model challenges

A perturbation in a dissipative system usually fades. Reinforcement can compensate for losses *only if energy, material, attention, voluntary contributions, or other convertible resources are available*. Positive feedback without damping can create instability and abuse. Universal everlasting growth in a finite bounded system is **not** a valid acceptance criterion.

Operational goal: **increase verified benefit, autonomy and learning per resource invested while constraining risk, resource use, and unsolicited effects.** Sometimes the correct cycle is to stop, cool, retract, or preserve a correction.

The toy model has (unitless) `useful`, `reserve`, and `capacity` stocks. Each turn (a) retains part of prior usefulness; (b) optionally spends from reserve only if evidence/consent/currentness/risk gates pass; (c) converts spent reserve into synthetic useful outcomes; (d) assumes some outcomes lead to **opt-in** reinvestment; (e) caps produced value by finite capacity. `reserve_from_benefit` is a social assumption about voluntary further help, **not** a thermodynamic law or proof of self-generated energy.

## CoHighlightedAsset+ / CoSpiff+ flywheel

`credible source → appropriate public doorway → visitor chooses task → demonstrable useful outcome → independent critique/correction → better asset and discoverability → opt-in reuse/contribution → capacity investment → next independent outcome`

**Reverse controls:** privacy gate, consumer exit, evidence calibration, recipient overload protection, capped promotion, diversity of challengers, no artificial traffic, and `STOP` when added value is not verified. Main-README `COINDEX_KEY` items are stable placeholders, not automatically verified live sites. CoSpiff and CoEBE already own first-use and survivability gates: this experiment contributes an adversarial *feedback* canary, not an additional control plane.

## Risks and reality checks

- A high click rate can accompany *low evidence quality*. The deliberate naive attention policy consumes resource in this case; its apparent popularity does not give it an earned promotion.
- Without reserve or fresh voluntary input, decay wins. With finite capacity, even profitable trajectories saturate.
- Human contribution, corrections, and recovery are valuable even when their benefits are delayed or cannot be represented by this scalar model. Never silently choose the scalar over privacy, rights, fairness, or consent.
- Different moral values cannot be legitimately collapsed into a supposedly objective `G_d` scalar. Keep `G_d` as a typed stewardship, metaphor, or falsifiable research-hypothesis lane, separate from this engineering controller.
- No asset is promoted based on real audience data: the `qualify_highlight()` demonstration allows **public, source-qualified candidates only** and returns `NO_INDEPENDENT_USER_OUTCOME`.
- Technical PASS is not proof of real user benefit. Engagement-only success should FAIL the high bar. A user can always exit and take their data.

## Independent promotion gate

1. Link one *actually working* safe public first-use asset, after custom-domain privacy/routing review.
2. With informed consent, have someone outside CoCivium complete a useful task; preserve correction and explicit negative feedback.
3. Compare ordinary Start Here versus a highlighted contextual asset; measure **task outcome, comprehension, attention cost, accessibility, privacy errors, and opt-out**, not just clicks.
4. Have a second unrelated reviewer reproduce the result and a separate custodian cold-recover the artifact.
5. Publish negative results and only elevate a workflow when the benefit holds after a source/domain/model failure.
