# CoAkimbo+ / CoInterstice+ | 24 discriminating questions

These are prioritized suggestions, not 24 answered research results. Every inquiry requires an explicit null, falsifier, budget, receiver and provenance. Existing CoQuestionAtlas and CoQA corpora should be de-duplicated against these before any expansion.

## P0 | physical observation versus interpretation
1. Can a blinded reviewer classify geometric shadow and detector speckle in the toy figure above chance with axes hidden and then revealed?
2. Does reversing only illumination direction move predicted shadow regions while source geometry remains pinned?
3. How much of a detector dark patch remains when phase-screen realization changes but geometry is unchanged?
4. In a measured sample, do co- and cross-polarized contrasts change as independently predicted by a stated Maxwell-based solver?
5. Which detector artifacts survive raw dark-frame, flat-field, fixed exposure and camera-rotation controls?
6. What residual would genuinely differentiate existing rough-scattering models, rather than merely exceed a visually chosen threshold?

## P0 | unambiguous evidence classes
7. What evidence distinguishes PHYSICALLY_ABSENT, BELOW_DETECTION, SHADOWED, DARK_BY_INTERFERENCE and NOT_TESTED?
8. Can a beautiful CoOrb projection make an uncertain claim look established even when its typed source remains UNKNOWN?
9. Are negative results still visible after a source is compressed into a one-node CoOrb?
10. When a source dataset is withdrawn or a consent grant revoked, do all derived renderings and worker permissions correctly fail closed?
11. Can two reviewers independently reproduce one quantitative result from raw inputs rather than mutually cite the same agent-generated summary?
12. Which patterns vanish under blinded, shuffled or mismatched-controls tests?

## P1 | controlled amplification versus runaway
13. Under what measured conditions does positive feedback improve externally verified benefit per resource unit?
14. At what boundary does the same feedback amplify false positives, maintenance debt, coercion or attention burden?
15. When does a balancing controller retain a healthy dynamic cycle rather than freeze innovation?
16. Can a selective amplifier lose to a simple damped baseline under adversarial distribution shift? Preserve that loss.
17. Can a viable newcomer complete a useful task with the founding core entirely unavailable?
18. Does the edge-to-core return of a critical correction change the next selected experiment or just produce another public pointer?

## P1 | alternate inversions, G_d / ET hypothesis lanes
19. Does swapping core and edge authority create ECC-style local mini-cores rather than real CoEBE balance?
20. Which CoG_dIn candidate specifies observables, units (if empirical), a genuine contrasting prediction and a possible falsifier?
21. Can an anomaly be reproduced on an independently controlled detector and ruled out as instrument, human processing or known natural physics?
22. Does the ET narrative predict anything more accurately than existing astrophysical and terrestrial baselines, without hindsight-based pattern selection?
23. Can a symbolic/spiritual CoOrbWare rendering inspire inquiry while its factual and causal status stays visibly unpromoted?
24. Which CoBook/Substack representation leads a new consenting visitor to an actual reproducible result with less friction than plain text, while preserving rights and accessible alternatives?
